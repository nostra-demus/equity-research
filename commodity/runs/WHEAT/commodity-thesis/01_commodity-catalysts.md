## The One to Watch

**USDA WASDE — August 12, 2026**

This is the highest-impact single dated event in the next four months. The August WASDE delivers the first survey-based estimate of Russia's final harvest — the market's single largest supply uncertainty, with a range of 85–91 MMT between the bear and bull scenarios. Russia's crop is the primary offset to the US and Australian shortfalls: at 91 MMT it shrinks the global deficit to near zero; at 85 MMT it widens it to 9 MMT and potentially triggers a revision to the world exporter STU toward 7.5%, a level with no precedent since the 2007–08 food-crisis era. On top of that, the August WASDE coincides with the close of the US spring-wheat (HRS) grain-fill window, so the report also delivers the first survey-based US HRS production estimate from a crop that is still in its weather-sensitive phase now. The combination of the two largest remaining supply uncertainties — Russia harvest + HRS yield — makes August 12 the single most market-moving scheduled date in the calendar. It falls within a week of the second scheduled NOAA ENSO update (August 13), making the two-day window of August 12–13 the highest-information period for wheat pricing in the next quarter.
