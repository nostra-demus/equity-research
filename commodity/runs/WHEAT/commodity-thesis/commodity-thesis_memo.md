## 2. What This Module Found

The supply side is genuinely tighter than the headlines look: world use is running about 4 MMT ahead of world production in MY 2026/27 (823.2 vs 819.1 MMT, USDA WASDE Jul-2026), and the major-exporter stocks-to-use ratio (how many months of supply the big exporters are carrying, roughly speaking) is near 8% — its tightest since the early 2010s. US HRW production is at a 56-year low, and NOAA puts an 81% probability on a very strong El Niño weather pattern by Oct–Dec 2026, which could push Australia's crop below the already-reduced 26.7 MMT ABARES forecast.

The problem is what the price already knows. Front-month CBOT SRW is 632¢/bu; the module estimates the fundamental-only floor at $5.50–5.80/bu — implying $0.50–0.80/bu of the current price is a Black Sea war-risk premium (Kerch Strait / Azov / Odesa disruption). That premium is binary: a ceasefire or maritime-corridor deal (as happened in 2022) removes it in days. Meanwhile the futures curve is in unbroken contango (each later contract prices higher than the front one — costly for a long holder), draining roughly 8–9%/yr from a long position via roll drag, and ETF investors have already pulled about $39.5M from WEAT over the past month despite the rally.

The one asymmetric upside: managed money is deeply net short (~−60,432 contracts on CBOT SRW as of 2026-07-01, near a 3-year bearish extreme), so any sustained supply shock forces short-covering on top of the fundamental move. But crowded shorts have persisted for months before without a catalyst.

## 4. What Would Change This Read

An upgrade requires observed (not forecast) evidence. The synthesis lists four upgrade triggers:

1. **Aug 12 WASDE** confirms Russia's crop at or below 85 MMT (vs USDA 88 MMT / private 89–91 MMT) — the world's biggest supply offset has failed.
2. **Aug 13 NOAA/CPC ENSO update** raises the very-strong-El-Niño probability above 90%, combined with an ABARES September downgrade of Australia below 24 MMT.
3. **Weekly CFTC COT** shows managed-money net short narrowing below 45k contracts — real short-covering in motion, not just a precondition.
4. **USDA Weekly Export Sales** show US wheat taking share from Black Sea sources — buyers re-routing, disruption sustained.

Any one justifies revisiting Buy; all four together would be a high-conviction entry.

Downgrade trigger (the bear case): a Black Sea ceasefire or maritime-corridor deal restores Russian exports (88 MMT crop, zero export duty), price retreats 8–13% to the $5.50–5.80/bu fundamental floor, funds do not cover into a fall, ETF outflows accelerate. This is the single killer risk — binary, reversible, and it happened in days in 2022.

## 6. Plain-English Glossary

- **CBOT SRW (ZW) front-month:** the nearest-to-expiry wheat futures contract on the Chicago Board of Trade for Soft Red Winter wheat.
- **Contango / roll drag:** later-dated futures priced higher than the front contract; a long holder loses value each time they roll to the next contract — roughly −8 to −9%/yr here.
- **Stocks-to-use ~8%:** the major exporters are carrying about 8% of a year's usage as inventory — a rough proxy for how thin the buffer is; low is tight.
- **Managed money net short (CFTC COT):** the position report showing large speculators are net betting on prices falling; crowded short means most of the natural sellers have already sold.
- **WEAT ETF:** the retail-accessible wheat futures ETF; flow into or out of it is a rough gauge of investor appetite.
- **Fundamental-only price floor $5.50–5.80/bu:** the price the module estimates supply/demand alone (without the war-risk premium) would support — used as the reference for how much of spot is geopolitical.
- **MMT:** million metric tons.
