## The one to watch

**FOMC September 15–16, 2026 (dot-plot meeting) — the single highest-impact scheduled event in the next four months.**

By September 16, the Fed will have in hand two additional NFP reports (August 7, September 5) and two additional CPI prints (August 12, September 11) beyond what it saw at the June meeting. The September FOMC is the only meeting in this window that includes the dot plot, meaning it will produce a formal, published revision to the rate-path forecast — not just a statement. That makes it qualitatively different from the July decision (no dot plot, less market impact).

The stakes are binary and asymmetric for gold. If the June NFP miss (57k vs 110k) is confirmed by a second soft print and inflation declines toward 2.5–2.7%, the September dot plot will almost certainly show at least one 2026 cut. A confirmed rate-cut cycle, even a shallow one, would unwind the real-yield headwind that has been the primary driver of gold's 25% correction — the setup for a strong move back toward $4,500+. If, on the other hand, July–August data re-accelerates (inflation sticky, jobs rebound), the September dot plot could show a hike, sending the 10y real yield above 2.3–2.4% and erasing the July rebound entirely.

The three catalysts between now and September 16 — July CPI (2026-07-14), July FOMC (2026-07-29), and August CPI (2026-09-11) — each function as a leading indicator of what the September dot plot will say. Watch them in sequence: they are not independent events but a chain whose cumulative direction determines gold's largest near-term outcome.
