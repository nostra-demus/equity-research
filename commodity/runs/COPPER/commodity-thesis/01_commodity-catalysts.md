## The one to watch

**White House decision on refined copper tariff (Section 232) — imminent as of 2026-07-02.**

This is the single highest-impact dated event for copper over the next four months. The Commerce Department delivered its refined-copper market assessment to the President on or around June 30, 2026, as required by the July 2025 proclamation. The President's decision on whether to impose a 15% (January 2027) rising to 30% (January 2028) tariff on refined copper (cathodes and anodes) could arrive at any point in July 2026 — it is provably imminent, with a known legal trigger, rather than vague.

The reason it dominates: the entire ~$1,000–1,500/t tariff-expectation premium currently embedded in LME copper (from ~$11,500–12,000/t structural floor toward ~$13,200/t current) is contingent on this binary. A tariff confirmation triggers a second frontloading wave and Goldman Sachs' $14,000+/t target; a deferral or rejection removes the premium and exposes the 652,000 t COMEX inventory overhang as a demand air-pocket for the next 12–18 months. Critically, speculative positioning (COT Index ~96/100) and fading ETF flows (CPER –$221M outflows in Q2 2026) mean the downside scenario is self-reinforcing: a tariff disappointment combined with a crowded-long unwind has the structure of a sharp selloff, not a gentle fade. No other catalyst in the next four months is as imminent or as binary as this.
