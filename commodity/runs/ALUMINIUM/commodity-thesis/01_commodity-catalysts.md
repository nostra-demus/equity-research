## The one to watch

- **The Yunnan wet-to-dry hydropower transition (~November 2026 onward)** is the single highest-impact item on this calendar: the supply-demand module already identifies it as the biggest swing factor over the next two to three quarters, and a repeat of past curtailments (up to ~400,000 t) would hit supply exactly when the exchange-visible buffer is already at its tightest since 2022 (~284,600 t, 2026-07-15) — a compounding, not offsetting, shock. Its timing is a seasonal pattern rather than a fixed calendar date, so the nearer-term item to monitor for entry/exit timing is the **weekly LME COTR** (next due 2026-07-21): it is the only lever that can confirm or deny whether the record Nov-2025 fund net long has persisted into mid-2026, which the macro-positioning module flags as the live risk that a sound supply-deficit thesis could still get a rough, headline-driven entry.
