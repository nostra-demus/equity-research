## 3. Did the original thesis play out?

Partially, and in a way that needs to be read carefully. The **price** has moved strongly in the stock's favor — a real, benchmark-beating rally. But the original thesis's Watchlist call and its rating cap were never about near-term price direction; they were conditioned on two specific binaries (Iveco financing structure, TMF Holdings AGM outcome) that were explicitly *not yet resolved* at decision time. Neither is resolved now either. The rally therefore looks like the market re-rating the CV business's underlying quality (72% ROCE, improving margins, record June auto sales industry-wide) ahead of those two risks being cleared — which is exactly the situation DECISION_LEDGER.md §10 exists to flag: a price outcome that has **decoupled** from the thesis's actual, still-open decision drivers. It is too early to call this "luck" or "skill" — that judgment has to wait until Iveco's financing structure and the AGM's real vote margin are both known.
