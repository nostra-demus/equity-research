## 7. Bar Assessment

**Bar is low.**

Three months ago, the Street was modelling FY 2026 EPS at AED 2.32 and revenue at AED 58.2bn. Today those estimates stand at AED 1.95 and AED 53.1bn — cuts of ~16% and ~9% respectively. The primary driver of this compression was a single large revision on Jun-11-2026 (EPS from AED 2.46 to AED 1.93, target from AED 20.50 to AED 15.00), which appears to have followed the Q1 2026 print and a re-assessment of the FY 2026 delivery cadence. Against this sharply lower bar, Emaar's recent track record is one of consistent beats: +11% to +23% EPS surprises across FY 2023–2025, with Q1 2026 also ahead of pre-quarter estimates. The contracted sales backlog of ~AED 127bn globally provides strong revenue visibility, and FY 2026 consensus revenue of AED 53.1bn compares to FY 2025 actual revenue of AED 49.6bn — only a ~7% growth assumption, which looks undemanding given the backlog size and the Q1 2026 run-rate of AED 12.4bn (which annualises to roughly AED 49–50bn before seasonal H2 weighting). In short, estimates have been cut hard, the company has a history of beating, and the bar is now below where it was when the year began.
