# Customer And Geography Map — EMAR

## 1. Customer Map

Emaar Properties PJSC (EMAR) sells to a fragmented retail customer base. No individual residential buyer is disclosed as a material revenue concentration. The Capital IQ Customers export contains only intra-group entities (subsidiaries, JV companies) and two retail mall tenants — none of which represent external customer revenue concentration. The company does not disclose any single external customer by name or percentage in the FY2025 investor deck or FY2024 annual report.

| Customer Type | Importance (% of revenue if disclosed) | Long-term Contract? (Y/N/Not disclosed) | Evidence | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential property buyers (UAE) — individuals purchasing off-plan units | ~73% of group revenue (UAE Development AED 36,443 Mn = 73.5% of FY2025 total AED 49,557 Mn) | N — off-plan sales, typically 3–5 year instalment plans per project; not long-term recurring contracts | FY2025 Investor Presentation (Feb-12-2026), slide 13 — UAE Development revenue AED 36,443 Mn; slide 7 — UAE Development segment |
| Mall retail tenants (UAE Malls segment) | ~12% of group revenue (AED 5,754 Mn of AED 49,557 Mn in FY2025) | Y — 3–5 year base lease terms (WALT ~2.9 years); base rent escalates at a pre-defined rate | FY2025 Investor Presentation (Feb-12-2026), slides 22, 24–25 — Emaar Malls revenue AED 5,754 Mn; WALT ~2.9 years; 11% of GLA expires 2026 |
| Hotel guests and F&B customers (Emaar Hospitality) | ~5% of group revenue (AED 2,326 Mn of AED 49,557 Mn in FY2025) | N — transactional, no long-term customer contracts disclosed | FY2025 Investor Presentation (Feb-12-2026), slide 32 — Hospitality revenue AED 2,326 Mn; 82% occupancy |
| International residential property buyers (Egypt, Turkey, India, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, Lebanon) | ~5% of group revenue (International Development AED 2,570 Mn = 5.2% of AED 49,557 Mn) | N — off-plan sales, similar instalment structure to UAE | FY2025 Investor Presentation (Feb-12-2026), slides 13, 20 — International Development ~5.2% contribution |

**Note on individual customer concentration:** No single named external customer is disclosed as exceeding any threshold. The company explicitly notes a customer default rate of ~0.5% of UAE sales value, consistent with a highly fragmented individual-buyer base. [FY2025 Investor Presentation (Feb-12-2026), slide 16]

## 4. Read

Emaar is not concentrated on the customer side but is heavily concentrated on the geography side. No single buyer, tenant, or named customer accounts for a material share of revenue — the residential development business sells to tens of thousands of individuals and the malls business to hundreds of retail tenants, keeping customer concentration low. The geographic story is the opposite: 93% of FY2025 revenue came from the UAE, and nearly all of that from Dubai. This geographic concentration is not contractually locked: the 73% of revenue from UAE residential development flows from off-plan sales that convert to recognised revenue as units reach completion — a pipeline that depends on continued buyer demand in a single city. The single biggest dependency the synthesiser must carry forward is that Emaar is, in substance, a bet on Dubai's property market and tourism economy, with international operations representing a small and recently declining offset at roughly 7% of revenue.
