## 2. Dominant Segment

**Real Estate (UAE Development) is the dominant segment by both revenue and profit.**

By revenue it contributed 80% of the FY2025 total (AED 39,550 Mn out of AED 49,557 Mn), and by segment profit before tax it contributed approximately 77% (AED 19,752 Mn of AED 25,656 Mn total NPBT). Within Real Estate, UAE Development alone — at AED 36,443 Mn — accounts for 73% of group revenue and, per the investor deck, 66% of group EBITDA. The business is therefore a property developer first: nearly three-quarters of the cash it generates comes from selling homes and commercial units in the UAE, recognised progressively as construction milestones are hit under IFRS 15.

The Leasing and Malls segment, while only 15% of revenue, punches well above its revenue weight in profit terms (21% of NPBT at the segment level, before group costs), because its margins are structurally higher — Emaar Malls EBITDA margin is ~86% versus ~46% for UAE Development. This gap matters: if and when the property sales cycle turns, the malls business is what sustains the group's cash generation. The management deck explicitly flags that recurring businesses (malls + hospitality + others) represent 21% of revenue but 32% of EBITDA, signalling the margin quality difference.

## 3. Segment Disclosure Quality

**Consistency year-over-year:** The three IFRS reportable segments — Real Estate, Leasing/Retail/Related, and Hospitality — have been consistently defined across FY2020 through FY2025 per the Capital IQ extract, with no reclassifications in the five-year window. The investor deck added a "JV reclassification" footnote in FY2025 (Malls JV assets moved from "Others" to "Emaar Malls"), which is a management-presentation change only and does not affect the IFRS segment numbers.

**"Other" / "Corporate" bucket:** The "Others" segment in the IFRS segment note is small — AED 447 Mn of NPBT in FY2025 (roughly 2% of the segment total before unallocated items). Unallocated SG&A (AED −1,314 Mn) and net finance income (AED +583 Mn) are kept separate from the named segments, which is standard practice. These items are clearly disclosed in the Capital IQ extract and are not hidden inside a large opaque bucket.

**Profit metrics disclosed at segment level:** Yes — Net Profit Before Tax (NPBT) is disclosed at segment level for all three named segments. Depreciation and amortisation by segment is also available. Capital expenditure by segment is disclosed through FY2021 but is absent for FY2022–FY2025 in the Capital IQ extract, which is a gap. Asset allocation by segment is available and complete for all five years.

**Segment definitions changed in last 3 years:** No change to the IFRS segment structure. The investor deck presentation was refined in FY2025 to separately call out "Entertainment, Leasing & Others" as a fifth management bucket, but this is a presentation enhancement, not a restatement of the primary IFRS segments.

**Disclosure gaps for downstream agents:** (1) Capex by segment is missing for FY2022–FY2025 — unit economics and capital intensity analysis must use aggregate capex or asset-based proxies. (2) The audited FY2025 Annual Report with full notes to accounts has not yet been filed in this data pool (the pool holds the preliminary FY2025 results press release and investor deck, plus the full audited FY2024 Annual Report). The segment note in the FY2025 audited filing — when available — may carry additional sub-disclosures. (3) The "Leasing, Retail & Related" IFRS segment bundles Dubai Mall, entertainment venues, and commercial leasing; the investor deck separates entertainment into its own bucket, but IFRS-level margin by sub-activity within this segment is not available.
