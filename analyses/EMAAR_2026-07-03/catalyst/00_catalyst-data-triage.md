## 3. Triage Verdict

**Partial**

The calendar has two hard-dated events that anchor it: the **Sukuk 3 maturity on 15 September 2026** (a specific and evidenced refinancing event, though with limited capital-markets consequence given Emaar's AED 9.75 Bn unrestricted cash vs AED 2.75 Bn sukuk — low distress risk but a monitoring point) and the **Q2 2026 earnings release around 10 August 2026** (CIQ-derived estimate, consistent with the prior quarterly cadence). These two carry proven or near-proven dates and are the strongest time-anchors in the calendar.

The remainder of the forward catalyst set — government policy announcements (AED 200 Bn master plan, Dubai Square opening), new property launch cadence, hotel pipeline commissioning, and potential dividend increase for FY2026 — are either event-dependent with no hard date, or are multi-year in horizon (Dubai Square "3 years"). The Q1 2027 AGM and FY2026 annual results cycle are real but outside a 12-month window from today (2026-07-03).

The large ownership transfer (ICD → Emirates Power Investment LLC, May-11-2026) is a completed structural event, not a forward catalyst.

The calendar will carry two proven near-term events (Q2 earnings, Sukuk maturity) plus a set of material but soft-window catalysts (property launches, government policy, re-rating). It will be a real catalyst calendar, not purely thematic, but one that is weighted toward "events that could happen in the next 12 months" rather than "events with a hard date and two-sided triggers." The verdict is Partial — not Insufficient, because the two hard-dated events and five fully-populated upstream modules give the calendar agent meaningful material to work with.
