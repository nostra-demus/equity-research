## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

The nearest catalyst with a proven date is **Q3 FY2026 earnings, estimated Nov-03-2026**. It matters because Uber has beaten its own EBITDA guidance in four straight quarters and cleared the guided high end in the last two by a near-identical ~2.5% — a real, cost-driven pattern (genuine ex-UK cost-of-revenue improvement of +377 basis points, or 3.77 percentage points, in Q2 FY26 alone) rather than a one-off. Set against that: revenue has missed consensus, narrowly, in each of the last two quarters, and the freshest revision data shows a clear net-downward skew (8 up vs. 19 down analyst revisions for FQ3 2026 revenue in the month after the Q2 print). The two-sided outcome is genuine — a beat on the guided metrics (EBITDA, EPS Normalized) combined with a revenue miss beyond the recent ±0.6% range would be a mixed, not clean, print, and the market's reaction will likely hinge on which side dominates the headline.

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

The catalyst with the largest potential impact is the **Delivery Hero SE acquisition and its financing**, spanning the Q3 2026 bridge-to-term-loan conversion (proven window, imminent) through the H2 2027 tender-offer completion (vague, beyond the 12-month window but already shaping today's balance sheet). At $14.8bn equity value, funded by a €14.2bn bridge equal to roughly 1.2 times Uber's entire pre-deal debt stack, this is the largest capital-allocation decision in the company's history — large enough that, if the bridge is drawn in full, pro-forma net leverage would rise from 1.09x to 3.14x on today's peak EBITDA and the illustrative debt-covenant break point would collapse from a ~73% EBITDA decline (today, comfortably survivable) to a ~30% EBITDA decline — an ordinary recession, not a tail event [`balance-sheet-survival/99_balance-sheet-survival-synthesis.md` §1]. The bullish path is a clean close, on-time integration, and the delivery-scale synergies management has cited actually materializing. The bearish path is a repeat of Uber's own M&A record: one full write-off (Drizly, shut down within 2.5 years) and one reversed divestiture (Careem, sold down in 2023 and bought back to control by mid-2026) already sit in the same pattern, and this deal triggers CLAUDE.md §24 Filter 4 (serial acquirer, debt-funded, near/above the company's own scale) — `management-governance/02` explicitly caps the capital-allocation score on this basis (RF-CAP-004). **A buyer cannot cleanly time this catalyst.** The bridge-conversion terms (maturity, pricing, covenants) are undisclosed anywhere in this pool, and the tender-offer completion is a stated half-year window, not a date, sitting outside this calendar's primary 12-month horizon even though its financing is already live today.

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **Delivery Hero bridge-to-term-loan conversion (Q3 2026, vague window)** — the single largest downside-skewing event on this calendar. Undisclosed terms; if priced above the ~5.2–5.7% indicative market range or if it introduces the first disclosed maintenance covenant (none exists on the current stack), it would confirm the balance-sheet-survival module's finding that this collapses the illustrative covenant-break point from ~73% to ~30% EBITDA decline [`balance-sheet-survival/99` §1].
- **2026 Term Loan maturity (Dec-2026, proven date)** — small in isolation ($2,000M, covered 1.65x by unrestricted cash alone) but a real refinancing event landing on a balance sheet simultaneously funding a $15.7bn-remaining buyback authorization and the Delivery Hero deal.
- **Getir Turkey and Blacklane deal closes (2H 2026 / by end-2026, vague windows)** — both continue the same serial-acquisition pattern (§24 Filter 4) that produced Drizly's full write-off and Careem's reversed divestiture; neither has a disclosed standalone-return framework in this pool.
- **Delivery Hero tender-offer completion (H2 2027, vague, outside the primary window)** — deal failure, repricing, or an extended regulatory review would leave the €14.2bn bridge drawn without the offsetting asset.
- **Undated, thematic legal/regulatory risk (no scheduled date — recorded as undated per CLAUDE.md §17, not as a dated catalyst):** the unresolved shareholder derivative lawsuit (N.D. Cal., filed Jun/Jul-2026) naming the CEO, Chairman, and every sitting director over alleged false safety-oversight disclosures [`management-governance/05_board-and-shareholder-rights.md` §5, RF-MGT-005]; multi-jurisdiction driver-classification litigation (US, France, Switzerland, Spain, Netherlands, New Zealand) that the 10-K itself says could force Uber to "fundamentally change" its business model [`business-model/10_external-dependency.md` §1]; and the disclosed $1.8bn UK HMRC VAT assessment under appeal. None carries a hearing or decision date in this pool — they are risks to monitor, not events to time.

## 5. Calendar Read

Of the nine dated/windowed catalysts identified, three carry proven dates (Q3 FY2026 earnings, the Dec-2026 Term Loan maturity, and the mechanical Q1 FY27 UK-VAT lap) and six are windows — a calendar that is real and evidenced but more vague than proven, roughly one-third proven by count. The near-term proven events skew cautiously constructive on operations (the EBITDA/EPS guidance-beat streak favors another beat) but the calendar's single largest-scale event — the Delivery Hero financing, spanning an imminent but undated bridge conversion through a tender completion outside this window — skews the risk register toward leverage and integration risk, not toward operating momentum. The single most important timing caveat: the catalyst most capable of moving this stock materially (Delivery Hero) is also the one whose actual terms (bridge pricing, covenants, exact completion date) are the least disclosed anywhere in this pool — a buyer betting on the calendar is really betting on operational continuity through Q3/Q4 earnings while the balance-sheet risk from an undated, unpriced acquisition financing builds in the background.
