# Segment Map — UBER

## 1. Segment Table

Figures below are FY2025 (year ended Dec-31-2025), the latest audited annual filing. Revenue and profit shares are calculated on the sum of the three segments (Mobility + Delivery + Freight), i.e. before the unallocated Corporate G&A and Platform R&D line, which is how the company itself presents Segment Adjusted EBITDA in its reconciliation [FY25 10-K, p.57 (Segment Results of Operations); FY25 10-K, Note 13 — Segment Information and Geographic Information].

Revenue shares sum to 100.0% ($29,670M + $17,248M + $5,099M = $52,017M total revenue) [FY25 10-K, p.8484–8502]. Segment Adjusted EBITDA sums to $11,438M before the Corporate G&A and Platform R&D deduction of -$2,708M, reconciling to total Adjusted EBITDA of $8,730M [FY25 10-K, p.8620–8667]. Corporate G&A and Platform R&D is 23.7% of segment-level Adjusted EBITDA ($2,708M / $11,438M) — the "Other" / unallocated bucket for profit purposes is large enough to flag (see Section 3).

## 2. Dominant Segment

Mobility is the dominant segment on both measures: 57.0% of FY25 revenue ($29,670M of $52,017M) and 69.1% of FY25 segment-level Adjusted EBITDA ($7,899M of $11,438M) [FY25 10-K, p.8421–8613]. It is not a single-segment business (Delivery alone contributes 33.2% of revenue and 31.2% of segment profit, both material), but Mobility is the clear profit engine — it earns more than twice Delivery's Adjusted EBITDA dollars ($7,899M vs. $3,572M) despite Delivery growing revenue faster (25% vs. 18% YoY) [FY25 10-K, p.8421–8591]. Freight is immaterial to group profit — it lost $33M at the segment level in FY25 and its Adjusted EBITDA is roughly 0% of the group total either way you cut it [FY25 10-K, p.8601–8613]. Most-recent-quarter data confirm the same ranking under the new segment measure: in H1 FY26 (six months ended Jun-30-2026), Mobility Segment Operating Income was $4,244M (68.4% of the $6,206M segment-level total) versus Delivery's $2,016M (32.5%) and Freight's -$54M (-0.9%) [Q2 FY26 10-Q, p.20687–20785].

## 3. Segment Disclosure Quality

Segments are defined consistently across FY24 and FY25 (Mobility, Delivery, Freight, unchanged since at least FY2019 per the CIQ annual segment history) [Uber Technologies Inc NYSE UBER Financials_Annual.xls, Segments tab], but the profit metric changed: beginning Q1 FY26, Uber switched its segment operating performance measure from Segment Adjusted EBITDA to Segment Operating Income, and recast the comparable prior-year periods to match [Q2 FY26 10-Q, Note 10, p.14216–14221]. This is a genuine measurement-basis change, not just a relabeling — Segment Operating Income includes items (e.g., stock-based compensation, certain depreciation/amortization) that Segment Adjusted EBITDA excluded — so FY25 Adjusted EBITDA figures used in Section 1 above are not directly comparable to the FY26 Segment Operating Income figures cited in Section 2; downstream agents should not blend the two without re-basing. The unallocated "Corporate G&A and Platform R&D" bucket is large relative to segment profit — $2,708M in FY25, equal to 23.7% of the $11,438M segment-level Adjusted EBITDA total, and the company explicitly states its allocation methodology "is periodically evaluated and may change," meaning even the segment-to-corporate split is not fixed methodology [FY25 10-K, p.8621–8637]. Profit is disclosed at the segment level (not just revenue), which is a strength, but sub-segment detail is not: Mobility's financial-partnerships and advertising revenue, and Delivery's Grocery & Retail vs. core restaurant-delivery revenue, are described qualitatively but never quantified separately in the filings reviewed [FY25 10-K, Note 13, p.29312; Note 1]. Geographic segment revenue is disclosed by region (US & Canada, LATAM, EMEA, APAC) but not cross-tabulated against product segment, so a Mobility-vs-Delivery split by geography cannot be derived from the filings [Uber Technologies Inc NYSE UBER Financials_Annual.xls, Segments tab, Geographic Segments section]. No standalone investor deck is present in the data pool to cross-check the filing splits against a management-facing summary [00_data-triage.md, §3].
