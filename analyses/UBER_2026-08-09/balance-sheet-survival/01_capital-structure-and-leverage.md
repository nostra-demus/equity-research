# Capital Structure & Leverage — UBER

Reporting currency: **USD** (millions unless stated). Reporting standard: **US GAAP** — operating leases sit on the balance sheet under ASC 842 as right-of-use assets/liabilities but are **not** treated as debt in the company's own debt note; finance leases are debt-like and are included in gross debt below. All balance-sheet figures below are as of **June 30, 2026** (Q2 FY2026 10-Q, filed Aug-05-2026), the most recent balance sheet in the data pool, with December 31, 2025 (FY2025 10-K, filed Feb-13-2026) and prior fiscal year-ends shown for trend. [Q2 FY26 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets; FY25 10-K, Item 8]

**Headline forward-looking item (read this first):** on July 16, 2026 — two weeks after the June 30, 2026 balance-sheet date and three weeks before this report — Uber signed a business combination agreement to acquire Delivery Hero SE (cash consideration €41.50/share, implied equity value $14.8 billion for 100%, expected close H2 2027) and executed a **€14.2 billion bridge credit agreement** for senior unsecured loans to help fund it. None of this is in the June 30, 2026 gross-debt figures below — it is a subsequent event, undrawn as of the 10-Q filing date. If drawn in full it would roughly double Uber's existing ~$13 billion gross debt stack. [Q2 FY26 10-Q, p.21659–21694 (Pending Acquisition of Delivery Hero) and p.23727 (Liquidity and Capital Resources); business-model/11_capital-allocation-governance.md]

**EBITDA basis used:** "Reported/unadjusted" EBITDA = income from operations + total depreciation & amortization, the same construction `earnings/01_historical-financials.md` uses — **not** Uber's company-defined "Adjusted EBITDA." TTM (Jul-2025–Jun-2026) reported EBITDA is $7,474M. **Uber stopped disclosing company-level Adjusted EBITDA on a quarterly basis starting with the Q1 FY26 10-Q** — the term appears zero times in either the Q1 or Q2 FY26 earnings-call transcripts, and neither 10-Q reconciles an Adjusted EBITDA figure. The $8,730M Adjusted EBITDA figure used in the "On Company Adjusted EBITDA" column above is therefore the **last disclosed value, for FY2025 (year ended Dec-31-2025), roughly seven months stale as of this report's date** — not a current-quarter figure. Every ratio using it should be read with that staleness in mind. [FY25 10-K, Item 7; earnings/01_historical-financials.md §4]

**Cycle-position caveat (not a hard "deep cyclical" flag, but real):** `business-model/07_business-quality.md` scores Uber's cyclicality 47/100 (mixed band) and explicitly warns that "FY2025 EBITDA margin of 12.1%... [is a] near-peak, post-recovery reading" following the one severe demand shock on record (FY2020 revenue −14.3% in COVID) and a still-cyclically-depressed Freight segment (FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA margin −0.6%). Uber has not been GAAP-profitable at the EBITDA line through a full down-cycle — it ran negative unadjusted EBITDA in FY2021–FY2022 — so a true mid-cycle EBITDA cannot be constructed from its own history. As the most conservative available proxy, this report shows leverage against the **3-year average unadjusted EBITDA (FY2023–FY2025: (1,933+3,536+6,312)/3 = $3,927M)**, labelled "recent average, not a true mid-cycle figure":

|---|---:|---:|
| Gross debt / EBITDA | 1.74x | **3.32x** |
| Net debt (strict) / EBITDA | 1.09x | **2.08x** |

The gap between 1.09x and 2.08x is the size of the "how much of today's low leverage ratio is really margin-cycle timing" question — a reader should not treat 1.09x as the through-cycle number.

[Figures: gross debt and cash from Capital IQ Financials_Annual.xls Balance Sheet tab, reconciled exactly to FY25 10-K Item 8 and Q2 FY26 10-Q Note 5 for the two most recent periods; EBITDA from earnings/01_historical-financials.md §1–2]

Leverage fell for two straight years (net debt/EBITDA 2.73x → 1.36x → 0.54x, FY2023–FY2025) as EBITDA more than tripled (margin expansion, not debt paydown — gross debt actually rose modestly) while cash built up from strong free cash flow (FY2025 FCF $9,763M [earnings/01_historical-financials.md]) and from the September 2025 redemption of the higher-coupon 2027 Senior Notes ($700M) and 2028 Senior Notes ($500M) funded by new, lower-coupon 2031/2035 issuance [FY25 10-K, p.9831]. That reversed sharply in H1 FY2026: net debt rose $4.4 billion in six months (0.54x → 1.05x on TTM EBITDA) as Uber drew $2.0 billion under a new June-2026 Term Loan and spent $3.5 billion on share buybacks, pulling cash down from $7,105M to $4,870M even as EBITDA kept growing [Q2 FY26 10-Q, MD&A liquidity discussion; business-model/11_capital-allocation-governance.md]. None of this yet includes the pending €14.2bn Delivery Hero bridge facility (Section 1), which is the far larger swing factor still to come.

## 6A. HoldCo / OpCo & Structural Subordination

Not applicable — no material HoldCo-level debt indicated. Uber Technologies, Inc. (the parent, NYSE-listed entity) is the direct obligor on all Senior Notes and the Term Loan/Credit Agreement; the 2029 Senior Notes are guaranteed by certain material domestic restricted subsidiaries, which strengthens rather than subordinates the structure. The 2028 Exchangeable Senior Notes are collateralized by a pledge of the parent's own Aurora shares, not by a subsidiary claim structurally senior to public debt holders. No separate HoldCo issuing debt structurally subordinated to OpCo cash flows is disclosed. [Q2 FY26 10-Q, Note 5; FY25 10-K, Note 8]

## 7. Leverage Anchor Summary (canonical numbers for downstream agents)

- **Gross debt: $13,033M** (Note 5 bonds/notes/term loan $12,723M + finance leases $310M) as of Jun-30-2026, USD. Excludes $2,008M of operating lease liabilities (US GAAP does not treat these as debt); including them would give an alternative gross-debt view of ≈$15,041M.
- **Net debt: $8,163M — strict basis (§15), the canonical figure for this module.** (Gross debt $13,033M − cash & equivalents $4,870M only; short-term investments and all restricted cash/investments excluded.) A broad basis netting in $521M of short-term investments gives $7,642M — shown for completeness but **not** the canonical figure (the strict/broad gap is immaterial here, ~6%, so strict is used by default per MODULE_RULES with no override reason needed).
- **Cash & liquid investments: $5,391M unrestricted** (cash $4,870M + short-term investments $521M). A further $11,793M of restricted cash + restricted investments exists but is excluded — it collateralizes insurance reserves, not available for debt service.
- **EBITDA base used: $7,474M — reported/unadjusted, TTM (Jul-2025–Jun-2026), latest available.** This is a near-peak, post-recovery reading (business-model/07_business-quality.md cyclicality flag) on a company that has not yet been profitable through a full down-cycle; the most conservative available proxy is the 3-year (FY2023–FY2025) average of $3,927M, labelled "recent average, not a true mid-cycle figure." Company-defined Adjusted EBITDA (last disclosed: $8,730M, FY2025) is **stale** — Uber discontinued quarterly Adjusted EBITDA disclosure after Q4 FY2025.
- **Net debt / EBITDA:** **1.09x** on latest/TTM reported EBITDA; **0.94x** on the stale FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA; **2.08x** on the recent 3-year average EBITDA (conservative proxy).
- **Reporting currency: USD.**

**Flags every downstream agent should carry forward:**
1. The pending **€14.2 billion Delivery Hero acquisition bridge facility** (executed Jul-16-2026, undrawn, not in any figure above) is the dominant forward capital-structure event — potentially near-doubling gross debt. `02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing` and `06_downside-stress-test` should treat it explicitly, not silently ignore it because it postdates the balance sheet.
2. The finance-lease figure in gross debt ($310M) is a **Dec-31-2025** balance carried forward — no Jun-30-2026 update was located in this pool's 10-Q text.
3. Any leverage ratio computed on "Adjusted EBITDA" downstream is riding a **stale, ~7-month-old** company figure the company itself has stopped updating quarterly; prefer the reported/unadjusted TTM EBITDA basis unless a fresher adjusted figure surfaces.
4. Net debt roughly **doubled** in six months (Dec-31-2025 → Jun-30-2026, strict Note-5 basis: $3,416M → $7,853M) on a debt-funded buyback and a new term loan — a real, recent, and rapid re-leveraging trend, before the Delivery Hero deal is even layered on.

Uber is **not net cash** as of the latest balance sheet — net debt (strict) is a positive $8,163M. No net-cash framing applies.
