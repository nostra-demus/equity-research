## What This Module Found

- **Sales are falling, not growing.** Four straight quarters of year-over-year revenue decline (TTM revenue −4.5%, $1,392.2M vs $1,457.6M), driven by Atkins losing shelf space and a genuine share loss — consolidated retail takeaway fell 6.7% while the category it sells into grew 10% in the same 13 weeks, a roughly 17-point share loss, not a soft market [`01_historical-financials.md` §2–3; `02_revenue-drivers.md` §4].
- **Margins are at a five-year low.** Gross margin fell from a 40.7% peak in FY2021 to 36.2% in FY2025 and about 32.5% in the latest quarter. Adjusted EBITDA margin (a cleaned-up measure of operating profit as a share of sales) dropped a genuine ~330 basis points (~3.3 percentage points) over the latest twelve months — real compression, not just an accounting artifact from write-downs [`01_historical-financials.md` §1–2; `03_margin-drivers.md`].
- **The single biggest swing factor is unhedged input costs.** Mainly protein and packaging. About a $27 million swing in Adjusted EBITDA either way — near 12% of the full-year FY2026 profit guide — with zero hedges (no financial protection against the cost moving) and no pass-through clause. SMPL is a pure price-taker on its inputs, with only a lagging, elasticity-uncertain price lever to defend itself [`07_earnings-sensitivity.md` §5–7].
- **The biggest risk is a misread.** Three straight beats that got bigger each time (revenue +0.1%→+7.3%, EPS +8.1%→+19.5%) could easily be narrated as "earnings accelerating," when the real trajectory underneath is four quarters of declining sales, ~330bps of margin damage, and next-year estimates still being cut every month.

## What Would Change This Read

**Would upgrade it (toward improving):** a fourth straight beat plus an initial FY2027 guide showing the September 2026 price increase landing with volume drop-off (elasticity) below management's own "1 or higher" assumption, FY2027 Street estimates stabilizing instead of falling, and Quest bar consumption re-accelerating.

**Would downgrade it (toward "Earnings decelerating"):** a miss below the FQ4 FY26 guided floor ($322M net sales / $52M Adjusted EBITDA); input-cost inflation worsening beyond the guided ~375bps gross-margin decline; Atkins distribution losses accelerating; or a fourth write-down confirming the acquisition value-destruction pattern is not finished. With no proven 2–3 year turnaround record yet (management itself says "early stages"), a downgrade is the more likely near-term move.

**Data that would settle it:** the FQ4 FY26 print and initial FY27 guide (due 2026-10-23); a company-disclosed volume/price/mix walk; a brand-level P&L; a licensed alt-data consumption panel for early warning ahead of the reported number.

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict: Mixed earnings setup** — conflicting signals across revenue (falling), margins (compressing), and quality (material concerns), not one clean direction.
- **Why it could be better than it looks:** the near-term bar is genuinely matched, not sandbagged; three straight widening beats; Quest household penetration (63.7% of nine-month FY26 sales) at a multi-year high and still rising.
- **Why it could be worse than it looks:** four quarters of declining sales, a real ~330bps margin drop, $391.9M of cumulative write-downs, and next-year estimates still being cut every month.
- **What's missing:** no brand-level P&L is disclosed anywhere (SMPL reports one segment), so every brand-level dollar figure here is a labeled inference, not a filed number.
- **Watch next:** FQ1 FY27 — the first full quarter under the September 2026 price increase, with no company guidance yet. The "balanced" FQ4 read does not resolve the 12-month setup.
- **Watch item (non-triggering):** net debt rose 57.6% ($206.0M → $324.6M) on $242.3M of buybacks funded while profit was falling — below the leverage triggers, but a capital-allocation choice worth monitoring at a margin trough.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Adjusted EBITDA** — the company's cleaned-up measure of operating profit (before interest, tax, depreciation, and items it calls one-off); here it excludes the write-downs, which flatters it.
- **Basis points (bps)** — hundredths of a percentage point; 330bps = 3.3 percentage points.
- **Impairment / write-down** — a non-cash accounting charge admitting an acquired brand is worth less than what was paid; $391.9M cumulative here.
- **Hedges** — financial contracts that lock in a cost; "zero hedges" means no protection against input-cost moves.
- **Elasticity** — how much sales volume drops when price rises; "1 or higher" means a price increase is fully or more-than-fully offset by lost volume.
- **Net debt** — total debt minus cash; here $324.6M at FQ3 FY26.
- **Accrual-quality flags** — checks for reported profit outrunning the cash actually collected; 4 of 5 fired.
- **Revision breadth** — the net count of analysts raising vs cutting estimates; −4 to −6 means far more cuts than raises.
