# Catalyst Module — SMPL (Synthesis)

## Abstract

SMPL has one proven, dated near-term catalyst rather than only a thematic story: the FQ4 FY2026 earnings release, estimated Oct-23-2026, where management has already put numbers on the table ($322M–$332M net sales, $52M–$57M Adjusted EBITDA). That print, and the FY2027 guide that comes with it, tests whether a below-sell-through shipment reset masks a real slowdown. The single most important catalyst is the high-single-digit price increase taking effect September 2026, whose net revenue effect — management itself warns elasticity could run "1 or higher" — will not be provable until an unconfirmed FQ1 FY2027 print. The biggest bearish catalyst is that same elasticity outcome, alongside a possible FQ4 miss below the guided floor on Oct-23-2026. The verdict: dated, evidenced near-term catalysts exist, but the one that matters most cannot yet be fully timed.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** The September-2026 price increase meeting or exceeding management's own "1 or higher" elasticity base case would cut roughly $12.8M off Adjusted EBITDA on the affected base — this is not a tail scenario, it is close to what management itself says is plausible on the call that announced the increase [`earnings/07_earnings-sensitivity.md` §2; `catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md` §4].
- **Strongest bull point:** A fourth consecutive beat on Oct-23-2026 (revenue >$338M / Adjusted EBITDA >$60M) paired with an FY2027 initial guide that frames the price increase as adding net revenue and shows Atkins's decline narrowing — a proven date, with a Street consensus matched within 0.5% of the midpoint, so it is a real test, not a layup [`catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md` §1 row 1, §2; `earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md` §3].
- **Single killer risk specific to the calendar:** Reading the Oct-23-2026 beat streak as proof the turnaround has arrived — it cannot be, since it covers only the third and fourth quarters under the new CEO, well short of the 2–3 years of delivered operating improvement §24 requires, and the same print's underlying pattern (FQ3: revenue/EBITDA beat but GAAP gross margin missed) could recur and be missed if a headline beat is taken at face value [`management-governance/01_management-and-track-record.md` §4A; `earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md` §4–§5].
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** Net leverage has already more than doubled in a year (0.54x to 1.18x) funding continued buybacks even as Adjusted EBITDA has been under pressure — the calendar's one capital-return "catalyst" (the Jan-6-2026 $200M authorization increase) is occurring alongside, not instead of, rising leverage, which cuts against reading buyback activity as a clean bullish signal [`balance-sheet-survival/99_balance-sheet-survival-synthesis.md` §1A; `catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md` §4].

## 4. Note To The Final Synthesizer

- The nearest dated, evidenced catalyst is the Oct-23-2026 FQ4 FY2026 print (with attached FY2027 guide) — it tests whether SMPL's below-sell-through shipment reset this quarter masks real demand softness, against a Street consensus matched within 0.5% of management's own guided midpoint.
- The single most important catalyst — the September-2026 price increase and its elasticity outcome — has a proven trigger date but an unconfirmed resolution date. A buyer can time when the price increase starts; a buyer cannot yet time when its net effect becomes provable in the numbers (likely the FQ1 FY2027 print, roughly January 2027, with no confirmed report date in this pool).
- The near-term calendar is two-sided, not clearly bullish: management's own framing of both the FQ4 shipment reset and the price increase carries an explicit, self-disclosed downside case (elasticity "1 or higher," a possible miss below the guided floor).
- The biggest bearish catalyst is the price-increase elasticity meeting or exceeding management's own stated fear (roughly −$12.8M Adjusted EBITDA), closely followed by a possible FQ4 miss below the guided floor on Oct-23-2026.
- The Oct-23-2026 FQ4 print carries a §24 Turnaround flag (Filter 2) and must not be read as conviction-lifting proof of a delivered turnaround on the strength of one more beat — only two quarters under the new CEO have been completed. Separately, a third acquisition before OWYN stabilizes (§24 Filter 4, serial-acquirer) is a named standing risk that, if announced, should not be read as bullish "growth platform" news.
- Timing is proven for the two events that matter most (results date, price-increase start month) but vague or absent for everything else (AGM inferred, buyback-authorization expiry undisclosed, no rating update, no tariff/GLP-1 decision date) — this should drive a moderate, not a low or high, catalyst-timing confidence cap in the master synthesizer's §17 read.
- The biggest missing scheduled-event data point is a confirmed report date for the quarter that actually proves out the price increase's net effect (FQ1 FY2027) — nothing in this pool fixes that date yet.

## 5. Simple Summary

- There is one real, dated catalyst, not just a story: earnings on Oct-23-2026, with management's own numbers already on the table.
- The nearest dated event is Oct-23-2026 — the FQ4 FY2026 print and the first FY2027 guide.
- The most important catalyst is the September-2026 price increase — whether customers keep buying at the higher price or walk away.
- The biggest thing that could go wrong on the calendar is that price increase costing more in lost volume than it gains in price, which management itself says is a real possibility.
- The thesis can lean on timing for the next quarter's print and the price-increase start date, but not for when the price increase's true effect will actually show up in the numbers — that date is not yet confirmed.
- This module is useful for the master synthesizer: it gives one hard near-term date, one dated but unresolved swing factor, and a clear list of what is still vague or undated.
- Do not treat a beat on Oct-23-2026 as proof the turnaround has worked — it is only the fourth quarter of evidence, not the two-to-three years §24 requires.
