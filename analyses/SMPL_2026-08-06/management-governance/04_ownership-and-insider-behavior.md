A vendor cross-check (CIQ Public Ownership Summary, various position dates through Mar/Apr-2026) shows "Individuals/Insiders" at 9.30%, broadly consistent; note CIQ's own ownership-summary total sums to 108.78% of shares outstanding because, per its own footnote, "reporting requirements for holdings data is not aligned with the financials reporting of shares outstanding" — a data-quality quirk in the vendor file, not a real economic overlap, and it does not change the proxy's 8.76% figure.

Separately, subsidiary equity interests are pledged as loan collateral under the company's corporate Credit Agreement [FY2025 10-K, Debt note] — this is ordinary secured corporate borrowing by the company itself, not promoter/insider share pledging, and is a balance-sheet matter outside this agent's scope.

## 5. Ownership / Insider Read

Insiders own a modest 8.76% of SMPL, and most of that is concentrated in one person — Chairman James Kilts — whose stake is roughly three-quarters a costless legacy distribution from the company's 2017 SPAC formation rather than cash paid at market prices; strip him out and the rest of the executive/director group holds under 5%. Set against that thin baseline, the last 12 months show a clear, if modest, positive signal: the Chairman, the CFO, an independent director, and the Chief Commercial Officer collectively put about $1.4 million of their own cash into the stock on the open market, with the two largest buys landing after — not before — the company's own guidance-miss disclosure, while the only comparable open-market sale was a departing accounting officer's routine $114,053 disposition. The biggest risk to minority holders is not pledging (none exists, and it is explicitly banned) or a controlling bloc (there is none — single share class, no controlled-company status, the former sponsor's board rights lapsed in 2022) — it is simply that ownership alignment here is thin: no one in management or on the board has enough personal capital at risk to make this look like an owner-operator story, and the current CEO's post-return stake is not yet disclosed at all.
