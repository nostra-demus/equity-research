## 3B. Lineage & Phoenix Read (A17-02, A17-03)

**Does a predecessor exist?** Yes, and the company names it itself. nVent Electric plc was incorporated in Ireland on 2017-05-30 as the vehicle for **Pentair plc's** separation of its Electrical business, completed 2018-04-30 by distributing one nVent share for every Pentair share (record date 2018-04-17); nVent began trading on 2018-05-01. Behind Pentair sit two brand-lineage entities the company also names: **Federal-Hoffman Corporation** (Pentair's 1988 acquisition, source of the HOFFMAN brand, itself founded by Harry Hoffman in October 1945) and **ERICO Global Company** (Pentair's 2015 acquisition, now nVent's Electrical Connections business). The registry adds one thing the filings do not: the company carried the name **"nVent Electric Limited" from 2017-10-12 to 2017-11-03**, a 22-day private-to-plc re-registration during formation.

**Did the predecessor fail?** **No.** Pentair plc is solvent and still listed. Federal-Hoffman was acquired as a going concern by a listed buyer in 1988. ERICO was acquired as a going concern in 2015 and is now a consolidated nVent subsidiary. No insolvency, liquidation, strike-off or resolution process was found on any of the three.

**Did assets, brands, staff, address, products or customers continue into the current entity?** Yes — the entire Electrical business of Pentair did, brands included. **On what disclosed legal basis and for what consideration?** A **pro-rata spin-off to Pentair's own shareholders** under a separation and distribution, disclosed in nVent's Form 10-12B registration statement, its 8-Ks of April–May 2018, and repeated in every annual report since. That is the textbook clean case: continuity through a formal, disclosed process, with the "consideration" being shares delivered to the predecessor's own shareholders rather than assets moved to an insider at a discount. **No related party re-bought failed assets. No RF-NET-002.**

**Are the marks still shared with a live unrelated user?** No. The nVent, CADDY, ERICO, HOFFMAN, ILSCO, SCHROFF and TRACHTE marks are owned or licensed by **nVent Services GmbH**, a wholly-owned Swiss subsidiary on nVent's own Exhibit 21. The RAYCHEM and Thermal marks left the group with the Thermal Management divestiture on 2025-01-30 — a disclosed sale, not a shared-brand ambiguity. The D-4 corroboration test therefore never engages, because there is no live unrelated proprietor to corroborate.

**The one live lineage fact the synthesis must carry:** the predecessor is clean on lineage but **not clean on its current record** — Pentair plc is a defendant in 2026 securities-fraud class actions covering 2025–26 conduct in its Pool segment. That is set out at 2B.3 with the RF-NET-003 test written out, and it is handed to `12` labelled `predecessor_entity`.
