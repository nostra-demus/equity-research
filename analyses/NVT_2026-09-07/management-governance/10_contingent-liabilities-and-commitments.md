**How A7a-01 is graded, stated plainly.** On the balance sheet as it stood at 30 June 2026, nVent's disclosed contingent exposure is **$168.7m, or 4.23% of the $3,986.9m equity base — inside the Green band.** Add the signed-but-unclosed Maverick earnout and it becomes **$718.7m, 18.03% of net worth (Amber) and 120.12% of $598.3m of LTM profit (Red on the profit limb)**. Applying the conservative default (CLAUDE.md §4), **the item is graded Amber overall and RF-FIN-003 fires** — contingent liability above 5% of net worth.

**What that flag does and does not mean.** The $550.0m driving it is a performance earnout on 2027 and 2028 results at a business nVent has agreed to buy but does not yet own; it becomes payable only in the state of the world where the acquired business hits its numbers, and management's own words point toward paying it — returns are "significantly better if the potential additional considerations are paid" [`nVent news release, 2026-08-24`]. It is not a tax demand, not a judgment, not an adjudicated loss, and not a related-party obligation. **It is also not yet a disclosure failure:** the agreement was signed 24-Aug-2026 and the most recent nVent filing in this pool is the Q2 FY26 10-Q filed 31-Jul-2026, so no filing has yet come due in which it should have appeared. Materiality is graded **High, not Critical**, because the Materiality Thresholds table reserves Critical for adjudicated or adversarial exposure, and this is a commercial purchase-price term.

**RF-CL-001 fires** on the bonds/LC/guarantee line: 10.7 → 75.7 is a 7.08x year-on-year rise, comfortably past the A7a-02 red band, with no item-specific narrative.

**The explanation the company never gave, supplied here and labelled.** nVent completed the acquisition of Avail Infrastructure Solutions' enclosures, switchgear and bus-systems businesses (the "Electrical Products Group") on **1 May 2025** for approximately **$979.6m in cash** [`Q2 FY26 10-Q, Note 5`]. That is a project-and-backlog business selling to power utilities and data centres — exactly the kind of work that requires performance bonds. The FY2025 jump in bond and LC face value is chronologically consistent with that acquisition, and with revenue rising 46.2% over the LTM period [`earnings/01_historical-financials`, §2]. **Inference, not from filings** — nVent nowhere links the two, and does not disclose whether it assumed existing Avail instruments or posted new ones. **The mechanism is benign; the silence is the finding.**

## 11. Read

**nVent's disclosed off-P&L exposure at the balance-sheet date is $168.7m — 4.23% of a $3,986.9m equity base [`Q2 FY26 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets`] — and there is not one adverse ruling, confirmed tax demand, dismissed appeal, regulator penalty, related-party guarantee or recourse receivable financing anywhere in five years of disclosure.** Add the $550.0m Maverick Power earnout signed on 24 August 2026 and the figure becomes $718.7m, 18.03% of net worth and 120.12% of $598.3m of LTM profit [`CIQ → Income Statement, LTM Jun-30-2026`], which fires RF-FIN-003 — but that item is a performance earnout on a business nVent does not yet own, payable only if it hits its 2027–2028 numbers, and no filing has yet come due in which it should have been disclosed.

**The single worst item is not a dispute at all — it is the one number nVent does publish.** Bonds, letters of credit and bank guarantees went 10.5 → 10.7 → 75.7 → 82.6 → 102.0 across December 2023, December 2024, December 2025, March 2026 and June 2026: **a 7.08x rise in FY2025 and a further 34.7% in six months, carried at $0 of booked liability, described by the same unchanged paragraph in every filing.** The lead finding of this report is the mirror image of the flat-total trap: **the disclosure text stayed still while the number moved 9.5-fold.** The most plausible cause is benign and sits elsewhere in the same filings — the $979.6m Electrical Products Group acquisition closed 1 May 2025 brought in a switchgear and bus-systems project business that posts performance bonds [`Q2 FY26 10-Q, Note 5`] — but nVent never connects the two, and that inference is this agent's, not the company's. RF-CL-001 fires on the trend.

**On provisioning, management provides rather than parks — but it quantifies almost nothing.** The unrecognized-tax-benefit book is fully booked at $9.4m (0.24% of net worth), falls every single year, comes with a complete line-by-line roll-forward, and has lost nothing at any level in Germany, India or the U.S. state audits it discloses; that is honest and testable. Against it: **no aggregate litigation reserve in any period, an environmental reserve and a warranty accrual described only as "not material", asbestos named with no claim count and no reserve, a Tonka Bay captive whose reserves are never disclosed, indemnities the company says cannot be estimated, and a $12.5m guarantee liability released straight into FY2024 income — 5.19% of continuing-operations profit — on a single MD&A sentence.** RF-CL-002 and RF-CL-003 do not fire, because nothing has been lost and nothing has crystallized; the failing is completeness, not candor about defeats.
