## 9. Read

**Clean record, machinery real — with two named soft spots.** After a logged sweep of SEC enforcement, DOJ, federal dockets, the consolidated sanctions and debarment screen, the aggregated US regulatory-penalty database and both rating agencies the company actually engages, the public record adds nothing adverse to nVent's self-portrait: no enforcement action, no investigation, no forensic audit, no rating downgrade or non-cooperation tag, no listing deficiency, no insider-trading order, no sanctions match, and twelve consecutive quarters of filings inside the statutory clock with not one late-filing notification in eight years as a public company. The compliance machinery is more than ornamental — a board-adopted code, a third-party helpline with an explicit non-retaliation clause, an insider-trading policy that names the General Counsel as owner of the trading window and bars hedging and pledging, and a lender group moving toward the company rather than away from it.

**The single worst legal/regulatory fact is not a case — it is that the SEC's own staff had to make this company reconcile a non-GAAP measure it was publishing without a reconciliation.** In 2024 the Division of Corporation Finance told nVent that "Other" did not belong in its reportable-segment totals, that consolidated **segment income** needed a GAAP reconciliation and a stated management use, and that its non-GAAP measures needed labelling as such. nVent fixed all three. That is a small event with a long shadow, because segment income is the measure `00_governance-data-triage` records the CEO uses *"for setting incentive compensation targets"* — and it travels with its qualifier: `business-model/12_red-flags-sweep` graded that a **disclosed item, not an integrity finding**, and nothing here upgrades it.

**Nothing in this module belongs in front of the synthesis as a cap.** No red flag fired; the Non-Negotiable Gate is untouched by anything found here; `business-model/01_disqualifier-scan`'s "no verdict-lock" stands unchallenged. Two things go forward as watch items rather than findings: the **Russian subsidiary**, whose current group status and Russian-language record are both unswept and which no post-divestiture Exhibit 21 in this pool can settle; and the **agencies' verdict on Maverick Power**, announced fourteen days before this run, which takes pro-forma net leverage to roughly 2.43x against a Fitch expectation of *"below 2.5x"* — the closest thing to a live rating question this company has.
