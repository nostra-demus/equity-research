2. **2026-10-30 — the order book, tested for the first time since it turned.** Organic orders decelerated from ~40% (Q1 FY26) to "low double digits" (Q2 FY26) against 46.9% organic sales; implied book-to-bill fell to about **0.93x** from ~1.2x; backlog fell **$2.6bn → $2.5bn**. This is the only forward-looking series in the pool, and it has already turned down.
3. **2026-10-30 — the GAAP line.** Consensus GAAP EPS of **$1.24636 sits above management's guided high end of $1.21**, the only bar in the set the guidance range cannot reach at its top. Any print inside the guided GAAP range is a consensus miss.
4. **Q4 2026 — the Maverick financing disclosure.** Named by three specialists as the single highest-value missing document. A short bridge opens a **$557.9–565.4m twelve-month funding gap at zero profit decline**; floating unhedged acquisition debt turns a +200bps rate move from a $4.0m-a-year cost into roughly **$35.0m a year**.
5. **Q4 2026 — regulatory clearance slipping.** No decision date exists. A slip past 2026-12-31 removes revenue the Street has not yet modelled and extends the period during which the bridge commitment sits unresolved.
6. **Q4 2026 into H1 2027 — capacity landing into a falling order book.** Blaine 1 is still ramping, Blaine 2 opens in H1 2027, a third site was announced 2026-07-31, and FY2026 capex is up ~40% at ~$130m. Because **109% of the Q2 FY26 operating-margin gain was fixed-overhead absorption**, the margin engine reverses mechanically if volume disappoints — which is why the bear case runs a −25.6% profit-metric decline to an 18.14% EBITDA margin, close to the company's own FY2022 trough of 17.2%.
7. **2027-07-22 — the 2024 buyback authorization lapsing with $96.5m unused.** Programme buying was **zero in Q2 FY2026**. A lapse alongside a Maverick-loaded balance sheet would say the cash is committed elsewhere.
8. **Q1 2028 (outside the window) — the $550m earn-out becoming payable.** The perverse case: Maverick performing well is what triggers the payment, and with it paid on a mid-cycle profit base covenant leverage reaches 3.41x, only **−9.1% from breaching** the 3.75x ceiling.
9. **Undated, so not a calendar row but a standing downside:** ~$190m all-in annual tariff cost (about 3.5% of guided FY2026 sales) in an environment management calls "highly fluid", with **no end date stated** and no commodity hedging programme — copper, steel and aluminium go straight into cost of goods sold. The measured mitigation offset of more than 100% in Q2 FY26 was achieved on **47% organic volume growth**; it should not be assumed at flat or falling volume.

## 5. Calendar Read

**Only three catalysts in this calendar carry a hard date, and two of them are inside twelve months: the Q3 results on 2026-10-30 (a Capital IQ vendor date, not company-confirmed, though the 2026-05-01 and 2026-07-31 releases corroborate the cadence) and the 2024 buyback authorization expiring on 2027-07-22; the third, the $500.0m note maturity on 2028-04-15, sits outside the window.** Everything else is either a company-stated window ("fourth quarter of 2026", "fall", "first half of 2027") or an inference from one or two prior years' cadence, and each is labelled as such above. **The near-term calendar skews bearish, and not marginally:** the two biggest in-window events — the Q4 guidance test embedded in the 2026-10-30 print and the disclosure of how a $1.75bn acquisition is financed — are both downside-weighted against a panel positioned 15 Buys of 18 with zero down-revisions in three months, while the upside limb of each requires the data-centre order book to re-accelerate from a book-to-bill already near 0.93x. **The single most important timing caveat: the catalyst that can move this stock most has no date at all.** Maverick's close is a quarter, not a day; its regulatory clearance has no decision date; and its financing terms — tenor, pricing, fixed-versus-floating — are not in this data pool in any form. A buyer can time the 30 October print. A buyer cannot time the thing that decides whether the balance sheet stays Solid or becomes dependent on refinancing access.
