## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

Strictly by date, the nearest is the **~2026-10-30 Brazilian Central Bank deadline to apply for virtual-asset service-provider authorisation** — 270 days from the framework's 2026-02-02 effective date, with the 20-F stating that a firm failing to apply must cease operations within thirty days. It is dated and it is binary, but **it cannot be sized: NU discloses no crypto revenue anywhere in the pool**, so nobody can say what is at stake. The nearest dated catalyst that can actually move the share price is the **Q3 2026 results on 2026-11-12, about nine weeks out — and that date comes from a Capital IQ vendor export, not from a company 6-K**. It matters because valuation has already measured what the price assumes: at US$14.30 the residual-income model reproduces the price only on the **top** of the Street's earnings range (US$14.65), so the mean forecast is not what is being paid for. The two sides are specific. Up: diluted EPS of **US$0.243 or better** — which needs the filed IFRS tax rate to land near management's guided 15–20% rather than the ~24.8% the consensus pair embeds, a gap that produced **96.7% of the entire Q2'26 beat**. Down: pre-tax profit of **US$1,232m or less** (a −12.4% surprise), which is past the point where even a 14.17% tax rate reaches the net-income bar — and pre-tax has missed by more than that in **two of the last four quarters** (−16.63% in Q4'25, −24.59% in Q1'26).

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

**The 2026-11-12 Q3 2026 print, and it is not close.** It is the only dated event inside twelve months that carries all four of the questions this company is being valued on, and it resolves three of them in one filing: whether the tax gap converts again (the Street carries ~24.8% against filed rates of 16.97%, 8.68% and 14.17% in the last three quarters); whether operating cost steps up as management's own ~20% full-year efficiency guide requires (H1'26 came in at 18.63%, so H2 has to run **21.0–21.5%**, while the Street models costs **66bp better** than Q2'26 — a gap worth roughly **US$106–126m of pre-tax profit**); and whether the buyback is still being executed, since **no repurchase data has been published since 2026-06-30**. It is also the first test of the divergence `management-governance` flagged: Tier 1 at 13.4% and falling on a multi-year view while non-performing loans reach 8.6% of loans, with repurchases running through it. A buyer **can** time this one — the date is nine weeks out, the bars are published, and the triggers above are testable on the day. Two honest limits travel with that. The date itself is vendor-sourced, so it can move without a company announcement to warn you. And the mechanism most likely to deliver an upside surprise is the same one the earnings module scored **58/100 on quality** for: **40.4% of the last twelve months' reported profit is a non-cash deferred-tax credit**, so a tax-driven beat is a low-quality beat that reverses by construction once loan growth slows — and sequential FX-neutral portfolio growth has already more than halved, +11% → +7% → +5%.

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

Dated or windowed events that skew **down**. A buyer who reads only the top half of section 1 is reading half the calendar.

1. **2026-11-12 — the print itself is two-sided, and its downside triggers have recently fired.** Pre-tax profit **≤ US$1,232m** (−12.4%) breaks through the tax cushion entirely; it happened, worse, in **two of the last four quarters**. A filed tax rate **≥ 20.0%**, or the tax guide being reframed onto the "managerial" 30–35% basis, removes the beat mechanism outright with nothing changing in the business. An efficiency ratio **≥ 21.0%** (against 20.3% in the year-ago quarter) confirms the cost step-up management guided and the Street has not modelled.
2. **2026-11-12 — the buyback can be shown to have stalled.** If treasury holdings are still **40,659,600 Class A shares at 2026-09-30**, the US$499.6m is running down the clock toward 2027-06-03, and the only route by which cash reaches a shareholder in a company that pays no dividend is closing.
3. **~2026-10-30 — crypto permission risk.** Failure to apply for BCB virtual-asset authorisation triggers, in the 20-F's own words, *"suspension of our ability to offer crypto-related services in Brazil"*. Unmeasurable size, binary outcome.
4. **~September 2026 — S&P annual review** (*inference from a twelve-month cadence, not from filings*). BB- / Stable since 2023-09-22, reviewed 2025-09-22. A downgrade or a Negative outlook lands into a falling Tier 1 ratio and rising bad loans.
5. **2027-01-01 — the leverage-ratio floor rises 2.0% → 2.5%** for the group's payment-institution perimeter, then to 3.0% from 2028-01-01. The impact on NU is **not disclosed in the filing**. It arrives while the Tier 1 ratio has fallen 16.2% → 14.4% → 13.4% and NPLs have risen 6.7% → 8.6% of loans, and while capital is being returned.
6. **2027-06-01 — US$1,867.9m secured margin-loan maturity.** Not a solvency event for a bank with US$45.3bn of deposits, and it must not be written up as one — but it is a dated, secured, floating-rate refinancing of 14.1% of parent equity, **with no upstream analysis behind it** because `balance-sheet-survival` refused to rate. That absence is itself a risk: nobody in this run has stress-tested it.
7. **2027-06-03 — the buyback authorisation simply lapses** if the remaining US$499.6m is not spent. The note is explicit that the company is not obligated to buy anything.
8. **June 2027 — Banxico payroll portability** can just as easily take Nu México's own payroll customers to a competitor's loan as bring a competitor's customers to Nu's. Unsized in either direction.
9. **Undated — the Banco Porto Real approval** is a **§24-flagged compliance cost with no price disclosed and no decision date**. Refusal or delay leaves the Nubank brand exposed under Joint Resolution No. 17.
10. **Undated — the US launch** carries a management-capped drag of *"less than 100 basis points"* on the efficiency ratio in each of 2026 and 2027, against a full-year guide of ~20%. It is a cost that lands before any revenue, in an industry this engine scores at **38/100 for rate of change (`RF-BQ-005`, §24 Filter 5)**.
11. **Just outside the window, recorded so it is not missed: 2028-01-01 — the Brazilian CSLL social-contribution tax rate steps to 15% for payment institutions (from 12% in 2026–2027) and to 20% for credit, financing and investment companies (from 17.5%)** [`Q2 2026 Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, filed 2026-08-14, income-tax note (ii), citing Complementary Law No. 224/2025`]. It raises the tax rate mechanically — the same line the entire beat case rests on — and it is already legislated. **Also dated and just outside: the Joint Res. 14 minimum-capital transition ends 2027-12-31**, though the company states *"We have not been affected by capital requirements under this rule."*

## 5. Calendar Read

**Seven of the thirteen catalysts carry a proven date or a hard disclosed deadline; six are vague, and the two largest strategic questions — the Brazilian Central Bank's decision on Banco Porto Real and the US launch — have no date at all.** Timing visibility is therefore real but shallow: the only dated event inside six months that can move the stock is the **2026-11-12 Q3 print**, and after it the calendar goes quiet until **June 2027**, when three separate things land in three days (the margin-loan maturity on 2027-06-01, the buyback expiry on 2027-06-03, and Banxico payroll portability that month). **The near-term skew is bearish, and the reason is the bar rather than the business:** consensus was raised hard into the print (FQ3'26 revenue +7.3% in one month, 8↑/0↓ on FY2026 EPS), the price already embeds the top of the Street's earnings range, and three of the four testable Q3 triggers above are downside triggers — two of which have fired inside the last four quarters. **The single most important timing caveat: the nearest date in this entire calendar is tier-5 Capital IQ vendor data, not a company 6-K.** No filing in this pool schedules the 2026-11-12 call, so a buyer timing the thesis to that date is timing it to a vendor's calendar entry — and the two events that would most change the business, the Porto Real approval and the US charter, cannot be timed at all.
