# Catalyst Module — NU (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Nu Holdings has real dated catalysts, not only a story: seven of thirteen events carry a proven date or a hard disclosed deadline. The nearest one that can move the stock is Q3 2026 results on 2026-11-12 — a date taken from a tier-5 Capital IQ vendor export, not a company 6-K — which tests whether the tax gap that produced 96.7% of the Q2 2026 beat converts again. That print is also the single most important catalyst, helped by diluted EPS of US$0.243 or better and hurt by pre-tax profit of US$1,232m or less. The clearest one-sided negative is 2027-06-03, when US$499.6m of unspent buyback can simply lapse. Timing is proven but shallow.
