## 9. Read

The public record does not contradict this company's filings — it very nearly matches them, which after a real sweep is a finding rather than an absence. Across BCB, CVM, CNBV, SFC, SEC, NYSE and the US federal courts the only thing the regulators know that the filings do not mention is a **R$12,500 Banco Central administrative fine against Nu Pagamentos, decided on 22 October 2021** — five orders of magnitude below any materiality line, and immaterial enough that its non-disclosure is a completeness note rather than a disclosure-integrity breach, though it is enough to stop A9-01 being Green inside the five-year window. Everything the sweep found that is genuinely material, the company had already disclosed first, including the US$2.5 billion Colombian GMF class action it could have buried and instead explained. The compliance machinery is real where it can be tested — five annual filings on time with the margin widening each year, nineteen unbroken quarters of results, no NYSE deficiency notice, two rating upgrades and an AAA.br affirmation with no non-cooperation tag and no withdrawal, and a lender roster headed by the US DFC and the World Bank's IFC — and untested where it cannot: **no whistleblower complaint statistics are disclosed anywhere, and nobody has ever publicly accused this company of anything, so how it treats a critic is unknown.** The single worst legal fact is not a case at all but a trajectory: **32,703 active consumer proceedings and a civil provision that grew 39% in six months to US$30.7m**, with SENACON and Procon-SP information demands sitting on top of it — small money against US$13.3bn of equity, and the exact line that stops being small if consumer-conduct scrutiny in Brazil hardens.

**Verdict: clean record, machinery real — with the caveat that the messenger-response half of the machine has never been switched on.** Nothing here belongs in front of the synthesis as a cap. The triage-anticipated "sweep did not run" floor of 40 does **not** apply, because the sweep ran; what should travel forward instead is the narrower set of things that genuinely could not be checked — Brazilian court dockets case-by-case, a certified sanctions query, the Cayman register, and the conduct alleged in the one BCB fine, whose primary decision document is unreadable.
