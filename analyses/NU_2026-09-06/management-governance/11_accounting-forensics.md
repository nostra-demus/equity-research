# Accounting Forensics — NU

**Scope and boundary.** This report answers one question: do Nu Holdings Ltd.'s financial statements show the measurable patterns that precede accounting blow-ups? It does not re-derive the earnings-quality baseline (`earnings/06_earnings-quality` owns CFO/profit conversion, accruals and non-GAAP aggressiveness — its figures are consumed here, not recomputed), does not judge the auditor (`08`), does not size related-party transactions (`09`) or contingent liabilities (`10`), does not run the company-level enforcement sweep (`12` — this report owns only A8-20, the regulator-inspection-versus-reported-numbers divergence check), and produces no valuation or rating.

**Reporting regime (CLAUDE.md §27).** Nu Holdings Ltd. is a Cayman-incorporated, NYSE-listed foreign private issuer operating in Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. It reports under **IFRS Accounting Standards** in **US dollars**, fiscal year ending **31 December**. The audited annual filing is a **Form 20-F**; the quarterly disclosure is an unaudited interim condensed consolidated financial statement furnished on Form 6-K. There is no 10-K, no 10-Q, and no DEF 14A, and their absence is not a data gap. All figures below are **US$ millions, reported (IFRS)** unless a cell says otherwise; source figures are stated in the filings in US$ thousands and are converted here by dividing by 1,000, with no other adjustment.

**Cross-module inputs.** `earnings/06_earnings-quality.md` and `earnings/01_historical-financials.md` are present in this run root and are consumed. **`balance-sheet-survival/01_capital-structure-and-leverage.md` cross-module input not available — proceeding on this module's own read of the data pool** (only `balance-sheet-survival/00_solvency-data-triage.md` exists in `analyses/NU_2026-09-06/balance-sheet-survival/`; `NOSTRA_CONTINUATION_RUN_ROOT` is set to this run root, so no prior run was searched). ## 2. Beneish M-Score (A8-14) — NOT COMPUTED (sector exclusion)

**The Beneish M-score is Not Applicable (sector — financials overlay applies) and no composite is reported.** The M-score is a manipulation-probability model built on eight ratios drawn from an industrial company's income statement and classified balance sheet. Four of the eight — GMI (gross-margin deterioration), AQI (asset-quality softening, which needs a current-asset subtotal), SGAI (overhead versus sales) and DSRI (receivables outrunning sales) — have **no valid denominator or no valid meaning** in a bank's IFRS accounts, for the reasons set out in the sector gate. Computing the remaining four and presenting a partial composite would be a faked number, which this report will not produce.

*A note on what this does and does not mean.* An M-score that was not run is a **stated gap in one test**, not a clean bill and not a finding of manipulation. It is also not the same gap it would be for an industrial: the model's whole diagnostic content for this issuer would sit in TATA (accruals versus assets), and TATA's economic content — reported profit that operating cash did not confirm — is measured directly and more honestly in Section 6 from the company's own cash-flow statement, and by `earnings/06`.

**No pre-computed web M-score has been substituted.** No web M-score was used, quoted, or sought as a stand-in; per the self-check, a web score may only ever be labelled corroboration of a computation performed here, and no computation was performed here.

## 3. Dechow F-Score & Accrual Battery (A8-17, A8-18) — NOT COMPUTED (sector exclusion)

**The Dechow F-score is Not Applicable (sector — financials overlay applies) and no composite is reported.** Of its seven inputs, `ΔINV` is structurally zero (no inventory), `ΔREC` measures deliberate loan origination rather than uncollected sales, `RSST` requires a working-capital accrual that an unclassified bank balance sheet does not define, and `SOFT` (soft assets = the share of the balance sheet resting on estimates rather than on cash or hard assets) would read **~81% for this issuer purely because a bank's assets ARE financial claims** — total assets 74,893.9 less net PP&E 27.6 less cash 15,003.6 = 59,862.7, i.e. 79.9% at Dec-31-2025 and 83.6% at Jun-30-2026 — which would trip the model's ">65% and rising" red band on every solvent bank on earth. That is a definitional artefact, not a finding, and it is exactly why the overlay exists.

## 12. Forensics Read

**The batteries did not run, and that is a sector fact, not a hiding place: for a deposit-funded lender the Beneish M-score and the Dechow F-score measure banking rather than manipulation** — soft assets alone would read 83.6% and trip a red band on every solvent bank in the world — so what stands in their place is the set of tests that do work, and those come back clean on the things that cannot be faked. Revenue grew 37.0% in FY2025 while cash taxes actually paid grew 30.0% to US$1,641.7m and cash interest collected grew 45.0% to US$8,440.0m; the credit-loss allowance was built faster than the book in every period examined (coverage 15.37% → 16.86% in six months, over-90-day coverage 266% → 277%, no release anywhere); goodwill is 3.1% of equity and static; the consolidation perimeter, the accounting policies, the fiscal year-end and the segment presentation have not moved in three years; there has been no restatement; and both the financial statements and internal control over financial reporting carry unqualified PCAOB opinions from an auditor of nine years' standing, whose sole Critical Audit Matter is the credit-loss estimate rather than the existence of cash. **The cash is real:** 57.6% of the US$15,003.6m balance sits on deposit at the central banks of Brazil, Mexico and Colombia, remunerated at 100% of CDI in Brazil with the rate disclosed in the note, none of it "held in trust" by a third party — neither the Satyam nor the Wirecard pattern has a foothold.

**The single worst measured signal is the effective tax rate, and it rhymes with a pattern of earnings that migrate below the operating line rather than with fraud.** The reported rate has fallen 33.04% → 29.45% → 25.77% → 11.78% in H1'26 against a Brazilian statutory rate that *rose* to 42.5% — a 30.7-percentage-point gap — and upstream `04` measured 96.7% of the Q2'26 net-income beat as coming from that one line while pre-tax profit landed within 0.5% of estimate. It is graded **Amber, not Red**, for two reasons stated rather than assumed: the gap is itemised in an audited Note 30 reconciliation, and this company paid US$2,108.1m of cash tax over the last twelve months — 2.7 times the tax charged to the income statement. Fabricated profits do not pay that. What is genuinely at risk is **durability and recoverability**: the deferred credit is self-reversing by construction, arriving only while the provision build outruns tax-deductible write-offs (i.e. only while the loan book grows fast — in H1'25 the line was a net expense), and it has built a US$3,649.1m deferred tax asset now worth 27.5% of parent equity, with recovery on the loss-carryforward slice capped by Brazilian law at 30% of taxable profit a year.

**What a rational minority holder should conclude about the books.** They are not being manipulated on any measure this report could compute, and the balance sheet is conservative where it counts — net cash of US$7,744.6m (strict basis), total debt at 0.44× equity, borrowing that sits at the regulated subsidiaries and is collateralised by government securities rather than by controller shares, non-core advances at 1.03% of assets and falling, and capital ratios above every regulatory minimum in all three countries with no supervisory finding surfacing in the sweep. But roughly 40% of the last twelve months' reported profit is a non-cash tax timing benefit tied to a growth rate and a corporate structure, and a holder should read the reported earnings-per-share as containing that, watch the FY2026 effective tax rate and the loan-book growth rate together, and treat a rate that stays below ~15% while growth decelerates as the point at which this item stops being Amber.
