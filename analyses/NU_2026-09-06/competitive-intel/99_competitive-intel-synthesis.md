## Abstract

The pool holds no competitor benchmark at all: all 20 transcripts and all 115 sources are Nu Holdings' own, with zero peer calls and zero permitted broker paraphrases. There is therefore no read-through for NU's next print — direction, weight and direction-confidence are all Not assessable, and none of the three is a soft "mixed" read. The reporting peer set speaks to 0% of NU, including the 90.9% of the revenue base in Brazil where NU's own 20-F names five direct competitors. The chapter's finding is the coverage gap itself, and it is fixable before 12 November 2026.
