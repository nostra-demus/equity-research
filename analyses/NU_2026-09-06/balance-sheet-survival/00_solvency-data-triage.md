## 6. Sufficiency Verdict

- **Verdict: Insufficient data**
- **Reason:** Nu Holdings is a **financial institution** — a diversified banking group (Capital IQ Primary Industry Classification "Diversified Banks", SIC 6020; Banking = 100% of FY2025 revenue; interest-bearing deposits of US$45,328.4m at Jun-30-2026 dominate the liability side against roughly US$5.9bn of total debt) — so the Business Type Applicability Gate in `MODULE_RULES.md` returns Insufficient data regardless of how complete the pool is. **Financial institution — requires a separate solvency framework (CET1 / LCR / NSFR / asset quality).**
- **Sections that can run:** None of this module's six sections should run on the debt/EBITDA framework. Capital structure, maturity wall, liquidity, coverage/covenants, contingencies and the downside stress test all assume an operating company with corporate debt serviced out of EBITDA; NU has no EBITDA line, funds itself with customer deposits, and is supervised on regulatory-capital and liquidity ratios. Forcing debt/EBITDA leverage or an EBITDA-haircut stress test onto a bank would produce a confidently wrong survival read, which §24 and §13 exist to prevent. What *is* readable from this pool, and belongs to a bank-specific framework rather than to `01`–`06` here: CET1 and Basel III capital adequacy (the 20-F describes the Brazilian prudential-conglomerate regime, the Mexican Basel III adaptation and the Colombian Decree 2555 framework), the Liquidity Coverage Ratio at the Brazil sub-consolidated perimeter, deposit funding mix and cost, the loan book and its allowance (gross loans US$40,274.6m less allowance US$6,612.9m at Jun-30-2026), and wholesale/market funding (Financial Bills at CDI + 1.800% maturing 2029, the margin loan facility maturing 2027, and repurchase agreements).
- **Active partial-data caps:** Not applicable — the verdict is Insufficient, not Partial. For the record, had the framework fitted, the binding caps would have been: covenant headroom "Not assessable" (usefulness max 75), downside resilience "Not assessable" (usefulness max 70), liquidity runway max 60 (revolver availability unknown), covenant headroom max 60 (no covenant-EBITDA definition).
- **Critical missing items:**
  - A bank-solvency framework itself — this module has no CET1, Tier 1, leverage-ratio, LCR, NSFR or asset-quality machinery, and no amount of extra data fixes that.
  - No EBITDA base exists or can exist (`ciq_facts.json`: "Income Statement sheet has no 'EBITDA' row"), so the mandatory −30% / −40% / −60% EBITDA stress test cannot be run.
  - No covenant disclosure, credit agreement, indenture or trust deed anywhere in the 115 sources.
  - No committed-facility, undrawn or borrowing-base detail for the Margin Loan Credit Facility (US$1,867.9m drawn, maturing 2027-06-01).
  - No change-of-control, cross-default or rating-trigger disclosure at instrument level.
  - No pension/OPEB data (Capital IQ tab returns "No Data Available") — likely genuinely immaterial for this issuer, but unconfirmed from the pool.
  - Note what is *not* missing: extraction failures (zero across 115 sources), the balance sheet, the cash flow statement, the debt-by-instrument detail, the maturity schedule, interest expense, the group structure, and the S&P rating are all present — and the Portuguese-language annual financial statements are present at full tier, not a gap (§27).
- **Single highest-value missing document:** The **regulatory capital and liquidity disclosure pack for the Brazilian prudential conglomerate** (Pillar 3 / Circular 3.678 report: CET1, Tier 1 and total capital ratios, RWA, the leverage ratio, LCR and NSFR) — the one document that would let a correctly-framed bank-solvency read be built. Within this module's own framework, the single highest-value missing document is the **credit agreement for the Margin Loan Credit Facility** (covenants, commitment size, availability, cross-default and change-of-control terms).

**Orchestrator note:** Verdict: Insufficient data. Downstream agents `01`–`06` and the synthesizer should not attempt a debt/EBITDA survival read on NU.
