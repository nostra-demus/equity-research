The senior notes are senior unsecured Visa Inc. obligations, are not guaranteed by subsidiaries, and ranked equally with its other unsecured and unsubordinated debt; Visa stated compliance with the related covenants at June 30. Legal coupons on the notes are fixed, but Visa also has interest-rate swaps on an undisclosed portion of senior notes, so the post-swap fixed/floating mix is not disclosed. `[Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 8 (Debt), p.18; Note 6 (Fair Value Measurements and Investments)]`

Visa repaid the €1.35bn June 2026 notes and $4.0bn December 2025 notes during the first nine months, issued $3.0bn of fixed-rate notes in February 2026, and had $0.5bn commercial paper outstanding on July 28. The July figure is a subsequent-date disclosure and is not substituted for the June 30 balance-sheet amount of $1.5bn. `[Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 8 (Debt), p.18]`

## 5. Leverage Ratios

Visa does not disclose either reported EBITDA or company-defined adjusted EBITDA. The primary basis below is **GAAP-derived LTM EBITDA of $28,338**—reported operating income plus reported depreciation and amortization—ending June 30, 2026. Its build is FY25 Q4 $6,464 plus FY26 nine-month $21,874. This is latest-period EBITDA, not a normalised mid-cycle figure; the business-model inputs describe Visa as only partly externally driven and do not identify it as a deep cyclical requiring a mid-cycle leverage row. `[Visa FY2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Operations and Cash Flows, pp.60, 65; Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p.4; analyses/V_2026-09-23/earnings/01_historical-financials.md, TTM Snapshot]`

Capital IQ reports $31,094 LTM EBITDA and $10,066 broad net debt, or 0.32x broad net leverage; the sidecar is the authoritative read of that workbook and the arithmetic reconciles exactly. It is a vendor special-item-excluding EBITDA basis inferred from its $2,756 difference from the $28,338 GAAP-derived measure, not Visa-reported or company-defined adjusted EBITDA. On that vendor basis, gross leverage is 0.77x and strict net leverage is 0.37x; neither replaces the GAAP-derived canonical ratios. `[ciq_facts.json, `ltm_ebitda_m`, `net_debt_m` and `net_debt_ebitda_x`, Jun-30-2026 — CIQ vendor basis; analyses/V_2026-09-23/earnings/01_historical-financials.md, TTM Snapshot]`

Strict net debt rose $3,492 from FY25: carrying debt fell $1,313 ($25,171 to $23,858), but cash and equivalents fell $4,805 ($17,164 to $12,359), so the net-debt change is `−$1,313 − (−$4,805) = +$3,492`. In the nine months to June 2026, Visa spent $16,430 on Class A buybacks, $3,852 on dividends and $705 on acquisitions, while CFO was $16,342; it also repaid $5,565 of senior notes, issued $2,995 of senior notes and raised $1,496 net from commercial paper. Those are the stated financing and investment flows, not a complete bridge to the cash-equivalents change because the cash-flow statement also includes restricted cash and foreign-exchange effects. `[Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p.10; Consolidated Balance Sheets and Note 8, pp.4, 17–18]`

## 6A. HoldCo / OpCo & Structural Subordination

| Item | Evidence | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|

The $7bn revolver names Visa Inc., Visa International Service Association, Visa U.S.A. Inc. and Visa Europe Limited as borrowers; no June 2026 drawing was reported by CIQ. `[Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 8, p.18; Visa FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 10 (Credit Facility), p.84; Capital IQ Financials Quarterly, Jun-30-2026 — vendor export]` | Senior-note creditors have no subsidiary guarantee. Any recovery therefore ranks behind subsidiary-level claims if those arise. |
| Upstreaming constraints (dividend blockers, regulatory) | Not disclosed in the data pool. | The parent’s ability to rely on subsidiary cash upstreams cannot be independently tested from the admitted documents. |
| Material restricted / trapped cash | $6,409 of restricted cash and equivalents/customer collateral is excluded from the $12,359 cash-equivalents line used in strict net debt. `[Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 4, p.14]` | It prevents customer collateral and litigation escrow from flattering liquidity or net debt. |
| Cross-default, change-of-control puts and rating-linked pricing triggers | Not disclosed in the data pool. The revolver’s borrowing margin and commitment fee are linked to the rating on senior unsecured long-term debt. `[Visa FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 10 (Credit Facility), p.84]` | A rating change can affect facility cost; the other contractual triggers cannot be assessed from the pool. |

## 7. Leverage Anchor Summary (canonical numbers for downstream agents)

- **Broad net debt cross-check:** **$10,066m broad basis** = $23,858m − $12,359m cash − $1,433m current investment securities. This exactly reconciles to the CIQ sidecar and must always retain the “broad” label. `[ciq_facts.json, `net_debt_m`, Jun-30-2026 — CIQ vendor basis]`
- **Cash and liquid investments:** $12,359m cash and equivalents; $1,433m current investment securities; $150m non-current investment securities not netted. Restricted cash/collateral of $6,409m is excluded. `[Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Consolidated Balance Sheets and Note 4, pp.4, 14]`
- **EBITDA base:** **$28,338m LTM GAAP-derived**, latest period ending June 30, 2026, not normalised/mid-cycle. Visa does not disclose company-adjusted EBITDA. The CIQ vendor special-item-excluding basis is $31,094m and is a cross-check, not an adjusted-EBITDA substitute. `[Visa FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.60, 65; Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.4; ciq_facts.json, `ltm_ebitda_m`, Jun-30-2026 — CIQ vendor basis]`

Vendor cross-check: 0.37x strict and 0.32x broad using CIQ’s $31,094m special-item-excluding EBITDA. `[Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.4, 17–18; ciq_facts.json, `net_debt_ebitda_x`, Jun-30-2026 — CIQ vendor basis; calculation]`
