## 5. Contingency Read

The largest live exposure is the settlement indemnification guarantee: its disclosed maximum daily gross exposure was $168.6bn, while Visa does not estimate the future obligation or loss after the $9.5bn collateral pool. That scale can create a liquidity problem if several clients fail together, even though Visa says historical losses have been minimal; it must not be treated as $168.6bn of debt or an expected loss. [data/V/Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 9 (Settlement Guarantee Management), p.19]

Litigation is also live: $1.274bn is accrued, including $822m for U.S. covered interchange matters and $89m for VE matters, but Visa cannot estimate additional loss ranges. The recognized U.S. accrual fell only after $3.007bn of nine-month payments, so the lower closing balance is not evidence that the legal risk has ended. [data/V/Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 16 (Legal Matters), pp.24–27]
