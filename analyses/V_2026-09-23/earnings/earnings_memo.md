## What This Module Found

June-2026 trailing-12-month revenue was $44.488bn, up 14.4% year on year. Global payment volume and processed transactions each grew 10% in Q3, but they are only the closest broad drivers: the filing directly assigns only 1.0 percentage point of Q3’s 14.4% revenue growth, leaving 13.4 points unallocated on a common basis. [Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.3–4; MD&A pp.31–33; Capital IQ Financials Quarterly, LTM June 2026 — vendor export]

Margins and cash point the other way. Q3 EBIT—operating income—margin was 59.1%, down 161 basis points, while Q3 GAAP-derived EBITDA (operating profit before depreciation and amortization) margin was 62.3% versus 63.8%, down 157 basis points. [Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.4, 34–35]

Client incentives were $4.680bn in Q3, up 18% versus 14% net-revenue growth, and management expects incentive growth slightly above Q3 in FQ4 while deferred marketing expense lands. The single killer risk is that promotional timing, days mix and FIFA-related activity fade as renewals lift incentives and marketing, creating a margin or EPS miss even if revenue is acceptable. [Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A p.33; Visa Q3 FY2026 earnings call, CFO prepared remarks and Q&A, 2026-07-28]

The corrected FQ4 revenue consensus is $12.097bn, or 12.8% growth against the matched FQ4 FY2025 base of $10.724bn. It is within management’s low-double-digit to low-teens range, so the earlier 18.9% “high bar” conclusion is rejected; the estimate set is at least 43 days old, making the setup provisional. [Visa FY2025 Form 10-K, p.60; Capital IQ Estimates → Consensus, FQ4 FY2026; Visa Q3 FY2026 Financial Results Presentation, p.20]

Cash generation remains substantial, but the latest trend is weaker: nine-month FY2026 net income rose $2.534bn while cash from operations (CFO) fell $479m, with operating asset and liability movements worsening by $3.621bn. That does not prove a cash breakdown: FY2023–FY2025 CFO covered 81.0%–94.6% of GAAP-derived EBITDA and FY2025 free cash flow was $21.577bn. [Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.10; Visa FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.60, 65]

## Bottom Line

- **Mixed earnings setup:** revenue growth is faster, but incentives, deferred marketing, margin pressure and the recent cash-versus-profit gap create **Material concerns**.
- It could be better than it looks if payment volume and transactions hold up, revenue clears a refreshed bar, and same-period margin and cash conversion improve.
- It could be worse if temporary activity fades as incentive and marketing pressure rises, causing a margin or EPS miss despite acceptable revenue.
- A common-basis revenue-driver bridge, a standalone FQ4 operating-profit bar and refreshed estimates are missing; category-level margins are also unavailable.
- Watch the refreshed FQ4 consensus, then revenue versus the matched bar, GAAP-derived EBITDA margin, cash conversion, incentives and marketing.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **EBIT:** operating income, the profit remaining after operating costs.
- **EBITDA:** operating profit before depreciation and amortization.
- **Basis point:** 0.01 percentage point.
- **Cash conversion:** the share of operating profit that becomes cash from operations.
- **Free cash flow (FCF):** cash from operations less total capital spending.
- **EPS:** earnings per share, the profit allocated to each share.
- **Consensus:** the vendor estimate used to judge a reported result.
