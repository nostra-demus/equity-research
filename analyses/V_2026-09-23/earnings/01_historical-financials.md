# Historical Financials — V

Basis: Visa Inc. (NYSE: V) reports in USD under U.S. GAAP and has a September fiscal year-end. Amounts below are USD millions except per-share data. EBIT is operating income; EBITDA is EBIT before depreciation and amortization. Visa does not report EBITDA, so every EBITDA figure labelled “GAAP-derived” is reported operating income plus reported depreciation and amortization. CFO is cash generated by operations; FCF is CFO less total capex.

## 6. Key Trend Summary

Revenue is accelerating in the latest reported periods: FY24 growth was 10.0%, FY25 was 11.3%, and the June 2026 TTM reached $44.488bn, up 14.4%.[1][3][4] Reported GAAP margins are compressing against FY24: GAAP-derived EBITDA margin fell from 68.6% in FY24 to 63.0% in FY25 and was 63.7% in the latest TTM; the 69.9% CIQ vendor-defined TTM margin is a different special-item-excluding basis (inference from reconciliation) and does not contradict that reported result.[1][3][7] Revenue has modest seasonality, with Q4 the largest quarter at a 26.6% three-year average share, rather than a quarter dominating the year.[4] The clearest reporting-basis inflection is FY25: revenue rose 11.3% but GAAP EBIT rose only 1.7%, while reported CFO and FCF then fell 3.9% and 4.8%, respectively, in the latest TTM versus the prior TTM; this report records the divergence without assigning a driver.[1][3][4]
