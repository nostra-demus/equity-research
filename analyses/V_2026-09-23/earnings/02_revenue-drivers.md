# Revenue Drivers — V

Visa Inc. (NYSE: V) reports in USD under U.S. GAAP. The latest reported quarter is Q3 FY26 (three months ended June 30, 2026).

## 1. Segment Decomposition Status

**Single-segment business (>85% from one segment) — consolidated analysis.** Payment Services was Visa's only reportable segment and produced all FY25 net revenue of $40.0 billion. The frozen CIQ facts sidecar's authoritative workbook read is the same: Payment Services $40.0 billion, or 100% of total FY25 revenue; the audited filing is the higher-tier source for the reported figure. Visa does not disclose a separate P&L for the underlying revenue categories or geographies. [FY25 Form 10-K, Note 14, p.87; CIQ Financials→Segments (Revenues, latest annual column), 12 months ended 2025-09-30 — vendor export]

## 2. Revenue Driver Tree

| Business Type | Revenue Formula |
|---|---|
| Payment network / transaction processor | Payment volume × service-fee rate + processed transactions × data-processing/network-access fee + cross-border volume × cross-border fee + value-added-service, licence and account fees − client incentives |

**Visa-specific formula:** `Net revenue = service revenue (priced on prior-quarter payment volume) + data-processing revenue (current-period processed transactions and related services) + international-transaction revenue (cross-border volume and conversion) + other/VAS fees − client incentives.` Service revenue uses current-quarter pricing applied mainly to prior-quarter payment volume; the other activity-based categories are recognized in the current period. [FY25 Form 10-K, Note 1, p.69; Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, MD&A, pp.31–33]

## 6. Revenue Growth Decomposition

Q3 FY26 net revenue was $11.633 billion versus $10.172 billion in Q3 FY25: a $1.461 billion increase, or **14.4%** when calculated from the rounded reported amounts (the filing reports 14% using unrounded data). The filing gives the direction of volume, price, mix and incentive effects, but not their contribution in percentage points on a common net-revenue basis. It also separates the service-revenue volume period from the current revenue quarter. A full volume/price/mix decomposition would therefore be inference, not from filings. [Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, MD&A, pp.31–33]

| Component | Contribution to Growth (pp) | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|

## 6a. Decomposition Attribution and Residual (MODULE_RULES "Driver Attribution" / §15)

**FX:** Asserted from disclosure, no ratio applied: management states that exchange-rate movements added approximately **+1.0pp** to Q3 reported net-revenue growth. [Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, MD&A, p.32]

**Other / residual:** `14.4pp total reported growth − 1.0pp reported FX = 13.4pp`. No volume, price, mix, VAS, incentive or acquisition ratio is applied because the filing does not disclose those components on the same reported-net-revenue basis; service revenue also uses prior-quarter payment volume. Any finer allocation would be inference, not from filings.

**Reconciliation:** 1.0pp directly explained + 13.4pp residual = **14.4pp** total reported growth. The 13.4pp residual is 93% of the total and prevents a filing-proven claim that any one operating driver accounted for most Q3 growth.

## 7. The Single Biggest Revenue Driver

**Payments volume is the closest single biggest driver, but it is not proven to explain most Q3 net-revenue growth.** It is the direct input to service revenue, and its 10% constant-dollar Q3 growth becomes the primary volume input to the following quarter's service revenue because of Visa's one-quarter recognition lag. [Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, MD&A, pp.31–33] The direction is improving, but the conclusion is capped: processed transactions directly set the larger Q3 data-processing category, while VAS, pricing, cross-border mix and incentives also matter, and the disclosed bridge leaves 13.4pp of 14.4pp unallocated. For the next quarter, a change in global consumer payment volume is still the most likely broad cause of a revenue change; the June/July promotional and event-related lift should not be treated as a normal run rate. [Q3 FY26 earnings call, prepared remarks and Q&A, 2026-07-28]
