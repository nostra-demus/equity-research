## 7. Bar Assessment

**Bar is high** — provisional because the latest dated workbook update is 43 days old as of 2026-09-23 and must be refreshed before an investment decision.

Street FQ4 revenue of $12.097bn requires 18.9% year-on-year growth, while Visa's GAAP nominal outlook is only low-double-digit to low-teens, placing the revenue bar above management's stated qualitative range. The direct FY Street revenue estimate is also $0.544bn (1.2%) above the $45.2bn vendor-transcribed guidance, whereas FY normalized EPS is only $0.03 (0.2%) above the vendor-transcribed $13.19; that revenue comparison carries less weight because the company presentation did not state the dollar guide. EPS guidance is roughly consistent with Street's mid-teens normalized-EPS growth, and four normalized-EPS beats temper the conclusion, but the stale data and the conflicting GAAP-EPS revision breadth mean the bar assessment remains provisional.
