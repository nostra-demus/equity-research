## Plain-English Glossary

- **Fair value:** an estimate of what one share should be worth over the stated horizon.
- **Margin of safety:** the discount from the share price to base fair value.
- **EV/EBITDA:** enterprise value divided by earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.
- **WACC:** the blended cost of capital used to discount future cash flows.
- **Discounted cash flow (DCF):** a valuation that discounts estimated future cash flows back to today.
- **Terminal value:** the part of a DCF that represents cash flows beyond the forecast period.
- **Free cash flow:** cash from operations minus total capital spending.
