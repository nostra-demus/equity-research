## 5. Minority-Shareholder Protection Read

**Adequate.** Ten of 11 directors are independent, the chair and CEO roles are split, all directors face annual majority voting, and holders of 15% of Class A can call a special meeting. The board is not a blank cheque: three independent directors have more than 10 years’ tenure and two directors require disclosed board-limit waivers; the 07 integrity read is also coverage-limited, though it reports no Material-concerns or Disqualifying grade.

The biggest residual shareholder-rights constraint is that Class A holders cannot act by written consent and B/C holders retain special class protections over certain structural transactions. Those features, plus missing multi-year voting-result data and unquantified related-person transactions, keep this from a “strong protection” conclusion. No hard disqualifier was routed from `business-model/01_disqualifier-scan`. [business-model/01_disqualifier-scan, §§1–3]
