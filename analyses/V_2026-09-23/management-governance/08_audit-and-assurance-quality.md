## 10. Read

**Mixed — watch the named link.** Visa’s current consolidated audit and ICFR opinion are unqualified, the fee table is detailed, and the committee reports direct engagement with KPMG. The weakest link is not a Visa audit qualification: it is KPMG LLP’s documented 2019 SEC enforcement order, compounded by the inability to identify and sweep Visa’s engagement partner and to measure component-auditor coverage. To trust the numbers more, obtain the FY25 PCAOB Form AP, a component-auditor/coverage schedule, and three prior annual audit reports/CAMs.
