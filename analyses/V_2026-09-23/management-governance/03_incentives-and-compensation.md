## 4. Incentive Alignment Read

**Mixed.** Visa's largest long-term performance component is a 50% performance-share award tied to three-year EPS and relative TSR, while 95% of the CEO's target annual compensation is variable. But the 140% annual cash payout also rests on revenue, net-income, transaction, volume and other operating goals, and Visa does not disclose the weights or every goal; that prevents a conclusion that management is paid chiefly for per-share value. The single biggest signal is the contrast between a well-specified EPS-plus-TSR long-term formula and an unweighted annual scorecard that includes size-based measures. [FY26 DEF 14A, Executive Compensation—Performance-Based Compensation for FY2025, p.52; Annual Incentive Plan, pp.58–61; Long-Term Incentive Compensation, pp.62–66]
