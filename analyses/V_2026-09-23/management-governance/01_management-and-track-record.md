## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Management’s stated growth plan has four lanes: consumer, commercial and money-movement payments; AI and agentic commerce; stablecoins and blockchain; and value-added services. [Visa Q2 FY26 Earnings Call, CEO prepared remarks, 2026-04-28] The explicit current targets are near-term rather than long-term: Q4 FY26 GAAP net-revenue growth of low-double-digit to low-teens and FY26 growth at the low end of mid-teens; on an adjusted constant-dollar basis, the respective targets are high-end low-double-digit and low-end low-teens. [Visa Q3 FY26 Financial Results Presentation, pp.20–21]

No multi-year numeric margin, return-on-capital, leverage, or synergy target was identified in the supplied management materials. That is a disclosure limit, not evidence that management has no internal targets.

## 5. Management Read

Visa has a stable disclosed senior team: the six current executive officers’ median Visa tenure is 9.7 years, while the CEO has been inside the company since 2013 and CEO since February 2023. [FY26 DEF 14A, “Executive Officers,” pp.47–48; “Director Nominee Biographies—Ryan McInerney”] The two fully observable FY26 quarterly guides were met or exceeded on their own adjusted, constant-dollar bases. [Visa Q1 FY26 Earnings Call, CFO prepared remarks, 2026-01-29, pp.9–10; Visa Q2 and Q3 FY26 Financial Results Presentations, p.1]

The limiting signal is sample length, not a documented miss: the pool does not establish three-year management turnover, a full-cycle promise record, or the still-open FY26 outcome. This supports a solid but not exceptional management-quality score; it is not a claim of a long-term record across different economic conditions.
