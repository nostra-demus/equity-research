## 11. Read

Recognised contingent liabilities are $3.005bn against $35.178bn of June 2026 equity, or 8.5%, so the materiality trigger RF-FIN-003 applies even though the total is below the 25% net-worth Red band. [analyses/V_2026-09-23/balance-sheet-survival/05_off-balance-sheet-and-contingencies.md, §4; Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.4] The largest off-P&L item is the settlement indemnification guarantee: $168.6bn maximum daily gross payment exposure, mitigated by $9.5bn of collateral but with no determinable future obligation or loss estimate; it is a scale and liquidity-risk indicator, not $168.6bn of debt. [Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 9, p.19] Litigation provisioning appears candid on the available record because Visa shows both accruals and large payments, including $3.007bn paid on U.S. covered litigation in 9M FY26, but an unquantified loss tail and the adverse UK competition finding keep the read Amber. [Visa Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 16, pp.24–27] A rational minority holder should watch the year-end litigation accrual and payment bridge, the UK appeal, any quantified loss range, and whether the settlement-collateral pool rises alongside gross exposure.
