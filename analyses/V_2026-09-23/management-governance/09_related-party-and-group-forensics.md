# Related-Party & Group Forensics — V

**Scope.** Visa Inc. (NYSE: V) is a U.S. GAAP, U.S. SEC registrant with a September fiscal year-end. This review uses only the frozen evidence set. The FY25 proxy discloses the related-person policy but no named transaction or amount ledger; therefore a reported zero is **not** evidence of zero economic activity.

## 1. RPT Intensity & the Applicable Regime (A5-01)

Applicable materiality rule per period: U.S. SEC Regulation S-K Item 404(a). It requires disclosure of a registrant transaction exceeding $120,000 in which a related person has a direct or indirect material interest, including the related person, relationship, amount and, for debt, peak balance and terms. ## 14. Read

No annual dollar amount of value moving to a promoter or related person is disclosed, so value leakage and its percentage of FY25 PAT are **not assessable**; reported zeros must not be promoted into economic zeros. The only identified channel is a policy-governed but unquantified related-person process, while Visa's core brand is evidenced as group-owned rather than controller-owned. Four significant subsidiaries are disclosed and the vendor graph identifies 10 internal group entities, but neither source maps all entity layers, cash location or intra-group funding. The highest-value next disclosure is a transaction-level Item 404 / related-party ledger with every counterparty, annual amount, approval date, pricing basis and any loan/guarantee peak balance.
