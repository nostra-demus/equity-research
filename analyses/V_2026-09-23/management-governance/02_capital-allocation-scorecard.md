The three deals' disclosed purchase consideration totals $3.375bn. This is a repeated acquisition programme, but the available facts do not establish the serial-acquirer red flag: no transaction is disclosed as debt-funded, and the programme's deal-level outcomes are not yet reported. That absence is not proof of value creation; it is the reason M&A discipline scores only 12/20. [FY25 Form 10-K, Note 2—Acquisitions, p.68; Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, Note 2—Acquisitions, p.11]

**Serial-acquirer + opportunity-cost test (CLAUDE.md §24, Filter 4).** The company states strategic rationales for each deal, so RF-CAP-001 (large acquisition with vague rationale) is not triggered. But neither the annual filing nor the current 10-Q supplies the post-close return or synergy figures needed to show that the programme beat its opportunity cost. No RF-CAP-004 serial-acquirer cap is applied: the available disclosures do not establish the full pattern of repeated material, debt-funded acquisitions that the trigger requires. Future deal reporting that shows weak integration, further debt used for acquisitions, or a materially larger deal pattern would change this conclusion.

## 5. Capital-Allocation Read

**Verdict: value-creative / disciplined.** FY23–FY25 returned $59.732bn through cash buybacks and dividends, while FCF covered the dividends 4.76 times and the as-converted share count fell 2.7% in FY25; those are direct per-share positives. The best use was the combination of a low-payout, FCF-covered dividend and open-market repurchases that reduced the share count, although the filings do not establish that repurchases were below intrinsic value. The weakest use is M&A: $3.375bn of disclosed consideration since FY24 has clear strategic rationales but no disclosed deal-level return, synergy, or payback result. The single biggest capital-allocation signal is the current FY26 funding tension: identified nine-month cash uses were 162.5% of CFO and strict net debt rose $3.492bn, so a repeat of this pace needs to be judged against future cash generation and litigation cash needs rather than extrapolated from the FY23–FY25 record. [FY25 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows and Note 15, pp.65, 88–90; Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows and Balance Sheets, pp.4, 10]
