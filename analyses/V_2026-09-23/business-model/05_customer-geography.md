## 4. Read

Visa has a disclosed large-client dependency—one unnamed client supplied 11% of FY25 net revenue—but customer concentration beyond that is not proven because the filing does not disclose the top-three aggregate. [FY25 Form 10-K, Note 14—Segment Information] Geography is concentrated in the aggregate International bucket at 61%, although the underlying country mix is broad: no non-U.S. country contributed 10% or more. [FY25 Form 10-K, Item 7—Results of Operations: Net Revenue; Note 14—Segment Information] Contract security is partial: Visa reports long-term incentive contracts and certain protections generally, but does not disclose terms for the large client and says some financial-institution clients can leave on relatively short notice; the single biggest disclosed dependency is international commerce, not a named country or customer. [FY25 Form 10-K, Note 1—Client Incentives; Item 1A—Business Risks: client and seller base]
