## 2. Aggregate Quality Score

**66 /100 — Strong.** The weighting is 15% each for pricing power, repeat revenue and margin stability; 12% for regulatory dependence; 10% each for customer stickiness and capital intensity; 8% competitive intensity; 5% each for rate-of-change and cyclicality; 3% commodity dependence; and 2% disclosure quality. This puts 55% of the read on fees, repeated transaction revenue, margins and regulation, rather than allowing the 92 capital-intensity or 95 commodity-dependence score to outweigh price, client and policy risk; the result is within the 70 cap implied by the second-lowest row (50).

## 4. Read

Visa is a capital-light, transaction-volume-linked payments network with recurring use, high margins and meaningful exposure to regulation, client incentives and alternative payment rails. It is not a commodity business, but it is not immune to the economic cycle: adverse consumer spending and travel conditions can reduce payment and cross-border volumes. [FY25 Form 10-K, Item 1A—Global Economic, Political, Market, Health and Social Conditions, pp.28–29] Over the next 24 months, the quality factor to watch is regulatory dependence, including whether required pricing or routing changes combine with rising client incentives; in Q3 FY26, client incentives grew 18% while net revenue grew 14%. [Visa Q3 FY26 Earnings Release, 2026-07-28, pp.2, 4]
