## 2. Classification

**Standard professional management** — Visa is a U.S. GAAP, USD-reporting issuer with a separate independent Board Chair, ten of eleven director nominees independent, and all standing committees made up of independent directors. The company has returned substantial cash and has not shown a serial-acquirer pattern that triggers Filter 4, but the available acquisition disclosures do not yet establish post-close returns and litigation/settlement guarantees are a material constraint on an otherwise clean governance read. [data/V/Visa DEF 14A, Dec. 8 2025, Board Leadership Structure, pp.95–96]; [data/V/Visa Form 10-Q, Q3 FY26, Notes 2, 9 and 16, pp.11, 19 and 24–25]

## 3. Most Material Signal

The off-balance-sheet items signal is most material. Visa states that its settlement guarantee cannot be quantified in advance and that losses on many legal proceedings cannot be estimated; at the same time, it had paid $3.142bn of legal matters in the first nine months of FY26, leaving a $1.274bn accrued litigation balance and $0.888bn of restricted litigation escrow. Further adverse rulings or a change in collateral needs would make the current cash-return pace less defensible and could move the classification to capital allocation concerns. [data/V/Visa Form 10-Q, Q3 FY26, Note 16 and Liquidity and Capital Resources, pp.24–25 and 39]
