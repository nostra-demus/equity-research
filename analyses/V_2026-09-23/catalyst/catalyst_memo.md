## What This Module Found

The nearest and most important event is the expected 27 October 2026 FQ4 FY2026 and FY2026 result. It tests the $12.097bn vendor revenue bar and whether cross-border activity can offset incentive and marketing pressure, but the date comes from Capital IQ and is not issuer-confirmed. [data/V/Visa Inc NYSE V Events Calendar.xls, 2026 Events Calendar, Oct-27-2026—CIQ Derived; analyses/V_2026-09-23/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1–3]

The October release is two-sided. FQ4 revenue at or above $12.097bn, 12.8% above FQ4 FY25's reported $10.724bn on a matched FQ4/FQ4 basis, together with FY27 commentary that does not confirm weaker cross-border activity or a larger incentive/cost drag, would help. A result below $12.097bn—particularly at or below the $11.855bn material-miss screen—and weaker FY27 commentary would hurt. [Capital IQ Estimates, FQ4 FY2026 Consensus; Visa FY25 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p.60; analyses/V_2026-09-23/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §4; Visa Q3 FY26 Financial Results Presentation, p.20]

The other dated events are $1.500bn of 1.90% senior notes maturing in April 2027, $0.500bn of 0.75% notes in August 2027, and $0.750bn of 2.75% notes in September 2027. They are proven funding checkpoints, but Visa reported sufficient liquidity for the April payment and held $12.359bn of cash at 30 June 2026, more than the $2.750bn named 2027 maturity build. [Visa Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, Consolidated Balance Sheets; MD&A—Liquidity and Capital Resources; Note 8 (Debt), pp.4, 17–18]

The largest timing risk is treating the 27 October estimate as firm, or treating undated interchange-litigation and Brazil settlement-rule outcomes as scheduled resolutions. The catalyst read therefore cannot carry high timing-based conviction until Visa confirms the earnings date or another material event receives a firm schedule. [Visa Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, Note 16—Legal Matters; MD&A—Liquidity and Capital Resources]

## Bottom Line

- **Catalysts exist but timing vague:** the expected FQ4/FY26 results are the one material operating event, but its date is not confirmed by Visa.
- The read could improve if Visa confirms the date and delivers revenue at or above the $12.097bn vendor bar with FY27 commentary that does not confirm weaker activity or higher cost pressure.
- The read could worsen if results fall at or below the $11.855bn material-miss screen with weaker FY27 commentary, or if cash and contingent liabilities weaken the self-funded debt-maturity read.
- No future AGM, dividend record/ex-date, buyback completion date, scheduled regulatory decision, legal hearing, operational launch or market-structure event is proven in the frozen evidence.
- Watch for Visa’s issuer-confirmed FQ4/FY26 release date, then the result against the vendor bar and FY27 commentary.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** a dated event that can change the investment read.
- **Revenue bar:** the vendor revenue estimate used to assess a result.
- **Debt maturity:** the month when a debt principal payment is due.
- **Refinancing:** repaying or replacing debt with cash or new borrowing.
- **Liquidity:** cash and other available funds that can meet payments.
