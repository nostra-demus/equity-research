- The April, August and September 2027 note maturities are scheduled funding events, not an identified solvency cliff: $1.500bn + $0.500bn + $0.750bn = $2.750bn of named principal. The bearish version would require declining liquidity or an exceptional settlement/legal cash requirement alongside continued discretionary cash returns; it is not proven by the current maturity schedule. - Two material risks do **not** qualify as calendar catalysts. The injunctive-relief class plaintiffs filed their final-approval motion on 15 July 2026, but no future hearing or decision date is disclosed. Visa also says Brazil's central-bank approval of enhanced operating rules would raise settlement exposure, but gives no decision date. These are unbounded downside risks, not timing support. [Visa Q3 FY26 Form 10-Q, Note 16—Legal Matters; MD&A—Liquidity and Capital Resources]

## 5. Calendar Read

There are four evidenced forward events: one vendor-estimated earnings date and three contractual debt-maturity months. The only potentially material operating event is not issuer-confirmed, while the three proven funding windows are currently self-funded on the reported cash balance. The key timing caveat is that the material litigation and Brazil settlement-rule risks have no disclosed decision date, so the thesis cannot treat their resolution as a scheduled event.
