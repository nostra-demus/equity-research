## 5. Ownership / Insider Read

Bezos holds approximately 8.8% of Amazon (web-sourced, unverified) as the founder — this is original founding equity, steadily reduced through 28 years of sales, not a cash conviction buy. His stake is material as a corporate governance anchor and as a symbol of alignment, but it is declining and he is no longer the day-to-day operator. The CEO and all other named executives hold shares that are almost entirely RSU grants; no open-market purchases have been identified for any executive in the last 12 months, which is common for large-cap tech and is a neutral signal rather than a negative. All insider sales are pre-scheduled 10b5-1 transactions, executed against a backdrop of consistently strong business results — the net insider signal is "orderly harvesting of compensation equity" rather than any expression of pessimism about the business. The biggest governance positive in the control structure is the absence of dual-class shares, a classified board, or any misaligned controlling owner — minority shareholders hold full one-share-one-vote rights with relatively low takeover barriers by S&P 500 standards. No pledging is disclosed, and no structural control risk is present.
