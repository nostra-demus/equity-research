## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

The nearest hard date is 30 September 2026, when the disclosed Q3 conversion window for the $1.725bn 2033 Notes ends. This is not a scheduled issuer announcement: the Q2 filing said no holder had submitted notes through 7 August, and the frozen evidence has no later status. No material conversion is the favorable path; a material exercise would require cash principal before the notes’ 2033 maturity. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18]

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

The key event is the Q4 2026–Q1 2027 CIS revenue conversion. Management expects roughly $35m combined Q4 revenue from two larger deals and a greater Q1 ramp; delivery and power timing can still move revenue by weeks while infrastructure costs have already risen. A buyer cannot precisely time this test because neither a deployment date nor the relevant results-release date is disclosed. [Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, 2026-09-09, CFO discussion; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A]

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **By 30 Sep 2026 — 2033 Note conversion:** exercise of the currently convertible $1.725bn notes would create a cash-principal call; no exercise status after 7 August is proven. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18]

- **3 Nov 2026 vendor-expected Q3 results:** an in-range but below-consensus print, a guide cut, or a deferred Q4 CIS ramp would challenge the claimed conversion timetable. The publication date needs issuer confirmation. [Capital IQ Estimates Report, Consensus worksheet header, frozen 2026-09-14 — vendor; Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Financial guidance, p.2]

- **Q4 2026–Q1 2027 — CIS deployment window:** a one- or two-week equipment, power, or setup delay can defer reported revenue, while accelerated cloud capacity continues to consume cash. This is a Filter-5 fast-changing-industry deployment risk, not a proven inflection. [Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, 2026-09-09, CFO discussion; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A]

- **1 May and 1 Sep 2027 — 2027 Notes conversion and maturity:** the $1.150bn notes become freely convertible on 1 May and mature on 1 September. Cash covered the maturity at 30 June, but the leverage-covenant threshold and headroom are not disclosed. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, p.3; Note 7, pp.17–20]

## 5. Calendar Read

Three dates are contractual and proven: the 30 September 2026 end of the 2033 conversion window, the 1 May 2027 2027-Note conversion date, and the 1 September 2027 maturity. The closest operating date, 3 November, is vendor-listed rather than issuer-confirmed, while the decisive CIS ramp is only a Q4 2026–Q1 2027 management window; it cannot support timing conviction. No future AGM, regulatory/legal decision, policy decision, or M&A closing date is evidenced in the frozen pool. [Catalyst Data Triage, §1; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7, pp.17–20]
