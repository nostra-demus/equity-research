# Catalyst Module — AKAM (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Akamai has dated, evidenced catalysts, but hard dates test balance-sheet risk while upside remains softly timed. The nearest is 30 September 2026, when the $1.725 billion 2033 Notes’ conversion window closes and any holder exercise could call cash principal [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7, pp.17–18]. The most important event is the Q4 2026–Q1 2027 conversion of cloud infrastructure contracts into revenue: delivery would validate the ramp, while power or setup delays would defer revenue [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A]. The biggest negative is a material 2033-Note conversion by 30 September 2026 [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7, pp.17–18]. Verdict: dated events exist, but timing cannot support a bullish thesis because the main operating catalyst is vague and §24-flagged.

- **Data quality /100:** 76 — the triage was Sufficient after scanning 16 source documents and 49 extracts, but no issuer-confirmed Q3 publication date, 2026 proxy/AGM notice or future regulatory date was available [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/00_catalyst-data-triage.md, opening inventory and §§1–3].
- **Overall usefulness /100:** 72 — the calendar can time funding-risk checks, but not the central operating upside.
- **Nearest dated catalyst:** 30 September 2026 — end of the Q3 2026 conversion window for the $1.725bn 2033 Notes [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18].

- **Biggest bearish catalyst:** By 30 September 2026 — a material 2033-Note conversion would require cash principal before the 2033 contractual maturity [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18].
- **Any §24-flagged catalyst:** Q4 2026–Q1 2027 CIS capacity deployment — Filter 5, fast-changing-industry risk; it is lower-confidence and must not lift conviction [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1, 3].

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** The 30 September 2026 end of the 2033-Note conversion window could expose a cash-principal use on $1.725bn of notes; no holder submission was reported through the Q2 filing, but the post-7 August status is not proven [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18].
- **Strongest bull point:** No proven-dated operating bull catalyst is available. The best candidate is the Q4 2026–Q1 2027 CIS revenue ramp, for which management expects roughly $15m plus roughly $20m, or roughly $35m combined, from two large deals in Q4 before greater Q1 acceleration [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A; Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, 2026-09-09, CFO discussion].
- **Single killer risk:** The central bullish event is only a management window and a §24 Filter-5 capacity deployment; treating it as a timed catalyst would manufacture timing confidence [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1, 3].
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** Management said a one- or two-week equipment, power or setup delay can move revenue between periods, while infrastructure costs have already risen [Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, 2026-09-09, CFO discussion; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A].
- **What would change the conclusion / force a downgrade:** Company-disclosed deployment and reporting dates followed by the stated CIS revenue conversion would improve the timing read; another delay, a material 2033-Note conversion, or a Q4-ramp deferral at the vendor-expected 3 November Q3 report would weaken it [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1–4].

## 4. Note To The Final Synthesizer

- The nearest dated, evidenced catalyst is the 30 September 2026 end of the 2033-Note conversion window; it tests whether holders create a cash-principal use on $1.725bn of notes [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18].
- The single most important catalyst is the Q4 2026–Q1 2027 CIS revenue conversion, but a buyer cannot time it precisely because no deployment or issuer reporting date is disclosed [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A; Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, 2026-09-09, CFO discussion].
- The 12-month calendar skews bearish: its hard dates primarily expose conversion, refinancing and maturity risks, while the main operating upside has only a soft window [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1–5].
- The biggest negative is a material conversion of the $1.725bn 2033 Notes by 30 September 2026; the latest proven holder-status check was through 7 August [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18].
- The CIS ramp carries a §24 Filter-5 fast-changing-industry flag and must not be read as conviction-lifting without reported revenue conversion [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1, 3].
- Timing is mixed: contractual note dates are proven, the 3 November Q3 date is vendor-expected, and the central CIS ramp and June 2027 buyback decision are vague [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1–5].
- The biggest missing scheduled-event data point is a company-disclosed deployment and revenue-recognition schedule for the two large CIS deals [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/00_catalyst-data-triage.md, §§1, 3].
