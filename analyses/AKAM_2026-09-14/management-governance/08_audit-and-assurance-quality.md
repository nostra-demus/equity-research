## 10. Read

**Assurance mixed — watch the independence and coverage evidence.** PwC issued an unqualified FY2025 consolidated and ICFR opinion covering the supplied FY2023–FY2025 statements, and the firm says it has served since 1998; that supports the current reported numbers. But the fee split, current engagement partner, partner-rotation evidence, component-audit coverage and audit-committee session detail are absent, while PwC U.S. has a documented 2024 PCAOB independence-quality-control sanction unrelated to AKAM; the FY2026 DEF 14A plus the current PCAOB Form AP are the minimum needed to trust the assurance chain more fully.
