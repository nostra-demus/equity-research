## 9. Read

**Hygiene lapses, watch.** The filings' generic statement that legal matters are routine is not contradicted by a confirmed current material enforcement case, but it omits the useful historical context: a 2016 SEC non-prosecution agreement over China-related improper-payment conduct and a 2013 former-employee insider-trading settlement. The hotline and Audit Committee review look real as mechanisms, but the company does not publish complaint volumes or outcomes. The single most decision-relevant current item is not a legal fine: third-party sources report two S&P downgrades within 54 days and a negative outlook, while the full agency records and lender syndicate still need confirmation; this is a watch item, not a legal/regulatory verdict cap.
