## 4. Takeover Defenses & Voting Rights

AKAM has one disclosed Nasdaq-listed common-stock class and the proxy states one vote per common share. That supports a simple common-share voting structure. Its charter and by-laws nevertheless contain standard U.S. takeover defences: the Board can issue undesignated preferred stock, fill vacancies, impose advance-notice rules for nominations and proposals, and Delaware’s business-combination statute can restrict certain transactions with 15% holders for three years without Board approval. These provisions can make a change of control harder even if an offer could benefit shareholders. [data/AKAM/Akamai Technologies, Inc., 2025.pdf, FY2025 Form 10-K, cover page and pp. 24–25; SEC EDGAR 2026 DEF 14A, pp. 108–110 — public filing not in frozen pool]

| Right / defense | Current position | Minority-holder implication |
|---|---|---|
| Common-share voting | One vote per common share; no unequal common-share voting class disclosed. | Positive. The conclusion is limited to common stock; the charter’s undesignated preferred-stock authority remains. [SEC EDGAR 2026 DEF 14A, pp. 108–110 — public filing not in frozen pool; FY2025 Form 10-K, pp. 24–25] |
