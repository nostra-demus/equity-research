## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Management’s stated strategy is to use Akamai’s distributed platform to provide security, compute, delivery and AI-infrastructure services, with investment focused on higher-growth security and Cloud Infrastructure Services (CIS). [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, pp. 3–5; Item 7, p. 29] In Q2, management said that CIS commitments signed so far in 2026 exceeded $2.8 billion and anticipated total-company revenue growth accelerating into the low teens in 2027; this is a forward target, not delivered revenue. [Q2 2026 earnings call, CEO prepared remarks, p. 4] Its published FY2026 targets are revenue of $4.445–$4.530 billion, CIS constant-currency growth of at least 50%, Security growth in the high single digits, a mid-single-digit decline in Delivery and other cloud applications, and non-GAAP operating margin of 25%–26%. [Q2 2026 earnings call, CFO prepared remarks, pp. 7–8]

## 5. Management Read

This is a stable, founder-led management group: the CEO has served 13.7 years and the CFO 7.5 years, while the five-role operating group has a 5.5-year median role tenure. [Web: 2026 DEF 14A, pp. 24–26 — unverified, used only for background and tenure] The only completed current-year guide was kept, but Q2 reduced FY2026’s revenue and EPS high ends and extended the non-GAAP margin range down to 25%; this is a limited, mixed execution record rather than proof of a long record of promises kept. [Q1 2026 earnings release, Financial guidance, p. 2] vs [Q2 2026 earnings release, Results and Financial guidance, pp. 1–2]

The biggest management signal is that the CIS-led plan is measurable but still unproven: signed commitments above $2.8bn and a low-teens 2027 growth target do not establish revenue conversion or return on the extra capital. [Q2 2026 earnings call, CEO and CFO prepared remarks, pp. 4, 6–8]
