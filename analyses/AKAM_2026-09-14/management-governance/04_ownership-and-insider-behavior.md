## 5. Ownership / Insider Read

Founder-CEO ownership is meaningful as personal exposure but not control: Leighton held 1.9% and the entire executive-officer/director group 2.3% at the disclosed February date. The trust-held founder stake is distinct from the small near-term RSU component, but its original cash cost is not proven. [Web: SEC EDGAR 2026 preliminary proxy, Security Ownership, pp.26–27, filed 2026-03-05 (unverified)]

The observed $2.15m of sales were all under Rule 10b5-1 plans, while vesting and tax-withholding entries are not discretionary buys or sales. The main minority-holder risk from this lens is evidence quality: the missing frozen proxy/ownership export means actual pledges, complete net insider activity, institutional trends and any shareholder bloc cannot be assessed; they must not be assumed benign.
