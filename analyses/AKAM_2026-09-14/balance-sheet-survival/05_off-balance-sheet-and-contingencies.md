## 5. Contingency Read

The largest known gross obligation is $2,331.0m of lease-related payments and uncommenced commitments at 31 December 2025, 46.8% of same-date equity; it is live, although $1,570.0m was already recognized as lease liabilities and management expects $731.2m of future sublease income to offset part of the gross payments. Purchase commitments, litigation claims, tax-audit claims and performance bonds are not quantified, while indemnities are contractually unlimited; undisclosed exposures cannot be ruled out, and the balance-sheet synthesis should apply its module cap where the leverage condition is met.
