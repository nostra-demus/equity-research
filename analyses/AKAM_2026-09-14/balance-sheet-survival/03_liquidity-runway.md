## 3. Runway

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total committed liquidity | $4,355.4m |
| Annual FCF (LTM) | $629.9m = $1,447.2m CFO − $817.3m total capex |
| Basis used (net-of-FCF / gross-obligations) | Net-of-FCF — FCF is positive and cash-backed, though working-capital use increased in H1 FY2026. |
| Annual net cash burn | $520.1m = ($1,150.0m scheduled maturity + $0.0m committed dividends/buybacks) − $629.9m FCF |
| Monthly net cash burn | $43.3m = $520.1m ÷ 12 |
| **Liquidity runway (months) = liquidity ÷ monthly net cash burn** | **100.5 months = $4,355.4m ÷ $43.3m** |

Formula: **$4,355.4m ÷ [(($1,150.0m + $0.0m) − $629.9m) ÷ 12] = 100.5 months.** This is a net-of-FCF calculation: the $23.9m of cash interest and $817.3m of total capex are within CFO/FCF and are not re-added. Dividing liquidity by the $520.1m annual burn would give 8.37x annual coverage, which is the same 100.5 months only after multiplying by 12.

The deterministic CIQ facts sidecar reports LTM CFO of $1,447.2m, matching the primary-source LTM calculation above. Its $710.1m levered-FCF figure is not substituted: the sidecar identifies it as a different after-interest vendor metric, while this report uses the required CFO minus total-capex FCF definition of $629.9m. ## 4. Sources & Uses Bridge

For the scheduled 12-month maturity, $3,355.4m of cash and current marketable securities already held exceeds the $1,150.0m use by $2,205.4m; no refinancing, asset sale, FCF, or revolver draw is needed to meet that scheduled obligation. The $629.9m LTM FCF run-rate covers 54.8% of the maturity, leaving $520.1m (45.2%) to be funded from liquidity in the runway calculation; the $1.0bn revolver is not required in this base case. The 100.5-month figure nevertheless depends on FCF continuing while accelerated cloud-infrastructure spending is funded: the May 2026 note issuance was intended in part for accelerated capital expenditure. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, p.3; Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–20; Item 2 (Liquidity and Capital Resources), p.41; calculation from stated rows]

## 5. Liquidity Read

The base liquidity runway is **100.5 months** on the clean net-of-FCF basis, with $4,355.4m of committed liquidity and a $520.1m annual financing shortfall after LTM FCF. The base case does not require external refinancing for the $1,150.0m September 2027 scheduled maturity; even excluding the $1.0bn revolver, cash plus current marketable securities cover it 2.92x. The single biggest liquidity risk is a full $1,725.0m cash-principal conversion of the currently convertible 2033 Notes during Q3 FY2026: combined with the scheduled $1,150.0m maturity, $2,875.0m would leave only $480.4m of cash plus current marketable securities before operating cash needs, although it remains within the $4,355.4m committed-liquidity total. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, p.3; Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–20; calculation from stated rows]
