## Plain-English Glossary

- **Peer read-through:** What a competitor’s reported results or management comments imply for AKAM.
- **Read-through weight:** How much influence a valid peer signal should have on the AKAM view.
- **Direction confidence:** How certain the evidence makes a positive or negative peer implication.
- **Narrative triangulation:** Testing AKAM’s story against matched evidence from competitors.
- **Peer coverage:** The share of AKAM’s revenue categories addressed by eligible peer evidence.
- **Benchmark data sufficiency:** Whether the peer evidence is broad and reliable enough to support a comparison.
- **Dispersion:** The spread among valid peer observations.
- **Normalized EPS:** Earnings per share after the adjustments used by the cited consensus data.
- **Same-basis comparable:** A prior-period figure measured for the same line item, reporting period, and definition.
