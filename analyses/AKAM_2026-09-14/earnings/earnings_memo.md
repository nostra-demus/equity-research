## What This Module Found

Q2 FY2026 revenue rose **5.4% year on year to $1,099.7m**, but profit moved the other way. GAAP-derived EBITDA margin was **24.2%**, down **716 basis points**—a unit for changes in percentages—and diluted GAAP EPS was **$0.52**, down **26.8%**, versus company-defined non-GAAP EPS of **$1.59**. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 7; Q2 2026 Supplemental Financial Information, GAAP to Non-GAAP Reconciliation sheet]

Security is the main growth driver. It generated **$604.4m**, or **55.0%** of Q2 revenue, grew **10%**, and added **5.03 percentage points** to company growth. CIS added **2.67 points**, while Delivery subtracted **2.32 points**; those category contributions sum to the reported **5.38-percentage-point** increase with no residual, although the price-versus-volume split is undisclosed. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 11 p.23 and Item 2 pp.30–31]

Margin pressure is broader than one line item. Cost of revenue explains **331** of the **721-basis-point** GAAP EBIT-margin decline—operating profit as a share of revenue—while research and development, sales and marketing, and general and administrative expense explain another **440 basis points** before small offsets; the accounting bridge has no residual. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 32–34] CIS capacity costs can arrive before revenue, and management said large signed deals normally take **six to nine months** to begin producing revenue. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A]

Cash backing is a real counterpoint. Cash from operations, or CFO, exceeded GAAP-derived EBITDA in FY2023–FY2025, and FY2025 CFO-to-EBITDA was **119.1%**; free cash flow—CFO minus total capital spending—remained positive in each of FY2023–FY2025. [FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.54–56] But FY2025 non-GAAP operating income exceeded GAAP operating income by **$686.7m**, including **$459.4m** of recurring stock-based compensation, or SBC—employee pay made with shares. [FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.34–36, 54; Q2 2026 Supplemental Financial Information, GAAP to Non-GAAP Reconciliation sheet]

The Q3 consensus bar is fair rather than clearly easy. Revenue consensus is **$0.653m, or 0.06%, above** management’s midpoint, while normalized EPS—the vendor’s adjusted earnings-per-share measure—is **1.16% below** the non-GAAP midpoint; Q3 revenue and normalized EPS estimates each fell **1.2% over 90 days**. The adjusted profit definitions are not formally bridged, so these small gaps are directional rather than precise. [Q2 2026 earnings release, p.2; Capital IQ Estimates Report, Guidance and Trends worksheets]

The biggest risk is another CIS deployment delay that leaves capacity cost in the quarter without the expected revenue, while strict net debt—debt less cash only—divided by GAAP-derived EBITDA is already **5.14x**. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.3–4; 01_historical-financials, §2] The strongest counterevidence is that CFO exceeded GAAP-derived EBITDA in FY2023–FY2025 and Akamai beat final revenue and normalized-EPS consensus in the last **four** reported quarters, but that sample is only four and the beat sizes narrowed. [FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.54–56; Capital IQ Estimates Report, Surprise worksheet, FQ3 2025–FQ2 2026]

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict: Mixed earnings setup.** Revenue is growing, but reported margins and profit are falling much faster than revenue is rising. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.5; Q2 2026 Supplemental Financial Information, Supplemental Metrics sheet]

- It could be better than it looks because Security grew **10%**, CIS grew **39%**, management reported more than **$2.8bn** of signed 2026 multi-year CIS commitments, and free cash flow stayed positive in FY2023–FY2025. The commitment figure is management commentary, not reported backlog. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.30; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks; FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.54–56]

- It could be worse because CIS revenue can be delayed after capacity costs arrive, while strict net leverage is **5.14x** and Q2 GAAP operating margin was **7.21 percentage points** lower year on year. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.3–5]

- The key missing evidence is solution-level CIS profit and capital spending on one matched basis, including realized cost recovery. Without it, signed commitments do not prove the return or margin effect. [Earnings Module synthesis, 2026-09-14, §8 Note To The Final Synthesizer]

- Watch the Q4 CIS ramp: upgrade only if reported growth arrives with year-on-year GAAP margin and cash-conversion improvement; downgrade if deployment slips again while costs and leverage remain elevated. [Earnings Module synthesis, 2026-09-14, §§7–8]

## Plain-English Glossary

- **GAAP-derived EBITDA:** Operating profit before depreciation and amortization, built from standard-accounting figures rather than the company’s adjusted measure.
- **Diluted GAAP EPS:** Standard-accounting profit divided by shares after including potential share dilution.
- **Gross margin:** Revenue left after the direct cost of providing services, shown as a percentage of revenue.
- **Basis points:** A unit used to describe a change in a percentage.
- **GAAP EBIT margin:** Standard-accounting operating profit as a percentage of revenue.
- **Cash from operations (CFO):** Cash generated by normal business operations.
- **Free cash flow:** CFO minus total capital spending.
- **Stock-based compensation (SBC):** Employee pay made with shares or share-linked awards.
- **Non-GAAP EPS:** Earnings per share after company-defined adjustments outside standard accounting rules.
- **Normalized EPS:** The vendor’s adjusted earnings-per-share measure used for consensus comparisons.
- **Strict net leverage:** Debt minus cash only, divided by GAAP-derived EBITDA.
