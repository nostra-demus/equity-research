## What This Module Found

Akamai sells security, cloud infrastructure, and content-delivery services over a distributed network, with customers paying through contracts and usage. [FY25 Form 10-K, Item 1, pp.3–5; Item 7—Revenue] Security is the main revenue category: it contributed **$604.436m**, or **55.0%**, of Q2 2026 revenue and grew **10%** year on year, while Delivery and other cloud applications fell **6% to $395.9m**. Security’s profit share is not disclosed. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Notes 11 and 14, pp.23, 25; MD&A—Revenue, p.30]

The clearest advantage is distribution: more than **4,300** edge points of presence across over **130 countries**. Remaining performance obligations, or RPO—contracted revenue expected in the future—were **$7.6bn** at June 30, 2026, but exclude uncommitted usage and anticipated renewals. [FY25 Form 10-K, Item 1, p.3; Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 11, p.23] That supports contracted visibility, but it does not prove a moat that protects profits.

Profit economics are weakening. Q2 GAAP gross margin fell **330 basis points to 55.8%**; on a longer basis, gross margin fell from **63.3% in FY21 to 57.5%** in the 12 months through June 2026, EBIT margin—profit before interest and tax as a share of revenue—fell from **23.3% to 11.9%**, and return on invested capital, or ROIC—the profit earned on invested money—fell from **7.0% to 2.7%**. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 25; CIQ Financials→Income Statement and Ratios, FY21–LTM Jun. 2026 — vendor export] The contradicting measure is CFO-to-EBITDA cash conversion—cash from operations compared with EBITDA—which rose from **117.7% to 134.0%**. That blocks a blanket erosion claim, but does not prove better pricing or returns on new capital. [CIQ Financials→Income Statement, Cash Flow and Ratios, FY21–LTM Jun. 2026 — vendor export]

The main weakness is missing solution-level economics. Akamai does not disclose Security customer count, revenue per customer, direct service cost, contribution margin, acquisition cost, retention, or lifetime, so incremental customer value and category returns cannot be tested. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Notes 4, 11 and 14, pp.15–16, 23, 25] Group capital intensity—how much spending the business needs to grow—is proven: H1 capital spending was **$417.600m**, or **19.2%** of revenue. Security-specific capital intensity is not proven because category capital spending is undisclosed. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Statements of Cash Flows; Note 14, p.25; Citi Global TMT Conference, 2026-09-09, management discussion]

The biggest risk is that debt-funded cloud capacity fails to earn enough while Delivery renewal prices and group margins keep falling. Cloud expansion depends on third-party data-centre space, power, servers, and memory; Q2 cost of revenue rose **14%** against revenue growth of **5%**, but the filing does not separate outside inflation from Akamai’s own capacity build. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, pp.30–32, 52; Note 7, pp.17–20]

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict: Average business — worth deeper work only if valuation is cheap.** No automatic disqualifier triggered, but the missing proxy keeps insider pledging and related-party transactions from being fully tested. [FY25 Form 10-K, Items 12–13, p.95]

- It could be better than it looks because the network has more than **4,300** edge points across over **130 countries**, and **$7.6bn** of RPO supports contracted visibility subject to its usage and renewal exclusions. [FY25 Form 10-K, Item 1, p.3; Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 11, p.23]

- It could be worse because gross margin fell from **63.3% to 57.5%**, ROIC fell from **7.0% to 2.7%**, and category profit and returns from the cloud build remain undisclosed. [CIQ Financials→Income Statement and Ratios, FY21–LTM Jun. 2026 — vendor export; Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 14, p.25]

- The key missing evidence is a Security customer-cohort table. Without customer-level revenue, direct service cost, retention, lifetime, and commissions, the module cannot prove incremental value creation. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Notes 4, 11 and 14, pp.15–16, 23, 25]

- Watch whether gross margin improves year on year, renewal pricing stabilizes, and disclosed category returns begin to show that the cloud build earns enough. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, pp.30–32]

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Gross margin:** The share of revenue left after the direct cost of providing services.
- **Moat:** A durable advantage that protects profit from competitors.
- **Capital allocation:** How management uses the company’s money across investment, acquisitions, debt, and shareholder payouts.
- **Convertible note:** Debt that can convert into shares under stated terms.
- **Asset impairment:** An accounting write-down when an asset is judged to be worth less.
- **Remaining performance obligations (RPO):** Contracted revenue expected to be recognized in the future, subject to stated exclusions.
- **Basis points:** A unit used to describe a change in a percentage.
- **EBIT margin:** Profit before interest and tax as a share of revenue.
- **Return on invested capital (ROIC):** Profit earned on the money invested in the business.
- **CFO-to-EBITDA cash conversion:** Cash from operations compared with EBITDA.
- **Capital intensity:** How much capital spending a business needs to operate and grow.
- **Strict free cash flow:** Cash from operations minus total capital spending.
