## 5. Ownership / Insider Read

Insider skin in the game is real but almost entirely option-derived, not cash-bought on the open market: Ellison's 40.21% stake (worth roughly $168.5bn) grew through decades of exercise-and-retain option activity rather than a single disclosed open-market purchase, and no other named executive has bought shares with cash in the trailing 12 months either. The trailing-12-month insider signal is unambiguous net selling — every dollar-material Form 4 from the co-CEOs, the Executive Vice Chairman, the Chief Accounting Officer, and the Chief Legal Officer is a disposal, mechanically laddered in a way consistent with (but not confirmed as) pre-scheduled 10b5-1 plans, so this reads as routine liquidity monetization rather than a conviction signal in either direction. The biggest control-related fact for minority holders is Ellison's 40.21% vote concentrated with an active Executive Chair/CTO role sitting alongside a newly-installed non-founder co-CEO structure — not a pledging or encumbrance risk (pledging is company-policy-prohibited and none is disclosed), but a real concentration-of-influence factor to track once the 2026 proxy is available to confirm formal board-designation rights, if any.
