This is flagged, not glossed over.

## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Management says Oracle is pivoting from an enterprise-software/database incumbent into a large-scale AI cloud-infrastructure (OCI) supplier, funded by a $638 billion remaining performance obligation (RPO) backlog that grew 363% in one year [FY26 10-K, Item 7 MD&A; Q4 FY26 earnings call, CFO Maxson]. The explicit, checkable targets set on the Jun 10, 2026 (Q4 FY26) call and press release are: FY2027 total revenue of $90 billion (point guidance), FY2027 non-GAAP EPS of $8.05 (raised from a prior figure, ~18% growth once one-time gains are excluded from the base), FY2027 net cash capex of roughly $70 billion (implying gross capex in the $90–95 billion area once customer prepayments are added back), and a capital-financing plan to raise about $40 billion through debt and equity (including the already-announced $20 billion at-the-market equity program), with no additional debt issuance planned in calendar 2026 [Oracle_Earnings Press Release Q4FY26.pdf, "Guidance for Full FY 2027"; Q4 FY26 earnings call, CFO prepared remarks].

## 5. Management Read

Oracle's leadership bench is deep and internally seasoned — both new co-CEOs are long-tenured Oracle veterans (11 and 16+ years) rather than outside hires, and the 26-year-veteran who bridged the CFO seat prevented a leadership vacuum during the transition — but the CFO chair itself changed hands twice in eight months, landing in the exact fiscal year Oracle's debt rose 54% to $167.4bn and its credit rating was cut to BBB-, which is a genuine timing-and-stability concern, not a cosmetic one. The promise-vs-delivery record is mixed, not clean: the clear, dated miss is the ~$15 billion FY2026 capex-guidance overshoot that management itself disclosed and that cost the stock 10.8% in a day, while the quarterly beat streak that looks strong on the surface (beats in 3 of the last 4 EPS prints) shrinks to roughly a coin flip once one-time Ampere/Bloom Energy gains are stripped out of two of those beats. The single biggest management signal is the unresolved securities class action alleging the AI-deal growth outlook itself was overstated — an allegation this report could not clear or dismiss from the available filings, so it is carried forward as a live, capped risk (RF-MGT-005) rather than discounted because the company's audited financials are otherwise clean.
