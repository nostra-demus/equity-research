The single most important event is the **FQ1 FY2027 earnings release on 2026-09-04**. A buyer can genuinely time this one: the date is fixed and the bar is published — revenue +27–29% (constant currency and in dollars) and non-GAAP EPS (earnings per share after management's own adjustments) of $1.72–$1.76 [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md` §2-3]. The catch is the gap between that bar and what the share price already assumes. At $153.94, the reverse-DCF (working backwards from the price to the growth it implies) requires 51.3% year-over-year FY2027 revenue growth — about 18 points above management's own 33.6% guide [`valuation/05_reverse-dcf.md` §2, §5]. So a quarter that merely clears guidance does not clear the bar the price sets. An in-line beat is itself a risk, not only a miss.

The biggest bearish event is the **overdue Netherlands Privacy Class Action ruling from the Dutch Supreme Court**. It was scheduled for 2026-06-28 and had still not landed as of 2026-08-14. The non-binding Advocate General opinion of 30-Jan-2026 already recommended dismissing Oracle's appeal grounds, and the per-user damages formulas (€500 + €100 per Dutch internet user) have no disclosed aggregate size [`balance-sheet-survival/05_off-balance-sheet-and-contingencies.md` §3, FY26 10-K Note 15]. This is not a "coming soon" catalyst — it is already late and can land any day, with no re-scheduled date.

That ruling sits on top of a refinancing wall. $2,250mn of 2.80% senior notes mature in Apr-2027 and a ~$4,645mn term loan residual matures 2027-08-16 — part of $17,355mn due within 24 months — against FY2026 levered free cash flow (cash left after operating costs, capital spending and interest) of **−$23,686mn**, an S&P downgrade to BBB− on 2026-07-09, and 5-year CDS spreads (the market's annual price for insuring the debt) near an 18-year high [`balance-sheet-survival/02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md` §1, §3, §4]. A contingent $3.3bn Oracle guarantee of a data-centre lessor's borrowing matures in September 2026, the same month as the earnings print [FY26 10-K, Note 9].

The near-term calendar therefore skews bearish: of the seven proven-dated items, three are refinancing exposures and one is an active securities class-action deadline, against only two items with clear positive optionality (AGM, earnings) and one procedural item (the proxy filing) [`99_catalyst-synthesis.md` §4].

## 6. Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** a specific dated event that can move the stock or settle a question about the business.
- **§24 Filter 5 (fast-changing industries):** the house rule that in industries changing this fast, winners are not knowable in advance — so a good print caps conviction instead of lifting it. **Filter 3** is the matching rule on debt and survival.
- **Say-on-pay:** the yearly shareholder vote on executive pay; a high "against" share signals investor unhappiness with how managers are paid.
- **Guide / guidance:** management's own published forecast for the coming quarter — the bar results get judged against.
- **Non-GAAP EPS:** earnings per share after management's own adjustments, which strip out items they call one-off.
- **Reverse-DCF:** working backwards from today's share price to the growth rate that price already assumes.
- **Levered free cash flow:** cash left after operating costs, capital spending and interest; negative means the business consumed cash rather than produced it.
- **RPO (remaining performance obligations):** contracted revenue signed but not yet delivered — the backlog that must convert into actual sales.
- **CDS spread:** the market's annual price for insuring a company's debt against default; wider means the market sees more risk.
- **BBB−:** the lowest investment-grade credit rating, one notch above junk.
- **Basis points (bps):** hundredths of a percentage point — 180bps is 1.80%.
- **Weighted-average coupon:** the average interest rate paid across existing debt, weighted by how much of each there is.
- **Contingent guarantee:** a payment owed only if something specific goes wrong — here, only if the lessor cannot refinance.
- **Capex / net capex:** money spent on physical assets such as data centres.
- **Payout ratio:** the share of earnings paid out as dividends.
- **ATM equity issuance:** an "at-the-market" program selling new shares gradually into the open market; it raises cash but dilutes existing owners.
- **Motion to dismiss:** a defendant's early request that the court throw out the case before evidence-gathering begins.
