The calendar is real, not a story. Fourteen events were identified; seven carry proven dates, and five of those cluster inside the next six weeks [`01_catalyst-calendar.md` §1].

The nearest dated event is the **Annual General Meeting on 2026-08-25**. It tests whether shareholder opposition to executive pay falls below the 18–22% dissent seen at the last two meetings. That is a governance signal, not something that moves the stock on its own [`01_catalyst-calendar.md` §1, §2].

The single most important event is the **FQ1 FY2027 earnings release on 2026-09-04**. A buyer can genuinely time this one: the date is fixed and the bar is published (revenue +27–29% in constant currency and in dollars, non-GAAP EPS $1.72–$1.76) [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md` §2-3]. The catch is the gap between that bar and what the share price already assumes. So a quarter that merely clears guidance does not validate the price. An in-line quarter is itself a risk, not just a miss.

The biggest bearish event is the **overdue Netherlands Supreme Court GDPR ruling**. It was scheduled for 2026-06-28 and had still not landed as of 2026-08-14. The non-binding Advocate General opinion of 30-Jan-2026 already recommended against Oracle, and the damages formulas (€500 + €100 per Dutch internet user) have no disclosed total [`01_catalyst-calendar.md` §4; `balance-sheet-survival/05` §3, FY26 10-K Note 15]. This is not a "coming soon" catalyst — it is already late and can land any day.

Past the FQ1 print, the dated events tilt bearish. $2,250mn of 2.80% senior notes mature in Apr-2027 and a ~$4,645mn term loan matures 2027-08-16 — part of $17,355mn due within 24 months — against FY2026 levered free cash flow (cash left after operating costs, capex and interest) of **-$23,686mn**, with S&P having cut Oracle to BBB− on 2026-07-09 and 5-year CDS spreads (the market's price for insuring its debt) near an 18-year high [`balance-sheet-survival/02` §1, §3-§5]. And the $2.00/share dividend (33.4% payout) has now gone two straight fiscal years uncovered by free cash flow (RF-CAP-003).

## 6. Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** a specific dated event that can move the stock or settle a question about the business.
- **§24 Filter 5 (fast-changing industries):** the house rule that in industries changing this fast, winners are not knowable in advance — so evidence of a good quarter caps conviction instead of raising it.
- **Say-on-pay:** the yearly shareholder vote on executive pay; a high "against" percentage signals investor unhappiness with how managers are paid.
- **Guide / guidance:** management's own published forecast for the coming quarter — the bar the results get judged against.
- **Reverse-DCF:** working backwards from today's share price to the growth rate the price implicitly assumes.
- **Non-GAAP EPS:** earnings per share after management's own adjustments, which strip out items they call one-off.
- **Levered free cash flow:** cash left over after operating costs, capital spending and interest — negative means the business consumed cash rather than generated it.
- **RPO (remaining performance obligations):** contracted revenue signed but not yet delivered — the backlog that must convert into actual sales.
- **CDS spread:** the market's annual price for insuring a company's debt against default; wider means the market sees more risk.
- **BBB−:** the lowest investment-grade credit rating, one notch above junk.
- **Basis points (bps):** hundredths of a percentage point — 180bps is 1.80%.
- **Weighted-average coupon:** the average interest rate Oracle pays across its existing debt, weighted by how much of each it has.
- **Contingent liability / guarantee:** a payment owed only if something specific goes wrong — here, only if the lessor cannot refinance.
- **Payout ratio:** the share of earnings paid out as dividends.
- **ATM equity issuance:** an "at-the-market" program selling new shares gradually into the open market; it raises cash but dilutes existing owners.
- **Motion to dismiss:** a defendant's early request that the court throw the case out before evidence-gathering begins.
