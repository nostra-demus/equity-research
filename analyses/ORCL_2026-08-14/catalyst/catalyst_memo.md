## 2. What This Module Found

The calendar is real and dated, not a thematic AI story. Fourteen events; seven carry proven dates and seven are windowed or vague, including two — the next ex-dividend date and the Netherlands GDPR ruling — that are inferred or overdue rather than freshly scheduled [`01_catalyst-calendar.md` §5].

The nearest dated event is the **Annual General Meeting on 2026-08-25**. It gives a live tally on director elections and the say-on-pay vote (the yearly shareholder vote on executive pay), testing whether opposition falls below the 18–22% seen at the last two meetings. The 2026 proxy statement is not yet filed, so the exact items on the ballot are not independently confirmed even though the date is proven [`01_catalyst-calendar.md` §1, §2; `management-governance/05` §0].

The single most important event is the **FQ1 FY2027 earnings release on 2026-09-04**. A buyer can genuinely time this one: the date is fixed and the bar is published — revenue $18,956M–$19,255M (+27–29%, both in constant currency and in dollars) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.72–$1.76, meaning per-share profit after management's own adjustments [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md` §1, §2-3]. The catch is the gap between that bar and what the price already assumes. At $153.94, the reverse-DCF (working backwards from the price to the growth it implies) requires 51.3% year-over-year FY2027 revenue growth — about 18 points above management's own 33.6% guide [`valuation/05_reverse-dcf.md` §2, §5]. A quarter that merely beats guidance does not clear the bar the price sets.

The biggest bearish event is the **overdue Netherlands Privacy Class Action ruling from the Dutch Supreme Court**. It was scheduled for 2026-06-28 and had still not landed as of 2026-08-14. The non-binding Advocate General opinion of 30-Jan-2026 already recommended dismissing Oracle's appeal grounds, and the per-user damages formulas (€500 + €100) have no disclosed aggregate size [`balance-sheet-survival/05_off-balance-sheet-and-contingencies.md` §3, FY26 10-K Note 15]. It can land any day and has no re-scheduled date.

That ruling sits on top of a refinancing wall. $2,250mn of 2.80% senior notes mature in Apr-2027 and a ~$4,645mn term-loan residual on 2027-08-16 — part of $17,355mn due within 24 months — against FY2026 levered free cash flow (the cash left after operating costs, capital spending and interest) of **−$23,686mn**, an S&P downgrade to BBB− on 2026-07-09, and 5-year CDS spreads (the market's annual price to insure the debt) near an 18-year high [`balance-sheet-survival/02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md` §1, §3, §4]. A contingent $3.3bn Oracle guarantee of a data-centre lessor's borrowing matures in September 2026, the same month as the earnings print [FY26 10-K, Note 9].

**New this refresh — a downside amplifier, not a new catalyst.** Executive Chair Larry Ellison has pledged 346,000,000 shares against personal loans: 29.9% of his own stake and ~12.0% of total shares outstanding. The Governance Committee reviews it quarterly and represents it is not a margin or hedging arrangement, and no margin-call threshold or forced-sale date is disclosed anywhere in the pool. Because it carries no date or window, §17 keeps it out of the dated calendar. It is carried instead as risk texture: a real channel for extra selling pressure if the FQ1 print or the Dutch ruling drives the stock sharply lower [`management-governance/99`, finding 99-001, RF-OWN-001; `99` §1A, §4].

The six weeks from 2026-08-25 through ~2026-09-28 carry unusually dense, proven-dated event risk in both directions. Beyond that window, most of what happens is windowed, not dated [`01_catalyst-calendar.md` §5].

## 6. Plain-English Glossary

- **Say-on-pay** — the yearly, usually non-binding shareholder vote on executive pay.
- **Non-GAAP EPS** — per-share profit after management's own adjustments; sits alongside, not instead of, the audited figure.
- **Reverse-DCF** — working backwards from today's share price to the growth rate the price implies.
- **Levered free cash flow** — the cash left after operating costs, capital spending and interest.
- **BBB−** — the lowest investment-grade credit rating, one notch above junk.
- **CDS spread** — the annual price the market charges to insure a company's debt; wider means more perceived default risk.
- **Basis points (bps)** — hundredths of a percentage point; 180bps = 1.80%.
- **RPO** — remaining performance obligations: contracted revenue not yet delivered or recognised.
- **ATM equity program** — an "at-the-market" facility to sell new shares gradually into the open market; it raises cash and dilutes existing holders.
- **Contingent liability / guarantee** — an obligation that becomes a real cash cost only if a specific event happens.
- **§24 Filter 5 (fast-changing industry)** — the doctrine's rule that in industries changing fast, winners are hard to know in advance, so such a bet caps conviction rather than lifting it.
