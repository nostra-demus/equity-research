# Competitive Map — BURL

## 1. Dominant Segment

Retail - Apparel is Burlington's sole reportable segment and represents 100% of FY2025 revenue and the CODM's net-income performance measure. [FY2025 Form 10-K (US GAAP), Note 1—Segment Information, pp. 52–53]

## 2. Named Competitors

### Competitor A — Ross Stores, Inc.

- **Ticker / listing:** NasdaqGS:ROST.
- **Where they compete:** Ross operates Ross Dress for Less and dd's DISCOUNTS, U.S. off-price apparel and home-fashion stores aimed at middle-income and lower-to-moderate-income households. It is the closest listed match to Burlington's store-led U.S. off-price apparel and home-goods offer. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Business Description, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export]
- **Scale:** LTM revenue was $24.512 billion for the period ended 2026-08-01. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Financial Data, LTM period ended 2026-08-01, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export]
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM EBITDA margin—earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation as a share of sales—was 14.9%; EBIT margin was 12.7%; net margin was 10.85%. ROIC and ROE are not reported in the admitted peer export, so a return-on-capital comparison is not assessable from available data. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Operating Statistics, LTM period ended 2026-08-01, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export]
- **Source named in:** Capital IQ's BURL Quick Comparable Analysis includes Ross in the peer set. BURL's FY2025 filing identifies off-price retailers as a competing format but does not name individual chains. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Business Description, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export; FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1—Competition]
- **One-line read:** Ross is a larger, direct U.S. off-price rival with an especially close customer and merchandise overlap.

### Competitor B — The TJX Companies, Inc.

- **Ticker / listing:** NYSE:TJX.
- **Where they compete:** TJX is an off-price apparel and home-fashions retailer operating Marmaxx, HomeGoods, TJX Canada and TJX International, selling through stores and e-commerce sites. Its U.S. apparel and home businesses compete with Burlington, although the reported company total includes international and home-focused operations. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Business Description, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export]
- **Scale:** LTM revenue was $62.363 billion for the period ended 2026-08-01. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Financial Data, LTM period ended 2026-08-01, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export]
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM EBITDA margin was 14.3%; EBIT margin was 12.2%; net margin was 9.73%. ROIC and ROE are not reported in the admitted peer export, so a return-on-capital comparison is not assessable from available data. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Operating Statistics, LTM period ended 2026-08-01, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export]
- **Source named in:** Capital IQ's BURL Quick Comparable Analysis includes TJX in the peer set. BURL's FY2025 filing identifies off-price retailers as a competing format but does not name individual chains. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Business Description, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export; FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1—Competition]
- **One-line read:** TJX is a much larger, multi-banner off-price competitor; its scale gives it a wider sourcing and store base, but its consolidated figures are not a pure U.S. apparel comparison.

## 3. Competitive Position

**Position vs peers: Holding, provisional.** No market-share series is disclosed in the admitted data. BURL's Q2 FY2026 sales rose 11.0% to $2.998 billion, of which $254.8 million came from 149 net-new and non-comparable stores and $42.0 million from 2% comparable-store sales growth; store-led growth therefore cannot by itself establish share gains. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q (US GAAP), MD&A—Results of Operations]

The cleanest available peer proxy is LTM total-revenue growth through 2026-08-01: BURL was 10.9%, between Ross at 14.02% and TJX at 7.66%. This supports a holding read rather than a clear gain or loss, but it is only a proxy: it uses consolidated sales, includes store openings, and TJX's result includes non-U.S. and home businesses. [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis (BURL), Operating Statistics, LTM period ended 2026-08-01, as of 2026-09-28 — vendor export]

## 4. Competitive Shape

**Fragmented.** BURL's FY2025 filing says the U.S. retail apparel and home-furnishings markets are “highly fragmented and competitive” and identifies department stores, off-price retailers, specialty and online retailers, discount stores, wholesale clubs, outlet stores, and full-price chains with off-price concepts as competing formats. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1—Competition] The admitted data contains no HHI, top-N share series, or U.S.-only off-price market-share split, so the degree of concentration within the narrower off-price niche is not assessable.

## 5. Caveat

BURL's filings name competing retail formats, not individual chains. Ross and TJX are direct off-price peers named in the supplied Capital IQ peer export; I do not add a third name by treating a general apparel or discount retailer as equivalent to a direct off-price competitor. The pool lacks peer annual filings, peer ROIC/ROE, and a market-share series; those inputs would resolve the position and competitive-economics gaps.
