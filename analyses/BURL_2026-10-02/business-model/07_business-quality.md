## 2. Aggregate Quality Score

**47/100 (Mixed/Average).**

Pricing power, margin stability, capital intensity, and competitive intensity receive 15% each; customer stickiness 10%, repeat revenue 8%, cyclicality 6%, rate-of-change 5%, regulatory and commodity dependence 4% each, and disclosure quality 3%. Those weights yield 47 after rounding and deliberately keep the better commodity-dependence and disclosure scores from offsetting weak competition, cyclicality, and capital intensity.

## 4. Read

This is a store-led, discretionary retail business whose sourcing model supports value but whose quality is held back by intense price competition, material reinvestment, and exposure to U.S. consumer spending and weather. It is not a fast-changing technology business, so the 54 rate-of-change score does not trigger the §24 Filter-5 cap; nevertheless, a store-only model remains exposed to e-commerce and retail-format change. Over the next 24 months, a buyer should watch comparable-store sales alongside gross margin excluding discrete tariff-refund effects: FY25 gross margin was 43.8%, up 60 basis points, while Q2 FY26’s reported 46.2% included $55.5m of tariff refunds. [FY25 Form 10-K, MD&A—Gross Margin, p.30; Q2 FY26 Form 10-Q, MD&A—Gross Margin]
