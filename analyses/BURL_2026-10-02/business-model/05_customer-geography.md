## 4. Read

BURL appears to have a broad consumer-customer base rather than a named-account model: more than 99% of sales is made through its own stores, though the company does not disclose a top-customer or top-three-customer calculation. Geographic concentration is absolute in the reported data: CIQ shows 100% of FY2025 revenue in the United States, even though the store base is spread across 47 states, Washington, D.C. and Puerto Rico. The concentration is not contractually secured; the biggest dependency is U.S. value-oriented consumer traffic and spending, particularly among the $25,000–$100,000-income core shopper. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Customer Demographic; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2 — Overview]
