## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Emaar's stated strategy, as articulated in the FY2025 Investor Presentation (Feb-12-2026), is to grow its master-planned residential development business in the UAE at scale (deepening its land bank of ~344 million sq ft in the UAE and continuing to launch integrated communities), while expanding the high-margin recurring revenue base (malls, hospitality, entertainment) to reduce earnings cyclicality. The company targets "strong CAGR" in revenue and profitability with "disciplined" execution on construction delivery, and targets growing recurring revenue as a share of total EBITDA. The FY2025 deck cites a backlog-to-revenue target of maintaining 5+ years of revenue visibility. The FY2025 Investor Presentation also frames a commitment to growing dividends, maintaining investment-grade credit ratings (BBB+/Baa1 achieved in 2025), and keeping net leverage near zero.

Emaar does not publish formal numerical forward guidance for revenue, EBITDA, or EPS. No specific margin targets, revenue growth rate commitments, or return-on-equity targets are disclosed in any filing in the data pool. The AED 200 bn masterplan announcement (June 11, 2026) represents the largest strategic commitment by management since the company's founding, but no phased targets, pre-sale thresholds, or timeline milestones were disclosed alongside it.

## 5. Management Read

Amit Jain has been CEO for nine years with no gap in strategy or stability; the senior team below him is long-tenured and internally developed. The directional record over FY2022–FY2025 is one of consistent delivery — every annual-period narrative commitment on UAE development, backlog conversion, mall margins, and dividend growth was met or exceeded. The one failure is the international segment: Emaar Misr's margin collapse in FY2025 (EGP net income down 64%) was not signalled or disclosed until after the fact, which is a candor mark against management's track record on international disclosure. The single biggest management signal is the AED 200 bn masterplan announced at what looks like a market peak: this is a commitment of unprecedented scale made at a time when Dubai residential prices are 50–80% above their 2020 trough and Emaar's own pipeline is already the largest it has ever been — taking on this scale of future obligation on the strength of cycle-peak margins, without disclosed phasing or pre-sale gates, is management's highest-risk decision to date.
