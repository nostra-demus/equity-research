## What This Module Found

The calendar is real, not thematic. Two events have proven or near-proven dates within the next two months and dominate the near-term read. The rest of the 12-month calendar is soft-window or undated, which is why timing visibility scores only 55/100.

The single most important driver is the **AED 200 Bn urban masterplan capital-discipline disclosure** (H2 FY2026, no hard date) [Capital IQ Key Developments, Jun-11-2026; management-governance module 02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md §5]. This is the largest strategic commitment since founding, and the AED 200 Bn is 84x one year of FY2025 free cash flow (FCF — cash the business throws off after capex). It will show whether Emaar's proven 93% pre-sell model (selling the units before spending the money to build them) holds at unprecedented scale, or whether equity capital gets committed ahead of buyer deposits.

The single most important risk on the calendar is a **Q2 new UAE property sales deceleration** confirmed at the Aug-10-2026 results. Unit volumes were already down 27% year-on-year in FY2025 despite value growth [earnings module 01_historical-financials.md §5]. If Q2 new sales come in below AED 12 Bn (vs AED 15 Bn as the sustaining threshold), it validates the Jun-11-2026 analyst's 22% consensus cut and triggers incremental estimate reductions for FY2027 and FY2028 — the years the AED 134.3 Bn UAE backlog is supposed to deliver.

- **Biggest reason it could be better than it looks:** Q2 results delivering EPS at or above AED 0.52 against the most deeply cut consensus bar in two years, plus Sukuk 3 repaid from AED 9,754 Mn cash with no market access needed — removes 26% of gross debt from the stack.

- **What evidence is missing:** the Q3 2026 results date (not yet published on the Dubai Financial Market); the English-language FY2025 annual report with RPT disclosures (expected H1-2027); any DFM board-meeting intimation disclosing the first phasing milestone for the AED 200 Bn masterplan.

Do NOT let the AED 200 Bn masterplan headline lift catalyst conviction — the phasing details have not been disclosed, and this catalyst cannot be timed by a buyer.

## Plain-English Glossary

Terms are listed in the order they first appear in this memo; each is used with a number the synthesis already carried.

- **Related-party transactions (RPT)** — deals a company does with parties linked to it (its owners, insiders, or affiliated companies), which can quietly move value out of minority shareholders' hands.
- **Margin of safety** — the discount between the price you pay and what you think the business is worth; it is the buffer that protects you when you are wrong.
- **Free cash flow (FCF)** — the cash the business generates after paying for its capital spending; it is the cash actually available to shareholders and lenders.
- **Sukuk** — an Islamic-finance bond structured to comply with Sharia rules; economically similar to a conventional bond maturity.
- **Earnings per share (EPS)** — the company's net profit divided by its shares outstanding.
- **Reverse-DCF** — working backward from today's share price to solve for the growth (or contraction) the market is implicitly pricing in, instead of forecasting forward.
- **Basis points (bps)** — one basis point is 0.01% (so 117 bps = 1.17 percentage points); used for small changes in rates and margins.
