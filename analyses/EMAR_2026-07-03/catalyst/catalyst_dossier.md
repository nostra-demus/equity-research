## 5. Calendar Read

The 12-month forward calendar has two events with near-proven or hard dates: the Q2 2026 results (~Aug-10-2026) and the Sukuk 3 maturity (Sep-15-2026). The government-ownership structure (§24 Filter 6) is the timing caveat that matters most: even if fair value is AED 27.7 and the backlog evidence supports it, a value-indifferent government owner with no catalyst to close the AED 12.20-to-AED 27.7 gap means the timing of any re-rating is genuinely unknown.
