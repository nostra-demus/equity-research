## 7. The Single Biggest Revenue Driver

**China domestic demand — governed jointly by the national appliance trade-in subsidy cycle and the real-estate market — is the single driver most likely to move group revenue by a large amount if it swings 10–20%, on both size and correlation grounds, even though it was NOT the largest contributor to FY2025's realized growth.** China is 48.5% of FY2025 revenue (RMB146,555mn of RMB302,347mn); a 10–20% swing in this single, policy-and-property-linked bucket moves 4.85–9.7pp of total group revenue — a High-magnitude, correlated move, unlike "overseas" (51.5% of revenue but split across 8 regions the company itself says "mitigat[e] the impact of fluctuations in a single region," `2026 First Quarter Report, p.3`). Its current direction is deteriorating: Q1 2026 China retail sales (ex-3C) fell 6.2% YoY (AVC), and management's own FY2026 outlook explicitly flags "under the pressure of a high base from the subsidy years, 2026 industry-wide growth is expected to face pressure" (`FY2025 Annual Report, p.3`) — a direct, company-sourced statement that the FY2023-2025 subsidy-driven demand was not run-rate and is now reversing. As a rough supporting check (**Inference, not from filings**: FY2025's full-year domestic/overseas revenue WEIGHTS, 48.5%/51.5%, applied to the disclosed Q1 2026 figures — total revenue −6.86% YoY and overseas revenue −3.2% YoY — implies domestic revenue fell roughly 10-11% YoY in Q1 2026, sharper than the group-level decline, though the company does not disclose the actual Q1 2026 domestic/overseas split so this cannot be verified against a filed number). This is distinct from, and does not contradict, Section 6's finding that Europe (+2.24pp) and Other Business (+2.14pp) — not China (+1.52pp) — were actually the largest single contributors to FY2025's realized growth: that decomposition describes what happened last year; this section answers what single lever carries the largest correlated swing risk to the next few quarters, and the company's own words point at the same lever (subsidy/property) that Q1 2026 has already started to show reversing.
