## 7. Bar Assessment

**Bar is high** — miss risk elevated.

No formal consolidated guidance exists to anchor a "sandbagged, ready-to-beat" case, and the one quantified segment target in the filing (CCR "high single-digit" revenue growth) cannot be gap-tested against a segment-level consensus that does not exist in this pool. Estimates HAVE been cut sharply over the past 6–12 months (FY2026 EPS consensus down ~14% from six months ago, FY2026 revenue down ~4.8% from twelve months ago) — the classic ingredient of a "low bar" setup. But that cutting has not been validated by results: the two most recently reported quarters (FQ4 2025 and FQ1 2026) both missed the ALREADY-lowered consensus on both revenue and EPS, and the FY2025 full-year print itself missed by −7.1% on EPS. Revision breadth in the trailing ~3 months remains heavily net negative into the next print (EPS 1 up vs. 11 down; gross margin 3 up vs. 20 down), meaning the Street has not yet finished cutting. Per CLAUDE.md §4's conservative-default rule — when evidence conflicts, weight the more recent, higher-quality signal — two consecutive missed prints against a falling bar outweigh the fact that the bar has fallen. Treat any read of "estimates have already been cut enough" as unproven going into FQ2 2026 (due 2026-08-27).
