### Contradictions Between Agents

No agent directly asserts the opposite of another agent's conclusion — the six specialist reads (01 through 07) converge on the same overall direction (deceleration, margin pressure, elevated miss risk). Two genuine **unresolved internal conflicts**, both self-flagged by the agent that found them rather than papered over, are carried forward as red flags in Section 2.9 rather than listed here as agent-vs-agent contradictions:

1. `03_margin-drivers.md` §2 flags that its own COGS-embedded depreciation line (worsening, +12.66% YoY) and CapIQ's broader total-D&A line (improving, falling 7.3% YoY) move in opposite directions and cannot be reconciled from the available disclosure — a basis conflict within one agent's own evidence base, not between two agents.
2. `06_earnings-quality.md`'s cash-conversion assessment (CFO/EBITDA never below 93.9%, "earnings are backed by cash") does not examine — because it is outside that agent's scope — that ~57% of the cash underpinning that conclusion sits with the controlling shareholder's own finance company (`business-model/11_capital-allocation-governance.md`). This is a coverage gap between modules, not a factual disagreement.
