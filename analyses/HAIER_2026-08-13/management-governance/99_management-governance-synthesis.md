# Management-Governance Module — HAIER (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Haier Smart Home's stewardship reads as standard and mixed — a stable, internally promoted CEO and an orderly CFO succession sit above a dividend-led capital-allocation record free of goodwill impairment, but whose newest invested capital turned value-destructive (incremental ROIC -8.3% in FY2025) even as the payout ratio keeps rising. Long-term pay is anchored entirely to ROE and profit-growth hurdles that genuinely missed in FY2025; individual insiders hold a negligible 0.066% of shares, almost all plan-vested, against a 34.49% Haier Group controlling bloc. The biggest risk is the deepening Haier Group Finance Co. relationship — 57% of Group cash parked there, related-party borrowing up roughly 19-fold. No hard disqualifier applies; this is a Watchlist-band governance profile, competent but not confidently aligned.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** The related-party financing relationship with Haier Group Finance Co. is deepening fast and on both axes at once — 57% of Group cash (RMB33,988mn) parked with an affiliated, non-independent finance company, and related-party borrowing up ~19x year-on-year to RMB3,767mn (37.7% of a RMB10,000mn cap) [`05_board-and-shareholder-rights.md`, 05-011, 05-012] — combined with a combined Chair/President/CEO role and a sub-majority-independent (60%) Audit Committee [`05`, 05-003, 05-004], the structural counterweights to self-dealing are thinner than the clean anti-takeover profile suggests.
- **Strongest bull point (steelman):** Long-term incentive pay — roughly 70–77% of CEO/CFO total compensation — is anchored entirely to ROE and net-profit-growth hurdles with zero revenue/EBITDA/deal-count/headcount exposure, and those hurdles genuinely bit in FY2025: achieved weighted-average ROE of 16.98% missed the 17.7% full-vesting bar for the newest plan vintage [`03_incentives-and-compensation.md`, 03-006]. This is real, checkable evidence that the pay structure is not rigged to reward growth-for-its-own-sake.
- **Single killer risk:** If the Haier Group Finance Co. relationship keeps growing at its current pace (borrowing cap utilization already at 37.7% after a 19x jump in one year) or a future related-party asset injection repeats the 2018 Fisher & Paykel pattern of an undisclosed transfer price [`02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md`, 02-003, RF-CAP-001], minority shareholders could face expropriation-style leakage that this module's own disclosure-candor score (56/100, itself capped by the absence of any post-2019 Q&A record) would not surface until well after the fact.
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** 0% pledging across every disclosed Haier-affiliated holder [`04_ownership-and-insider-behavior.md`, 04-006]; no dual-class stock, cumulative voting for board seats, and a 10%-holder right to call an EGM [`04`, 04-007; `05`, 05-015 to 05-017]; no goodwill impairment across five acquisitions in 12+ years [`02`, 02-002]; the FY2025 buyback tranches priced into a declining stock, not a rising one [`02`, 02-008]; and the FY2025 LTIP hurdle was genuinely missed rather than rubber-stamped [`03`, 03-006] — a real, evidenced counterweight already sitting in the record, not merely asserted.

## 5. Stewardship Summary

Management has delivered on the concrete commitments it controls — the Mar-2025 buyback closed on schedule, the nine-year-old GE Appliances integration produced enough internal credibility that its CEO now sits on Haier's main board, and no goodwill has been impaired across five acquisitions in over a decade — but the business itself has missed already-lowered Street consensus in each of the last three reported periods, and the most recent tranche of invested capital destroyed value on a marginal basis (incremental ROIC -8.3% in FY2025) even as diluted EPS reversed on a trailing basis. Incentives are genuinely pointed at per-share value, not empire-building: roughly three-quarters of CEO and CFO pay is multi-year-vesting equity anchored solely to ROE and profit-growth hurdles, and those hurdles bit in FY2025 rather than rubber-stamping vesting. Skin in the game sits almost entirely with the corporate controller (Haier Group Corporation, 34.49%), not with individual executives, whose combined holding (0.066%) is nearly all plan-vested rather than bought with personal cash — a real, if common-for-the-regime, alignment gap. Minority shareholders benefit from a clean anti-entrenchment profile (no pledging, no dual-class stock, cumulative voting, a 10%-holder right to call a meeting) but face a combined Chair/President/CEO role, a sub-majority-independent Audit Committee, and a related-party financing relationship with Haier Group Finance Co. that is growing quickly (borrowings up ~19x year-on-year, 57% of Group cash parked with the same affiliate) — disclosed and independent-director-reviewed today, but the single item most likely to change this read if it keeps deteriorating. The single most important reason to trust this team with shareholder capital is that its long-term pay structure has no growth-for-its-own-sake metric in it and those targets were missed, not gamed, in the most recently completed year; the single most important reason for caution is that the controlling shareholder's financial arm is becoming a larger, faster-growing counterparty to the listed company's own cash and credit.
