# Governance Data Triage — HAIER

Controlling shareholder: Haier Group Corporation, a Qingdao-registered collectively-owned enterprise (集体所有制企业) — not a family promoter, not a central-government SOE, not an unrelated conglomerate. There is no proxy/DEF 14A, no SEBI-style AGM notice, and no Form 4 in this pool — because none exist for a China-incorporated, dual-listed issuer. The correct local equivalents (SSE Annual Report Board's Report / Corporate Governance section, HKEX Annual Report Connected-Transactions & Directors' Report, CSRC shareholding-pattern disclosures, and Capital IQ ownership/board/compensation exports) are present and are used per CLAUDE.md §27 and this module's Jurisdiction-Aware Source Mapping. Both FY2025 annual reports (SSE/China-ASBE and HKEX/IFRS) were read in their original Chinese and are disclosed, not opaque, per §27.
