# Management & Track Record — HAIER

Company: Haier Smart Home Co., Ltd. — dual-primary listed A-share (SSE 600690) and H-share (HKEX 6690), plus a Frankfurt D-share and a US Level-I ADR. Controlling shareholder is Haier Group Corporation (海尔集团公司), a Qingdao-registered collectively-owned enterprise (集体所有制企业, not a family promoter, not a central-government SOE) holding 37.45% of shares as of Mar-27-2026 [Haier Smart Home Co Ltd SHSE 600690 Public Ownership Summary.rtf, Top Holders, position date 2026-03-27]. No proxy/DEF 14A or SEBI-style AGM notice exists for a China-incorporated dual-listed issuer; the local equivalents used here are the FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, China ASBE) and FY2025 Annual Report (HKEX, IFRS) — both read in their original Chinese and translated — plus CapIQ board/compensation/ownership exports, per CLAUDE.md §27. No prior shareholder letter or recent earnings-call transcript exists in the data pool (the only verbatim transcripts are ~7 years stale, from Q2/Q3 2019); this caps how much of this report can test dated prior promises against outcomes — see §4 and the Partial-Data note below.

## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Management frames the company as evolving from an appliance maker into a "user-centred, platform-based technology ecosystem enterprise" [FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), p.2305 region — 以用户为中心的平台服务型科技生态企业], with a near-term product push around "unmanned housework" (无人家务) — appliance-plus-robotics "swarm intelligence" [FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Business Review §III, p.2373 region]. The explicit, checkable targets set for FY2026 are: a CCR (Carrier Commercial Refrigeration) segment revenue growth of "high single digits" (roughly 7–9%) with further operating-margin improvement; a formal three-year capital-return floor of ≥58% of net profit paid as dividends in FY2026, stepping up to ≥60% in FY2027 and FY2028 (versus 55% actually paid out of FY2025 net income, up from ~48% in FY2024); and a set of China retail-channel build-out KPIs (3,000+ new specialty stores, 6,000+ store upgrades, 100 new premium Casarte experience centers, 100% direct-to-consumer warehousing by year-end) [Haier_Smart_Home_Co_Ltd_-_Form_Annual_Report(Apr-27-2026).pdf, Business Review §VI "Operating Plan," p.64–65, cited in `earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md`]. The company does **not** issue formal consolidated revenue, EBITDA, or EPS guidance — the last recorded guidance entry in the CapIQ Guidance tab is from FH1 2012 [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md`, §2].

## 5. Management Read

Leadership is stable and internally promoted, not charismatic-founder-led: CEO Li Huagang has run the company for seven years and the recently retired 15-year CFO was promoted rather than pushed out, which is a genuine stability signal, though the new CFO's background is a real disclosure gap in this pool. On promise-vs-delivery, the only two dated, checkable items both came back Kept (the FY2025 buyback closed as executed, and the nine-year-old GE Appliances acquisition delivered enough credibility that its CEO now sits on Haier's global board) — but the sample is thin because no prior shareholder letter or recent transcript exists to test against, and the company gives no formal consolidated guidance at all. The single biggest management signal is a mixed one: management has quietly kept the concrete commitments it has made (buybacks, integration, a step-up dividend trend), while the business itself has missed already-lowered Street expectations in each of the last three reported periods (FQ4 2025, FQ1 2026, and the FY2025 full year) — competent stewardship of what it controls, against a harder operating backdrop than the Street had modeled.
