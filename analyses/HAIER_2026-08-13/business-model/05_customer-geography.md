# Customer And Geography Map — HAIER

Company: Haier Smart Home Co., Ltd. — dual-primary listed on SSE (A-share, 600690, China ASBE/CAS basis) and HKEX (H-share, 6690, IFRS basis), plus a Frankfurt D-share and a Level-I ADR. Figures below are FY2025 (ended Dec-31-2025) unless stated. Reporting currency is RMB (CNY); no cross-currency conversion is needed as both filings report in RMB.

## 1. Customer Map

Haier Smart Home is a business-to-consumer household-appliance maker. It does not sell to a small set of named corporate customers the way a component supplier or contract manufacturer would — it sells through mass-market retail and distribution channels to millions of end consumers. The company's own filings confirm this structure and give one aggregate concentration figure; they do not name individual customers.

## 4. Read

This is a geography-concentrated business, not a customer-concentrated one: no single customer or small customer group is material (top 5 combined are only 19.6% of sales, certified as not severely dependent), but Mainland China alone still supplies 48.5% of FY2025 revenue — just under the 50% single-country threshold and above 50% as recently as FY2020. The China concentration is naked, not contractually secured: the filing itself describes distributors, retail chains and online platforms as ordering "at such time and in such quantities as they deem appropriate," with no minimum-volume or contract-term commitment disclosed for any channel, in China or overseas. The single biggest dependency the synthesizer should know: just under half of group revenue still depends on discretionary, non-contracted order flow from the Chinese domestic appliance retail and distribution channel, with North America (26.4%, built substantially on the GE Appliances brand) as the second-largest and similarly uncommitted geography.
