# Segment Map — HAIER

Company: Haier Smart Home Co., Ltd. — dual-primary listed SSE (A-share, 600690) and HKEX (H-share, 6690), plus a Frankfurt D-share and a US Level-I ADR program. Fiscal year ends Dec-31. Figures below are FY2025 (ended Dec-31-2025) unless stated otherwise, reported in RMB million (CNY mn).

Two audited annual filings cover the same FY2025 period on different accounting standards and were cross-checked against each other:
- A-share Annual Report (China ASBE / CAS basis) — `FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Note 11 (分部信息/Segment Information), pp.222-225`
- H-share Annual Report (IFRS basis) — `FY2025 Annual Report (HKEX, IFRS), Note 4 (經營分部資料/Operating Segment Information), pp.227-228`

The two filings' segment revenue figures reconcile to within RMB 18mn on a RMB 302,347mn base (a 0.006% difference — CAS external revenue by segment sums to RMB302,346.78mn vs IFRS's RMB302,329mn) — consistent with normal CAS/IFRS presentation differences, not a data conflict. Segment profit differs slightly more (CAS "Other Business" segment profit RMB354.29mn vs IFRS RMB336mn) because IFRS nets a small amount differently in the "Other Business" line; the four core product segments' profit figures match to the RMB million. No `ciq_facts.json` sidecar exists for this ticker (per data-triage), so all figures below are this agent's own sourced read from the filings, cross-checked against the CapIQ Segments workbook, which independently reproduces the identical CAS figures (`Haier Smart Home Co Ltd SHSE 600690 Financials Segments.xls, tab: Segments`).

## 2. Dominant Segment

Household Food Storage & Cooking Solutions (refrigerators/freezers plus kitchen appliances, disclosed as one reportable segment with two product-line splits) is the dominant segment by both measures: 41.7% of FY2025 external revenue (RMB125,975mn of RMB302,347mn) and 43.4% of segment profit before tax (RMB9,008mn of RMB20,768mn) `[FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Note 11(1), pp.222-223; cross-checked FY2025 Annual Report (HKEX, IFRS), Note 4, p.227]`. It leads on both revenue and profit share by a wide margin over the next-largest segment (Home Laundry Care Solutions, 21.6% of revenue / 31.8% of profit), so there is no revenue-vs-profit conflict to resolve. This is not a single-segment company (no segment exceeds 85% of revenue or profit) — Haier Smart Home is a multi-category white-goods manufacturer with a clear lead product line, not a de facto single-product business.

## 3. Segment Disclosure Quality

Segment definitions changed materially between FY2021 and FY2022 and have been consistent since: CapIQ's longer segment history (`Haier Smart Home Co Ltd SHSE 600690 Financials Segments.xls, tab: Segments`) shows a standalone "Overseas Home Appliance and Smart Home Business" segment worth RMB100,623mn / RMB113,725mn (roughly 44-50% of total revenue) in FY2020/FY2021 that disappears entirely from FY2022 onward, with overseas revenue folded into the five product-based segments used today (evidenced by Kitchen Appliances jumping from RMB3,496mn in FY2021 to RMB38,953mn in FY2022 — a reclassification, not 11x organic growth). Downstream agents should not compare pre-2022 and post-2022 segment figures without treating this as a structural break; the current 5-segment structure has been stable and comparably disclosed for FY2022-FY2025 (`FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Note 11(1)`, showing both current-period and prior-period figures on the same basis). The "Other Business" bucket is large — 13.0% of external revenue (RMB39,229mn) — which exceeds the 10% materiality threshold for a catch-all line, and it is disclosed only as a single aggregate ("channel distribution, equipment/component parts, small appliances, logistics services and other") with no further product-level split, unlike the four named product segments. Profit is disclosed at segment level, not just revenue — segment profit before tax, segment assets, and segment liabilities are all reported for every segment in both the CAS and IFRS filings, a meaningfully deeper disclosure than a revenue-only segment note. One real gap: geography and business segment are not cross-tabulated — the geographic-revenue note (`FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Note 11(2), p.225`) breaks external revenue into 8 regions (Mainland China, Americas, Australia, South Asia, Europe, Southeast Asia, Middle East & Africa, Japan, Others) and non-current assets into 2 buckets (Mainland China vs Others), but discloses no profit by geography and no segment-by-region matrix, so a reader cannot see, for example, how much of Air Solutions' profit comes from China versus the rest of the world.
