# Competitive Map — HAIER

Both required upstream inputs are present: `business-model/02_business-identity.md` and `business-model/03_segment-map.md`.

## 1. Dominant Segment

Household Food Storage & Cooking Solutions — refrigerators/freezers plus kitchen appliances, reported as one segment with two product-line splits — is Haier Smart Home's dominant segment: 41.7% of FY2025 external revenue (RMB125,975m of RMB302,347m) and 43.4% of segment profit before tax [`03_segment-map.md`, citing FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Note 11(1), pp.222–223]. Refrigerators/freezers alone are 27.9% of revenue and the larger of the two product lines.

## 2. Named Competitors

Selection basis: Haier's own Chinese-language annual report discusses market share and industry rank ("行业第二", "位居行业前二") without naming specific rival companies by name in the pool extract reviewed. The company-level "named by company" source is therefore thin. The names below come from the S&P Capital IQ competitor-relationship export in the data pool (`Haier Smart Home Co Ltd SHSE 600690 Competitors.rtf`), which is built from third-party filings that name Haier alongside these companies as home-appliance rivals — this counts as "named by third party" per the selection criteria, and is the deepest competitor evidence available in the pool. All three below make refrigerators and/or kitchen appliances (Haier's dominant segment), and all are within roughly 3x of Haier's scale.

### Competitor A — Midea Group Co., Ltd. (SZSE:000333)

- **Ticker / listing:** Shenzhen Stock Exchange, 000333
- **Where they compete:** Global — household and central air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, kitchen appliances, and small appliances under the Midea brand; direct product overlap with Haier's dominant refrigerator/kitchen-appliance segment as well as air conditioning and laundry [`Haier Smart Home Co Ltd SHSE 600690 Competitors.rtf`, business description sourced from Whirlpool Corporation (NYSE:WHR) 2026 Form 10-K, which names Midea as a home-appliance competitor alongside Haier].
- **Scale:** LTM total revenue $68,169.1m (as of LTM filing date 2026-04-29, USD, Capital IQ) vs. Haier's LTM total revenue $44,028.5m on the same comp-set basis — Midea is ~1.55x Haier's revenue [`Company Comparable Analysis Haier Smart Home Co Ltd .xls`, tab: Financial Data]. On Haier's own FY2025 audited revenue of RMB302,329m (≈$42bn), the ratio is similar.
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM gross margin 26.3%, LTM EBITDA margin 11.5%, LTM EBIT margin 9.8%, LTM net income margin 9.6% — versus Haier's own LTM EBIT margin of 6.9% and net margin of 6.3% on the identical comp-set basis [`Company Comparable Analysis Haier Smart Home Co Ltd .xls`, tab: Operating Statistics]. ROIC/ROE not disclosed in this comp export — margin is the only peer-economics figure available from the pool for Midea.
- **Source named in:** Capital IQ competitor export (third-party source document: Whirlpool Corporation 2026 Form 10-K); independently corroborated by inclusion in Haier's own Capital IQ comparable-company set (`Company Comparable Analysis Haier Smart Home Co Ltd .xls`), which groups Midea with Haier as a China appliance peer.
- **One-line read:** A larger, more diversified Chinese appliance conglomerate (adds robotics/building tech/industrial segments) that on this comp set earns a higher EBIT margin than Haier in the same product categories.

### Competitor B — Gree Electric Appliances, Inc. of Zhuhai (SZSE:000651)

- **Ticker / listing:** Shenzhen Stock Exchange, 000651
- **Where they compete:** Primarily China's #1 or #2 domestic residential-air-conditioner brand, but its product line also includes refrigerators, washing machines, kitchen appliances, and small appliances [`Haier Smart Home Co Ltd SHSE 600690 Competitors.rtf`, business description sourced from AB Electrolux (publ) 10-K filing, which names Gree as a home-appliance competitor alongside Haier]. Overlap with Haier's dominant refrigerator/kitchen-appliance segment exists but is secondary to Gree's core air-conditioner business — flagged as a partial segment fit, included because Gree is one of the three most commonly cited Chinese white-goods majors alongside Haier and Midea, and Haier's own filing places it directly against Gree in air conditioning ("公司中央空调国内市场份额同比提升 0.5 个百分点至 11.3%，位居行业前二" — Haier ranks #2 domestically in central air conditioning, implying Gree or another rival leads) [FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Business Review, p.30].
- **Scale:** LTM total revenue $25,491.4m (LTM filing date 2026-04-28, USD, Capital IQ) vs. Haier's $44,028.5m on the same basis — Haier is ~1.73x Gree's revenue [`Company Comparable Analysis Haier Smart Home Co Ltd .xls`, tab: Financial Data].
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM gross margin 29.8%, LTM EBITDA margin 20.2%, LTM EBIT margin 17.4%, LTM net income margin 17.0% — roughly 2.5x Haier's LTM EBIT margin (6.9%) and 2.7x Haier's net margin (6.3%) on the identical comp-set basis [`Company Comparable Analysis Haier Smart Home Co Ltd .xls`, tab: Operating Statistics]. ROIC/ROE not disclosed in this comp export.
- **Source named in:** Capital IQ competitor export (third-party source document: AB Electrolux 10-K); independently corroborated by inclusion in Haier's own Capital IQ comparable-company set.
- **One-line read:** Smaller in overall revenue but far more profitable on every disclosed margin line — a materially higher-return operator concentrated in a single, higher-margin product category (air conditioning) rather than Haier's broader, thinner-margin multi-category mix.

### Competitor C — Whirlpool Corporation (NYSE:WHR)

- **Ticker / listing:** New York Stock Exchange, WHR
- **Where they compete:** North America and Latin America primarily — refrigerators, freezers, laundry appliances, cooking/kitchen appliances, and dishwashers under the Whirlpool, Maytag, KitchenAid, and other brands; directly overlaps with Haier's dominant refrigerator/kitchen-appliance segment, especially in North America where Haier competes through its GE Appliances brand (acquired 2016) [`Haier Smart Home Co Ltd SHSE 600690 Competitors.rtf`, business description sourced from AB Electrolux (publ) 10-K filing, which names Whirlpool as a home-appliance competitor alongside Haier; `02_business-identity.md` confirms GE Appliances as Haier's North America brand].
- **Scale:** FY2025 GAAP net sales $15,524m [Whirlpool Corporation, Fourth-Quarter and Full-Year 2025 Results press release, 2026-01-28 — Web, company's own results announcement, not in HAIER data pool]. Against Haier's FY2025 audited revenue of RMB302,329m (≈$42bn) or the $44,028.5m LTM figure in Haier's own Capital IQ comp set, Haier is roughly 2.8x Whirlpool's scale — at the outer edge of, but within, the ~5x comparable-scale threshold.
- **Profitability / return on capital:** FY2025 GAAP operating income $838m on $15,524m net sales = GAAP operating margin 5.4%; ongoing (non-GAAP) EBIT margin 4.7%; GAAP net earnings margin 2.2% [Whirlpool Corporation, Fourth-Quarter and Full-Year 2025 Results press release, 2026-01-28 — Web, unverified, not in HAIER data pool]. This is below Haier's own LTM EBIT margin of 6.9% and net margin of 6.3% [`Company Comparable Analysis Haier Smart Home Co Ltd .xls`, tab: Operating Statistics]. ROIC not stated in the source located; Whirlpool discloses a non-GAAP ROIC metric in its own filings but the specific FY2025 figure was not found in the sources checked — treat as not confirmed.
- **Source named in:** Capital IQ competitor export (third-party source document: AB Electrolux 10-K); Whirlpool is the most commonly cited global legacy peer to Haier in trade press given the two companies' direct North America rivalry (Whirlpool vs. GE Appliances).
- **One-line read:** The most scale-comparable non-Chinese peer, but running thinner operating and net margins than Haier on the most recent full year — a name-brand incumbent still absorbing US tariff and cost pressure that Haier's North America segment also flagged as a headwind.

## 3. Competitive Position

**Position: Gaining share** in the dominant segment. Haier states its China offline refrigerator retail-value share rose to 47.7% in FY2025, up 3.6 percentage points year on year, and that Haier's refrigerator and freezer retail volumes have ranked #1 globally for 18 and 15 consecutive years respectively (Euromonitor-sourced company claim) [FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Business Review, "（四）产业经营分析 1、智慧大厨房 (1) 制冷产业", p.27]. The adjacent laundry-care segment shows the same pattern — domestic offline share up 1.9 points to 47.4%, and global washing-machine share rising to 14.4% (#1 globally, per Euromonitor as cited by the company) [FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Business Review, p.29]. No independent (non-company) share-tracking source in the pool corroborates these Euromonitor-attributed figures, so this read rests on the company's own citation of a third-party data provider, not on the provider's report directly — a Level-3/4 claim, not Level-5. Partially offsetting: the FY2025 shareholder letter separately states that US tariffs and high interest rates hurt the North America segment's operating results, a profitability headwind that coexists with the share-gain claim rather than contradicting it [Preliminary Annual Report (HKEX Results Announcement), Mar-26-2026, "致股東的函件"].

## 4. Competitive Shape

The market is best read as fragmented globally but concentrated domestically. Globally, dozens of national and regional manufacturers compete in large appliances — the Capital IQ competitor export for Haier lists more than 50 distinct rival relationships spanning Whirlpool, Electrolux, Midea, Gree, LG Electronics, Samsung Electronics, BSH Hausgeräte (Bosch/Siemens, privately held), Panasonic, Arçelik, Hisense, Sharp, Daikin, and many India/Bangladesh-focused regional players [`Haier Smart Home Co Ltd SHSE 600690 Competitors.rtf`] — consistent with a fragmented global structure with no single dominant player (Haier's own claimed global refrigerator volume-share rank is #1, but no absolute global share percentage is disclosed in the pool). Within China specifically, the structure is concentrated among a small number of brands: Haier holds 47.7% offline refrigerator share, 47.4% washing-machine share, and 32.5% water-heater share domestically [FY2025 Annual Report (SSE, CAS), Chairman's Letter, p.3], with Midea and Gree as the next-largest named domestic rivals — consistent with an oligopoly-leaning domestic market led by these three groups, though the pool does not disclose Midea's or Gree's category-specific China refrigerator share needed to state a precise top-3 concentration ratio.

## 5. Caveat

Haier's own annual report (Chinese-language, SSE/CAS filing) discusses competitive rank in relative terms ("行业第二", "位居行业前二") without naming the specific rival company in the text reviewed, so none of the three profiled competitors above is "named by company" in the strict sense — all three are sourced from the Capital IQ third-party competitor-relationship export, cross-checked against Haier's own Capital IQ comparable-company set for two of the three (Midea, Gree). This is a real gap: a targeted search of Haier's own risk factors, MD&A, and available earnings-call transcripts in the pool (2019 vintage only — no recent transcript was in the pool) did not surface company-named competitors by name. BSH Hausgeräte (Bosch/Siemens) is a comparably large global rival named in the same Capital IQ export (LTM revenue $13.1bn as of a stale Dec-2014 figure) but is privately held as a subsidiary of Robert Bosch GmbH, so no current audited margin or return figure could be sourced for it — it was excluded from the three profiles for this reason, not because it is not credible. AB Electrolux (publ) (OM:ELUX B) is another highly credible, comparably scaled competitor (LTM revenue $13,463.41m per the Capital IQ export) that was considered but not profiled in the top 3 to keep the list to the most segment-relevant and best-evidenced peers; its FY2025 operating margin (2.8%, on SEK 131,282m net sales and SEK 3,657m operating income excluding non-recurring items) is available from its own year-end report [AB Electrolux/Electrolux Group, Year-end Report Q4 2025, 2026-01-30 — Web, unverified, not in HAIER data pool] and downstream `moat` may want it as a fourth peer data point.
