# Catalyst Module — HAIER (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Haier's calendar carries real, dated, evidenced catalysts, not just a thematic story: the nearest one that can actually move the stock is the H1 2026 interim results due 2026-08-27/28, about two weeks from today, which tests whether North America's claimed post-weather-shock recovery is durable against a China demand drag that is already visible in the data. The single most important catalyst is not one date but the margin-erosion trajectory — raw-material cost inflation against China's fading trade-in subsidy — that separates the valuation module's CNY 31.78 bull case from its CNY 15.90 bear case, and it will only be confirmed or denied print by print over the next two to three quarters. The biggest bearish catalyst is the H1 print itself: two straight misses against an already-cut consensus and net-negative revision breadth going in, layered on the ongoing, undated China subsidy fade. Verdict: dated, evidenced near-term catalysts exist, and the nearest one skews bearish on setup.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** The H1 2026 print (2026-08-27/28) is the nearest catalyst that can move the stock, and its own setup points down — the last two reported quarters (FQ4 2025, FQ1 2026) both missed an already-cut consensus, and estimate-revision breadth is net negative (1 up / 11 down) going into this print [`earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md` §1; `01_catalyst-calendar.md` row 2].
- **Strongest bull point:** The CNY 6,000mn buyback program (authorized 2026-03-26, 12-month window through 2027-03-26) is a proven-date, evidenced, self-limiting capital-return catalyst — 24.8% completed (CNY 1,488.06mn / 71.04mn shares) through 2026-06-30, with unused shares required to be cancelled after three years rather than sitting as open-ended dilution [Key Developments log, Buyback Transaction Announcement 2026-03-26 and Tranche Updates through 2026-06-30].
- **Single killer risk specific to the calendar:** Management's claim that "tariff impact will gradually ease in 2026" is written into the calendar as the bullish trigger for the US/EU tariff catalyst, but it is an unverified forecast with no scheduled policy decision, hearing, or exemption date anywhere in the pool — treating it as conviction-lifting would be exactly the undated "catalyst soon" language CLAUDE.md §17 bans. It must be read as vague, not proven, timing.
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** China's trade-in subsidy fade is not a forecast — Q1 2026 China retail (ex-3C) already printed −6.2% YoY, and raw-material (copper) cost inflation already cut FY2025 gross margin by 1.1 percentage points despite the cost-reduction program [`business-model/10_external-dependency.md` §5; `earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md` §3]. Both bearish drivers are already partly realized, not merely hypothesized.

## 5. Simple Summary

- Yes, there are real, dated catalysts — not just a story: a dividend payment, an earnings date, a buyback deadline, and two bond maturities all have exact dates or exact windows.
- The nearest one that matters is the H1 2026 earnings release, due 2026-08-27/28 (about two weeks away).
- The most important catalyst is not a single date — it is whether Haier's shrinking profit margin stabilizes, and that only shows up gradually, print by print, over the next several quarters.
- The biggest thing that could go wrong on the calendar is the upcoming H1 print itself: the last two quarters both missed, and analysts have been cutting estimates going in.
- Yes, the thesis can lean on timing for the next two weeks (the print) but not for the bigger question (margin trajectory), which has no fixed resolution date.
- This module is useful for the master synthesizer: the near-term calendar is dated and evidence-backed, and it flags exactly where the calendar cannot substitute for judgment.
- No §24 red-flag catalyst (bad M&A, unproven turnaround) is on this calendar being sold as good news.
- The related-party cash concentration with the controlling shareholder's own finance company is a slow-moving, undated risk to watch, not a dated event.
