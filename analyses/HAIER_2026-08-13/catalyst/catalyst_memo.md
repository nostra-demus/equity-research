## What This Module Found

Haier's calendar carries real, dated catalysts, not just a thematic story: a dividend payment, an earnings date, a buyback deadline, and two bond maturities all have exact dates or exact windows. Five of the eleven catalysts are hard-dated; the other six are real but genuinely undated and are labelled vague, not dressed up as scheduled events.

The nearest catalyst that can actually move the stock is the H1 2026 interim results, due 2026-08-27/28 — about two weeks out. (The 2026-08-21 final FY2025 dividend, RMB 8.9151 per 10 shares, is already priced in: its ex-date passed on 2026-08-03.)

The single most important catalyst is not one date. It is whether gross-margin erosion — raw-material cost inflation (copper, steel, aluminium) against China's fading trade-in subsidy — stabilizes or keeps going. That question separates the valuation module's CNY 31.78 bull case from its CNY 15.90 bear case, and it will only be answered print by print over the next two to three quarters (H1 in Aug 2026, Q3 ~late October, FY2026 ~March 2027).

The biggest bearish catalyst is the H1 print itself. Its setup points down: the last two reported quarters (FQ4 2025, FQ1 2026) both missed an already-cut consensus, and estimate-revision breadth going in is net negative — 1 analyst up, 11 down over the trailing ~3 months [`earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md` §1]. This layers on an already-realized China demand drag: Q1 2026 China retail (excluding 3C) already printed −6.2% year-on-year [`business-model/10_external-dependency.md` §5]. Both bearish drivers are partly real, not merely forecast: copper cost inflation already cut FY2025 gross margin by 1.1 percentage points despite the cost-reduction program.

The clearest bull item is the CNY 6,000mn buyback (authorized 2026-03-26, running through 2027-03-26), 24.8% completed (CNY 1,488.06mn / 71.04mn shares) through 2026-06-30, with unused shares required to be cancelled after three years rather than left as open-ended dilution (new shares that shrink each holder's slice) [Key Developments log, 2026-03-26 through 2026-06-30].

## What Would Change This Read

The observable events the synthesis named that would move the module's read:

- **H1 2026 print (2026-08-27/28).** Better if North America normalizes post-blizzard shock and gross margin holds near FY2025's 26.3% full-year level despite copper inflation. Worse if China demand deteriorates further on the subsidy high-base effect and/or raw-material inflation outpaces the cost-reduction program, pushing gross margin below 26.3%.
- **Buyback pace (through 2027-03-26).** Better if the pace accelerates or the full CNY 6,000mn completes early, signaling management sees the stock cheap against the base-case CNY 23.67 fair value. Worse if it stalls or lapses materially underspent, readable as lost conviction or a cash-priority conflict with the stepped-up dividend.
- **China trade-in subsidy (ongoing, undated).** Better if Beijing extends or re-scopes it (no such announcement exists in the pool). Worse if it keeps fading with no replacement.
- **US/EU tariffs (ongoing, undated).** Better if rates are cut/exempted or supply-chain relocation offsets the cost. Worse if tariffs persist or escalate.
- **Related-party cap utilization with Haier Group Finance Co. (annual cycle).** Better if utilization stabilizes/falls or an arm's-length pricing benchmark is disclosed. Worse if borrowing keeps climbing toward the RMB 10,000mn cap.

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** dated, evidenced near-term catalysts exist, and the nearest one (H1 2026 results, 2026-08-27/28) skews bearish on setup.
- **Could be better than it looks:** the buyback is a proven-date, self-limiting capital return (24.8% done, unused shares cancelled after 3 years), and a genuine North America normalization would flip the near-term print.
- **Could be worse than it looks:** the H1 print is set up to miss — two straight misses against a cut consensus, revisions 1 up / 11 down — on top of an already-realized China slowdown (−6.2% YoY) and copper-driven margin pressure.
- **Missing evidence:** no confirmed Q3 2026 or FY2026 results date (both inferred from filing cadence), and no scheduled subsidy-policy or tariff-policy decision date anywhere in the pool.
- **One thing to watch next:** the 2026-08-27/28 H1 print — specifically whether gross margin holds near 26.3% — but do not let a single H1 beat or miss stand in for resolving the multi-quarter margin-erosion question.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** a specific, datable event that could move the stock.
- **Consensus:** the average of analysts' forecasts for the company's results.
- **Estimate-revision breadth:** the count of analysts raising versus cutting their forecasts (here 1 up, 11 down).
- **Gross margin:** the share of sales left after the direct cost of making the goods; here about 26.3% of sales in FY2025.
- **Buyback:** the company using its own cash to buy back its shares, shrinking the share count.
- **Dilution:** new shares that shrink each existing holder's slice of the company.
- **Bond maturity / refinancing:** the date a bond must be repaid; refinancing is raising new debt to repay it.
- **Related-party transaction:** dealing with an affiliated company (here the controlling shareholder's own finance arm), which can carry a conflict of interest.
