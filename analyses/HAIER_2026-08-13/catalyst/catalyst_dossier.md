## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

The single biggest swing factor for this stock is not one dated event — it is **whether Haier's margin erosion stabilizes or keeps going**, which is what separates the valuation module's bull case (CNY 31.78/share) from its headline structural-reset bear case (CNY 15.90/share), a 2.0x spread. That trajectory is driven by the same two forces named above (raw-material commodity costs and China's subsidy-fade demand), and it cannot be resolved by a single print — it will be tested incrementally: first at the 2026-08-27/28 H1 results (does gross margin hold near FY2025's 26.3%?), then at the inferred ~late-October 2026 Q3 results, then at the inferred ~March 2027 FY2026 annual results, where the moat module's "eroding" verdict (gross margin down 460bp over five years, ROIC falling for two straight readings) would either be confirmed or broken. A buyer cannot time this to one date — they can only watch it print-by-print, and the reverse-DCF's own honest caveat applies: today's price could be pricing in unprecedented (and possibly excessive) pessimism about this trajectory, or it could simply reflect a market discount rate materially higher than the DCF module's own low, mechanically-derived 4.10% WACC. Neither this module nor the valuation module can resolve that ambiguity from the data available.
