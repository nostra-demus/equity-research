# Balance-Sheet-Survival Module — HAIER (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Haier is net cash on every basis since FY2021 — CNY 4,952.2mn net cash strict, CNY 16,975.1mn broader — even as gross debt rose 43% since FY2022 to fund a rising dividend, not weaker operations. Some 55% of gross debt sits in one within-12-month bucket, but FY2025 cash alone covers it 2.0x, with no refinancing dependence. Liquidity runway is about 27.3 months, though no committed bank facility or covenant package is disclosed, leaving true covenant headroom "not assessable." The stress test shows liquidity never runs out even at a full EBITDA wipeout, and only an indicative, unconfirmed covenant breaks, at a 54% EBITDA decline. Verdict: Fortress balance sheet, capped by disclosure gaps, not by the numbers.

## 1. Solvency Verdict

- **Verdict: Fortress balance sheet** — net cash on every basis at every period FY2021 through Mar-2026, deep committed cash-plus-liquid-investments liquidity that outlasts even a complete (100%) EBITDA wipeout for 12 months, and wide indicative covenant headroom. The one thing that keeps this from being an unqualified Fortress read: real covenant headroom is genuinely "Not assessable" (no indenture/credit-agreement/covenant package in the data pool) and no credit rating exists anywhere in the pool — the fortress picture rests on transparent, reproducible math against the company's own filings, not on a confirmed lender's own risk assessment.
- **Net leverage (net debt / EBITDA):** net cash of CNY 4,952.2mn (strict basis, canonical) ÷ CNY 26,543.4mn FY2025 reported EBITDA = **(0.19x)**, i.e. net cash, not net debt. On the broader basis (also netting short-term investments/trading securities): net cash of CNY 16,975.1mn ÷ EBITDA = (0.64x). Gross debt / EBITDA (shown alongside, not inverted by cash) = **1.61x** on latest-year EBITDA, 1.84x on the 5-year mid-cycle/normalised EBITDA basis. [`01_capital-structure-and-leverage.md` §4/§5/§7]
- **Liquidity runway:** **≈27.3 months** (net-of-FCF basis: [12-month maturities CNY 23,452.2mn + committed dividends/buybacks CNY 19,873.4mn − FY2025 FCF CNY 17,151.3mn] ÷ 12 = CNY 2,181.2mn/month; CNY 59,644.6mn liquidity ÷ CNY 2,181.2mn). Confidence capped at 60/100 because no committed/undrawn bank-facility figure exists anywhere in the pool — no revolver instrument was even identified. [`03_liquidity-runway.md` §3/§5]
- **Maturity wall (% within 24 months):** **55.0%** of gross debt (CNY 23,452.2mn of CNY 42,669.5mn), all bucketed to a single fiscal-year-end date (Dec-31-2026) — no additional dated instrument matures before Dec-2027. Fully self-funded: FY2025 cash on hand alone (CNY 47,621.7mn) covers it 2.0x over, without needing FCF, a revolver, or new issuance. [`02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md` §1/§2/§4]
- **Tightest covenant + headroom:** **Not assessable** — no real covenant package is disclosed anywhere in the pool (CapIQ: "no Indentures or Credit Agreements available"). The indicative, labelled market-typical assumption used for reference — a max 3.5x gross debt/EBITDA ceiling — shows +54.1% headroom today. [`04_coverage-and-covenants.md` §2/§3]
- **Stress break point (EBITDA decline that breaks it):** the indicative assumed leverage covenant (3.5x max gross debt/EBITDA) breaks at a **54.1% EBITDA decline**; committed liquidity is never exhausted on an EBITDA decline alone (the solve returns h ≥ 1 — even a full 100% EBITDA wipeout still leaves a CNY 10.7bn liquidity surplus against 12-month obligations). [`06_downside-stress-test.md` §3]
- Solvency strength /100: **85** — net cash, modest 1.61x gross leverage, coverage 9.91x, no hard cap triggered (a maturity schedule exists, interim data exists, no material off-balance-sheet undisclosed exposure). Capped below "very strong" for the undisclosed revolver/covenant/rating picture.
- Liquidity runway /100: **60** (hard cap — no revolver/committed-facility figure disclosed anywhere in the pool; per MODULE_RULES this caps liquidity-runway confidence at 60 even though the raw computed runway of 27.3 months would otherwise score higher).
- Refinancing risk /100 *(higher = worse)*: **28** — low risk in substance (wall self-funded 2.0x by cash, no refi cost step-up evident, two 2025 bond placements prove current market access) but not zero, because 36.8% of the stack carries no disclosed maturity date and there is no credit rating to independently corroborate market access.
- Covenant headroom /100 *(or "Not assessable")*: **Not assessable** (no covenant disclosure in the pool; indicative-only headroom computed for reference, capped at 60 per MODULE_RULES even if it were scored).
- Downside resilience /100 *(or "Not assessable")*: **90** — survives every EBITDA haircut through −60% with no liquidity gap at any haircut level and only an indicative (unconfirmed) covenant breach at the most severe haircut.
- Data quality /100: **62** — a recent, reconciled balance sheet, an instrument-level debt note, and a cash flow statement are all present and consistent, but no committed-facility figure, no covenant package, no credit rating, and no confirmed size for the one offshore (UK) preferred instrument leave real gaps in an otherwise strong dataset.
- Overall usefulness /100: **72** (hard cap — "no covenant disclosure" caps Overall usefulness at max 75; set at 72 to also reflect the missing rating and revolver detail).
- Biggest solvency risk (one line): not leverage or liquidity but **disclosure** — no committed/undrawn bank facility, no formal covenant package, and no credit rating exist anywhere in the pool, so the fortress-like numbers cannot be independently corroborated against a real lender's own terms.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** 55.0% of gross debt (CNY 23,452.2mn) is concentrated in a single within-12-month bucket, and CapIQ's own proprietary Credit Health Panel ranks Haier bottom quartile (4th of 4) for "Overall," "Solvency," and "Liquidity" among 11 China appliance peers as of 2026-08-12 — a relative-scoring signal that conflicts with this module's own net-cash finding and cannot be reconciled from this pool. [`02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md` §4]
- **Strongest bull point:** Haier is net cash on both the strict and broad bases at every period from FY2021 through the latest quarter (Mar-2026), and the stress test shows committed liquidity is never exhausted by an EBITDA decline alone — even a complete 100% EBITDA wipeout for a full year still leaves a CNY 10.7bn liquidity surplus. [`06_downside-stress-test.md` §3]
- **Single killer risk specific to solvency:** the total absence of a disclosed committed/undrawn credit facility, covenant package, or credit rating means the "fortress" read cannot be cross-checked against any lender's own contractual assessment — if a real (undisclosed) bank covenant is tighter than the labelled 3.5x assumption used here, or if the CNY 15,717.3mn of undated long-term bank facilities were not actually rolling/evergreen but callable on short notice, the true wall and headroom could be materially worse than this report shows.
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** the narrowing net-cash cushion trend (net debt strict moved from CNY (24,316.9)mn in FY2022 to CNY (4,952.2)mn in FY2025, driven by a dividend payout ratio rising from ~35% to ~71% plus resumed buybacks) shows leverage is rising off a very light base — not a crisis today, but the direction of travel is toward less cushion, not more, and that trend is not addressed by this stress test's snapshot re-leveraging assumption (net debt held flat within each EBITDA-haircut column). [`01_capital-structure-and-leverage.md` §6]

## 3. Reconciliation

Two items are worth reconciling explicitly, though neither is a genuine specialist-to-specialist disagreement — both are flagged consistently across the module:

1. **CapIQ-internal EBITDA inconsistency.** CapIQ's own precomputed "Capital Structure Summary"/"Ratios" tabs use an EBITDA figure roughly CNY 1,785mn (6.7%) higher than the Income Statement tab's own reported FY2025 EBITDA (CNY 26,543.4mn) for the same period — a discrepancy inside CapIQ's own product, not a filing difference (Haier discloses no EBITDA at all). `01` and `04` both independently flagged this and both chose the more transparent, reproducible Income Statement-tab EBITDA. This synthesis adopts that same figure as canonical, consistent with §5's rule to cite the number that actually appears in the cited source.
2. **CapIQ Credit Health Panel vs. this module's own computed ratios.** CapIQ's proprietary relative-scoring panel (as of 2026-08-12) ranks Haier bottom quartile for Solvency and Liquidity among 11 China appliance peers, while every ratio computed in this module from the company's own filings and CapIQ's own instrument-level tabs shows net cash, 1.61x gross leverage, and 9.91x interest coverage. Per CLAUDE.md §4, the more transparent, reproducible, and source-traceable figures (computed line-by-line from filing/instrument-level data) are preferred over an opaque proprietary quartile score whose formula is not disclosed in this pool. The panel's finding is carried forward as an unresolved flag (Section 7), not adopted as the module's own view, and it should not be read as evidence this synthesis is overstating solvency strength.

No other material disagreements between specialists were found — all seven agents used the same canonical gross-debt (CNY 42,669.5mn), net-debt-strict (CNY (4,952.2)mn), and EBITDA (CNY 26,543.4mn) figures throughout.

## 5. Survival Summary

Haier is levered very lightly, and the direction is one of leverage rising off an extremely light base rather than deteriorating operations: net debt (strict) moved from CNY (24,316.9)mn net cash in FY2022 to CNY (4,952.2)mn net cash in FY2025 — a CNY 19.4bn swing toward less cushion — driven almost entirely by a dividend payout ratio climbing from ~35% to ~71% and resumed share buybacks, not by EBITDA or revenue weakness (both grew over the same window). The near-term maturity wall is large in headline terms (55.0% of gross debt bucketed to a single FY2026 date) but is fully self-funded: cash alone covers it 2.0x over, so it does not depend on refinancing markets, and the only precisely-dated fixed-rate tranche (the 2028 bonds) would reprice roughly 15bps lower, not higher, against current benchmarks. The liquidity runway of ≈27.3 months rests mostly on cash already on the balance sheet rather than on FCF materializing, but its confidence is capped at 60/100 because no committed bank-facility figure exists to add, and the tightest covenant is genuinely "not assessable" — no real covenant package is disclosed anywhere in the pool, so the +54.1% indicative headroom shown is a labelled proxy, not a contractual fact. On the stress test: a normal recession (−30% to −40% EBITDA) is survived comfortably with no liquidity gap and no real covenant breach at any haircut level tested, and even the severe −60% haircut only trips the indicative, unconfirmed leverage-covenant proxy — committed liquidity itself is never exhausted by an EBITDA decline alone, even at a full 100% wipeout. No equity raise, asset sale, or waiver is required in any scenario this module tested.

## 6A. Survival Playbook (non-speculative levers)

- **Refi actions taken:** two Medium-Term Notes totaling CNY 3,500.0mn were issued in 2025 at fixed coupons of 1.66%–1.99% — direct, recent evidence of market access, not a speculative assumption. [`02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md` §4]
- **Asset-sale programs announced:** none disclosed in the data pool.
- **Capex cuts feasible:** total capex (CNY 8,851.6mn, FY2025) includes a disclosed washing-machine capacity-expansion project — some share is growth, not maintenance, capex, which is at least partly deferrable in a downside, though no maintenance-vs-growth split is disclosed to size the deferrable portion precisely. [`03_liquidity-runway.md` §2]
- **Dividend / buyback suspension ability:** dividends are declared annually at the AGM (not a multi-year legal commitment), and the CNY 6,000mn buyback authorization announced 2026-03-26 has no specified 12-month execution pace — both are levers the company could pull if needed, though the trend (payout ratio ~35%→~71%) suggests a strong institutional/shareholder preference against cutting them. [`03_liquidity-runway.md` §2]
- **Covenant-amendment likelihood:** not assessable — no covenant package is disclosed, so there is no prior amendment history to judge.

## 7. Note To The Final Synthesizer

- Leverage is very low and net cash, not net debt, at every period FY2021 through the latest quarter (Mar-2026) — net debt (strict) CNY (4,952.2)mn, net debt (broad) CNY (16,975.1)mn, gross debt/EBITDA 1.61x. The direction is a narrowing net-cash cushion (down from CNY (24,316.9)mn in FY2022), driven by rising shareholder distributions, not operating weakness.
- The maturity wall is large in headline terms (55.0% of gross debt in one within-12-month bucket) but is fully covered by cash alone (2.0x), so refinancing is not exposed — this is a self-funded wall, not a refinancing-dependent one.
- The liquidity runway is ≈27.3 months and depends mostly on cash already on the balance sheet, not on FCF materializing — but its confidence is capped at 60/100 because no committed/undrawn bank-facility figure exists anywhere in the pool.
- The tightest covenant is genuinely "not assessable" — no covenant package, indenture, or credit agreement is disclosed anywhere in the pool for Haier's own debt; the +54.1% headroom shown against a labelled 3.5x max-leverage proxy is indicative only, not a contractual fact.
- The largest off-balance-sheet item is CNY 9,969.0mn of endorsed/discounted bank-acceptance bills not yet due — routine trade finance where credit risk sits mainly with the accepting bank, not with Haier. `05_off-balance-sheet-and-contingencies.md` explicitly did NOT emit an `RF-OBS-001` (contingent-liability spike) tag — both the CAS and IFRS filings make an explicit audited negative representation on material contingent liabilities, and this module concurs there is no compounding accounting-integrity mosaic here.

- Because Haier is net cash on every basis, the strategic-flexibility read applies directly: this net-cash position funds counter-cyclical action (continued dividends, buybacks, supplier support, or opportunistic bolt-on M&A) if China's property cycle or US tariff conditions worsen further, per CLAUDE.md §24 Filter 3 — it should be read as a positive, not as "lazy" capital.

- Biggest missing data point (single highest-value next data request): the company's bank credit-facility agreements, or a disclosure of total committed/undrawn credit-line capacity — this single document would remove both the liquidity-confidence cap and the "no revolver identified" gap on an otherwise strong, low-leverage balance sheet.

## 8. Simple Summary

- Total debt is CNY 42,669.5mn (about CNY 42.7bn) gross, but Haier holds more cash than debt — CNY 4,952.2mn more on the strict basis, CNY 16,975.1mn more on the broader basis — so it is net cash, not net debt, and leverage (gross debt ÷ earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization, or "EBITDA") is a modest 1.61x.
- More than half the debt (55%, CNY 23.5bn) is due within about a year, but cash on hand alone covers that twice over — the company does not need to refinance to pay it.
- The cash-and-liquid-investments cushion would last roughly 27 months against near-term bills and dividends, though this figure carries reduced confidence because no bank credit line total is disclosed anywhere in the data.
- No loan covenant (the lender rule that, if broken, can trigger a default) is disclosed anywhere in the data pool — so the true distance to a covenant breach cannot actually be measured, only estimated against a labelled, assumed rule of thumb.
- The biggest off-balance-sheet item is CNY 9.97bn of bank-guaranteed bills the company has already cashed in early — routine trade finance, not a hidden liability risk, and no contingent-liability red flag was triggered.
- Yes, it survives a 30–60% drop in earnings: cash never runs out at any level tested, even a full year of zero EBITDA; only a hypothetical, unconfirmed covenant rule would be tripped, and only at a 54% earnings decline.
- No credit rating (from Moody's, S&P, or a local agency) exists anywhere in this data pool, and no bank credit-facility total is disclosed — these are the two biggest missing pieces.
- This module is useful for the master synthesizer: the leverage and liquidity picture is clear and well-sourced, but the covenant and rating gaps mean the "fortress" read cannot be independently confirmed against a real lender's terms.
