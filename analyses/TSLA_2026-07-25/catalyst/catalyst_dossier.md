## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

The nearest catalyst with a genuinely proven date is the start of the **China Working Capital Facility's contractual refinancing window (Sep-2026)** — about six weeks out from this report. It matters because $5,888M, 63% of Tesla's total gross debt, is booked long-term on nothing more than management's stated "intent and ability to refinance," with no signed replacement facility disclosed. The bullish path is a clean roll or expansion, consistent with the facility's own three-year track record of successful re-draws ($2,740M to $4,288M to $5,888M since Dec-2024). The bearish path is a failed or materially costlier re-draw if Chinese bank credit tightens — the balance-sheet-survival module's own "single killer risk." Even in the bearish case, parent-level liquidity ($48.2bn) covers the wall roughly 2.4x-6.6x over on different bases, so this is a disclosure-quality and concentration risk, not an immediate solvency threat. (The Q3 earnings date, Oct-21-2026, is close behind but is explicitly a vendor pattern-projection, not yet company-confirmed — the maturity window is the more solidly evidenced near-term date.)

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

The single most important catalyst for this stock is not dated at all: **whether the market continues to price Tesla's unmonetized robotaxi/Optimus/FSD optionality at anything like its historical trading multiple, or re-prices toward what the filed Automotive-and-Energy segment economics actually support.** The valuation module frames this explicitly as "a binary sentiment/narrative question this valuation module cannot resolve, only frame." The reverse-DCF finds the current $319.69 price requires a 68.9% compound annual free-cash-flow growth rate sustained for seven years — a rate that would require Tesla to capture 75-100%+ of the entire global automotive industry by FY2032, a share no automaker has ever held — while the base-case triangulated fair value across peer, sum-of-the-parts, and DCF methods sits near $32/share. Every operational proof point that could resolve this one way or the other (Cybercab ramp pace, Optimus production start, Terafab site, AI5 volume production, robotaxi fleet/market count) is undated, hedged management language, not a scheduled event a buyer can actually time. A buyer cannot time this catalyst — it will resolve through an accumulation of undated operational prints and market sentiment, not a single calendar event, and every quarter without a dated, quantified proof point extends the gap the reverse-DCF identifies between price and disclosed economics.

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **China Working Capital Facility refinancing window (Sep-2026-Mar-2027).** 63% of total gross debt in one unhedged, non-US-market, floating-rate facility with no committed replacement financing on file — the single largest concentration risk on the calendar (`balance-sheet-survival/02` §1b, §4).

Not itself a forward-dated event, but a live, non-reversing drag sitting under every dated catalyst above.
