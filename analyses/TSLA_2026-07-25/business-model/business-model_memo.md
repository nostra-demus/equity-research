## What This Module Found

Tesla designs, builds, and sells electric cars straight to buyers, plus a smaller grid-battery business and an unmonetized bet on self-driving software and humanoid robots [`02_business-identity.md` §3]. Cars are the engine: 86.5% of FY2025 revenue and 77.7% of gross profit [CIQ Financials_Annual export, Segments tab, FY2025].

The single strongest positive is the balance sheet: $27.4 billion of net cash (Jun-2026) — more cash than debt — funding real vertical integration into battery cells and lithium refining [Capital IQ export, Capital Structure Summary tab].

The single most important risk is that the business does not yet create economic value: return on capital (ROIC — the profit earned on each dollar invested) is 2.75%–3.7% (last twelve months), roughly 770–885 basis points (7.7–8.9 percentage points) below an estimated 11.5% cost of capital (what that money costs to raise) [`09_moat.md` §3–5]. That gap has widened every year since FY2022 (18.6% → 8.9% → 6.0% → 2.9% → 2.75%), and operating margin has fallen three years straight (16.8% FY2022 → 4.1% LTM) [`07_business-quality.md` §1–2].

Layered on top: a $132.3 billion maximum-value, 423.7M-share CEO pay award, a 56% cumulative rise in share count since 2017 with zero buybacks, and an unresolved securities-fraud class action tied to the FSD/Robotaxi story the market's valuation appears to lean on [`11_capital-allocation-governance.md` §3; `12_red-flags-sweep.md` §2–3].

## What Would Change This Read

Toward higher quality:
- Automotive gross margin stabilizing (not just a one- or two-quarter rebound) as the regulatory-credit runoff completes.
- A disclosed, monetized revenue line for FSD/Robotaxi/Optimus that lifts consolidated return on capital sustainably above the ~11.5% cost-of-capital estimate.

Toward lower quality:
- An adverse ruling that lets the securities-fraud class action proceed to discovery.
- Further certified CEO award tranches without matching per-share value creation.
- A completed SpaceX-related merger that formalizes the related-party concentration already flagged.

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** Cyclical, technology-cycle bet — worth deeper work only with a strong timing edge, not a standing compounder thesis.
- **Could be better than it looks:** automotive revenue re-accelerated 23–27% year-on-year in Q1–Q2 FY2026 after an 11% Q4 FY2025 drop, Energy's gross-profit share nearly tripled to 22.2%, and $27.4bn net cash funds real vertical integration.
- **Could be worse than it looks:** return on capital sits ~770–885 basis points below cost of capital and the gap has widened four years running, while capex runs 1.4x–2.2x depreciation into an unmonetized self-driving bet.
- **Missing evidence:** the original standalone FY2025 10-K (audited financials, full auditor opinion, risk-factor and competition narrative) is not in the pool — a real document gap, not a language issue.
- **Watch next:** whether automotive gross margin stabilizes as regulatory credits run off, and any court ruling on the securities-fraud class action tied to the FSD/Robotaxi narrative.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Gross profit / gross margin** — what's left from sales after the direct cost of building the product, before other costs.
- **Return on capital (ROIC)** — the profit earned on each dollar invested in the business.
- **Cost of capital (WACC)** — what the money used to run the business costs to raise; if ROIC is below it, the business destroys value.
- **Basis points** — hundredths of a percentage point; 100 basis points = 1 percentage point.
- **Net cash** — cash on hand minus total debt; positive means more cash than debt.
- **Operating margin** — operating profit as a share of revenue, after all operating costs.
- **Moat** — a durable structural advantage that lets a company keep earning above its cost of capital.
- **Buyback** — a company buying back its own shares, which raises each remaining share's slice of the company.
