**Utilization is low despite the large notional.** As of December 31, 2025, based on directors' and officers' own written representations, the aggregate loan or investment amount actually collateralized by all pledged shares across the whole board and management team was **less than 1% of the total value of the pledged shares** [FY2025 10-K/A, Item 12, "Ownership of Securities" narrative]. A board policy caps CEO pledging-collateralized borrowing at the lesser of $3.5 billion or 25% of the pledged stock's value, and caps other directors/officers at 15% [FY2025 10-K/A, Item 11, "Pledging of Shares"].

**Residual denominator ambiguity (conservative default, CLAUDE.md §4).** The disqualifier-scan computed the "eligible to be pledged" ratio two ways: against Musk's full disclosed beneficial stake (717.1M shares) it is 28.9% — well below the 50% hard-disqualifier threshold; against only his directly-held trust shares (413,152,109, excluding option-derived shares the policy bars from pledging) it reads 50.2% — just over that threshold. The correct denominator under the standard formula is the person's total disclosed beneficial ownership, which reads 28.9%, so this does **not** meet the bar to trigger RF-OWN-001. The narrower reading is recorded here rather than silently discarded, and utilization (<1% of pledged value actually borrowed against) further limits the practical risk either way. This finding traces to the 10-K/A (a pool filing), so it is eligible to carry a red-flag designation, but it does not clear the materiality bar for one.

## 5. Ownership / Insider Read

Elon Musk owns a genuinely large stake — 20.3% as of the 10-K/A's Dec-2025 table, rising to 29.9% after his June-2026 option exercise — but that stake is overwhelmingly the product of three successive CEO performance-award grants (2012, 2018, 2025) rather than open-market buying; the one clear cash conviction signal in the last 12 months is a real ~$1.0 billion open-market purchase in September 2025, set against much smaller open-market sales by Kimbal Musk ($25.6M) and SVP Tom Zhu ($7.3M). Pledging is disclosed and bounded — 28.9% of Musk's stake is "eligible to be pledged" but under 1% of pledged value was actually borrowed against as of Dec-31-2025 — so it is a monitoring item, not a red flag. The biggest control risk to minority holders is not pledging or a dual-class structure (Tesla has neither); it is the compounding effect of the 2025 CEO Performance Award's Voting Agreement, which hands Musk full voting control over an additional 423.7 million shares as performance tranches are certified "Earned," on top of an already-concentrated economic and voting position and a network of transactions with the CEO's other controlled ventures.
