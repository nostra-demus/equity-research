## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Emaar's plan is to keep converting cheap legacy Dubai land into branded master-planned communities sold off-plan, funded by buyer instalments, wrapped around a recurring mall-and-hospitality annuity. **It issues no formal numeric (revenue/EBITDA/EPS) guidance** [earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md] and steers instead with operational signposts and policy statements. The explicit, checkable targets management has set: **~AED 65bn of capex over five years**; **gross margin "low 50s sustainably"** over the medium term; **dividend policy "unchanged … current payout in the near term"** (100% of share capital, AED 1.00/share); **50,800+ units under construction for delivery over 5–6 years** with deliveries "ramping materially 2026–28"; and revenue backlog expected to "continue to grow or remain stable unless a sustained slowdown in sales" [FAB Securities "Earnings Call Insight", 4Q25 (paraphrasing the call; unverified); Q4/FY2025 press release, 12 Feb 2026]. The headline strategic signal is scale-at-the-top-of-cycle: an **AED 200bn single-district masterplan** (4.5m sqm GFA, ~150,000 residents) unveiled June 2026, plus Dubai Square Mall and a Dubai Mall expansion by 2H28 [Key Developments, 2026-06-11].

## 5. Management Read

The operating team is **competent and has delivered what it said**: under a stable, 9-year CEO (Amit Jain) and a 29-year founder-MD, Emaar roughly doubled revenue, tripled EBITDA, lifted ROIC from ~5% to ~14%, grew property sales to a record AED 80bn and backlog to AED 163bn, and held its AED 1.00/share dividend across FY2023–25 — a genuine kept-promise record, though one clearly **aided by a Dubai property upcycle and a FY2025 one-off tax relief**, so credit it as cycle-strong execution rather than proof of through-cycle skill. The single biggest management signal is a **governance-alignment gap sitting on top of a good operating record**: control just passed between Dubai-government vehicles (ICD → Dubai Holding, 29.73%), the founder-MD holds only ~0.03% and chairs a **competing** developer, and the **Group CFO left abruptly and unexplained in May 2026** (Amber, RF-MGT-001 watch) days after that control change — competence is proven, but minority-aligned stewardship and finance-leadership stability are the things to watch.
