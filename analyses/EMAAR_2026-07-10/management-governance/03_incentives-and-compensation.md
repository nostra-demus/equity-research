*Compensation source (UAE proxy-equivalent, CLAUDE.md §27): the FY2025 Integrated Annual Report — Corporate Governance Report §3(c) (board remuneration proposal, approved at the AGM held 25 March 2026) and **Annex D — Senior Executive Employees** (actual salaries, bonuses and phantom-share entitlements paid for/during 2025). ## 4. Incentive Alignment Read

The pay has a **genuinely per-share spine for the professional managers**: a phantom-share LTIP whose payout is explicitly linked to Emaar's share price and vests over three years covers the CEO, CFO and senior team, and it sits alongside a dividend held at AED 1.00/share and a flat share count (no dilution, no buyback games — agent 11) — so the salaried CEO (~75% at-risk, ~32% share-price-linked) and CFO have a real share-price stake despite owning almost none of the company. **But the single biggest pay packet is the least aligned money in the building**: the founder-MD takes AED 61m (≈ USD 16.7m, ~3x the CEO), owns 0.03%, is **excluded from the share-price-linked LTIP**, draws a AED 36m bonus with **no disclosed metric**, plus a AED 13.3m travel/logistics line — and that package is waved through by a government-controlled register, not an independent vote. Because neither the annual bonus nor the LTIP discloses its **metric weights**, I cannot confirm the cash bonus rewards per-share returns rather than sheer scale. **Conclusion: mixed** — the mechanism points the CEO/CFO at per-share value, but the largest, most discretionary, least-transparent packet does not.

**Single biggest incentive signal:** the founder-MD is paid ~AED 61m/yr (3x the CEO) with **zero share-price-linked pay** and an **undisclosed bonus basis** while owning 0.03% — the biggest pay is the least aligned.
