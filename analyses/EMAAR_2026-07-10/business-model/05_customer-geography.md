# Customer And Geography Map — EMAR

**Company:** Emaar Properties PJSC (DFM: EMAAR) — Dubai (UAE) real-estate developer.
**Reporting standard:** IFRS. **Currency:** AED (UAE dirham, pegged to the US dollar at ~3.6725). US$ figures below are the company's own stated equivalents. Figures are FY2025 (year ended 31 Dec 2025) unless noted.

**Note on the CIQ "Customers" workbook:** the pool file `...EMAAR Customers.xls` is a Capital IQ *relationships* list — Emaar's own subsidiaries, intra-group borrowers, and two mall tenants (e.g. Jamjoom Fashion). It is **not** a customer-concentration disclosure, and I do not treat it as one. The real customer read comes from the audited credit-risk note and the segment note.

## 1. Customer Map

Emaar discloses **no individual customer and no top-customer concentration**. Its audited credit-risk note states the Group "earns its revenues from a **large number of customers spread across different geographies**" [FY2025 Annual Report (IFRS), Note 34(a) — Credit risk, p.213]. The table below is therefore a customer-**type** map, built from the three disclosed operating segments — each segment is effectively one customer type.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Off-plan property buyers** (individuals & investors) — *Real Estate* segment | **~80%** (AED 39,550m of AED 49,557m) | **Y** — per-buyer Sale & Purchase Agreements on an instalment basis; 89% of group revenue is recognised "over a period of time" (booked as construction progresses, IFRS 15); AED 155bn contracted revenue backlog. Legal title passes only after all instalments are paid. | [FY2025 AR, Segment note, p.187–188; Note 34(a), p.213; Q4 FY2025 Earnings Press Release, 12-Feb-2026] | Granular base — customer default rate 0.5% of sales value [FY2025 AR, UAE Development, p.23]. Identified concentrations of credit risks are controlled and managed accordingly" [FY2025 AR, Note 34(a), p.214].

## 2. Geography Map

The audited segment note splits geography only two ways — **Domestic (UAE)** vs **International** (defined as operations outside the UAE, including export sales) [FY2025 AR, Geographic segments, p.188]. Within the UAE, revenue is almost entirely **Dubai**: every named flagship community — Downtown Dubai, Dubai Creek Harbour, Dubai Hills, Emaar Beachfront, The Oasis — is in Dubai, and the results are datelined and framed around "Dubai's residential market" [Q4 FY2025 Press Release, 12-Feb-2026]. The filing does not give an emirate-level percentage, so the "Dubai" read is inference from the named assets, not a disclosed split.

| Geography | % of Revenue | Trend (Growing / Stable / Declining / Unknown) | Evidence | Risk |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **UAE** (Domestic — overwhelmingly Dubai) | **93%** (AED 46,153m) | **Growing** — 86% FY23 → 91% FY24 → 93% FY25 | [FY2025 AR, Geographic segments, p.188; FY23–24 from CIQ Financials→Segments (Geographic), reconciles to filing] | The entire business rides one city's property cycle. 97% of trade & unbilled receivables are from Middle East & North Africa operations [Note 34(a), p.213]. |
| **International** (Egypt — Emaar Misr; India — Emaar India; Turkey — Emaar Turkey; others) | **7%** (AED 3,404m) | **Declining share** — 14% FY23 → 9% FY24 → 7% FY25; roughly flat-to-down in absolute AED (3,725m → 3,311m → 3,404m) | [FY2025 AR, Geographic segments, p.188; CIQ Public Company Profile — subsidiaries] | Small and shrinking as UAE outgrows it; spread across several emerging markets. |

Shares sum to ~100% (UAE 93% + International 7%; there is no separate "Other"). The concentration is intensifying, not easing — UAE has risen ~7 points of revenue share in two years while International revenue is flat in absolute terms. The forward pipeline is similarly weighted: of a ~618m sq ft land bank, ~344m sq ft is in the UAE [Q4 FY2025 Press Release, 12-Feb-2026].

## 4. Read

This is a **geography-concentrated** business, not a customer-concentrated one. On the customer side there is no concentration to speak of — the audited credit-risk note describes a large, granular buyer base (default rate just 0.5% of sales value), no customer clears the 20% or top-3 40% tests, and the CIQ "Customers" file is a related-party list, not a concentration. On the geography side the concentration is extreme and rising: the UAE — in practice Dubai — is 93% of revenue (up from 86% two years ago) and ~344m of ~618m sq ft of land bank. That concentration is **contractually secured in the near term, not naked**: 95% of UAE revenue is recognised over time under instalment sale contracts, backed by an AED 134.3bn UAE backlog (~AED 155bn group-wide, US$42.1bn) that converts to revenue over about four years. The single biggest dependency the synthesizer must carry forward is that **Emaar is a leveraged bet on the Dubai property cycle** — 93% of revenue and the majority of the land bank sit in one city, so a Dubai real-estate or regional-macro downturn hits almost the entire top line at once, with only the multi-year backlog as a buffer.
