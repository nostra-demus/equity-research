## What This Module Found

Emaar has a real, evidenced catalyst calendar — this is not a story-only stock — but the calendar is front-loaded and then thin. Only two events carry a genuine date in the next few months: Q2 2026 results (estimated 10 Aug 2026) and the Sukuk 3 bond maturity (15 Sep 2026, a hard contractual date). Everything else with real impact is windowed into early 2027 or has no date at all.

The single most important catalyst is the quarter-by-quarter Dubai home-demand and backlog trajectory — new pre-sales (homes sold before they are built) and backlog growth (homes already sold but not yet booked as revenue). That is the whole ~AED 11/share swing between the bull and bear case, and the Q2 print on ~10 Aug 2026 is the first dated read on whether demand is holding or rolling over [earnings/04; 01 §3].

The biggest bearish risk is the ~167,000-unit 2026-27 Dubai supply wave stopping backlog from being replenished, first read at the Q2 print as a "good print, bad signal" risk, paired with the risk of a dividend cut at the ~Feb 2027 declaration if cash is steered to masterplan spending [earnings/05 §10; management-governance/02 §4].

The killer risk specific to the calendar: treating an undated, owner-gated item as if it were a timed bullish catalyst. Both the AED 200bn masterplan (announced 11 Jun 2026, no launch date) and the demand re-rate have no date and both serve or are gated by the government owner — neither can be timed, and neither may lift conviction on its own [Key Developments 2026-06-11; 01 §3].

## What Would Change This Read

- **Q2 2026 results (est. 10 Aug 2026):** a profit-line beat — EPS above ~AED 0.52 (>+10%) on mid-50s development margin plus ~AED 2bn/yr net finance income — **alongside** property sales re-accelerating above +16% YoY and backlog holding at ≥+29% YoY would break the "collapse" story (bull trigger). A revenue miss below ~AED 11,400m (−5%) paired with cooling pre-sales is the "good print, bad signal" sell-off (bear trigger).
- **Sukuk 3 maturity (15 Sep 2026):** repaid from cash (covered 4.4x by AED 12,179.5m unrestricted cash) or refinanced cheaply reaffirms the net-cash story; a broader GCC credit-spread widening or rating-outlook change is the mild downside. *A sukuk is an Islamic bond that pays a profit rate rather than interest.*
- **FY2026 results + dividend (~Feb 2027):** earnings holding near FY2025 and the ~AED 1.00/share dividend (~8% yield) maintained is the bull case; earnings decelerating or the dividend cut to fund masterplan spending is the bear case.
- **FY2026 AGM (~late-Mar 2027):** any move to quantify government-related-party flows, add a truly independent director, or disclose an MD/Chairman succession plan would be a positive surprise; the base case is an owner-controlled rubber-stamp (Filter 6).
- **AED 200bn masterplan (undated):** a dated sales launch with strong pre-sales would seed years of new backlog; launching into the supply wave, a heavy cash call, or unverifiable related-party land pricing is the downside. No date disclosed — must not lift conviction on its own.

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** dated, evidenced near-term catalysts exist, but the timing that matters most is vague and owner-gated. Catalyst strength 47/100 (capped at 55).
- **Could be better than it looks:** the Q2 print (~10 Aug 2026) is a dated, evidenced event that could break the collapse narrative — EPS beats landed in 3 of the last 4 quarters, and Q1-26 pre-sales were AED 22.4bn, +16% YoY, with backlog near +29% YoY.
- **Could be worse than it looks:** pre-sales roll over into the ~167,000-unit 2026-27 Dubai supply wave, giving a "good print, bad signal" Q2 and a possible dividend cut at the ~Feb 2027 declaration.
- **What is missing:** no company-published exact forward dates. The Q2 date is estimated, the FY2026 results/AGM/dividend dates are cadence-windowed, there is no numeric EPS/revenue guidance, and the masterplan has no launch date. Highest-value single item: a published DFM board-meeting notice fixing the exact Q2 2026 results date.
- **One thing to watch next:** the Q2 2026 results, est. 10 Aug 2026 — the first dated read on whether Dubai home demand is holding or rolling over.
- **Do not:** model a timed re-rate. The demand re-rate and the masterplan have no date and are gated by the government owner (a value trap — a stock that stays cheap because the controller has no interest in closing the gap), so timing cannot carry this thesis.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** a specific, datable event that could move the share price.
- **Pre-sales / property sales:** new home sales signed in the period — the leading indicator that feeds future revenue.
- **Backlog:** homes already sold but not yet booked as revenue.
- **Off-plan:** a home sold before it is built.
- **Sukuk:** an Islamic bond that pays a profit rate, not interest; a "bullet" maturity means the whole principal comes due on one date.
- **Net cash:** cash on hand exceeds total debt.
- **EV/EBITDA (~4.0x, at the 0th percentile of its own 16-quarter range):** enterprise value divided by earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation — a cheapness gauge; the lowest the stock has been on this measure in four years.
- **Net finance income (~AED 2bn/yr):** the interest and returns the company earns on its cash, net of what it pays on debt.
- **Margin of safety (~19%):** the gap between the estimated fair value and the current price — the cushion if the analysis is wrong.
- **Re-rate:** the market decides to pay more for the same earnings, lifting the price.
- **Value trap:** a stock that looks cheap but stays cheap because the controlling owner has no interest in closing the gap.
- **DMTT:** the new UAE 15% minimum corporate tax.
- **MTO (mandatory tender offer):** an offer a buyer must make to all shareholders once it crosses a control threshold (~30% under UAE norms).
