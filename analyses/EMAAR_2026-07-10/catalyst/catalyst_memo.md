## What This Module Found

Emaar has a real, evidenced catalyst calendar — this is not a story-only stock — but the calendar is front-loaded then thin. Only two events carry a genuine date inside the next few months, and the one that matters most has no date at all. [§2]

The **nearest dated event** is Q2 2026 results, estimated 10 Aug 2026 (about one month out). It is the first dated read since the Q1 print on whether Dubai home demand is holding or rolling over. About 80% of revenue is homes sold before they are built (off-plan), so the stock trades on new pre-sales (sales signed this period) and backlog growth (homes already sold but not yet booked as revenue) — the leading indicator (a figure that moves before reported profit, so investors watch it first) — not on the backlog-locked profit already in the books. [§2, §4]

The **single most important catalyst** is the quarter-by-quarter Dubai demand and backlog trajectory — the synthesis puts the whole bull-versus-bear swing at about AED 11 per share. But this is only partly timeable: the prints are dated, the cycle turn is not, and the re-rate it could drive has no date and sits behind a government owner. The synthesis says do not model a timed re-rate. [§1, §4]

The **single most important risk** is the roughly 167,000-unit 2026-27 Dubai supply wave stopping backlog growth. It is first read at the Q2 print as a "good print, bad signal" risk — profit could beat while new pre-sales quietly cool. It is paired with dividend-cut risk at the FY2026 declaration (about Feb 2027; dividend roughly AED 8.84bn, AED 1.00/share, about an 8% yield — the yearly dividend as a percentage of the share price) if cash is steered to masterplan spending. [§1, §4]

The market is already pricing structural decline: the mean analyst target fell from AED 19.15 to 17.07 (cut seven times in three months), long-term growth consensus is −14.8%, and the shares trade at about 4.0x EV/EBITDA (enterprise value — the whole business, debt included — versus yearly cash operating profit) — the lowest reading in their own 16-quarter range (0th percentile). The roughly 19% margin of safety (the gap between the price paid and what the business is estimated to be worth) only closes on a re-rate that has no date, while the owner can hold the price below value indefinitely. [§1A]

## What Would Change This Read

The synthesis frames the moves through the calendar's bullish and bearish triggers [§2, §4].

**Would improve the read (bull):**
- Q2 print (est. 10 Aug 2026): EPS (earnings per share — profit divided by the share count) clears about AED 0.52 (a beat above +10%) **and** property sales re-accelerate above +16% year-on-year with backlog growth holding at or above +29% — a dated, evidenced event that could break the "collapse" narrative (Q1-26 sales were AED 22.4bn, +16% YoY).
- A published DFM (Dubai exchange) board-meeting notice fixing the exact Q2 results date — the single highest-value missing item; it would lift timing visibility.
- Sukuk 3 (AED 2,752.6m, due 15 Sep 2026) — the only hard bond repayment in the next 12 months, falling due in one lump (a bullet maturity) — repaid from cash (covered 4.4x by AED 12,179.5m of unrestricted cash) or refinanced cheaply; or a rating upgrade toward A-.

**Would worsen the read (bear):**
- Q2 revenue miss (below about AED 11,400m, −5%) on percentage-of-completion timing (revenue booked in step with how much of each project is built, so it can swing between quarters), especially paired with cooling pre-sales, in the seasonally soft quarter that missed both lines a year ago (Q2-25 revenue −4.6% / EPS −5.0%).
- Pre-sales decelerating into the roughly 167,000-unit 2026-27 supply wave.
- FY2026 dividend cut or capped at the ~Feb 2027 declaration as cash goes to masterplan spending.
- A rating-outlook cut around the Sukuk issue.

**Note the trap:** the undated AED 200bn masterplan (announced 11 Jun 2026, no launch or sales date) and the demand re-rate both serve or are gated by the government owner. The synthesis says neither may lift conviction on its own. [§4]

## Plain-English Glossary

Terms in order of first appearance; only terms this memo uses. No new numbers or facts.

- **Re-rate:** the market decides to pay more (or less) for the same earnings.
- **Sukuk:** an Islamic bond — it pays a profit rate, not interest.
- **Off-plan:** a home sold before it is built.
- **Pre-sales / property sales:** new sales signed in the period — the leading indicator that feeds future revenue.
- **Backlog:** homes already sold but not yet booked as revenue.
- **Leading indicator:** a figure that moves before the reported profit does, so investors watch it first.
- **Yield (dividend yield):** the yearly dividend as a percentage of the share price.
- **EV/EBITDA:** enterprise value (the whole business, debt included) divided by yearly cash operating profit — a cheapness / richness gauge.
- **Margin of safety:** the gap between the price you pay and what the business is estimated to be worth.
- **Earnings per share (EPS):** profit divided by the number of shares.
- **Bullet maturity:** the whole bond principal falls due on one single date.
- **Percentage-of-completion:** revenue booked in step with how much of a project is built, so its timing can swing between quarters.
- **Mandatory tender offer:** an offer a buyer must make to all shareholders once its stake crosses a set control threshold.
