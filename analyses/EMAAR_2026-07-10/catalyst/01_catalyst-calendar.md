# Catalyst Calendar — EMAR

*Emaar Properties PJSC (DFM: EMAAR) — Dubai/UAE real-estate developer. IFRS; reporting currency **AED** (dirham pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725, so AED↔USD carries negligible currency risk); fiscal year ends 31 December. Today's date: **2026-07-10**, so the forward window runs to ~July 2027. All pool documents are in English — no translation gap. This module times the events; it does not forecast the print, value the company, assign probabilities, or rate the stock.*

*Plain-English note (first use): **sukuk** = an Islamic bond (pays a profit rate, not interest); **bullet maturity** = the whole principal comes due on one date; **backlog** = homes already sold but not yet booked as revenue; **off-plan** = a home sold before it is built; **pre-sales / property sales** = new sales signed in the period (the leading indicator that feeds future backlog); **ex-div date** = buy before this day to receive the dividend; **re-rate** = the market pays a higher multiple for the same earnings; **margin of safety** = how far the price sits below estimated worth; **EV/EBITDA** = enterprise value ÷ operating cash profit; **DMTT** = the new UAE 15% minimum tax; **MTO** = a mandatory tender offer a buyer must make to all holders once it crosses a control threshold.*

## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

The soonest event is the **Q2 2026 results, estimated 10 August 2026** (~1 month out). The date is CIQ-derived, not yet a company-published DFM board intimation, so treat the *event* as certain (quarterly cadence + statutory reporting) and the exact *day* as an estimate that can shift a few days. It matters because Emaar's stock moves on the leading indicator — new property sales and backlog growth — not the backlog-locked P&L, and this is the first read since the 11 May Q1 print on whether Dubai demand is holding or rolling over. Two-sided outcome: the profit line is set up to beat again (a cut, now-stable bar; EPS beats have landed in 3 of the last 4 quarters on margin + ~AED 2bn/yr net finance income), **but** Q2 is the exact seasonally-soft quarter that missed both revenue and EPS a year ago, so a percentage-of-completion revenue miss — especially alongside cooling pre-sales — could produce a "good print, bad signal" sell-off. The nearest **hard-dated** (contractual, not estimated) catalyst is the **Sukuk 3 AED 2,752.6m maturity on 15 September 2026**, which is well-covered and closer to a non-event than a swing factor.

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

The largest-impact catalyst is not one date but the **Dubai residential-demand trajectory revealed quarter-by-quarter in the property-sales and backlog figures**, with the first dated read on ~10 August 2026. Valuation puts the entire ~AED 11/share bull-to-bear swing on Dubai residential demand / the developer margin through the cycle, and the price (AED 12.20, as-of 2026-06-28) already bakes in **structural impairment** — the reverse-DCF shows the market pricing free cash flow to shrink ~13%/yr for a decade and a through-cycle margin below Emaar's own FY2021 trough, i.e. only 64% of intrinsic value. **Bullish path:** pre-sales stay resilient (Q1-26 was AED 22.4bn, +16%) and backlog holds near +29% YoY, breaking the "collapse" narrative and supporting a re-rate off a 0th-percentile 4.0x EV/EBITDA toward the ~6.5x own-history median. **Bearish path:** pre-sales roll over into the ~167,000-unit 2026–27 supply wave, backlog replenishment stops, and the out-year estimate slide (LT-growth consensus −14.8%, targets cut 7× in three months) accelerates. Can a buyer time it? **Only partly.** The quarterly prints are dated, so the demand signal is readable on a schedule — but the cycle turn itself is not precisely timeable, and, critically, whether any re-rate converts into realized return is **gated by §24 Filter 6**: a government controller (Dubai Holding 29.73%, RF-OWN-004) with a board of its own officials can keep a below-intrinsic price below intrinsic indefinitely. The most important catalyst is real in magnitude but has no fixable date and sits behind a value trap.

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **Q2 2026 print (~10 Aug 2026) — "good print, bad signal" risk.** The stock trades on the leading indicator; a clean profit beat can still sell off if pre-sales / backlog growth step down again in the seasonally soft quarter that missed both lines a year ago [earnings/05 §10; earnings/04 §7].
- **Every quarterly print is an occasion for further out-year cuts.** LT-growth consensus has flipped to −14.8%, FY2027+ EPS keeps sliding, the mean target fell from AED 19.15 to 17.07, and targets were cut 7× in three months; out-year estimates (thin, 1 analyst) imply an earnings peak ~2029 then decline [earnings/04 §4–§5].
- **Dubai supply wave — ~167,000 units completing 2026–27** (windowed). The demand/price risk that would stop backlog replenishment and pressure absorption; the AED 200bn masterplan would launch into it [business-model/10 §5; earnings/07].
- **FY2026 dividend (declared ~Feb 2027, paid ~Apr 2027) — cut risk.** A ~8% yield is the main return the owner extracts; a reduction — or cash diverted to the masterplan capex — would remove the yield floor [management-governance/02 §4].
- **15% DMTT tax — a permanent earnings ratchet, fully visible from FY2026.** It added AED 2,114m of tax in 2025 (quarterly charge AED 70m in 4Q24 → AED 1,234m in 4Q25); Q3-26 laps the one-off MD120 reversal, so the profit comp is harder [business-model/10 §1; earnings/03].
- **Permanent CFO still unresolved** (interim since 20 May 2026) — an undated governance overhang [Key Developments 2026-05-22].
- **Ownership creep at the control threshold (undated, inference).** Dubai Holding sits at **29.73%**, just below the ~30% level that triggers a UAE **mandatory tender offer** — a misaligned controller could inch toward full control without paying minorities a premium, or a crossing of 30% could force a mandatory offer. *Inference from the UAE takeover-threshold norm, not a company disclosure* [Key Developments 2026-05-11; §24 Filter 6].
- **Sukuk 3 refinancing step-up (15 Sep 2026)** — a real but immaterial ~+165 bps / ~AED 45m/yr if refinanced rather than repaid [balance-sheet-survival/02 §5].

## 5. Calendar Read

Only two catalysts carry a genuine near-term date — the Q2 2026 print (~10 Aug 2026, CIQ-estimated) and the hard Sukuk 3 maturity (15 Sep 2026); everything with real impact (the FY2026 results-plus-dividend, the AGM, the AED 200bn masterplan) is either cadence-windowed into early 2027 or undated, so this is a real calendar but a **front-loaded-then-thin** one. The near-term dated events skew **mildly bearish**: the Sukuk maturity is a non-event, and the Q2 print's live risk is a "good print, bad signal" reaction if pre-sales keep cooling into the 167k-unit supply wave, since the stock trades on the leading indicator, not the backlog-locked P&L. The single most important timing caveat: the one catalyst that could close the ~19% margin of safety — a re-rate off a 0th-percentile 4.0x EV/EBITDA — has **no date and is gated by a government controller (Dubai Holding 29.73%, RF-OWN-004, §24 Filter 6)** that can keep the price below intrinsic value indefinitely; that is a value trap, not a timed catalyst.
