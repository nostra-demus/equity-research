# Historical Financials — EMAR (Emaar Properties PJSC, DFM: EMAAR)

**Reporting basis:** IFRS. **Currency:** AED millions unless stated (AED is pegged to the US dollar at ~3.6725 AED/USD; the US$ figures in company press releases use that peg). **Fiscal year:** ends 31 December. **Shares (diluted):** ~8,838.8m [8].

**Two EBITDA definitions appear in this file and are kept apart (§15):** (a) **company-defined EBITDA** (non-IFRS, from press releases) — which *includes* net finance income and Emaar's share of joint-venture/associate results; and (b) **CIQ standardized EBITDA** = operating income + depreciation/amortisation only. Company EBITDA runs ~AED 1.0–1.5bn above CIQ standardized EBITDA. The multi-year and quarterly tables below use **CIQ standardized EBITDA** as the spine because it foots exactly across all 17 quarters and 5 years; the company-reported figure is reconciled in Section 4.

Plain-English glossary (first use): **EBITDA** = rough proxy for operating cash profit before interest, tax and depreciation; **EBIT** = operating profit; **EPS** = profit per share; **CFO** = cash generated from operations; **FCF** = free cash flow = CFO − capital spending (capex); **net debt** = total borrowings − cash; **bps** = basis points (100 bps = 1.0%); **TTM/LTM** = trailing/last twelve months.

(iv) **Capex "Stable":** PP&E capex is small and range-bound (AED 0.5–1.3bn); Emaar's real development outlay flows through operating cash (as development-property inventory) and a separate "purchase of real-estate properties" investing line (~AED 0.9–1.0bn/yr), not through PP&E capex [6].

## 6. Key Trend Summary

1. **Revenue growth is Accelerating.** After a −10.6% dip in FY2022, revenue grew +7.3% (FY23), +32.7% (FY24) and +39.6% (FY25), reaching AED 49,557m; on a clean trailing-twelve-month basis it is **+33.4%** (Section 2). Revenue and EBITDA have both roughly doubled since FY2022 (revenue 24.9bn→51.9bn LTM; EBITDA 9.3bn→25.2bn LTM), with a record AED 163.4bn presale backlog at Mar-2026 (+29% YoY) providing visibility [3].
2. **Margins Stable after an earlier step-up.** EBITDA margin jumped from 28% (FY21) to ~54% (FY23) as mix shifted to higher-margin Dubai build-to-sell, then settled at ~49% for three years (FY23–LTM); EBIT margin ~45% and gross margin ~55% are likewise steady after giving back ground from the FY23 peak. Direction: **Stable/Expanding, not compressing** — absolute EBITDA keeps rising even as the percentage plateaus.
3. **Material seasonality: Q4 is the quarter that matters** — ~32–33% of annual revenue every year, with Q1/Q2 the soft quarters (~21%). QoQ moves are seasonal; the YoY column is the clean read (every quarter +20% to +54%).
4. **Inflection points (last 5 years):** (i) FY2022 revenue trough/margin step-up as the group consolidated Dubai build-to-sell and the mix richened; (ii) balance sheet inflected from net debt (FY21 broad +16.3bn) to a large, growing **net-cash** position (broad +25.0bn at Mar-26; strict roughly net-flat given a cash/term-deposit reclassification), alongside S&P BBB+ / Moody's Baa1 upgrades noted for 2025 [4]; (iii) a **cash-vs-earnings divergence** in the latest TTM (earnings +31–33%, CFO +3.8%) driven by the swing in customer presale advances — a level worth watching, routed to earnings-quality (06).
