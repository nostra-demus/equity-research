## 7. Bar Assessment

**Bar is low.** (Not provisional — consensus is current as of ~28 Jun 2026, post the 11 May Q1 print.)

Rationale: For the earnings-module horizon (next 1–2 quarters and FY2026), the bar has been **cut ~9–16% over the last 3 months and has stopped falling** — FY2026 EPS-normalized was reset from 2.32 to 1.95 (roughly **flat vs FY2025's 1.99**, i.e. consensus already bakes in a sharp deceleration from FY2025's +40% revenue / +33% EBITDA year and the new UAE 9% corporate tax), yet near-term revisions turned **up** in the last 30 days (FY2026 revenue +2.3%, EPS-norm +2.6%). Against that reduced bar, Emaar **beat Q1 2026 EBITDA (+16%) and EPS (+9.6%)**, carries a **record AED 163bn backlog (+29% YoY, UAE dev AED 143bn convertible over ~4 years)**, and has beaten in **4 of 5 years / 3 of 4 quarters** — so the near-term profit bar looks **beatable**.

Two honest qualifiers keep this from being a multi-year all-clear: (1) **revenue can still miss on timing** — it did in Q1 2026 (−2.4%) and Q2 2025 (−4.6%) — so a top-line miss in any single quarter is live even in a good year; and (2) the low near-term bar sits **inside a deteriorating multi-year revision trend** — LT-growth consensus flipped to −14.8%, FY2027+ EPS keeps sliding, out-year estimates (thin, 1 analyst) imply an earnings **peak ~2029 then decline**, and targets were cut 7× in 3 months. The beatable read is a **near-term** one (profit line, next 1–2 quarters / FY2026); the multi-year direction is **down**.
