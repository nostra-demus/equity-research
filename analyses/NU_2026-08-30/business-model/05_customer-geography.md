# Customer And Geography Map — NU

The group-level revenue disclosures show no single customer at or above 10% of revenue; Nu Holdings itself is a holding company and its operating subsidiaries serve the customers. ## 4. Read

NU is not customer-concentrated in the disclosed revenue data: no single customer reached 10% of revenue, and the base consists of individual consumers and SMEs rather than a few named accounts. Geography is highly concentrated, however, because Brazil supplied 91.3% of FY2025 revenue and 90.9% of H1 2026 revenue, despite Mexico's share rising to 7.2% in H1 2026. The single biggest dependency is Brazilian economic, regulatory, and currency conditions; this is not protected by long-term customer contracts. [FY2025 Form 20-F, Note 34, p. F-97; Item 3.D Risk Factors—Risks Relating to the Countries in Which We Operate]
