For banks, gross and EBIT margins are not a clean common measure: their reported revenue and financial-service costs include funding cost and expected credit loss, while the peer export supplies LTM net-income margins. Those measures are shown only with their labels; they are not interchangeable.

The peer ROEs are management-defined current-quarter measures, whereas NU’s 17.2% is a calculated four-year IFRS average. They show directionally that NU is not a clear peer-return outlier; they do not support a precise league table.

**The economic moat test (required).** Return on capital **above** cost of capital: **17.2% average ROE** versus **16.51% cost of equity** (**+68 bps**) — calculated as the simple average of annual IFRS ROEs: FY2022 -7.8%, FY2023 18.2%, FY2024 28.1% and FY2025 30.3%. Each annual ROE is net income attributable to shareholders divided by average total equity; the FY2022 loss is included. The 16.51% is NU’s stated cost of equity for its Brazil-focused Investments activities CGU in the goodwill-impairment test, not a disclosed group-wide cost of equity; it is the only company-disclosed equity hurdle found and therefore limits precision in the comparison. [FY2025 Form 20-F, Consolidated Statements of Profit or Loss and Financial Position, pp.155, 157; FY2024 Form 20-F, Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity; FY2025 Form 20-F, Consolidated Financial Statements, goodwill impairment analysis, p.F-38]

No group management ROE headline was identified in the audited annual filing; the NU return above is a computed IFRS measure. Tax-rate normalization does not apply because the financial-institution test uses ROE, or net income divided by average equity, rather than NOPAT-based ROIC. The four-year average includes the FY2022 loss, but it is still available-history evidence rather than a fully proven credit-cycle return.

## 4. Where The Company Sits

1. **Relative to peers — Nu sits at the median of the named peers on the available return measure:** its 17.2% four-year IFRS ROE lies between Itaú’s 24.3% and Santander Brasil’s 12.5% and near Bradesco’s 16.2%. The period and definition differences above mean this is a cautious directional read, and there is insufficient matched gross- or EBIT-margin data to compare named peers.
2. **Absolute (the economic moat test) — the company earns a return on capital above its disclosed cost of equity** (17.2% four-year average ROE versus 16.51%, +68 bps). The narrow gap matters more than the peer ordering.

## 5. Moat Verdict

**Narrow moat — trajectory widening (provisional, not confirmed).** NU’s strongest evidenced advantages are low-cost digital distribution and a large customer/deposit base; they have coincided with returns above the disclosed equity hurdle, but only by 68 bps after including FY2022. The five-year durability test is whether annual IFRS ROE stays above the relevant cost of equity while gross economics and credit losses hold through a weaker Brazilian consumer-credit environment; the business-quality read is mixed on rate of change (50/100), so regulation and digital competition still shorten the period that can be underwritten. [Business Quality — NU, 2026-08-30; FY2025 Form 20-F, Item 3.D]

The panel has three supporting financial/customer rows, one contradiction and three metrics that are not assessable. The -150 bps gross-margin move must not be ignored: pre-tax margin rose 535 bps because operating expenses fell from 24.3% of revenue in FY2023 to 17.5% in FY2025, a 680 bps reduction, while gross economics weakened. ARPAC rose 36%, but it does not separate price from product mix or credit growth. Thus the current read is widening on operating leverage and customer monetization, not a confirmed widening of pricing power or market share. [FY2025 Form 20-F, Consolidated Statement of Profit or Loss, p.155; Item 5.A, p.177]

The single data point that would flip this trajectory read is FY2026 IFRS ROE, calculated as net income attributable to shareholders divided by average total equity, falling below the relevant cost of equity. It will be available with the FY2026 annual Form 20-F; the exact filing date is not disclosed in the pool. If it falls below the hurdle, the provisional widening read becomes eroding and the advantage is structure rather than economics.
