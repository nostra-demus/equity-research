## 10. Read

The assurance chain here is able and independently structured, and the two facts most likely to move a skeptic — the fee relationship and the rotation history — hold up under direct verification: zero non-audit fees to the auditor or its network (a fact the triage agent had not surfaced, found here in the LODR Schedule V table), a genuine cross-network rotation at listing (EY-network out, KPMG-network in), and no auditor resignation across the entire FY2015–FY2026 history this pool covers. CARO is clean on every clause that matters — no fraud, no fund diversion, no wilful-defaulter listing — and every gatekeeper handoff (CFO, Compliance Officer, internal-audit engagement entity) came with an immediate named successor and, in two of three cases, the outgoing person stayed with the company in another role rather than leaving.

The single weakest link is **not disqualifying but real: the identity of the auditors behind ~4% of consolidated assets and eight associate companies is never named in this pool**, and the audit committee's actual engagement with B S R & Co. (private sessions, meeting attendance) is asserted only as a policy mandate, not confirmed as a fact for this year. Layered on top of that: the auditor's own regulator, NFRA, has published routine (non-punitive) quality-inspection findings on this exact firm — documentation gaps, one flagged FY22/23 related-party-transaction test, and a Nov-2025 finding of "confirmation bias in fraud risk assessment" at the network level — and a same-family, differently-registered sibling firm (B S R & Associates LLP) was fined ₹10 crore and had two partners debarred over the Coffee Day Enterprises fraud in 2024. None of this touches IndiaMART's own engagement or its own signing partner on the evidence gathered here, but it means "Big-4-affiliate auditor" should not be read as a clean pass on its own — which is exactly this module's own instruction.

**Conclusion: assurance trustworthy.** No Critical or High red flag fires on any A4/A7-02 item. What would raise confidence further: the actual names of the component auditors for the four small subsidiaries and eight associates, and a specific factual confirmation (not just a policy mandate) that the Audit Committee held its private session with B S R & Co. this year.
