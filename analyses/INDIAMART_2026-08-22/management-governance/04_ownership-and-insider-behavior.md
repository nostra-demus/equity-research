## 5. Ownership / Insider Read

Skin in the game is real and it is founding equity, not option grants: the two co-founders have run IndiaMART continuously since 1999, hold 46.99% of the company directly between them, and their combined share count did not move year-on-year — the entire promoter-group % decline (49.17%→49.11%) is arithmetic dilution from ESOP issuance, not a sale. The net insider signal from the CIQ-tracked data is modestly positive (513,115 shares bought vs 0 sold in the most recent quarter) but the buyer identities and the open-market-vs-ESOP-exercise distinction are not resolved in this pool, so that signal carries limited weight. The biggest control-side risk to minority holders is not pledging (none is disclosed at the promoter level, and the one pledge-related fact in the pool is an unnamed, non-promoter, unquantified Designated-Person compliance lapse self-reported to the exchanges) — it is the unresolved ~1.1-percentage-point gap between the audited Annual Report's founder shareholding figures and Capital IQ's vendor figures for the same position date, which this report has flagged rather than silently resolved, and which should be checked against the underlying SAST shareholding-pattern filing before being relied on for any precision beyond "roughly 49% founder-controlled, one share one vote, no pledge disclosed."
