# Management-Governance Module — INDIAMART (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Two founder-controllers who built IndiaMART from 1996/1999 and still hold roughly 49% of it run a company with clean audits, near-zero related-party leakage, no debt, and candid quarterly disclosure of a real supplier-churn problem it has mostly failed to fix on schedule. Capital allocation is mixed: disciplined, all-shareholder-fair buybacks and dividends sit next to a decade of diffuse minority-stake investing whose FY26 return on newly deployed capital turned negative. Pay rewards an undisclosed scorecard, not a proven per-share metric, and 2026 saw executive pay rise while median employee pay fell. The one finding that overrides all of this: the FY26 Annual Report never discloses an unresolved 2021 criminal FIR (IPC Section 295, IT Act Section 66D) against the founder-CEO and co-founder-WTD — the non-disclosure, not the underlying allegation, fails this module's Non-Negotiable Gate. Verdict: Serious governance concerns, held there by one closable disclosure gap sitting on top of an otherwise reasonably clean governance architecture.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** the FY26 Annual Report's own text — 17,695 lines, searched exhaustively — contains no mention of a dated, five-outlet-corroborated 2021 criminal FIR against the two people who together control ~49% of the company and run it day to day. The only related disclosure is a narrower "not debarred by SEBI/MCA" boilerplate that does not cover a pending criminal matter [`12_regulatory-legal-and-compliance` Section 8].
- **Strongest bull point (steelman):** strip out that one finding and the governance architecture reads well by evidence, not adjectives — unqualified audit opinions and zero non-audit fees across the whole KPMG-network tenure [`08`], Beneish M=−3.29 and Dechow F=0.76, both comfortably Green with cash flow exceeding profit in every year read [`11`], RPT at 0.24–4.14% of revenue with no round-tripping across three years [`09`], net cash throughout with zero debt ever drawn [`02`, `11`], founders' share count unchanged and no pledge [`04`], and a functioning postal-ballot channel that actually recorded a 21.72%-against vote rather than manufacturing consensus [`05`]. Of the 33 people swept by `07`, 31 are Clean with no other adverse cross-linkage anywhere in the network.
- **Single killer risk:** if the Govardhan Shila FIR is confirmed still open and ultimately produces a chargesheet or conviction against either founder, that moves the person grade from Material concerns to Disqualifying and trips the Non-Negotiable Gate on **proof**, not concealment — a materially harder lock than the current one, and one that would sit directly on the two people who run the company and hold roughly half of it.
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** this sweep could not determine whether the FIR is still live or was quietly closed by police (a negative final report generates no reported judgment); no chargesheet, discharge, quashing order, or conviction was found on Indian Kanoon or in dated media through 2026, and the underlying allegation is a religious-offense/third-party-marketplace-content matter, not an economic-fraud charge against the founders' own conduct [`07` Section 2.1-2.2; `12` Section 8].
