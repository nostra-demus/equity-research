# Incentives & Compensation — INDIAMART

## 1. Compensation Structure

Reporting currency: Indian Rupees (₹). Period: FY2026 (year ended 31 March 2026), Ind AS, standalone company (IndiaMART InterMESH Limited). Multi-year context (FY2022–FY2025) is drawn from a Capital IQ compensation export and cross-checked against the FY2026 Annexure-4 disclosure, which reconciles well (CIQ's FY2025 CEO total of ₹7.49cr + the annexure's disclosed 11.17% FY2026 increase ≈ ₹8.33cr, matching the annexure's stated figure).

IndiaMART is founder-run: Dinesh Chandra Agarwal (Managing Director & CEO) and Brijesh Kumar Agrawal (Whole-Time Director) are co-founders who together hold 29.09% and 19.99% of shares outstanding respectively (combined ~49.1%, market value ~₹5,266cr as of 31-Mar-2026) [Capital IQ Public Ownership Summary, IndiaMART InterMESH Limited, position date 31-Mar-2026]. Their comp-plan pay (₹14.5cr combined in FY2026) is roughly 0.3% of the value of their existing shareholding — the dominant alignment mechanism for these two executives is pre-existing ownership, not this year's pay packet. This module's job is the design of the pay plan itself; ownership and insider dealing are covered in `04_ownership-and-insider-behavior`.

## 4. Incentive Alignment Read

The formal comp plan is cash-heavy, with the annual bonus tied to an undisclosed "Balanced Scorecard" (weights never published — not proven from available data whether it rewards ROIC/EPS/FCF-per-share or revenue/volume), and the only long-term incentive that exists at all vests purely on time served, never on a return metric — while the CEO and the senior Whole-Time Director, the two people running the company, received zero equity compensation in FY2026. What the plan actually pays for cannot be confirmed as per-share value creation because the metric behind roughly 20-30% of cash pay is not disclosed; what it does NOT do is reward size for its own sake either (no revenue/deal-count/headcount metric is named, and dilution/burn are low). The real alignment mechanism at this company is the founders' pre-existing ~49% combined ownership stake (~₹5,266cr), which dwarfs their annual pay by roughly 350×, not the incentive-plan design this agent evaluated. Set against that, FY2026 shows a genuine, filing-documented divergence a minority holder should watch: median employee pay fell 2.17% while every executive's pay rose (CFO +24.85%), and CEO/WTD pay rose in a year consolidated profit fell 14% — a one-year signal, not yet a confirmed multi-year pattern, since the 3-year CEO-pay CAGR (~12.3%) has run behind the 3-year PAT CAGR (~18.7%). **Conclusion: mixed** — not misaligned (no size-based metric identified, dilution modest, statutory caps cleared with wide headroom, no golden parachute, no problematic practice found), but not well-aligned either (metric opacity on the bonus, no performance condition on the LTI, no clawback policy, above-peer CEO pay by a web-sourced ~2.8-3.5× on one comparable, and the median-pay-vs-executive-pay divergence in the latest year).
