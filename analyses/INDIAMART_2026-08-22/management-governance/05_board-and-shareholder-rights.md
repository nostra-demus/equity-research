**RPT approval hygiene (the minority-protection question this section owns):** "All the Related Party Transactions were approved by the Independent Directors of the Committee" (Audit Committee) [FY26 AR, CG Report, p.115]. ## 5. Minority-Shareholder Protection Read

The board clears the structural minimums — a majority-independent board (55.6-62.5%, above the applicable 50% floor), 100% meeting attendance, a functioning committee structure whose chairs were actively de-concentrated in July 2025 (Vivek Gour previously chaired 4 committees, now chairs 2), a working Postal Ballot channel with an independent scrutinizer, negligible dilution, and no formal takeover defenses layered on top of the promoters' natural 49.17% control block. But "board is a real check on management" cannot be stated cleanly for two connected reasons this section is required to carry forward, not soften: (1) 07's own-record sweep grades the founder-CEO-MD and the co-founder-WTD — the company's two largest shareholders and its only two founder-directors — **Material concerns** on an unresolved 2021 criminal FIR, which is an allegation, not a conviction, but by rule caps conviction at no better than Watchlist until resolved; and (2) the one related-party transaction that was actually put to a shareholder vote this year — a promoter-family-relative office-of-profit appointment — drew **21.72% against**, comfortably past the significant-dissent line, with **no disclosed board response** found anywhere in this pool. Add to that a rotating, undesignated board chair (mitigated but not replaced by a Lead Independent Director), a skills matrix one category short of the registry's green band, a generically-worded board evaluation with no disclosed specific outcomes, and an undisclosed D&O insurance position on a company almost certainly inside India's top-1000-by-market-cap mandate, and the honest read is **adequate**, not **strong protection**: the mechanics of shareholder democracy function (the postal ballot itself proves that), but this year's one real test of them — the RPT dissent — was not visibly answered, and the two founder-directors carry an unresolved integrity question that a truly independent board would be expected to address explicitly rather than leave silent in the filings.

**Verdict: adequate.** The single biggest entrenchment/self-dealing risk to minority holders is not a formal defense (there is none) but the combination of (a) an unaddressed 22%-against vote on a promoter-relative RPT and (b) a board whose only accountable, always-present leadership figure (the Lead Independent Director) sits alongside — not above — two founder-directors currently graded Material concerns by the person-integrity sweep. `04_ownership-and-insider-behavior` has not yet run in this batch; if it independently confirms a structurally-unaligned-owner pattern (RF-OWN-004) on top of the above, this report's "adequate" read would need to be revisited downward toward "weak / entrenched" per the cross-module rule — that check could not be completed here and is flagged as the standing follow-up.
