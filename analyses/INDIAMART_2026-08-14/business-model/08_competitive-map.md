# Competitive Map — INDIAMART

Upstream dependencies check: both required upstream files are present — `02_business-identity.md` and `03_segment-map.md`.

## 1. Dominant Segment

Web and related Services — India's B2B online marketplace connecting buyers and suppliers — is the dominant segment, at 91.96% of FY26 consolidated revenue (₹14,429.38m of ₹15,690.42m) and more than 100% of FY26 segment profit [FY26 Annual Report (Ind AS), Note 32 — Segment information; restated from upstream `03_segment-map.md`].

## 2. Named Competitors

The company's own annual report describes a generic "Competition Risk" — "the emergence of new B2B marketplaces, alternative digital commerce platforms, and increasing direct interactions between suppliers and buyers" — without naming any rival by name [FY26 Integrated Annual Report, Risk Management section, "R3 Competition Risk"]. Specific names come from two other places in the pool: (a) the company's own founder/CEO on quarterly earnings calls, who repeatedly and consistently names the same handful of rivals, and (b) the Capital IQ Competitors export, a mechanically generated list that includes many companies with no real overlap (co-working space operators, study-abroad consultants) alongside the credible ones. This section keeps only names that clear the credibility bar in SELECTION CRITERIA.

### Competitor A — Just Dial Limited

- **Ticker / listing:** NSEI:JUSTDIAL (subsidiary of Reliance Retail Ventures Limited since October 2021) [Capital IQ Competitors export, IndiaMART InterMESH Limited].
- **Where they compete:** India-focused local search and business-listings platform; runs a direct B2B product line ("JD Mart," described by the vendor as "for B2B commerce") that overlaps with IndiaMART's core marketplace [Capital IQ Competitors export, Business Description]. Management itself frames the overlap as partial, not total: "5% to 10% overlap could be with Justdial... on the buyer side as well as on the supplier side" [Q2 FY24 (Oct 2023) earnings call transcript].
- **Scale:** LTM Total Revenue $130.4mm (period ended 2026-06-30, filed 2026-07-10) vs IndiaMART's own LTM Total Revenue of $168.9mm in the same comp run — Just Dial is roughly 0.8x IndiaMART's scale, comfortably inside the 5x credibility band [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis, "Financial Data" tab, IndiaMART InterMESH Limited, as-of 2026-08-13].
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM EBITDA margin 29.6% and LTM net income margin 40.5% (the high net margin is inflated by non-operating "other income" — Just Dial carries a large net-cash balance sheet, LTM net debt of −$604.7mm i.e. large net cash) [Capital IQ Quick Comparable Analysis, "Operating Statistics" and "Financial Data" tabs, as-of 2026-08-13]. Return on equity is not cleanly available from this pool; two web aggregators quoted diverging figures on the same day range (ROE ~3.4% per one source, ~8.5%–10.7% per others; ROCE ~7.0%–13.1%) — these are third-party estimates, not filing-sourced, and disagree by a factor of ~3x, so treat the ROE/ROCE figure as directionally low-single-to-low-double-digit but **not precisely pinned down**: Web: Screener.in, 2026-08-13 (unverified); Web: MarketsMojo / Alpha Spread, 2026-07-28 (unverified). The EBITDA/net-margin figures above, sourced to the Capital IQ comp export, are the more reliable anchor for the `moat` agent's peer-margin comparison.
- **Source named in:** Named by the company itself on multiple earnings calls (Q1 FY22, Q2 FY22, Q2 FY24, Q3 FY24, Q2 FY25 transcripts) and appears as a "Named by Company (Self)" entry in the Capital IQ Competitors export.
- **One-line read:** A same-country, comparable-scale rival with a large net-cash balance sheet and a much lower B2B-specific growth trajectory — management's own read is that Just Dial "woke up a little late" on paying-customer growth and IndiaMART moved from 150,000 to nearly 200,000 paying suppliers while Just Dial's numbers stagnated post-COVID [Q2 FY25 (Oct 2024) earnings call transcript].

### Competitor B — TradeIndia (Infocom Network Private Limited)

- **Ticker / listing:** Unlisted / private limited company [Q2 FY24 (Oct 2023) earnings call transcript: "both of them are private limited companies so they file their annual returns with the MCA"].
- **Where they compete:** Identical business model to IndiaMART's core marketplace — a B2B classifieds/lead-generation platform for Indian suppliers and buyers. Management explicitly ranks it, alongside ExportersIndia, as the closest like-for-like rival: "We have always said Trade India and Exporters India are the two which are the nearest one... purely doing the similar business model" [Q2 FY24 (Oct 2023) earnings call transcript].
- **Scale:** Not disclosed in the data pool (private company; MCA annual-return data is referenced by management as something they track internally but the figures themselves are not in this pool). A third-party aggregator estimate puts Infocom Network Private Limited's FY25 (year ended 31 March 2025) revenue in a ₹100–500 crore range (~$12–60mm at typical FX) — a wide, low-confidence range from a non-filing source: Web: RocketReach/Tofler aggregator estimate, 2026 (unverified, wide range, not from an audited filing). On any reading, this is meaningfully smaller than IndiaMART's ~$169mm LTM revenue.
- **Profitability / return on capital:** Not public / not disclosed. No margin or return-on-capital figure for Infocom Network Private Limited exists anywhere in this data pool or in the reliable source tiers.
- **Source named in:** Named directly and repeatedly by the company's own CEO in earnings-call Q&A (Q2 FY24, Q2 FY25 transcripts) as the nearest direct competitor.
- **One-line read:** The company's own most-cited "closest" rival by business-model overlap, but smaller, privately held, and — per management — "no significant change that we have observed from them" [Q2 FY25 (Oct 2024) earnings call transcript], i.e. not viewed internally as a growing threat.

### Competitor C — Alibaba.com / 1688.com (Alibaba Group Holding Limited)

- **Ticker / listing:** NYSE:BABA / HKEX:9988 (Alibaba Group Holding Limited); Alibaba.com Limited itself was taken private by its parent in 2012 and no longer files separately — the Capital IQ Competitors export still carries a stale LTM Revenue figure of $960.05mm dated 2012-03-31, which predates the privatization and should not be used as a current figure [Capital IQ Competitors export, IndiaMART InterMESH Limited].
- **Where they compete:** Global (Alibaba.com) and China-domestic (1688.com) B2B sourcing marketplaces — not a direct India-market competitor for supplier acquisition, but named repeatedly by IndiaMART's own management as the strategic reference point for where the India platform's monetization model could evolve, and as an overlap on the buyer side for cross-border sourcing [Q3 FY24 (Jan 2024) and Q4 FY26 (Apr 2026) earnings call transcripts].
- **Scale:** Management's own estimate, not an Alibaba disclosure: 1688.com has "about 1 million customers and ARPU of about $2,000... that makes it about $2 billion in revenue," built up over roughly 15 years including a phase of "customer-led growth" before shifting toward "ARPU-led growth" once it crossed roughly 750,000 paying sellers [Q4 FY26 (Apr 2026) earnings call transcript]. This is IndiaMART management's own inference about a competitor's business, not a cited Alibaba filing figure — label it accordingly: **Inference, not from filings** (management estimate, unverified against Alibaba's own disclosures). On this estimate, 1688.com's implied revenue is roughly 12x IndiaMART's ~$169mm LTM revenue — well outside the 5x scale band — so this name is included as a strategic/analog reference, not a same-weight-class rival.
- **Profitability / return on capital:** Not public / not disclosed at the 1688.com or Alibaba.com segment level — Alibaba Group Holding reports consolidated and China-Commerce segment results, but does not break out 1688.com's standalone margin or return on capital in the data available here, so no peer figure can be cited for this specific unit.
- **Source named in:** Named directly by the company's own CEO across three separate earnings calls (Q3 FY24, Q2 FY26, Q4 FY26 transcripts) and listed in the Capital IQ Competitors export as a "Named by Company (Self)" entry.
- **One-line read:** Not a same-market rival for supplier acquisition inside India, but the company's own chosen benchmark for what a mature, ARPU-led version of its own subscription model could look like at scale.

## 3. Competitive Position

Position vs peers: **Holding**, based on a proxy rather than disclosed market-share data. IndiaMART does not disclose a market-share percentage in the reviewed filings. The proxy comes from management's own comparative commentary: paying suppliers grew from ~150,000 to ~200,000 over the period cited while Just Dial's paying-customer base "declined for a long period... during... COVID" and only later recovered [Q2 FY25 (Oct 2024) earnings call transcript], and TradeIndia/ExportersIndia showed "no significant change" [same transcript]. Management's own qualitative claim is that current market share is "much higher" than the ~60% it estimates it held at IPO (2019) [Q2 FY24 (Oct 2023) earnings call transcript] — this is a company-sourced, unverified claim (Level 3, transcript) with no independent confirmation in this pool, so it should not be read as more than directional. Set against that read, the company's own most recent disclosed metric shows paying-supplier growth decelerating sharply — 220,000 in FY26 vs 217,000 in FY25, +1.4% YoY, well below the 8% five-year CAGR since FY21 [FY26 Integrated Annual Report, MD&A, p.82-83, cited in upstream `02_business-identity.md`] — which tempers a clean "gaining" read; "Holding" better reflects a business that is not visibly losing named-competitor customers but whose own growth engine has slowed.

## 4. Competitive Shape

The credible competitive set in India's B2B marketplace/classifieds segment is small and asymmetric: one comparable-scale public rival (Just Dial, via its JD Mart product) whose core business is local search rather than pure B2B marketplace; a smaller pure-play private direct competitor (TradeIndia/Infocom Network) that management itself has watched for years without observing material share shifts; and diffuse, partial (5–10%) overlap with horizontal platforms (Google, Amazon, and separately Meta properties) that management names as the four or five places SME advertising spend actually competes for attention [Q3 FY24 (Jan 2024) earnings call transcript: "there are only 4, 5 large platforms... between IndiaMART and Google and Amazon and Justdial... And Facebook and WhatsApp and Instagram"]. No HHI or independent third-party market-share dataset exists in this pool. Read together, this is closer to a **concentrated few-firm segment with one dominant player** (IndiaMART, on management's own unverified ~60%-plus estimate) than a fragmented market with many equal-sized rivals — but that read rests on a single company-sourced claim, not an audited or third-party share survey, and should be treated as directional only.

## 5. Caveat

Two limitations should be carried forward to `moat`:
1. **No independently audited or third-party market-share data exists in this pool.** Every share and "position vs peers" claim in Section 3 traces back to IndiaMART's own management commentary on earnings calls — useful as the company's internal view, but not corroborated by an external source (e.g., an independent industry report or a competitor's own disclosed customer count).
2. **TradeIndia/Infocom Network — the competitor management itself calls "nearest"** — discloses no public financials in this pool; its scale/margin data above rests on a wide, low-confidence third-party web estimate, not a filing. If a future data pull can obtain Infocom Network's MCA-filed annual return (referenced by management as something the company tracks internally), that would materially sharpen the closest same-business-model comparison.
