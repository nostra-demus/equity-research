# Customer And Geography Map — INDIAMART

## 1. Customer Map

IndiaMART does not name individual customers in its filings (its own segment note confirms no single customer clears the disclosure threshold). What it does disclose is a **tiered concentration**: revenue is split across roughly 220,000 paying suppliers (sellers), but a shrinking slice of that base drives most of the revenue. Buyers pay nothing — IndiaMART's paying "customer" is always the supplier/seller side of its marketplace.

## 4. Read

This is a geography-concentrated business layered on top of a customer base that is widening its dependence on a shrinking top slice of accounts. On geography, it is close to a single-country business: 99.6% of FY26 revenue and 100% of non-current assets sit in India, despite the company's own risk-management section claiming "diversified market presence across industries and geographies for risk distribution" [FY26 Annual Report, Risk Management, Economic Risk mitigation] — a claim the company's own segment note does not support. On the customer side, no single named account exceeds 10% of revenue, but the top 10% of paying suppliers already generate 49% of revenue and that share has risen every year since FY21 (41% → 49%), while the Gold-and-Platinum tier (about half the paying-supplier base) contributes nearly 75% of revenue. All of this is naked, not contractually secured — subscriptions are annual/monthly with no disclosed multi-year lock-in, and cash is simply collected in advance. The single biggest dependency for the synthesizer: IndiaMART's revenue is almost entirely a bet on Indian SME subscription spending (99.6% of revenue, one country, one buyer segment — small and mid-sized Indian sellers), with no geographic offset if that spending slows, and an increasing share of that revenue is concentrated in the willingness of the top-decile of paying suppliers to keep upgrading and renewing.
