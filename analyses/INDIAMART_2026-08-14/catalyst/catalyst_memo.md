## 2. What This Module Found

The forward calendar is real but soft. Nothing on it is a date the exchange has confirmed. The nearest item is Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) results, modeled by the data vendor at around 21-Oct-2026 off a five-year pattern of when the company reports — but no NSE/BSE board-meeting notice has been filed for it as of 14-Aug-2026 [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md` §1; `00_catalyst-data-triage.md` §1]. The FY27 annual general meeting is known only by its statutory deadline (~Sep-2027); the exact date is not filed [`00_catalyst-data-triage.md` §1, §3].

The single most important catalyst is not a date at all. It is whether the number of paying suppliers on the core platform stops shrinking within management's own "2, 3 more quarters" window — roughly the Q3 FY27 print through Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28. Net adds have been negative in 3 of the last 4 quarters. Management said it will give "no guidance on the net adds until we have multiple quarters of continual success" [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md` §2]. That one variable sits behind both the valuation module's ₹2,298 bull case and its ₹1,229 bear case [`valuation/07_scenario-and-fair-value.md` §3, §6].

The most important risk is the compounding-bear scenario across the Q2 FY27 to Q1 FY28 prints: net adds stay negative AND management resumes spending on acquiring customers. That would turn the revenue driver and the profit-margin driver negative at the same time, because the current margin help is simply the deferred spend — the same policy choice, read two ways [`earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md` §8, §10]. It is the exact construction of the valuation bear case at ₹1,229, −31.1% downside [`valuation/07_scenario-and-fair-value.md` §3].

On the bull side, net adds turning positive inside that window would remove the revenue drag while the margin help is kept — which lines up with the reverse-DCF finding (working backwards from today's price to the growth it implies) that the market is pricing a flat ~6.0% annual revenue growth rate, below the company's own recent growth [`valuation/05_reverse-dcf.md` §5].

The near-term calendar does not lean bullish or bearish on its own. The same variable produces both the best and the worst outcome at each print, so timing by itself offers no directional edge [99_catalyst-synthesis §4].

- **Watch next:** core-platform paying-supplier net adds at the Q2 FY27 print (~21-Oct-2026). One metric, print after print — there is no single date that settles this thesis.

## 6. Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst** — a specific, dated event that could move the share price or settle a debate about the company.
- **EBITDA** — rough operating profit before interest, tax and depreciation; a proxy for cash the operations throw off.
- **Net adds** — new paying suppliers signed up minus those who left, in a quarter. Negative means the paying base is shrinking.
- **Consensus** — the average forecast of the analysts who cover the stock; here, ₹4,222.5mn revenue for Q2 FY27.
- **Reverse-DCF** — working backwards from today's share price to the growth rate the price already assumes.
- **CAGR** — the average yearly growth rate over a period.
- **Net cash (broad basis)** — cash plus liquid investments, less all debt. "Broad" flags that investments are counted in, not just cash.
- **Buyback** — the company using its own cash to buy back its shares, which raises each remaining holder's share of the profits.
