## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

There is no hard, exchange-confirmed date on the forward calendar. The nearest evidenced item is Q2 FY27 results, CIQ-modeled at ~21-Oct-2026 based on a consistent five-year post-quarter-end release cadence — but no NSE/BSE board-meeting intimation has been filed for this quarter as of today, so this is a strong estimate, not a proven date [`00_catalyst-data-triage.md` §1]. What matters at that print is whether the two offsetting forces in the current setup — a paying-subscriber base that has shrunk in 3 of the last 4 quarters, and a margin tailwind from deliberately deferred customer-acquisition spend — continue to offset each other or start moving together in the same (adverse) direction, since both are the same underlying management policy choice [`earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md` §8].

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

The single most important catalyst is not a single dated event but the resolution (or non-resolution) of the core-platform paying-supplier net-add decline over the "2, 3 more quarters" window management itself named on the Jul-2026 call — roughly spanning the Q3 FY27 (Jan-2027) print through Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28 (Apr–Jun 2027). This is named independently as the dominant swing factor by both the earnings-sensitivity module (the compounding-bear interaction between subscriber losses and opex resumption) and the valuation scenario-and-fair-value module (the same variable is explicitly the swing factor between its Bull ₹2,298 and Bear ₹1,229 fair-value cases) [`earnings/07_earnings-sensitivity.md` §5; `valuation/07_scenario-and-fair-value.md` §6]. The bull path: net adds turn positive as retention/verification initiatives take hold, letting margin stay elevated without the revenue drag — reinforcing the reverse-DCF finding that the market is currently pricing in a conservative ~6.0% flat revenue CAGR, well below the company's own recent growth [`valuation/05_reverse-dcf.md` §5]. The bear path: net adds stay negative past the stated window, forcing management to either resume acquisition spend (hitting margin) or accept a structural, not transitional, SME-demand problem — the exact scenario the Bear fair-value case (₹1,229, −31.1% downside) is built on [`valuation/07_scenario-and-fair-value.md` §3]. A buyer cannot time this precisely — it is a qualitative management-stated window, not a fixed date — but it can be tracked at each of the next two-to-three quarterly prints.

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **The compounding-bear earnings scenario (Q2 FY27 through Q1 FY28 prints).** If paying-subscriber net adds stay negative and management resumes customer-acquisition spend to defend the funnel, both the revenue driver and the margin driver turn negative at once rather than offsetting — a specific, evidenced scenario, not a generic risk [`earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md` §10; `earnings/07_earnings-sensitivity.md` §5].
- **Busy Infotech segment's unproven turnaround (ongoing, undated).** Three straight years of negative segment EBITDA since the FY23 acquisition year, despite growth-CAGR guidance for the next couple of years — flagged under §24 Filter 2 above. A buyer treating the 27–30% CAGR target as a bullish catalyst without evidence of the segment actually turning profitable is buying a story, not a delivered result [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md` §2, row 02-004].
- **FY27 AGM repeat-dissent risk (~by Sep-2027).** FY26's postal ballot saw 21.72% of votes cast against a promoter-family related-party "office or place of profit" appointment (RF-SHR-001, RF-RPT-002) — a genuine minority-shareholder friction signal that could recur if another related-party item reaches the ballot [`management-governance/05_board-and-shareholder-rights.md` §4A, row 05-020].
- **IndiaMART Finance Limited regulatory/execution risk (undated).** A new NBFC-adjacent business line awaiting approval, with no disclosed capital commitment or completion date — a fresh, unproven initiative that echoes the same pattern (real capital and management attention, no evidenced value-creating outcome yet) already seen in the venture-investment portfolio [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md` §2, §5].
- **Standing, undated tail risk — contingent tax/GST appeals.** ₹521.86mn of active, appealed tax/GST demands (2.17% of equity) carry no resolution date in the pool; immaterial to solvency on its own but a real line item that could crystallize adversely at any point [`analyses/INDIAMART_2026-08-13/balance-sheet-survival/99_balance-sheet-survival-synthesis.md` §3A, §6].

## 5. Calendar Read

Nothing on this calendar is a hard, exchange-confirmed date — the nearest item (Q2 FY27 results, ~21-Oct-2026) is a strong pattern-based estimate, not yet a filed board-meeting intimation, and every other forward item is a qualitative window ("2, 3 more quarters," "next couple of years," "subject to necessary approvals") rather than a fixed trigger date. The near-term calendar does not skew cleanly bullish or bearish on its own — the same underlying variable (SME demand and the customer-acquisition-spend policy choice) drives both the best-case and worst-case outcomes at each of the next few prints, and the one clearly evidenced §24-flagged item (Busy Infotech's still-unproven segment economics) should not be read as a bullish catalyst on its growth-target language alone. The single biggest timing caveat: this is a "catalysts exist but timing vague" calendar, not a "no proven catalyst yet" one and not a "dated, evidenced near-term catalyst" one either — a buyer can track the right variable (paying-supplier net adds) at the right prints, but cannot point to a single hard date that will resolve the thesis.
