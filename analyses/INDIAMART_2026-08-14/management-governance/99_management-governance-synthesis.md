# Management-Governance Module — INDIAMART (Synthesis)

## Abstract

IndiaMART is run by its two founders, who together hold 49.08% of the company bought at incorporation in 1999, not granted through options, and have led it continuously for 27 years — real, not narrative, alignment. Capital allocation is mixed-to-strong: dividends are well covered, growth is self-funding with near-zero capex, and every deal was cash-funded with zero debt, but a ~₹31 billion treasury pile has grown with no buyback since September 2023 and the acquisition/venture program remains unproven on returns. The clearest weakness is incentive design: the annual bonus's "Balanced Scorecard" metrics are never disclosed, and CEO cash pay rose 11.17% in FY26 while diluted EPS fell 14.0% and the share price fell 9.75% — a direct, dated divergence. Verdict: standard/mixed stewardship — genuinely owner-led and candid, but not yet demonstrably paying for per-share results.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** The incentive-pay structure is opaque on the one question that matters, and the one year checkable in the data shows it failed its own sanity check — CEO cash pay +11.17% while diluted EPS fell 14.0% and the share price fell 9.75% (FY26), with the underlying "Balanced Scorecard" metrics never itemized across FY22–FY26 [`03_incentives-and-compensation.md`, rows 03-004, 03-006, 03-007]. Layered on top: a 27-year, ~49%-owning founder bloc has zero disclosed succession plan [`01_management-and-track-record.md`, row 01-007], and the core growth KPI (paying-supplier net-adds) was guided at 8,000–9,000/quarter in April 2024 and abandoned within the same fiscal year, with no replacement figure given more than a year later [`01`, row 01-013; `06_candor-and-disclosure-quality.md`, Section 1].
- **Strongest bull point (steelman):** The founders' 49.08% stake was bought at incorporation in 1999, not granted — real capital at risk for 27 years, with the company debt-free throughout and every acquisition/buyback/dividend self-funded from operating cash flow [`02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md`, row 02-013]. Management is directly, unpromotionally candid in bad quarters: it led Q1 FY27 prepared remarks with a negative supplier number and two named causes before any question was asked, and on the Q4 FY25 call the CEO refused to fabricate a recovery number under direct pressure ("I'm really not confident to give you any answer as of now") [`06`, Section 4]. The board clears independence mechanics with room to spare (62.5% independent, no designated Chairman, fully independent audit/NRC committees) [`05_board-and-shareholder-rights.md`, Section 1].
- **Single killer risk:** Undisclosed pay metrics plus a founder-controlled board (no designated Chairman, CEO and co-founder hold two of eight board seats and ~49% of the vote) mean there is no external check forcing the Balanced Scorecard to reward per-share value if the founders choose otherwise — the one data point available (FY26) shows pay rising as per-share value fell, and minority shareholders have no visibility into whether that will recur.
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** A minority-shareholder check IS working in practice — the Amit Agarwal (promoter-family) related-party "office or place of profit" appointment drew 21.72% of votes against in the FY26 postal ballot [`05`, row 05-013, RF-RPT-002/RF-SHR-001], showing institutional and public shareholders (50.88% of the register) are alert and willing to vote against founder-adjacent items even though the resolution still passed.
