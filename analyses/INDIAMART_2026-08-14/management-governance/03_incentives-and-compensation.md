## 4. Incentive Alignment Read

The pay structure itself is genuinely opaque on the one question that matters: the "Performance Linked Incentive," which is 28–30% of each founder's cash pay, is explicitly tied to an internal "Balance Score Card" whose metrics and weights are never disclosed — so it cannot be classified as per-share/returns-based or size-based from the filings, and the one dated fact the pool does supply (CEO pay +11.17% in the same year diluted EPS fell 14.0% and the share price fell 9.75%) points toward the payout not tracking per-share outcomes. Working against that: the two founders who set strategy hold no equity compensation at all — they are contractually barred from the ESOP scheme — so whatever alignment exists for them comes entirely from their pre-existing ~49% direct ownership stake (`01_management-and-track-record.md`, Section 1) rather than from any incentive plan a shareholder vote could recalibrate; the only executive with a fresh equity grant in FY26 (CFO, 13,330 SARs) received a time-vested, share-price-linked instrument with no disclosed performance hurdle. Net: this is not a size-based empire-building pay plan in the classic sense (no metric rewards revenue, deal count, or headcount that is disclosed), but it is also not demonstrably a per-share pay plan — it is an **undisclosed** plan that just missed its own most obvious sanity check this year. Verdict: **mixed**, leaning toward the opaque/misaligned side of that band on the pay-structure evidence alone (the founders' ownership stake is a separate, real alignment force this report does not credit to the compensation plan).
