- **Verdict:** Partial

- **Specialists that can run:** management track record (01, thin on multi-quarter transcript history but usable via guidance/surprise history + one transcript), capital allocation (02, full 5-year history), ownership (04, via CIQ vendor ownership/insider tables), board (05, composition/tenure usable; related-party sub-question not assessable), candor (06, usable via guidance-vs-actual and non-GAAP read, thin on multi-quarter transcript tone). Incentives (03) is the most constrained — no compensation structure data exists in this pool at all.
- **Hard disqualifier already flagged by business-model/01_disqualifier-scan?** N — "No disqualifier fact found in the available data pool," with an explicit caveat that rows for related-party transactions, restatement, and enforcement rest on absence-of-evidence in a vendor-export/transcript-only dataset, not a primary filing's clean attestation. Separately, `business-model/11_capital-allocation-governance.md` flagged a §24 Filter 4 serial-acquirer pattern (Delivery Hero, $14.8bn, ~€14bn debt-funded, ~10% of Uber's market value, plus 7+ bolt-ons in ~18 months) that caps THAT agent's Capital Allocation Score at 50/100 — this is a score cap under MODULE_RULES.md, not a hard disqualifier, and this module's `02_capital-allocation-scorecard` should apply the same RF-CAP-004 cap logic rather than re-litigate the underlying fact.
- **Active partial-data caps:**
  - No proxy / compensation disclosure → Incentive alignment max 50; Overall usefulness max 70 (agents 03, 99)
  - No related-party disclosure anywhere in the pool → board/shareholder-rights read (05) cannot assess related-party leakage; treat as "Insufficient Data," not "clean," per MODULE_RULES's conservative default
  - Ownership/insider data present only at Capital IQ vendor tier (confidence 3, not a filed 13D/13G/Form 4) → lower confidence on ownership/insider findings even though no hard score cap applies
  - Board composition present only via vendor profile, no formal proxy confirming committee charters or an explicit independence % → lower confidence on board findings
  - Only one quarter's transcript, no shareholder letter → promise-vs-delivery and candor reads (01, 06) limited to filings/guidance history plus one transcript, per the standard cap
- **Critical missing items:**
  - Compensation disclosure (CD&A / LTIP metrics and weights) — fully absent
  - Related-party transaction disclosure — fully absent
  - Any primary SEC filing (DEF 14A, 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, Form 4, Schedule 13D/13G) — none in the pool
- **Single highest-value missing document:** DEF 14A (proxy statement) — it would simultaneously fill the compensation-disclosure gap, confirm board independence/committee structure with a filed source, and disclose related-party transactions, closing all three critical gaps at once.
