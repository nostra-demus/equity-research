## What This Module Found

The core read is genuinely mixed — neither a clean pass nor a governance failure. Dara Khosrowshahi's team delivered a real, cash-backed turnaround: operating income (profit from the core business, before interest and tax) went from a $(1,832)mm loss in FY2022 to $5,565mm in FY2025, on capital spending that never topped $336mm a year — an incremental return on capital (profit earned on each new dollar invested) in the 25–35% range [`02`, §4]. On the two numbers management actually guides to — Adjusted EBITDA (a company-defined profit measure) and Adjusted EPS (adjusted earnings per share) — guidance has been met or beaten for several quarters running [`01`, §4].

The single most important driver of the cap is capital allocation. The acquisition program is at least seven deals in about 18 months, topped by the $14.8bn, debt-funded purchase of Delivery Hero — equal to 10.6% of Uber's own market value — financed by a fresh roughly €14bn bridge loan and paid for in part by pulling $4bn away from a buyback (share-repurchase) promise made to shareholders [`02`, RF-CAP-004]. Net debt (total debt minus cash) jumped from $76mm to $9,340mm in a single trailing-year window, before the much larger bridge loan is even drawn [02-014].

The single most important risk is that this same team has shown it will override its own capital-return promise to chase a large, debt-funded deal it cannot yet prove will work — and Delivery Hero does not close until H2 2027, with its $1.2bn synergy target still just a management goal, not a delivered result [`02`, §2].

Two other things weigh on the read. Insiders own just 0.18% of the company (3,586,107 shares, $256.8mm), almost all of it from stock grants vesting rather than bought with personal cash — so there is little "skin in the game" behind the promises [04-002, 04-005]. And what the pay plan actually rewards is unknown: no compensation disclosure was read for this module, so incentive alignment scores 10/100 and is unproven, not disproven [`03`].

## The Specialists, Briefly

- **Governance data triage:** Partial sufficiency — ownership, board, and 5-year capital-allocation history are usable via vendor exports; the two critical gaps are compensation and related-party disclosure.
- **Management & track record (65/100):** A real, delivered turnaround with guidance kept or beaten — undercut by three CFOs since 2018, the middle one lasting about 2 years, right as the company finances its largest-ever acquisition.
- **Capital-allocation scorecard (50/100, capped from 58):** Organic capital use creates value, but the M&A program is a §24 serial-acquirer pattern — 7+ deals in 18 months, Delivery Hero at 10.6% of market cap, debt-funded. RF-CAP-004 triggered.
- **Incentives & compensation (10/100, capped max 50):** Incentive alignment cannot be assessed — no proxy pay-plan section anywhere in the pool this agent used. Unproven, not disproven.
- **Ownership & insider behavior (55/100):** Clean pledge/control picture, but negligible, grant-driven insider ownership — 0.18% total; 0 of 44 six-month "purchases" confirmed as open-market cash buys.
- **Board & shareholder rights (84/100):** Genuinely independent board confirmed from a primary FY2026 proxy (DEF 14A) pulled from SEC EDGAR — 90% independent directors, zero disclosed related-party deals above $120,000 — offset by no shareholder right to act by written consent and a 25% bar to call a special meeting.
- **Candor & disclosure (56/100):** Direct, quantified answers to hard questions on the one available call, alongside a persistently unexplained ~25% slice of the Adjusted EBITDA add-back and a $10.1bn two-year tax-related boost to reported profit that was never flagged. RF-DISC-002 triggered.

**Most important disagreement (reconciled):** Four specialists said no proxy statement exists in the data; the board specialist (`05`) actually pulled Uber's FY2026 proxy from SEC EDGAR and used it. The synthesis resolved this by treating `05`'s primary-filing facts as authoritative where they overlap (zero related-party deals above $120,000; 91% average Say-on-Pay support) — but the pay-plan detail (CD&A / Summary Compensation Table) was still never read, so incentive alignment stays genuinely unproven, capped at 10.

## What Would Change This Read

**Would strengthen it:** a proxy pay section showing per-share / returns-based bonus and long-term-incentive metrics; a confirmed open-market insider purchase (bought with cash, not from grants vesting); Delivery Hero closing on schedule with leverage staying under the stated target and synergies materializing; buybacks resuming on the "months, not quarters" timeline; and no further debt-funded deals.

**Would weaken it:** a pay section showing size-based metrics (revenue, deal count, absolute EBITDA) that reward getting bigger; leverage overshooting the (web-sourced, unverified) under-2x target; another large debt-funded deal piled on before Delivery Hero even closes; a third straight year of the deferred-tax profit boost with no management comment; or any insider selling ahead of a subsequent miss (none found in this pool to date).

**Data needed:** the FY2026 proxy's Compensation Discussion & Analysis (already located, not yet read for pay); the FQ3/FQ4 2025 earnings-call transcripts (the actual weak quarters); the 2026 AGM vote-count filing; and post-close Delivery Hero integration data (expected H2 2027).

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict: Standard / mixed** — Governance Rating Weak (composite 49/100), confidence 65/100. Not a clean pass, not a governance failure.
- **Biggest reason it could be better than it looks:** a proven, delivered turnaround ($(1,832)mm operating loss FY2022 → $5,565mm profit FY2025) under a board independent by every checkable metric (90% independent, no poison pill, no dual-class stock, zero disclosed related-party deals above $120,000).
- **Biggest reason it could be worse:** a debt-funded M&A binge — 7+ deals in 18 months, topped by the $14.8bn Delivery Hero deal — paid for partly by breaking a buyback promise, from a team that has shown it will do exactly that under deal pressure.
- **What is missing:** what the pay plan rewards. The document to answer it (the FY2026 proxy) has already been located but was never read for compensation — the single largest open score cap in this module.
- **One thing to watch next:** whether leverage stays under the stated under-2x target through the Delivery Hero close (H2 2027) and whether the buyback actually resumes on the promised "months, not quarters" timeline.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Operating income:** profit from the core business, before interest and tax.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** a company-defined profit measure that adds back certain costs; here about a quarter of the add-back is never itemized.
- **Adjusted EPS:** adjusted earnings per share.
- **Return on capital (incremental ROIC):** profit earned on each new dollar invested — here 25–35%.
- **Net debt:** total debt minus cash; Uber's jumped from $76mm to $9,340mm in one trailing-year window.
- **Buyback:** company repurchasing its own shares; $4bn of planned buybacks was redirected to the Delivery Hero stake.
- **Bridge facility:** a large short-term loan used to fund a deal until permanent financing is arranged — roughly €14bn here.
- **Proxy (DEF 14A) / CD&A / Summary Compensation Table:** the annual shareholder filing and its pay sections; the pay sections were never read for this module.
- **Related-party transaction:** a deal between the company and its own insiders; zero disclosed above $120,000.
- **Say-on-Pay:** the shareholder advisory vote on executive pay; averaged 91% support over five years.
