## What This Module Found

Uber's forward calendar has real, evidenced events, not just a story — but only one clears a strict "proven, filing-sourced date" bar: a small $2,850 million cluster of debt (borrowings the company must repay) maturing in 2028. That cluster is 19.3% of total debt, but it is small and over a year out, so on its own it is not market-moving (§2).

The nearest event that actually matters is the **FQ3 2026 earnings print** — the quarterly results release. Management guided to Adjusted EBITDA (a measure of operating profit before interest, tax, and non-cash charges) of $2,860–$2,960 million and Adjusted EPS (adjusted profit per share) of $0.84–$0.88, issued 2026-08-05, after two straight quarters of beating on both. The numbers are proven from the filing pool; the calendar date (about 2026-10-29) is only an unverified web estimate (§1A, §2 row 1). This is the most credible near-term event that could lift conviction — but the earnings module calls it a genuinely two-sided setup, not lopsided upside (§4).

The **single most important catalyst** is the $14.8 billion Delivery Hero acquisition — signed 2026-07-16, expected to close in H2 2027, equal to 10.6% of Uber's own $139.3 billion market cap, financed by a committed roughly €14 billion bridge facility (short-term loan bridging to permanent financing) (§1).

The **single biggest risk** is that same deal: slipping past H2 2027, an antitrust block or delay on the required $1.6 billion sale of overlapping businesses to SSW Partners, leverage (debt load) overshooting, or a €700 million break-fee event. This is compounded by an **unidentified $1,997 million short-term debt** that appeared on the balance sheet — with no disclosed instrument, lender, or true maturity — the same period Uber spent about $4 billion buying Delivery Hero stock in the open market (§1, §1A).

Disconfirming evidence already visible: net debt (total debt minus cash) has already jumped from $76 million (FY2025) to $9,340 million (LTM Jun-2026) — before the €14 billion bridge has even been drawn — so the balance-sheet cost of this catalyst is showing up ahead of any of its claimed benefits (§1A).

## The Specialists, Briefly

This module's calendar draws on five upstream modules, all of which ran in full (§3). The load-bearing findings it carried:

- **Earnings** → FQ3 2026 guidance ($2,860–$2,960mm Adjusted EBITDA, $0.84–$0.88 Adjusted EPS) is specific and follows two straight beats, but Revenue has missed each of the last two quarters and the insurance-cost tailwind is fully reinvested with no disclosed buffer [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md`, `05_beat-miss-setup.md`].
- **Balance-sheet-survival** → the $1,997mm short-term debt has no itemized terms; the 2028 cluster ($2,850mm) is the first real maturity wall; refinancing the plain-vanilla portion today would cost roughly +94bps (0.94 percentage points) over the current 4.67% coupon [`balance-sheet-survival/02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md`].
- **Management-governance** → Delivery Hero is a §24 serial-acquirer event (RF-CAP-004, Critical); the ~50%-of-FCF buyback framework was overridden with no stated resumption date when $4bn was redirected to the deal [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md`].
- **Business-model** → driver-classification and regulatory risk is management's own top-named external risk; a UK model change already cut ~400bps of Mobility take rate this year; AV/robotaxi business quality scored 35/100 [`business-model/10_external-dependency.md`, `07_business-quality.md`].
- **Valuation** → contributed to the market-cap and financing context ($139.3bn market cap) used to size the deal.

**Most important disagreement in emphasis:** the Delivery Hero deal is the largest single item by size, which invites reading it as "the" re-rating catalyst — but the synthesis resolves this the other way: it is a §24 Filter 4 event with a bounded (not proven) H2 2027 window and must be sized as a risk, not treated as bullish (§1A). ## What Would Change This Read

The observable events the synthesis named that would move the module's read (§2):

- **FQ3 2026 earnings.** Up: Adjusted EBITDA clears the $2,960mm high end for a third straight quarter and Revenue stabilizes above the $14,697.4mm consensus. Down: Revenue misses a third straight quarter and/or the insurance-cost tailwind reverses, pulling Adjusted EBITDA below the $2,860mm floor.
- **Delivery Hero close.** Up: deal closes on schedule, the antitrust divestiture completes cleanly, synergy targets begin showing in guidance. Down: deal slips past H2 2027, a regulator blocks/delays the $1.6bn SSW Partners divestiture, leverage overshoots, or a €700mm/€200mm break-fee triggers.
- **The $1,997mm short-term debt.** Up: it rolls over or repays routinely against $4,870mm cash plus a $4,668mm undrawn revolver (over 4x coverage). Down: it carries unfavorable terms or a change-of-control trigger, or coincides with a stressed draw on the Delivery Hero bridge.
- **2028 debt cluster.** Up: refinanced at a modest step-up given the BBB+ rating, $10.1bn trailing FCF (free cash flow — operating cash after capital spending), and $4.9bn cash. Down: rates or Uber's own credit spread widen further, pushing refinancing cost well above the +94bps estimate.
- **Regulatory / AGM / buyback.** A driver-reclassification ruling in a major market, a drop in Say-on-Pay support (has run ~85–91% for five years), or buybacks staying paused well past the deal close would each be negative signals.

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict: catalysts exist but timing is vague.** Real, sourced calendar; almost none of the events that matter carry a hard date (timing visibility 38/100).
- **Could be better than it looks:** FQ3 2026 guidance is specific and follows two straight beats on Adjusted EBITDA and EPS — the most credible near-term event, if the numbers hold.
- **Could be worse than it looks:** the Delivery Hero deal front-loads balance-sheet cost (net debt already $76mm → $9,340mm before the bridge is drawn), and the $1,997mm short-term debt has no disclosed terms.
- **What's missing:** the exact FQ3 2026 results date is not filing-confirmed — the pool has the guidance numbers but not an exchange-filed reporting date, forcing reliance on an unverified web estimate.
- **§24 flag:** the biggest number on the calendar (Delivery Hero, $14.8bn) is a serial-acquirer red flag (RF-CAP-004, Critical) — a risk to size, not a reason to raise conviction. Catalyst strength is capped at 50.
- **One thing to watch next:** the FQ3 2026 earnings print (around late October 2026) — a genuinely two-sided setup, not lopsided upside.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Adjusted EBITDA** — a measure of operating profit before interest, tax, and non-cash charges, as management defines it.
- **Adjusted EPS** — adjusted profit per share.
- **Net debt** — total debt minus cash.
- **Bridge facility** — a short-term loan that bridges to permanent financing.
- **Free cash flow (FCF)** — operating cash left after capital spending.
- **Basis points (bps)** — hundredths of a percentage point; +94bps means +0.94 percentage points.
- **Take rate** — the share of each transaction Uber keeps as revenue.
- **§24 Filter 4 (serial acquirer)** — the firm's own rule that a pattern of frequent, debt-funded acquisitions is a risk to size, not a reason to be more bullish.
