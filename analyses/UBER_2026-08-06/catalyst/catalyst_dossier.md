## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

On the strict "proven, filing-sourced date" test, the nearest dated catalyst is over a year away: the **2028-05-15 maturity of the $1,125mm 2028 Exchangeable Senior Notes**, part of the $2,850mm FY2028 cluster — small on its own relative to Uber's $10.1bn trailing free cash flow and $4.9bn cash, so it is not a market-moving event by itself. The nearest catalyst that actually matters for the stock is the **FQ3 2026 earnings release**, whose reporting period and guidance ($2,860mm–$2,960mm Adjusted EBITDA, $0.84–$0.88 Adjusted EPS) are proven from the pool, but whose exact calendar date (2026-10-29) is only web-sourced and unverified. Two-sided: a third straight Adjusted-EBITDA beat above the guidance high end would extend a real, evidenced pattern; a Revenue miss for a third straight quarter, or a reversal of the currently fully-reinvested insurance-cost tailwind, would flip the setup from "balanced" to "inflecting negative" [`earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md` §7–§8].

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

The **Delivery Hero SE acquisition** (signed 2026-07-16, expected close H2 2027) is the single most important catalyst on this calendar, by size alone: at $14.8bn it is 10.6% of Uber's own market cap, financed by a committed ~€14bn bridge facility roughly the size of Uber's entire current debt stack, and it has already pushed net debt from $76mm (FY2025) to $9,340mm (LTM Jun-2026) before the bridge is even drawn [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md` §2, Finding 02-014]. The bullish path: the deal closes on schedule, the $1.2bn run-rate synergy target (web-sourced, not yet underwritten) begins showing up in guidance, and gross leverage stays near the web-sourced <2x target. The bearish path: closing slips, the required $1.6bn SSW Partners divestiture stalls on antitrust grounds, leverage overshoots, or integration drags on margins just as the market is testing whether Uber's own organic margin gains are durable. A buyer can time the *window* (H2 2027) but not an exact date — and per CLAUDE.md §24 Filter 4, this deal is a serial-acquirer pattern (7+ bolt-ons in ~18 months culminating here) that caps conviction rather than lifting it; RF-CAP-004 (Critical) already caps the capital-allocation score at 50/100 [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md` §2, §4]. This is the catalyst most likely to move the stock, not the catalyst that should raise confidence in the thesis.

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **Delivery Hero deal risk (H2 2027 window, §24 Filter 4-flagged).** Slippage past H2 2027, an antitrust block or delay on the $1.6bn SSW Partners divestiture, gross leverage overshooting the <2x target, or a €700mm termination-fee event if Uber itself walks [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md` §2].
- **The unidentified $1,997mm short-term debt (~by Jun-2027).** Instrument, lender, and true terms are undisclosed anywhere in the pool; if it behaves as a stressed facility rather than ordinary short-term financing, it compounds with the Delivery Hero bridge draw [`balance-sheet-survival/02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md` §1, §4].
- **FY2028 debt maturity cluster ($2,850mm, proven dates 2028-05-15 and 2028-12-01).** A real, if not crippling, refinancing cost step-up (~+94bps like-for-like) that widens further if Delivery Hero leverage pressures Uber's credit spread [`balance-sheet-survival/02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md` §3, §5].
- **Buyback-framework pause (undated).** The ~50%-of-FCF capital-return promise was overridden without a stated resumption date once $4bn was redirected to the Delivery Hero stake — a standing capital-return risk for as long as it stays paused [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md` §3].
- **Driver/worker-classification and insurance-regulation risk (undated, ongoing).** Management's own top-named external risk; the UK reclassification already cut ~400bps of Mobility's take rate this year, and the currently-reinvested insurance-cost tailwind carries no disclosed buffer against reversal [`business-model/10_external-dependency.md` §1, §5].

Buyers should not treat any of these as background noise: the balance-sheet and M&A items in particular sit on top of a net-debt position that has already moved from $76mm to $9,340mm in two quarters, before the largest single draw (the Delivery Hero bridge) has even happened.

## 5. Calendar Read

Only one item on this calendar clears the strict "proven, filing-sourced hard date" bar — the FY2028 debt maturity cluster — and it is small and distant enough not to matter much on its own; every other catalyst, including the two that matter most (the next earnings print and the Delivery Hero close), carries either a web-sourced/unverified date, a bounded window, or an inference, not a hard proven date. The near-term calendar skews toward event risk rather than clean upside: the nearest real event (FQ3 2026 earnings) is a genuinely two-sided, "balanced" setup per the earnings module, not a lopsided positive, and the single largest item (Delivery Hero) is a §24-flagged serial-acquirer catalyst that caps conviction rather than lifting it. The single most important timing caveat is that this calendar's biggest number — the ~€14bn Delivery Hero bridge facility — has not been drawn yet, so none of the balance-sheet or capital-allocation catalysts above have priced in what happens once it is.
