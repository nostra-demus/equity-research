# Catalyst Module — UBER (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Uber's forward calendar has real, evidenced events, not just a story, but only one clears a strict proven-date bar: a small $2,850 million debt cluster maturing in 2028. What actually matters sits on a window, not a hard date. The nearest event able to move the stock is the FQ3 2026 earnings print, guided 2026-08-05, though its date is only an unverified web estimate, testing an insurance-cost tailwind with no disclosed buffer. The most important catalyst is the $14.8 billion Delivery Hero acquisition, expected H2 2027, but it is a serial-acquirer deal flagged under Section 24 that should cap conviction, not lift it. The biggest bearish catalyst is that deal slipping or an antitrust block. Verdict: catalysts exist, timing is vague.

It is the calendar's largest and most important item and must not be read as conviction-lifting.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** The Delivery Hero deal is the single largest item on the calendar and carries real slippage/antitrust/leverage risk, compounded by an unidentified $1,997mm short-term debt addition (appeared on the balance sheet the same period as ~$4bn of open-market Delivery Hero stake purchases) with no disclosed instrument, lender, or true maturity date [`balance-sheet-survival/02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md` §1; `catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md` row 4].
- **Strongest bull point:** FQ3 2026 guidance itself is evidenced and specific — Adjusted EBITDA $2,860–$2,960mm and Adjusted EPS $0.84–$0.88, issued 2026-08-05, following two straight quarters of Adjusted EBITDA and EPS beats [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md` §2]. This is the most credible near-term conviction-lifting event on the calendar, but note its calendar date (2026-10-29) is only a web-sourced estimate, not a filing-proven date — the evidence for the NUMBERS is strong; the evidence for the DATE is not.
- **Single killer risk specific to the calendar:** The Delivery Hero acquisition is being sized by size alone as "the" catalyst that could re-rate the stock, but it is a §24 Filter 4 serial-acquirer event (RF-CAP-004, Critical) with a bounded, not proven, closing window (H2 2027) — treating it as bullish rather than as a risk-to-size would misread this module's own evidence [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md` §2, §4].
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** Net debt has already moved from $76mm (FY2025) to $9,340mm (LTM Jun-2026) — before the ~€14bn Delivery Hero bridge facility has even been drawn — showing the balance-sheet cost of this catalyst is front-loading ahead of any of its claimed benefit (synergies, footprint doubling) showing up anywhere in the pool [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md` §2, Finding 02-014].

## 5. Simple Summary

- There are real, dated events on the calendar, not just a story — but almost none of the ones that matter have a hard, proven date.
- The nearest dated event: the FQ3 2026 earnings print, guided 2026-08-05, expected around late October 2026 (date itself unverified).
- The most important catalyst: the $14.8 billion Delivery Hero acquisition, expected to close in the second half of 2027.
- The biggest thing that could go wrong: that same Delivery Hero deal slipping, hitting an antitrust block, or overshooting on leverage — plus an unexplained $1,997 million short-term debt addition with no disclosed terms.
- This deal is also a red flag under the firm's own serial-acquirer rule (§24) — it should not be read as a reason to get more bullish, even though it is the biggest number on the calendar.
- The thesis cannot lean heavily on timing right now: only a small, distant 2028 debt maturity is a truly proven date; everything bigger is a window, not a date.
- This module is useful to the final synthesizer as a real, sourced calendar and a caution flag on the Delivery Hero deal, but it cannot supply hard-date conviction the way a proven near-term catalyst would.
