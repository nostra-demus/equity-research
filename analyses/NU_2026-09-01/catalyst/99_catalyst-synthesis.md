# Catalyst Module — NU (Synthesis)

## Abstract

NU has dated, evidenced events rather than only a thematic story, though balance-sheet survival stopped at applicability triage. [analyses/NU_2026-09-01/balance-sheet-survival/00_solvency-data-triage.md, §6] The nearest dated event is Capital IQ's issuer-unconfirmed 12 November 2026 FQ3 release, testing US$5,936.74m revenue, US$0.22164 GAAP EPS, and credit quality. FQ3 matters most: a beat with stable credit quality supports growth; a miss or weaker credit costs works against it. It is also the biggest bearish event because its bars require 42.3% revenue and 39.0% EPS growth from Q3 2025. [analyses/NU_2026-09-01/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1–3] The verdict is Dated, evidenced near-term catalysts, with mixed timing; 1 January 2027 is the nearest proven date. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1.1, p. 12]

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** The vendor-scheduled 12 November 2026 FQ3 print has a high bar: 42.3% standalone revenue growth and 39.0% GAAP-EPS growth from Q3 2025; a miss or worsening credit cost or net interest margin would skew down. [data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §§1, 3]
- **Strongest bull point:** The filing-set 1 January 2027 tax start can remove a known uncertainty if disclosure shows a limited net effect or proven pricing, mix, or cost offsets. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1.1, p. 12]
- **Single killer risk:** The market may treat the main event as firmly scheduled and a numerical beat as durable even though the issuer has not confirmed the date, management has no numeric revenue or EPS guide, and temporary credit-cost relief could flatter the print. [data/NU/Capital IQ Events Calendar, 2026; data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026; data/NU/Q2 2026 earnings call, Q&A, 2026-08-13, pp. 8–11]
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** The pool has no future AGM, dated operational launch, market-structure event, or bounded regulator-decision window; even the FQ3 date is a vendor field that may change. [analyses/NU_2026-09-01/catalyst/00_catalyst-data-triage.md, §§1, 3]
