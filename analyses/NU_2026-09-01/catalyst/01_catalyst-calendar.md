## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

The earliest calendar entry is the 12 November 2026 FQ3 results date, but it is not a proven issuer-set date. The nearest proven date is 1 January 2027, when the Brazilian IBS/CBS financial-services tax regime starts. A limited disclosed effect would remove one uncertainty; a disclosed hit to earnings or returns would hurt the case. Nu has not yet quantified the outcome. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1.1, p. 12]

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

FQ3 2026 results are the most important observable event. The standalone revenue and GAAP-EPS bars imply 42.3% and 39.0% growth versus Q3 2025 after the revenue estimate rose 7.0% over 90 days, while management gives no numeric revenue or EPS guide. A clean beat with durable credit quality would support the growth case; a miss or a beat driven by temporary credit-cost relief would undermine it. A buyer can plan around the vendor-dated 12 November calendar entry, but cannot treat its timing as confirmed. Banco Porto Real is not a timed alternative: Nu has agreed to acquire 100% subject to Brazilian Central Bank approval, but no decision date or bounded approval window, consideration, funding, integration cost, or return is disclosed. It is not a proven serial-acquirer case and cannot lift conviction as an M&A catalyst. [data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus and Trends, FQ3 2026; data/NU/Capital IQ Events Calendar, 2026; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §§1–5; data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 35(b), p. 43; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md, §2]

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **October 2026 election window:** an adverse policy or market reaction could worsen Brazil's credit, funding, or regulatory outlook. Brazil is the dominant disclosed geographic revenue source. [data/NU/FY2025 Form 20-F, Item 3.D, pp. 131–132; Note 34, p. F-97]

- **12 November 2026 FQ3 results (vendor-dated):** the raised bar requires 42.3% standalone revenue growth and 39.0% GAAP-EPS growth versus Q3 2025. A miss, higher expected credit loss, or weaker risk-adjusted-NIM outlook would be adverse. [data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §§1, 3]

- **1 January 2027 tax-regime start:** the company says the effect is under assessment, so an adverse tax or pricing impact is a live risk rather than a quantified forecast. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1.1, p. 12]

- **1 June 2027 facility maturity:** a maturity is not itself a maturity wall or distress call for this financial institution, but a negative funding, liquidity, or capital signal around it would matter. [data/NU/ciq_facts.json, `debt_maturity_wall`; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/balance-sheet-survival/00_solvency-data-triage.md, §6]

- **3 June 2027 buyback expiry:** the authorization can end without a full execution or a lasting per-share benefit, because repurchases are discretionary and share-based issuance continues. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 31(e), p. 39]

The Banco Porto Real approval and Nu's U.S. bank-charter final approval remain potential negative or positive events but have no disclosed decision date or bounded window. They are not timed catalysts under CLAUDE.md §17. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 35, p. 43]

## 5. Calendar Read

Three of five calendar entries have hard dates: the statutory tax start, the vendor-dated facility maturity, and the buyback expiry. The October 2026 election window and 12 November 2026 FQ3 date are less reliable for timing; the latter is a vendor calendar field that the issuer has not confirmed. The October–November events skew more downside because Brazil concentration meets a high, recently raised FQ3 revenue bar; the biggest timing gap is the absence of any disclosed timetable for Banco Porto Real or final U.S. bank approval.
