## What This Module Found

NU has real scheduled events rather than only a thematic story, and three of the five calendar entries have hard dates. [analyses/NU_2026-09-01/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §5] The nearest scheduled event is FQ3 results on 12 November 2026, but that date comes from Capital IQ and has not been confirmed by NU. [data/NU/Capital IQ Events Calendar, 2026] FQ3 is the most important catalyst because it tests US$5,936.74m of revenue and US$0.22164 of GAAP EPS, or reported earnings per share under the stated accounting rules. Those bars require 42.3% revenue growth and 39.0% EPS growth from Q3 2025 on the same standalone-quarter basis. [data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §§1, 3]

A beat helps only if credit costs, meaning loan-loss charges, and risk-adjusted NIM, the lending spread after funding costs and credit losses, show that the improvement can last. [analyses/NU_2026-09-01/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §3] The biggest risk is a miss or worse credit-cost, delinquency, or NIM commentary against a high bar. [analyses/NU_2026-09-01/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §4] The nearest proven date is 1 January 2027, when Brazil's new financial-services tax regime begins, but NU says the financial effect is still under assessment. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1.1, p. 12] Taken together, the October election, high FQ3 bar, and unquantified January tax effect make the October–January cluster skew bearish. [data/NU/FY2025 Form 20-F, Item 3.D, pp. 131–132; Note 34, p. F-97; data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026; data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1.1, p. 12]

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** Dated, evidenced near-term catalysts.
- **Why it could be better:** A filing-set tax date can remove a known uncertainty if NU shows a limited net effect or proven pricing, mix, or cost offsets. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1.1, p. 12]
- **Why it could be worse:** FQ3 faces 42.3% revenue-growth and 39.0% GAAP-EPS-growth bars, and a beat may not last if credit quality weakens. [data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §§1, 3]
- **What is missing:** NU has not confirmed the FQ3 release date, and the pool has no bounded approval windows for Banco Porto Real or the U.S. bank charter. [analyses/NU_2026-09-01/catalyst/00_catalyst-data-triage.md, §§1, 3; analyses/NU_2026-09-01/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§3–5]
- **What to watch next:** The provisional 12 November 2026 FQ3 result, especially whether revenue and EPS clear their bars without worse credit costs, delinquency, or NIM. [data/NU/Capital IQ Events Calendar, 2026; data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026]

## Plain-English Glossary

- **GAAP EPS:** Reported earnings divided by each share under the stated accounting rules.
- **Credit costs:** Loan losses and the provisions recorded for expected loan losses.
- **NIM:** Net interest margin, or the lending spread after funding costs.
- **Risk-adjusted NIM:** That lending spread after also allowing for credit losses.
- **Fully diluted share count:** Shares outstanding after including potential dilution.
