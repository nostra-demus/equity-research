# Valuation Module — NU (Synthesis)

## Abstract

NU is materially overvalued: the US$8.47 base fair value is 40.8% below the pool-verified US$14.30 price. The 12-month bull/base/bear levels are US$11.10/US$8.47/US$2.95, with the base driven by a 60% equity-direct residual-income and 40% peer P/E blend. [Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1–3] The reverse read is not model-consistent; conditional on a CGU-specific 16.51% rate, price requires 47.36% terminal ROE or 80.89% FY2027–FY2030 profit growth, neither proven. [Reverse DCF — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1–2A; Intrinsic DCF — NU, 2026-09-01, §§3 and 6] There is no margin of safety: price is 68.8% above base value, while bear-case downside is 79.4%. Verdict: Materially overvalued. [Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §§4–6]

- **Bull / Base / Bear fair-value levels (points):** **US$11.10 / US$8.47 / US$2.95**, each on a 12-month horizon. [Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §3]

- **Valuation confidence /100:** **45** — synthesis judgment: two valid methods exist, but one is Medium-low confidence, the other is Low confidence, and the reverse model does not match the intrinsic model it claims to invert. [Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1–2]

- **Biggest valuation risk:** The duration and required return on NU’s excess profits: sustained through-cycle ROE well above the Group cost of equity could make US$8.47 too low, while a credit-cost reversal would push value toward the bear case. [Intrinsic DCF — NU, 2026-09-01, §§2–8; Earnings Module — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1A and 5]

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** US$14.30 is above the US$11.10 bull level, while NU’s observed 14.76x NTM P/E and 5.7x P/TBV carry 94.0% and 375.0% premiums to the named-bank medians; available moat and credit evidence does not prove that full premium durable. [Relative Valuation — Peers — NU, 2026-09-01, §§3–7; Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §§3–5]
- **Strongest bull point:** NU’s NTM ROTE proxy is 39.4% versus the 18.6% peer median, and vendor-basis LTM revenue growth is 44.3% versus 6.6%; those figures support some premium rather than raw peer parity. [Relative Valuation — Peers — NU, 2026-09-01, §§2 and 4]
- **Single killer risk:** The residual-income model’s 12.16% Group cost of equity and 15-year finite fade are not scope-matched company disclosures. If high residual returns last materially longer, the dominant 60%-weighted method understates value. [Intrinsic DCF — NU, 2026-09-01, §§3–7; Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §1]
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** Q2 revenue and diluted EPS rose 50.3% and 66.3%, risk-adjusted NIM rose to 12.4%, and both disclosed delinquency measures improved sequentially. That evidence argues against an immediate credit collapse, but it does not establish through-cycle returns. [Earnings Module — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1A and 3–5; Business Model Reality Check — NU, 2026-09-01, §1A]

## 3. Reconciliation

The valid value-producing methods do not closely agree. Peer valuation gives **US$9.70**, while equity-direct residual income gives **US$7.65**; the **US$7.65–US$9.70** field spans 26.7%. The reconciled **US$8.47** base is 60% × US$7.653661 + 40% × US$9.70. Residual income receives the larger weight because it is the type-appropriate intrinsic method for a financial company, but its Low confidence prevents it from standing alone. [Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1–2]

The reverse model is excluded from the fair-value field. It does not hold the intrinsic model’s rate and value fixed: it substitutes the 16.51% Brazil investment-activities CGU comparator for the intrinsic report’s 12.16% Group cost-of-equity build and describes the intrinsic point as US$5.55 rather than US$7.65. Its implied terminal ROE, profit-growth and cost-of-equity results are therefore conditional sensitivities, not a valid “what is priced in” inversion. [Reverse DCF — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1–2A; Intrinsic DCF — NU, 2026-09-01, §§3, 6 and 8]

Own-history reversion receives no value and no weight because seven observations over 17 months do not establish a three-to-five-year anchor. The US$7.38 collapsed SOTP check also receives no weight because one Banking segment makes it the same peer-P/E lens counted again. [Multiples — Own History — NU, 2026-09-01, §§2 and 4; Sum-of-the-Parts — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1–5]

**Sector Cycle Reality Test:** Both own-history and peer specialists state **“Not assessable — no sector-level multiple history.”** Neither fires RF-VAL-001 nor RF-VAL-002. There is therefore no same-direction cycle flag and no compounding cap, but the absence means the short NU range and current peer median are not proven stable anchors. [Multiples — Own History — NU, 2026-09-01, §5; Relative Valuation — Peers — NU, 2026-09-01, §6; Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §2]

## 5. Fair-Value Summary

The 12-month fair-value levels are **US$11.10 bull, US$8.47 base and US$2.95 bear**; the base is driven by a 60% equity-direct residual-income value and 40% growth-adjusted peer P/E value. [Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1–3] The US$14.30 price is above every case, but the supplied reverse model cannot cleanly say what the price implies; only its conditional 16.51%-rate sensitivity asks for 47.36% terminal ROE or 80.89% FY2027–FY2030 profit growth, neither proven by the earnings record. [Reverse DCF — NU, 2026-09-01, §§2–3] Margin of safety is **negative 68.8%**, because price exceeds base value, while downside to bear is a separate **79.4%** loss to US$2.95. [Scenario & Fair Value — NU, 2026-09-01, §4] The low-end own-history position is not a value signal: NU sits at the low end of only a 17-month series, while business quality is 52/100, the moat is narrow with direction not assessable, and credit/regulatory dependence remains material. [Multiples — Own History — NU, 2026-09-01, §§2–6; Business Model Reality Check — NU, 2026-09-01, §§1 and 3] RF-OWN-004 does not apply, though the founder’s 74.4% voting power from an 18.6% economic stake limits Class A holders’ ability to force a higher multiple. [Ownership & Insider Behavior — NU, 2026-08-30, §§1 and 4]
