## 2. Aggregate Quality Score

**52 /100 — Mixed/Average.** The explicit weighting is 55% to bank earnings and risk resilience (margin stability 15%, capital intensity 10%, regulatory dependence 15%, rate dependence 5%, cyclicality 10%), 25% to customer economics (pricing power 8%, recurring revenue 9%, stickiness 8%), competitive intensity 10%, industry rate-of-change 5%, and disclosure 5%. That weighting reconstructs to roughly 52 and puts the constraints on a deposit-funded lender ahead of its customer-growth measures.

## 4. Read

Nu is a regulated, deposit-funded digital retail bank/lender rather than a subscription-software business; its quality is therefore more credit-, rate- and capital-cycle dependent than its customer-growth figures alone suggest. [FY2025 Form 20-F, Item 5.A, pp.157–158] The single factor to watch over the next 24 months is risk-adjusted NIM — net interest income after credit cost relative to interest-earning assets — alongside NPLs and coverage: its Q2 2026 12.4% level was above the 9.3%–10.8% range in Q1–Q3 2025, so it should not be treated as a through-cycle margin. [Q2’26 earnings presentation, slides 15, 17–19] The Q2 NIM of 22.9% was also above the 18.8%–21.1% range in Q2 2025–Q1 2026; it is a current result, not evidence that credit-cycle risk has disappeared.
