## 2. Dominant Segment

Nu is effectively a single-segment business: its CEO, the chief operating decision maker, allocates capital and reviews performance for the whole Group on a consolidated basis, so the Group represents 100% of disclosed revenue and profit. [^1] Brazil is the economic concentration, but not a separately reported profit segment: the geographical disclosure records US$11.038bn (91%) of its defined revenue in Brazil, US$0.808bn (7%) in Mexico and US$0.237bn (2%) in other countries, with no country profit disclosed. [^5] Those geographical figures are not a substitute for reportable-segment shares because the table's US$12.084bn revenue total has a narrower product scope than consolidated IFRS total revenue of US$15.775bn. [^1][^5]

## 3. Segment Disclosure Quality

The single-segment definition is consistent across the FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 20-F filings; there is no reportable “Other” or corporate segment, and no product- or geography-level profit or EBIT measure. [^6] The current filing does disclose product categories in Note 6 and geography in Note 34, but the latter reports only defined revenue and non-current assets, not country profit; its “Other countries” line is 2% of the geographic revenue subtotal, not an “Other” business segment. [^2][^5] Management’s Q2 2026 commentary continues to describe Brazil, Mexico and Colombia as three core markets, but it does not create reportable country segments or provide country profit. [^7] Capital IQ’s latest annual business-segment export labels the sole business line “Banking” at US$6.991bn (100%); it reconciles exactly to the audited US$15.775bn total revenue less US$4.579bn interest and other financial expenses and US$4.205bn expected credit loss: US$15.775bn − US$4.579bn − US$4.205bn = US$6.991bn. This is a vendor presentation of the one consolidated business, not evidence of a second segment. Its geographic revenue subtotal of US$12.084bn (Brazil US$11.038bn, Mexico US$0.808bn, other countries US$0.237bn) matches the filing’s geographic table; it has no geographic profit measure.
