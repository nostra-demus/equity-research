Parentheses in the net-debt row denote net cash. Strict cash basis means filing cash and cash equivalents less the debt scope stated below; it does not net securities or other liquid investments. ## 6. Key Trend Summary

Revenue growth remains positive but is decelerating on the annual base: 182.2% in FY2022, 67.5% in FY2023, 43.4% in FY2024 and 37.0% in FY2025. [1][2][8] Gross margin is volatile: it recovered from 34.7% in FY2022 to 45.6% in FY2024, then fell 361bp to 42.0% in FY2025; the latest quarter improved 37bp year on year to 42.6%, after Q1 2026's 37.5%. [1][4][8] Reported net income and diluted EPS changed from losses in FY2021–22 to US$2.869bn and US$0.5846 in FY2025, but operating cash flow is the counter-series: it was US$(1.242)bn in H1 2026 and the filing-built TTM was US$(1.382)bn despite rising income. [1][2][4][8] Material quarterly seasonality is not proven from the available record.
