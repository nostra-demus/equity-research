# Earnings Module — NU (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Nu’s setup is mixed: Q2 revenue and earnings per share (EPS) accelerated, but annual growth slowed and FQ3 favors a miss. [H1 FY26 Interim Statements, p.5]; [FY2025 Form 20-F, F-6–F-7] Card and loan interest supplied 71.6% of Q2 revenue growth as the portfolio grew 37% FX-neutral. [H1 FY26 Interim Statements, Note 6]; [Q2 FY26 deck, slide 13] FQ3 requires 42.3% revenue growth after its estimate rose 7.0% in 90 days, without numeric guidance. [Capital IQ Estimates, FQ3 FY26, current export] The risk is risk expansion, which added 24 basis points (0.24 percentage points) to early delinquency and US$170m to allowances and could reverse the margin gain. [Q2 FY26 transcript, pp.7–8] A vendor-versus-filing cash-flow conflict caps usefulness. Verdict: Mixed earnings setup.

## 3. Reconciliation

- **Quarterly acceleration versus annual deceleration:** Historical financials show Q2 revenue and EPS growth of 50.3% and 66.3%, but annual revenue growth slowed from 182.2% in FY2022 to 37.0% in FY2025. The red-flag review therefore rejects a durable acceleration claim. The reconciled view is a strong latest quarter inside a still-unproven longer-term reacceleration, which supports **Mixed earnings setup**. [Historical Financials — NU, §§1, 3 and 6, 2026-08-30]; [Earnings Red Flags — NU, §2.10, 2026-08-30]
- **Sequential margin gain versus adverse counter-series:** Company-defined risk-adjusted NIM rose 294 basis points quarter on quarter, but the implied credit-cost rate was 10.5% versus 8.9% a year earlier. The management efficiency ratio improved to 19.5% from 21.3%, while the different-basis IFRS operating-expense/revenue ratio worsened to 20.1% from 18.2%. These measures are not interchangeable. The reconciled view is a real sequential spread gain with neither credit-cost nor operating-cost durability proven. [Q2 2026 Earnings Presentation, slides 15–16 and 22]; [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, p.5]
- **Cash-flow source conflict:** The filing-built trailing cash from operating activities (CFO) is US$(1.382)bn, while Capital IQ reports US$(10.305)bn, an unresolved US$8.923bn gap. The reviewed interim and annual filings govern; the vendor figure is not substituted, and the unresolved conflict caps overall usefulness at 65. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, Statement of Cash Flows, p.12]; [Nu Holdings Ltd., Form 20-F, FY2025, F-14–F-15]; [Capital IQ Financials→Cash Flow, LTM ended 30 June 2026 — vendor export]

## 5. Earnings Setup Summary

### Revenue Setup

The next 12 months still have support from a larger credit book and higher revenue per active customer, rather than a disclosed acquisition or divestiture contribution, but Q2's 50.3% reported pace is not proven as a normal run rate. Management says originations are normalising, and Q4 rather than Q3 carries the identified purchase-volume seasonality. [Q2 2026 Earnings Call, Q&A, p.9] The single factor that would flip revenue direction is weaker conversion of credit-card and loan balances into interest income, whether from slower book growth, lower realization or weaker Brazilian consumer credit. Reported IFRS revenue growth of 50.29% differs by 11.29 percentage points from management's 39% FX-neutral gross-revenue growth, but the numerator definitions differ; the gap cannot be called organic growth or FX. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, Statement of Income, p.5]; [Q2 2026 Earnings Presentation, slide 21]

### Margin Setup

Q2's 12.4% company-defined risk-adjusted NIM is an expansion-period result, not a proven cycle peak, floor or normalized margin; management itself said the level was not a floor. [Q2 2026 Earnings Call, Q&A, pp.10–11] Product and country profit are absent, and the filing supplies no earnings coefficient for a 10–20% adverse driver move, so such a stress cannot be quantified without invention. The largest disclosed adverse margin variable is credit cost: a 100% downside macro weighting raises ECL by US$504.700m before tax, while the same risk expansion that helps yield has already raised early delinquency. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, Note 3(a), p.15]; [Q2 2026 Earnings Presentation, slide 18] Nu has no disclosed automatic cost pass-through or measured realized recovery rate; it can change loan and deposit offers, but that is not proof of broad customer pass-through. [Nu Holdings Ltd., Form 20-F, FY2025, Item 3.D, pp.111–112 and 136]

### Quality Check

The largest current gap between reported and economic earnings is the US$991.0m deferred-tax benefit, equal to 51.3% of H1 net income; it does not prove improper accounting, but it makes the period less cash-backed. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, Statement of Income, p.5; Note 30, pp.36–37] Management's adjusted net income also adds back recurring share-based compensation: US$359.0m in FY2025, after US$408.2m in FY2024 and US$289.3m in FY2023. Normalized next-year earnings should therefore start from reported IFRS income and adjust explicit tax timing and credit performance, not start from management's adjusted figure. [Nu Holdings Ltd., Form 20-F, FY2025, Item 5, “Adjusted Net Income (Loss)” reconciliation]

### Consensus Bar

A material beat requires the existing credit book to keep converting into interest income while credit cost and operating spending stay controlled enough to exceed US$6.11484bn of revenue and US$0.23272 of reported EPS, 3% and 5% above consensus. [Beat / Miss Setup — NU, §4, 2026-08-30] The bar looks more likely high than low: revenue consensus rose 7.0% in 90 days, last-month revisions were 8 up / 0 down for FY2026 EPS and 5 up / 2 down for revenue, and management provides no numeric revenue or EPS guide. [Capital IQ Estimates — Trends and Revisions, FQ3/FY2026] The export does not isolate how much of consensus assumes Q2's one-time Desenrola benefit; management said it was a minority of the 115-basis-point sequential credit-cost help, so its exact consensus effect is not proven. [Q2 2026 Earnings Call, Q&A, pp.10–12]

## 8. Note To The Final Synthesizer

- Dominant trend: Q2 accelerated, but the annual growth path still slowed; interest income from cards and loans is the main driver.
- Earnings are not cleanly cash-backed: annual cash generation improved through FY2025, but the latest filing-built trailing CFO is negative and lender cash flow is driven by lending, securities and deposits.
- Consensus bar: FQ3 is a raised hurdle with no numeric company revenue or EPS guide; the setup favors miss.
- Next-quarter and look-ahead: FQ3 favors miss; FQ4 may benefit from seasonally higher purchase volume, but no FQ4 bar was carried by the specialist output.
- Top sensitivity: macro-scenario weighting of the credit-loss allowance; current direction is mixed because sequential credit cost improved while ECL and early delinquency remained adverse.
- No missing-data cap applied. The unresolved vendor-versus-filing trailing-CFO conflict limits overall usefulness, and the filing governs.
- Biggest missing data point: country/product profit and credit-cost detail that separates Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, cards and unsecured loans.
- Change the verdict only if FQ3 clears or misses its basis-matched bars together with same-basis evidence on delinquency, ECL coverage, credit cost and efficiency.
- High flag — spread durability: the Q2 gain is exposed to later credit losses from deliberate risk expansion and unsecured mix. [Q2 2026 Earnings Call, pp.7–8]
- High flag — expectations: the FQ3 bar already requires 42.3% revenue growth after upward revisions and has no numeric management support. [Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus and Trends, FQ3 2026]
- High flag — cash and accruals: credit growth consumed cash while ECL coverage rose to 16.86%; this is not proof of fictitious income, but current cash support is not established. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, p.12; Notes 13–14]
- High flag — tax quality: the US$991.046m deferred-tax benefit represented 51.3% of H1 net income; this is not evidence of improper accounting. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, p.5; Note 30, pp.36–37]
- High flag — macro sensitivity: 100% downside macro weighting raises the allowance by US$504.700m from the weighted case; this is a pre-tax allowance bound, not an EPS forecast. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, Note 3(a), p.15]
- Red-flag severity verdict: **Material concerns**

## 9. Simple Summary

- Revenue accelerated in Q2, but annual growth has been slowing.
- Card and loan interest drove most of the latest revenue increase.
- Risk-adjusted margin improved, but higher-risk lending can raise later losses.
- Reported IFRS income is a better starting point than adjusted income because share-based compensation recurs.
- The FQ3 consensus bar is hard to beat, and the setup favors miss.
- Credit-loss assumptions are the largest disclosed earnings sensitivity.
- Earnings volatility is high, and the cash and tax signals need caution.
- The module is useful to the master synthesizer, but the unresolved vendor cash-flow conflict caps that usefulness.
