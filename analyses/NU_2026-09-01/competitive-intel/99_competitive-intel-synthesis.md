## 3. Reconciliation

No material directional disagreement exists: the triage, extraction, matrix, read-through and triangulation specialists all find no usable peer-call benchmark. Two precision points require adjudication:

1. `00` and `03` sometimes describe the net peer weight as “zero.” Under the synthesis rule, **Read-through weight is Not assessable**, because a direction that is Not assessable has neither an H/M/L weight nor a probability band. “Zero” means no contribution to the master view; it is not a weight label.
2. Brazil's US$11,038m is 91% of the US$12,084m defined geographic-revenue subtotal in the deterministic vendor sidecar, but that subtotal is not NU's US$15,775m consolidated IFRS-revenue denominator and has no country-profit measure. It therefore cannot build the coverage score. The coverage calculation instead uses the 100% single reportable Group segment and zero eligible calls.
