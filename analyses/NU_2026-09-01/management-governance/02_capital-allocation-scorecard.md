## 5. Capital-Allocation Read

**Mixed.** Organic spending coincided with diluted EPS rising 175.6% from FY2023 to FY2025, but an incremental-ROIC calculation is not supportable for this lender and the filing does not isolate the contribution of the US$793.236m capex programme. The best observed capital-return action is the H1 FY2026 US$500.393m Class A buyback: it reduced end-period shares by 24.985m, or 0.5%, after equity issuance, although the weighted diluted share count was still 0.2% higher year on year because execution started late. The weakest area is M&A evidence: Hyperplane’s price and delivery, the associate investment’s outcome, and Banco Porto Real’s consideration are not disclosed, so value creation from these uses is not proven. The single biggest signal is a real but early buyback funded from a balance sheet with US$7.745bn strict net cash; it deserves monitoring for duration, seller participation and value discipline rather than credit for an unproven long-term record.
