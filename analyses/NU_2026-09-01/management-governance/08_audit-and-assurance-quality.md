## 10. Read

**Verdict: mixed — watch the named link.** NU’s consolidated annual opinions for FY2023–FY2025 are unqualified, FY2025 ICFR is effective, H1 FY2026 received an unmodified review conclusion, and disclosed non-audit fees are only 7.8% of audit fees. The weakest link is the incumbent firm: KPMG Brazil received a PCAOB censure, US$700,000 penalty and remedial actions in 2025 for failures in other issuer audits; the order does not identify NU, but it prevents a high-trust assurance conclusion. Trust would rise with evidence that the Brazil rotation extension applies, a component-auditor / coverage schedule, named internal-audit leadership, and current private-session / attendance disclosure.
