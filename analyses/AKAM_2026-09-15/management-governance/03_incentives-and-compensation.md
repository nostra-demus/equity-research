## 4. Incentive Alignment Read

**Mixed.** The disclosed equity plan contains TSR and financial-performance awards, but the filing gives neither the CEO/CFO cash/equity mix nor their actual performance metrics and weights; executive pay cannot therefore be judged as per-share-value or size-led. The strongest disclosed negative is conditional single-trigger change-of-control equity vesting, alongside FY2025 award burn of 4.73% of FY2025 weighted-average basic shares—above the 3% Red band—although these grants are company-wide rather than CEO/CFO-only. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 18, pp.84–86; Exhibit 10.5, §6(c)–(e)]
