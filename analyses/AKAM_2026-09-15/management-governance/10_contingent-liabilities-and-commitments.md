## 11. Read

The only quantified non-lease contingency is the $111.4 tax-audit reserve, 2.2% of FY2025 net worth of $4,977.4, and it is provided for; the filing does not show serial appeal losses or a tax demand that would make that reserve a Red item. The largest known contractual exposure is instead $2,331.0 of gross lease-related payments and uncommenced commitments, 46.8% of the same-date net worth; $1,570.0 was already recognized as lease liabilities and $731.2 of future sublease income is disclosed separately, so this is material but not a $2,331.0 unprovided loss. Management is reasonably candid on lease detail, tax-reserve movements, and the unlimited nature of indemnities, but it does not quantify purchase commitments, litigation claims, bills with recourse, bank guarantees, or group-entity contingencies. A rational minority holder should watch the next annual lease schedule, the unquantified bandwidth/purchase commitments supporting cloud expansion, and any named tax or litigation matter; no court or tribunal database sweep was run because the filings do not identify the largest disputed matters, so all posture statements are filings-only.
