## 5. Capital-Allocation Read

**Mixed.** The best proven use is the H1 2026 buyback result: 4.998m shares repurchased exceeded 3.879m issuances/reissues and reduced shares outstanding by 1.120m, so the programme was not just dilution offset. [data/AKAM/Akamai Technologies, Inc., Q2 2026.pdf, Form 10-Q, Statements of Stockholders’ Equity, pp.9–12]

The worst use is the repeated acquisition programme: $2.066bn of FY2021–FY2025 cash acquisitions and $205.0m for LayerX have no disclosed return, synergy, or alternative-use comparison in the admitted evidence, which triggers the serial-acquirer cap rather than an assumption of value creation. [data/AKAM/Akamai Technologies Inc NasdaqGS AKAM Financials.xls, Cash Flow sheet, FY2021–FY2025; data/AKAM/Akamai Technologies, Inc., Q2 2026.pdf, Form 10-Q, Note 6 (Acquisition)]

The single biggest signal is the May 2026 $3.500bn convertible issuance for accelerated cloud infrastructure: filed convertible principal reached $7.640bn at June 30 while H1 strict FCF was only $221.174m and buybacks were $615.744m. The contrary fact is that the notes carry zero coupons and CIQ reports 32.6x LTM EBITDA/interest coverage, so this is not an immediate cash-interest distress call; it is a return-on-investment and balance-sheet-capacity concern. [data/AKAM/Akamai Technologies, Inc., Q2 2026.pdf, Form 10-Q, Note 7, pp.17–20; Statements of Cash Flows, p.7; ciq_facts.json, interest_coverage_x, 2026-09-14 — CIQ vendor basis]

Per-share value creation is therefore not proven: H1 share count improved, but FY2023–FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS fell from $3.52 to $3.07 and the vendor return-on-capital series also fell. [data/AKAM/Akamai Technologies, Inc., 2025.pdf, FY2025 Form 10-K, Statements of Operations, p.54; ciq_facts.json, multi_year_trajectory, 2026-09-14 — CIQ vendor basis]
