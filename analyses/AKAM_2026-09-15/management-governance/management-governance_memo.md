## Plain-English Glossary

- **Stewardship:** How well leaders protect shareholders’ interests and use the company’s resources.
- **Strict free cash flow:** Cash from operations minus total capital spending.
- **Capital allocation:** How management divides money among operations, acquisitions, debt, and shareholder payouts.
- **RPT:** A related-party transaction involving the company and a connected person or entity.
- **Contingent liability:** A possible future obligation whose amount or timing is not fully settled.
- **Governance risk:** The chance that leadership, controls, incentives, or shareholder protections cause loss.
- **Proxy statement:** The shareholder filing that carries voting, ownership, board, and executive-pay information.
- **Stock-based compensation:** Employee pay made with shares or share-linked awards.
- **GAAP / non-GAAP:** Reported accounting rules versus a company-adjusted presentation that removes selected items.
- **Profit after tax:** Bottom-line profit after expenses and taxes.
- **Service-based grant:** A share award earned mainly by remaining employed for a stated period.
- **Grant burn:** New share awards during a period as a percentage of the relevant share base.
- **Overhang:** Potential future dilution from outstanding equity awards and remaining plan capacity.
- **Single-trigger vesting:** An award becoming payable after one event, such as a change of control, without a second condition.
- **Beneish/Dechow tests:** Accounting screens used here to check for manipulation or accrual-risk signals.
- **Net debt/EBITDA:** Debt minus cash divided by earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization.
- **Cost of capital:** The return investors and lenders require for providing money to the business.
