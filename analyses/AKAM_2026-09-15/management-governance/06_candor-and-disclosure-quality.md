# Candor & Disclosure Quality — AKAM

## 1. Owning the Misses

There is no completed management-guidance miss in the available record: Q2 revenue, non-GAAP EPS, and non-GAAP operating margin all landed within the Q1 ranges. The closest adverse communication event is the Q2 update to FY2026 guidance: the revenue high end fell by $20m, non-GAAP EPS high end by $0.10, and the operating-margin outlook moved from about 26% to 25%–26%. The new ranges were publicly reported, but the prepared remarks did not plainly label the changes as reductions or give a complete bridge; readers need to compare the two releases. [Management & Track Record, AKAM, §4; Q1 2026 earnings release, 2026-05-07, p. 2; Q2 2026 earnings release, 2026-08-06, pp. 1–2]

## 3. Disclosure Transparency

The releases give usable decision data: GAAP and non-GAAP results are shown side by side, the reconciliation is itemized, and revenue is broken into Security, Delivery and other cloud applications, and Cloud Infrastructure Services (CIS). [Q2 2026 earnings release, 2026-08-06, pp. 1–2; Q2 2026 Supplemental Financial Information, GAAP to Non-GAAP Reconciliation sheet] The solution-category KPI is only newly reported from Q1 2026, although prior FY2025 quarters were recast; this improves visibility into CIS but does not establish five years of stable KPI reporting. There is no evidence in the available record that a KPI was dropped or redefined after deterioration, so this is an Amber comparability limit, not RF-MGT-003. [Q1 2026 Supplemental Financial Information, Supplemental Revenue sheet; Q2 2026 Supplemental Financial Information, Supplemental Revenue sheet]

## 4. Tone in Bad Times

Q2 is the weakest current period available: the release disclosed the GAAP earnings and margin decline while the CFO also directly named Delivery and other cloud applications revenue down 6%, higher colocation, depreciation and headcount costs, and an expected step-up in capex. [Q2 2026 earnings release, Results, pp. 1–2; Q2 2026 earnings call, CFO prepared remarks, pp. 6–8] The CEO’s opening was heavily promotional about AI contracts and third-party recognition, but in Q&A the CFO answered revenue timing, cost pass-through, pre-revenue colocation costs, and the GPU-delivery slip; notably, he said CIS growth should not accelerate in Q3 before a projected Q4 ramp. No direct numerical contradiction between the management commentary and the disclosed results was identified in the two available verbatim 2026 calls. [Q2 2026 earnings call, CEO prepared remarks, pp. 4–5; CFO Q&A, pp. 9–14]

## 5. Candor Read

**Mixed.** Management does put the adverse operating facts and revised ranges into the public record and gives direct, useful answers on the timing and cost of the CIS build-out. But the economic earnings picture is made materially better by excluding recurring SBC and repeat restructuring charges, while the prepared-message tone gives much more space to AI validation than to falling GAAP profitability.

The single biggest candor signal is the reconciliation itself: FY2025 SBC of $459.4m exceeded GAAP net income of $452.0m, and Q2 2026 non-GAAP operating income was $270.7m versus $80.3m on a GAAP basis. This is aggressive adjusted framing, not evidence that the disclosed GAAP figures are false. [Q2 2026 Supplemental Financial Information, GAAP to Non-GAAP Reconciliation sheet, FY2025 and Q2 2026 columns]
