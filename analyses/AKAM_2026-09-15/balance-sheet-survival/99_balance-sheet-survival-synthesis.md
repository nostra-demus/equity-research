# Balance-Sheet-Survival Module — AKAM (Synthesis)

## Abstract

AKAM’s strict net leverage—debt minus cash divided by EBITDA, operating profit before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization—was 5.14x at 30 June 2026 versus 2.49x at FY2025. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q, pp.3–5; FY2025 10-K, pp.53–56] Gross leverage was 6.39x. The 24-month wall is $1.15bn (15.1% of principal) and cash-covered, though a separate $1.725bn conversion right can accelerate payment. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q, Note 7, pp.17–20] Committed liquidity of $4.36bn gives a 100.5-month runway, but covenant headroom—the distance before a lender test fails—is not assessable. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q, pp.3, 19–20] Cash survives a 100% EBITDA decline in the zero-mitigation model; net leverage first exceeds an analyst-only 6.0x warning after an 11.5% decline. The verdict is Adequate, capped by unknown covenant terms and unquantified contingencies.

- **Strongest bear point:** Strict net leverage rose from 2.49x at FY2025 to 5.14x at Q2 FY2026, and to 5.31x on the known-component LayerX pro forma; the same structure crosses the analyst’s 6.0x refinancing warning after only an 11.5% EBITDA decline. [FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.53–56; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.3–5; Note 6]
- **Strongest bull point:** $4,355.4m of reported committed liquidity comprises $1,480.3m cash, $1,875.1m current marketable securities, and a $1,000.0m committed undrawn revolver; the $1,150.0m scheduled maturity is covered without new debt. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.3; Note 7, pp.17–20; Capital IQ Financials → Capital Structure Summary, 30 Jun. 2026 — vendor basis]
- **Single killer risk:** The unknown maximum-leverage covenant could fail before cash runs out, especially if EBITDA falls while holders exercise the $1,725.0m 2033-note cash conversion right. The threshold and conversion status after 7 August 2026 are not proven from the frozen evidence. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7, pp.17–20]
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** Against a distress reading, the zero-mitigation −60% EBITDA case retains a $3,097.4m 12-month committed-liquidity surplus and 14.29x EBITDA/interest coverage on known components. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7, pp.17–20; downside-stress calculation from the stated filing inputs]

## 3. Reconciliation

- **Strict, broad, and vendor debt bases:** The filing-based headline is $7,562.8m gross debt and $6,082.6m strict net debt, producing 6.39x gross and 5.14x net leverage on $1,184.1m GAAP-derived EBITDA. The 2.49x broad sensitivity nets all $3,136.0m marketable securities. Capital IQ’s 4.37x uses $9,339.0m debt including $1,776.2m operating leases, $4,722.7m net debt after cash and all marketable securities, and $1,079.9m vendor EBITDA. These are definition differences, not interchangeable estimates; the strict filing basis governs the verdict, while the securities remain real liquidity. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.3–5; Note 2, pp.13–15; ciq_facts.json, `total_debt_m`, `net_debt_m`, `ltm_ebitda_m`, and `net_debt_ebitda_x`, present]
- **Reported versus post-acquisition timing:** The 01 leverage and 03 runway figures are at 30 June 2026. The 06 stress test conservatively adds the disclosed $205.0m July LayerX cash payment, taking known-component strict net debt to $6,287.6m and leverage to 5.31x, while adding no undisclosed target EBITDA, debt, or cash. The verdict block keeps the required 01 reported leverage and labels the later stress basis separately. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 6]
- **Current classification versus contractual wall:** The $1,705.6m carrying value of the 2033 Notes was current only because holders could convert in Q3 FY2026; it is not added to the $1,150.0m scheduled 24-month wall. The conservative liquidity view nevertheless tests the combined $2,875.0m cash use if both the conversion right and the 2027 maturity are paid. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7, pp.17–20]
- **Interest coverage versus fixed-charge coverage:** EBITDA/interest of 35.73x shows that coupon service is not the present constraint. Fixed-charge coverage of 0.23x points the other way because its denominator includes the past $1,150.0m FY2025 note repayment and $398.7m of lease cash payments; excluding that past bullet produces a 0.85x reference, still below 1.0x. The reconciled view is strong current interest service but weak one-year internal coverage of capex, leases, and a bullet repayment. [FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.54–56; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 7–8]
- **Covenant disclosure:** Triage correctly records that a maximum consolidated leverage covenant and compliance are disclosed. Coverage and stress specialists correctly find numerical headroom Not assessable because the threshold, current actual, Covenant EBITDA definition, addback limits, trigger, and cure rights are absent. Qualitative disclosure does not establish survival headroom. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7, pp.19–20]

## 5. Survival Summary

Gross leverage is 6.39x and strict net leverage is 5.14x at Q2 FY2026, up from 2.49x at FY2025; the known-component LayerX stress basis is 5.31x, so the direction is worse. [FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.53–56; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.3–5; Note 6] The $1,150.0m scheduled maturity within 24 months is self-funded by $1,480.3m cash and covered 2.92x by cash plus current securities, but the $1,725.0m 2033-note conversion right is a separate near-term cash risk. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.3; Note 7, pp.17–20] The reported net-of-FCF runway is 100.5 months, while the tightest covenant’s numerical headroom cannot be measured. Interest service is covered 35.73x by EBITDA, but 0.23x fixed-charge coverage—including a past bullet repayment—shows why low coupons should not be confused with internally funding all fixed uses. [FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.54–56; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 7–8] The −30%, −40%, and −60% EBITDA bounds remain cash-solvent without an equity raise, asset sale, waiver, or refinancing on known components; however, net leverage exceeds the analyst’s 6.0x warning after an 11.5% decline, and the true covenant failure point is unknown.
