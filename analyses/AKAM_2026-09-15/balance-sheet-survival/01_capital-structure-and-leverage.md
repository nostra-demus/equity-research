Strict net debt was broadly flat at roughly $3.0bn–$3.2bn through FY25, then rose $2,907.4m in H1 FY26. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheets, p.53; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, pp.3–4]

The direct driver was the May 2026 $3,500.0m principal issuance of 2030 and 2032 convertibles for accelerated cloud-infrastructure capital expenditure and general corporate purposes. H1 FY26 also included $615.7m of share repurchases, while $2,268.1m was used to buy marketable securities; those securities lower only the labelled broad views, not the strict measure. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), p.17; Statements of Cash Flows, pp.7–8]
