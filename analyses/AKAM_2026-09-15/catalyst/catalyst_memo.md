## What This Module Found

A catalyst is an event that can change the investment view. Akamai has evidenced dates, but the calendar is tilted toward downside checks: contractual dates test note conversion, refinancing, and maturity, while operating upside is softly timed. [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1–5]

The nearest event is **30 September 2026**, when the Q3 conversion window for the **$1.725bn** 2033 Notes closes. Principal on a conversion is payable in cash; no holder submission was reported through the Q2 filing, but the status after **7 August** is not proven. A material conversion by that date is the biggest bearish catalyst. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18]

The most important operating event is the **Q4 2026–Q1 2027** conversion of large signed CIS contracts into revenue. Management expects roughly **$15m plus roughly $20m, or roughly $35m combined**, from two large deals in Q4 before greater Q1 acceleration, but supplied a quarter window rather than a deployment date. A one- or two-week equipment, power, or setup delay can move revenue between periods while infrastructure costs have already risen. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A; Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, 2026-09-09, CFO discussion]

The expected Q3 report date of **3 November 2026** comes from Capital IQ, not the company, so it is vague. The report will test Security, cost pressure, and whether management keeps the Q4 CIS ramp, but it is not a proven-dated operating bull catalyst. [Capital IQ Estimates Report, Consensus worksheet header, frozen 2026-09-14 — vendor; Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Financial guidance, p.2]

The later hard dates are **1 May 2027**, when the **$1.150bn** 2027 Notes become freely convertible, and **1 September 2027**, when they mature. The maturity is **15.1%** of **$7.640bn** note principal and was cash-covered at 30 June 2026, but refinancing cost and undisclosed covenant headroom remain risks. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, p.3; Note 7, pp.17–20]

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict: Dated, evidenced near-term catalysts.** The calendar is useful for funding-risk checks, but not for timing the central operating upside. [Catalyst Module synthesis, 2026-09-14, §§1 and 4]

- It could be better than it looks if the two large CIS deals begin contributing roughly **$35m combined** in Q4 and then accelerate in Q1 2027 without another capacity, power, equipment, or margin setback. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, CFO Q&A; Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, 2026-09-09, CFO discussion]

- It could be worse if holders convert a material part of the **$1.725bn** 2033 Notes by **30 September 2026**, creating a cash-principal use years before contractual maturity. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 7 (Debt), pp.17–18]

- The highest-value missing evidence is a company-disclosed deployment and revenue-recognition schedule for the two large CIS deals. [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/00_catalyst-data-triage.md, §§1, 3]

- Watch whether Akamai keeps the Q4 CIS revenue window and then reports the stated revenue conversion; until then, the §24-flagged event must not lift conviction. [analyses/AKAM_2026-09-14/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1, 3]

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** An event that can change the investment view or market expectations.
- **Note conversion:** A bondholder’s right to exchange convertible debt under its stated terms.
- **Cash principal:** The face amount of debt that must be paid in cash.
- **Cloud Infrastructure Services (CIS):** Akamai’s cloud-infrastructure offering discussed in the cited management updates.
- **Refinancing:** Replacing or repaying debt as it becomes due.
- **Covenant headroom:** The distance before a lender test would be breached.
- **Normalized EPS:** Earnings per share after the adjustments used by the cited earnings analysis.
- **Guidance:** Management’s forecast range for a future period.
- **Non-GAAP EPS:** Earnings per share after company-defined adjustments outside standard accounting rules.
- **Dilution:** A reduction in each existing share’s ownership claim when more shares are issued.
- **Capital spending:** Cash used to build or acquire long-lived operating assets.
