# Competitive Map — TSLA

Both required upstream inputs are present: `02_business-identity.md` and `03_segment-map.md`.

## 1. Dominant Segment

Automotive is Tesla's dominant segment — 86.5% of FY2025 revenue and 77.7% of FY2025 gross profit (gross-profit basis only; Tesla does not allocate operating expenses by segment) [`CIQ Financials_Annual export, Segments tab, FY2025`; `FY26 Q2 10-Q, Note 14`], restated from `03_segment-map.md` §2.

## 2. Named Competitors

A hard limitation up front: the data pool does not contain Tesla's own Item 1A "Risk Factors" / "Competition" narrative — only a Part III-only Form 10-K/A amendment is present for FY2025, and neither the Q1 FY2026 nor Q2 FY2026 earnings-call transcripts in this pool name a specific competitor by name (management discusses "additional competitors" generically) [`Tesla_Inc_-_Form_10-KA(Apr-30-2026)`; `Tesla, Inc., Q1/Q2 FY2026 Earnings Call transcripts`]. The competitor set below is therefore built from the Capital IQ peer-comp export in the data pool (a Tier-5 vendor source, built specifically for TSLA using CIQ's own relevancy scoring) plus one competitor confirmed only through dated trade press, since it does not appear anywhere in the pool.

### Competitor A — BYD Company Limited

- **Ticker / listing:** HKEX:1211 / SZSE:002594 (not in the data pool's CIQ comp set)
- **Where they compete:** Battery-electric and plug-in-hybrid passenger vehicles globally, directly overlapping Tesla's Automotive segment in China, Europe, and emerging markets.
- **Scale:** FY2025 revenue of roughly $116 billion (USD-converted), above Tesla's LTM total revenue of $103,619 million [`Capital IQ Comparable Analysis export, Financial Data tab, LTM as of 2026-07-24`]; BYD delivered about 2.26 million battery-electric vehicles in 2025 versus Tesla's 1.64 million deliveries, a roughly 620,000-unit gap [Web: goodcarbadcar.net, "BYD Beat Tesla in Revenue — But Profits Fell 19% in 2025," pub. 2026-04-03, unverified — not in the data pool].
- **Profitability / return on capital:** FY2025 net profit approximately $4.7 billion on ~$116 billion revenue — a net margin of roughly 4.0% — down 19% year-on-year as a domestic Chinese price war compressed margins, "the company's first annual profit drop since 2021" [Web: goodcarbadcar.net, pub. 2026-04-03, unverified]. For comparison, Tesla's own LTM net-income margin is 3.67% and LTM return on capital is 2.75% [`Capital IQ Comparable Analysis export, Operating Statistics tab`; `Tesla Inc NasdaqGS TSLA Financials_Annual.xls, Ratios tab, LTM Jun-30-2026`]. ROIC/ROE for BYD specifically is not disclosed anywhere in this data pool — the ~4.0% net margin above is a web-derived estimate, not an audited figure, and should not be read as precise.
- **Source named in:** Not named in any Tesla filing or transcript in this pool. Named in dated industry trade press as the company that overtook Tesla as the world's top BEV seller in 2025 [Web: CnEVPost, "BYD leads global BEV market with 15.4% share in Q3," pub. 2025-11-24, unverified; Web: autovistagroup/visualcapitalist EV market-share summaries, 2026, unverified].
- **One-line read:** Higher volume, roughly comparable net margin, but the company that took the "world's top EV seller by volume" title away from Tesla in calendar 2025 — not proven from Tesla's own filings, since Tesla's pool documents never name BYD.

### Competitor B — Ford Motor Company

- **Ticker / listing:** NYSE:F
- **Where they compete:** US and global light-vehicle manufacturer with a growing EV lineup (Mustang Mach-E, F-150 Lightning), competing with Tesla's Automotive segment mainly in North America [`Company Comparable Analysis Tesla Inc .xls, Business Description tab`].
- **Scale:** LTM total revenue $189,861 million (LTM filing date 2026-04-30), about 1.83x Tesla's LTM revenue of $103,619 million [`Capital IQ Comparable Analysis export, Financial Data tab`].
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM gross margin 7.1%, LTM EBITDA margin 4.0%, LTM EBIT margin 0.8%, LTM net-income margin −3.2% (net loss on a trailing basis), LTM total debt/capital 81.0% [`Capital IQ Comparable Analysis export, Operating Statistics tab`]. ROIC/ROE for Ford specifically: not disclosed in this data pool.
- **Source named in:** `Company Comparable Analysis Tesla Inc .xls` — Capital IQ's own peer-comp set built for TSLA, sorted by "S&P Capital IQ's proprietary relevancy score" [Trading Multiples tab, Financial Data tab, Operating Statistics tab, Business Description tab].
- **One-line read:** Far larger revenue base built on legacy internal-combustion volume, but with gross, EBITDA, and net margins well below Tesla's on every line disclosed in this pool.

### Competitor C — General Motors Company

- **Ticker / listing:** NYSE:GM
- **Where they compete:** US and global light-vehicle manufacturer (Chevrolet, Cadillac, GMC, Buick) with its own EV lineup and a large China joint-venture presence, competing with Tesla's Automotive segment in North America and China [`Company Comparable Analysis Tesla Inc .xls, Business Description tab`].
- **Scale:** LTM total revenue $185,528 million (LTM filing date 2026-07-21), about 1.79x Tesla's LTM revenue [`Capital IQ Comparable Analysis export, Financial Data tab`].
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM gross margin 10.2%, LTM EBITDA margin 8.9%, LTM EBIT margin 5.5%, LTM net-income margin 1.05%, LTM total debt/capital 66.9% [`Capital IQ Comparable Analysis export, Operating Statistics tab`]. ROIC/ROE for GM specifically: not disclosed in this data pool.
- **Source named in:** Same `Company Comparable Analysis Tesla Inc .xls` CIQ peer-comp export.
- **One-line read:** Profitable at the operating line, unlike Ford, but still a fraction of Tesla's gross margin and net margin, carrying much more balance-sheet leverage (67% debt/capital vs. Tesla's 15.5%) [`Capital IQ Comparable Analysis export, Operating Statistics tab`; `Tesla Inc NasdaqGS TSLA Financials_Annual.xls, Ratios tab`].

## 3. Competitive Position

**Losing** global battery-electric-vehicle volume share to BYD on a full calendar-year 2025 basis: BYD held roughly 12.1% of the global BEV market in 2025 versus Tesla's 8.8% (Volkswagen third at 5.2%), and BYD's battery-electric volume (2.26 million units) grew 28% year-on-year while Tesla's global deliveries (1.64 million) fell from the prior year [Web: autovistagroup/visualcapitalist EV market-share summaries, 2026, unverified — not in the data pool]. That said, Tesla's own most recent quarters show a different, more recent trend: automotive-sales revenue was down 11% year-on-year in Q4 FY2025 but up 23–27% year-on-year in Q1–Q2 FY2026 [`03_segment-map.md` §1, citing `FY26 Q2 10-Q, Item 2 MD&A`]. The data pool does not disclose Tesla's own global market-share trend directly, so the full-year-2025 share loss and the early-2026 revenue rebound are the two best available reads and they point in different directions — the rebound could reflect regained share, an industry-wide demand recovery, or both; it is not proven from available data which.

## 4. Competitive Shape

**Fragmented.** No single manufacturer holds anywhere near a dominant share of the global BEV market: BYD leads at roughly 12.1%, Tesla second at 8.8%, Volkswagen third at 5.2% — the top three combine for only about 26% of the market, with the balance split across a long tail of Chinese domestic brands (Geely, SAIC, Xiaomi, Li Auto, NIO, XPeng, Wuling — none named in this data pool) and legacy Western/Japanese/Korean OEMs building out competing EV lineups, several of which appear in Tesla's own Capital IQ peer-comp set: Ford, General Motors, Honda, Mercedes-Benz, Renault, Kia, Stellantis, plus EV pure-plays Rivian and Lucid [Web: autovistagroup/visualcapitalist EV market-share summaries, 2026, unverified; `Company Comparable Analysis Tesla Inc .xls, Business Description tab`]. This is not a 2–4 firm oligopoly and not a consolidated market — it is a large field of scaled competitors with no HHI concentration data available in this pool to quantify precisely.

## 5. Caveat

The data pool's own Tesla documents do not name specific competitors: the only Form 10-K present is a Part III-only amendment (`Tesla_Inc_-_Form_10-KA(Apr-30-2026)`), which lacks the Item 1A "Risk Factors" / "Competition" section a full annual filing would carry, and neither in-pool earnings-call transcript (Q1 or Q2 FY2026) names a specific rival by name. As a result, two of the three profiles above (Ford, GM) are sourced to the Capital IQ Comparable Analysis export rather than to Tesla's own filings, and the single most consequential real-world competitor by volume — BYD — appears nowhere in this data pool at all and is sourced only to dated, unverified web trade press. A full FY2025 10-K with its Item 1A Competition discussion, plus a peer-comp export that itself included BYD, would resolve this gap and let the `moat` agent anchor its "earns more than peers" claim to filing-grade rather than web-grade evidence for the company's most important rival.
