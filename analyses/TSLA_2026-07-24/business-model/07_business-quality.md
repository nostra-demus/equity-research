## 4. Read

Tesla is a capital-intensive, demand-cyclical vehicle manufacturer with a smaller, faster-growing grid-battery business bolted on, plus an unmonetized bet on autonomous driving and robotics — not a durable compounder in its current, disclosed form. Return on capital collapsed from an 18.6% cyclical peak in FY2022 to 2.9% in FY2025 and 2.75% LTM [CIQ Financials_Annual export, Ratios tab, "Return on Capital %"]; that FY2022 peak was itself a cyclical high (pandemic-era supply shortage plus a near-monopoly EV position), not a through-cycle level, so today's ~3% figure is the more honest read of what this business earns on capital absent another unusual demand spike — and it sits well below any reasonable cost of capital. The single quality factor a buyer should watch over the next 24 months is margin stability: whether automotive gross margin (16.2% FY2025, 16.3% Q2 FY2026 ex-credits) stabilizes as the shrinking regulatory-credit line fully runs off, or keeps falling as EV competition intensifies and the robotaxi/Optimus story — still a zero-revenue-line item in the segment disclosure — consumes capital without a matching return. Because the industry rate-of-change / disruption-risk row scored 30 (≤40), this thesis is flagged explicitly as closer to a sector / technology-cycle bet (EV competitive dynamics plus an unresolved autonomous-driving regulatory race) than a durable compounder.
