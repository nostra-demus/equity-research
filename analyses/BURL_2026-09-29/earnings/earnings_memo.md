## What This Module Found

Q2 FY2026 sales were $2,997.8m, up 11.0%, but new and non-comparable stores supplied $254.8m, or about 86%, of the $296.8m increase. They contributed 9.4 percentage points of growth, compared with 1.6 points from comparable stores, while mature-store transactions were relatively flat. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.3 and MD&A—Net Sales; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, Q&A]

Reported Q2 gross margin was 46.2%, up 242 basis points—a basis point is one-hundredth of a percentage point—but the $55.5m tariff refund supplied about 185 points. Refund-excluded gross margin was 44.3%, up 60 basis points, and is the better starting point for the second half. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.3, 20; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks]

The strongest positive is that, excluding the refund and bankruptcy-acquired-lease costs, adjusted EBIT margin—adjusted operating profit as a share of sales—improved 100 basis points. FY2025 cash from operations equalled 99.2% of CIQ-standard EBITDA, or earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortisation. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks; FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.45–46; CIQ Financials Annual workbook, Income Statement]

The latest cash figure still needs adjustment: reported trailing free cash flow, or FCF—cash from operations minus total capital spending—was $412.5m, versus about $357.0m after a conservative full-refund normalisation. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.20, 30; Earnings Quality, Section 1]

The Q3 revenue bar looks fair rather than easy. Consensus of $2,992.38m is $10.90m, or 0.37%, above management's guidance midpoint and remains inside the range; the EPS comparison is Not assessable because vendor normalized EPS and company Adjusted EPS use unmatched definitions. [Capital IQ Estimates Report, Consensus sheet, CIQ FQ3 2027, latest logged change 2026-09-22; Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Outlook]

The biggest risk is that the planned $22.2m Q3 value investment fails to revive Fall traffic and instead raises markdown pressure. Higher inventory, weak weather, and flat transactions could then hurt sales and margin together. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks and Q&A; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A—Inventory]

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** Mixed earnings setup, with a **Balanced** next-quarter setup and **Material concerns** on red flags.

[Q2 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks; FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.45–46; CIQ Financials Annual workbook, Income Statement]
- **Why it could be worse:** About 86% of Q2's dollar sales increase came from new and non-comparable stores while mature-store transactions were relatively flat; the $22.2m Q3 value investment could reduce margin without enough traffic response. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A—Net Sales; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks and Q&A]
- **What is missing:** Price elasticity, store-cohort returns, operating coefficients for correlated downside, and a definition-matched EPS comparison.
- **One thing to watch next:** The Q3 transaction response, basket split, and refund-excluded margin bridge; this is the highest-value evidence for whether the value investment worked.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Earnings quality:** How closely reported profit reflects recurring operations and turns into cash.
- **Consensus setup:** How demanding analysts' forecasts are relative to company guidance and operating evidence.
- **Earnings volatility:** How much earnings can move when sales, margins, weather, or costs change.
- **Basis point:** One-hundredth of a percentage point.
- **EBIT margin:** Operating profit before interest and tax as a share of sales.
- **EBITDA:** Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortisation.
- **Free cash flow (FCF):** Cash from operations minus total capital spending.
- **Adjusted EPS:** Earnings per share after the company's stated adjustments.
- **Markdown:** A price reduction used to sell inventory.
- **Price elasticity:** How much customer demand changes when price or value changes.
