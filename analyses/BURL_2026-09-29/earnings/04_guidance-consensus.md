## 7. Bar Assessment: Fair

The revenue bar is inside company guidance in both periods: Q3 consensus is $10.90m (0.37%) above the midpoint but $16.21m below the high, while full-year consensus is $47.29m (0.37%) above midpoint and $10.55m below the high. Q3 normalized EPS is $0.03093 above the Adjusted EPS guidance high, but management's forward non-GAAP measures have no quantitative GAAP reconciliation, so this is not evidence of a clean estimate gap. The next-quarter normalized-EPS estimate was cut from $2.04 to $1.73 over the prior 60 days and then held flat over the last 30 days, while revenue was steady; that combination supports a fair rather than low or high bar.
