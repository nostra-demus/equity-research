# Revenue Drivers — BURL

Burlington reports under U.S. GAAP in USD. Its fiscal year ends on the Saturday closest to January 31; the latest reported quarter is Q2 FY2026, ended August 1, 2026. “Sales” below means net sales unless noted otherwise.

## 1. Segment Decomposition Status

Single-segment business (>85% from one segment) — consolidated analysis.

Burlington has one U.S. off-price retail segment, which supplied 100% of FY2025 reported revenue. The FY2025 10-K reports total revenue of $11,566.9m for that segment. The frozen CIQ facts sidecar reads `Retail - Apparel` revenue of $11,559m (100%); the $7.9m (0.07%) difference is a vendor-versus-filing definition/extraction difference, so the filing figure governs. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 1—Segment Information; CIQ Financials→Segments, 12 months Jan-31-2026—vendor export]

**Cycle position: Mid-cycle (inference, not from filings).** Q2 total sales grew 11%, while the reported mature-store comparable-sales metric was +2%, and FY2023–FY2025 annual net-sales growth slowed from 11.8% to 8.8%. That evidence does not establish a demand peak or a trough. The coming Fall is not a clean run-rate test: management plans to use a one-time $55m tariff refund to lower prices in Q3/Q4 and will lap prior-year weather disruption in Q3 and tariff-related supply constraints in Q3/Q4. ## 6. Revenue Growth Decomposition

Q2 FY2026 net sales increased $296.8m to $2,997.8m, or 11.0%, from Q2 FY2025. The filing gives a complete dollar bridge: $254.8m from 149 net new and non-comparable stores, plus $42.0m from comparable stores. The $42.0m comparable-store amount was driven mainly by a higher basket, but the call does not split it into price, mix and transaction dollars. ## 6a. Decomposition Attribution and Residual (MODULE_RULES "Driver Attribution" / §15)

**New and non-comparable stores:** reported $254.8m contribution ÷ $2,701.0m Q2 FY2025 net-sales base [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A—Results of Operations (Net sales)]
  = **9.43pp** of the 10.99pp observed growth
  → Asserted from disclosure, no volume/price/mix ratio applied. Basis matches: the component and total are both divided by the same prior-year quarterly net-sales base.

**Comparable stores:** reported $42.0m contribution ÷ $2,701.0m Q2 FY2025 net-sales base [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A—Results of Operations (Net sales)]
  = **1.55pp** of the 10.99pp observed growth
  → Asserted from disclosure, no basket, price or mix ratio applied. The transcript identifies higher basket and flat transactions, but gives no ratio that can allocate the $42.0m further. Basis matches: the same prior-year quarterly net-sales base is used.

Reconciliation: $254.8m + $42.0m = $296.8m. On the common Q2 FY2025 sales base, 9.43pp + 1.55pp = 10.99pp versus reported total growth of 10.99pp; **10.99pp reconciled, 0.00pp residual** before rounding. The bridge therefore supports a new-store conclusion, while it does not support a separate price/mix contribution claim.

## 7. The Single Biggest Revenue Driver

**New-store rollout is the single biggest revenue driver, and it is improving.** In Q2, recent and non-comparable stores supplied $254.8m of the $296.8m sales increase—9.4pp of the reported 11.0% growth, or about 86% of the dollar increase—so it clears the roughly half-of-growth test. The rollout is still active: trailing-12-month net store count grew 13% to 1,287 and management guides to about 115 net new FY2026 stores. The important qualification is that mature-store demand was only +2%, driven mainly by basket while transactions were flat; weaker traffic, warm Fall weather, or a smaller-than-expected response to H2 price investment would therefore reduce the benefit from the larger store base. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A—Results of Operations (Net sales); BURL Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Outlook; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, 2026-08-27, prepared remarks and Q&A]
