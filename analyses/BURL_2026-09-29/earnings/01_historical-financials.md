# Historical Financials — BURL

Burlington reports under U.S. GAAP in USD and uses a January fiscal year-end. This report uses the company's fiscal-year labels: FY2025 ended January 31, 2026; FY2023 had 53 weeks. Amounts below are USD millions except per-share data, percentages, and multiples. “Revenue” means reported net sales, not the small other-revenue line. Gross profit is net sales less cost of sales. Free cash flow (FCF, cash left after investment) is cash from operations (CFO) less the reported `cash paid for property and equipment` capex line, consistently throughout.

EBIT and EBITDA are not GAAP subtotals reported by Burlington. To keep the multi-year and quarterly series consistent, those two rows use the source-bound Capital IQ standard calculation, labelled as such; company-reported adjusted measures are separated in Section 4. Filing-based checks and the CIQ reconciliation are stated below rather than treating unlike definitions as interchangeable.

## 6. Key Trend Summary

Annual revenue growth has slowed from 11.8% in FY2023 to 8.8% in FY2025, although latest Q2 FY2026 net sales were still up 11.0% year on year. Margins have expanded across the annual record: gross margin rose from 40.5% in FY2022 to 43.8% in FY2025 and CIQ-standard EBITDA margin rose from 7.5% to 10.7%; the Q2 FY2026 gross-margin print of 46.2% is not a normalised run rate because the filing includes a `$55.5m` tariff refund. Seasonality is material: Q4 supplied an average 31.5% of annual sales in FY2023–FY2025 and its EBITDA margin ranged from 12.4% to 14.2%, above the first three quarters. FCF moved from `(17.0)m` in FY2024 to `$171.6m` in FY2025 and `$412.5m` over the latest TTM, while latest strict net debt was `$1,209.9m`, below the `$1,291.7m` same-date prior strict balance but above the FY2025 year-end **strict net-debt** balance of `$849.8m`. [FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.43, 45–46; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.3, 5, 20]
