# Catalyst Module — BURL (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Burlington has evidenced calendar events, but the only material operating catalyst—the Q3 FY2026 result—has a company-confirmed November window rather than a set release date. [analyses/BURL_2026-09-29/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1–3] The nearest hard-dated event is the November 3 opening of the proxy-access nomination window; it tests whether an eligible holder raises a governance or capital-allocation issue, not operating performance. Q3 is the central two-sided test: comparable-store sales above 3% versus Q3 FY2025 with an adjusted EBIT-margin decline under 60 basis points would help, while sales below 1% or a decline over 80 basis points would hurt. The biggest bearish event is therefore the November Q3 window. Verdict: Catalysts exist but timing vague.
