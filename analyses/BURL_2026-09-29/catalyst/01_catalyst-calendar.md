## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

The nearest hard-dated calendar item is the November 3, 2026 opening of the proxy-access nomination window. It could matter if an eligible holder files, but it is not an operating catalyst and the absence of a filing is not a positive earnings signal. There is no company-confirmed market-facing results date: the first operating read is Q3 FY2026, whose 13-week period ends October 31 and whose discussion is expected in November; the November 25 calendar entry is Capital IQ’s estimate, not a company date. A result above the stated Q3 sales, comparable-store-sales and margin tests would support the Fall demand read; a result below them would expose the risk that the Q3 price investment reduced margin without enough traffic response. ## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

Q3 FY2026 results are the most important catalyst because they test the customer response to the roughly $22.2m Q3 value investment, Fall weather and the balance between capacity-led sales growth and mature-store demand. The bullish path is comparable-store sales above the +1%–+3% range with a smaller-than-guided margin decline; the bearish path is weak transactions, markdowns and a margin decline worse than 80 basis points versus Q3 FY2025. A buyer can time only the November window, not the release day, and Q3 cannot settle the FY2026 case because Q4 carries the remaining roughly $33.3m of refund reinvestment and is the larger seasonal quarter. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks and Q&A, 2026-08-27] [analyses/BURL_2026-09-29/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §§1–5]

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **November 2026 Q3 results window:** The main downside event is comparable-store sales below +1% versus Q3 FY2025 or an adjusted EBIT-margin decline greater than 80 basis points versus the same quarter, particularly if warm weather, weak traffic and higher inventory lead to markdowns. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Outlook, 2026-08-27] [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A—Inventory, filed 2026-08-27]

- **January 30, 2027 FY2026 close:** A full-year guidance cut or shortfall in the expansion plan would matter more than a bare store-opening count, because the filings do not provide new-store contribution, build cost or cash payback. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Outlook, 2026-08-27] [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—Capital Expenditures]

- **November 2026–March 2027 governance deadlines:** A qualifying nomination or shareholder proposal could raise attention on the board’s long-tenured independent-director cohort and capital allocation. No such action is evidenced today. [FY2026 DEF 14A, pp. 11, 21–22, 75]

- **Within the next 12 months, no material refinancing wall is dated:** $20.1m is due by August 1, 2027, but its exact payment date is not disclosed; cash was $703.7m at August 1, 2026. The $186.1m convert due December 15, 2027 is a later, not calendar-period, maturity. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 3, pp. 10–11; Note 4, pp. 12–14] [analyses/BURL_2026-09-29/balance-sheet-survival/02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md, §§1–5]

## 5. Calendar Read

Five calendar rows have hard dates, but four are governance or authorization deadlines rather than operating events. The earliest operating read is the November Q3-results window, whose exact release date is vague; it contains both the clearest upside test and the clearest risk of a demand-and-margin miss. The key timing caveat is that neither Q3’s exact release date nor the later FY2026-results date is company-confirmed, while no scheduled policy, legal, M&A or refinancing decision exists in the available evidence.
