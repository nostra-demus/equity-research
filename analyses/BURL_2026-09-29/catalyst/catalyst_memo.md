## What This Module Found

The single most important catalyst—an event that could change the market's view—is Burlington's November 2026 Q3 FY2026 result. It will test Fall traffic, weather, and the planned Q3 value investment of about $22.2m, but Burlington has not disclosed the release day. [Q2 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks, Q&A and closing remarks, 2026-08-27]

Management guides Q3 sales growth of +9% to +11%, comparable-store sales—sales growth at mature stores—of +1% to +3% versus standalone Q3 FY2025, adjusted EBIT margin down 60 to 80 basis points, and Adjusted EPS of $1.60 to $1.70. A basis point is one-hundredth of a percentage point; EBIT margin is operating profit before interest and tax as a share of sales. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Outlook, 2026-08-27]

The bullish trigger is sales growth above +11%, comparable-store sales above +3%, and an adjusted EBIT-margin decline smaller than 60 basis points versus the same Q3 FY2025 period. That would show the value investment drew demand without the guided level of margin loss. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Outlook, 2026-08-27; Q2 FY2026 earnings call, closing remarks, 2026-08-27]

The main bearish event is sales growth below +9%, comparable-store sales below +1%, or an adjusted EBIT-margin decline greater than 80 basis points. Warm weather, weak traffic, or markdown pressure could make the investment reduce margin without enough demand response. ## Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** An event that can materially change the market's view of a company.
- **Timing visibility:** How precisely the date or window of an event is known.
- **Catalyst risk:** The chance that an event hurts rather than helps the investment case.
- **Comparable-store sales:** Sales growth from stores open long enough to allow a like-for-like comparison.
- **Adjusted EBIT margin:** Operating profit before interest and tax as a share of sales, after the company's stated adjustments.
- **Basis point:** One-hundredth of a percentage point.
- **Adjusted EPS:** Adjusted earnings per share, or adjusted profit allocated to each share.
- **Proxy access:** A process allowing eligible shareholders to nominate directors through the company's proxy materials.
- **Share-repurchase authorization:** Board permission to buy back shares, not a promise that the company will do so.
