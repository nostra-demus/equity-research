## 4. Read

Burlington is geographically concentrated: all reported revenue is from the United States. It is not proven to be concentrated in any named retail customer, but customer shares are not disclosed, so the customer tests cannot be cleared. The U.S. revenue concentration is not contractually secured by long-term customer agreements in the available disclosure; direct revenue is recognized on sale and delivery. The single biggest dependency is U.S. discretionary spending by its low-income core customer, which management identifies as exposed to inflation, employment, benefit and household-cost pressure. [Q2 FY26 Form 10-Q, Uncertainties and Challenges—General Economic Conditions, p.22]
