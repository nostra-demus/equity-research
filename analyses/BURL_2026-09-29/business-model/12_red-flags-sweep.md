## 3. Most Severe New Flag

Distribution-network concentration is the main additional concern. In FY2025, six facilities handled more than 99% of merchandise units, with three located in New Jersey and three in Southern California; Burlington itself says a loss of significant capacity at a primary centre could significantly disrupt inventory delivery. The planned Georgia and Arizona facilities are mitigating actions, but the available filings do not provide the current unit split after those additions. This is an operational point-of-failure risk, separate from the upstream tariff, freight and weather exposures. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Distribution and Warehousing, p.2; Item 1A — Distribution Network Risk]

## 4. Cross-Cutting Patterns

The distribution risk is most consequential in the already-identified higher-sales second half, when Burlington must carry more inventory before the holiday selling period. A disruption then could combine with the upstream seasonality, inventory-management and new-capacity execution risks; this is a connected operating-risk pattern, not a second capex or lease-liability flag. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Seasonality; Item 1 — Distribution and Warehousing, p.2; Item 1A — Distribution Network Risk]
