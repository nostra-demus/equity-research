## 4. Read

Burlington is a single-format, discretionary U.S. off-price retailer: a mixed-quality consumer business rather than a durable compounder. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Business Overview; Note 1 — Segment Information] Its operating model has sourcing flexibility and recently better margins, but it pairs uncontracted customer demand with high competition, store and supply-chain spending, and a weather- and holiday-weighted earnings pattern. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Off-Price Sourcing and Merchandising Model / Competition / Seasonality; Item 7 — Capital Expenditures] The reported Q2 FY2026 gross margin of 46.2% is not a through-cycle level because it includes $55.5m of tariff refunds; the cleaner anchors are FY2025’s 43.8% gross margin and the CIQ EBITDA-margin series of 7% in FY2023, 9% in FY2024, 10% in FY2025 and 11% LTM. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A — Gross Margin; FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 — Results of Operations: Gross Margin; CIQ Financials→Income Statement (margin % + absolute EBITDA reconciler), retrieved 2026-09-29 — vendor export] Over the next 24 months, the key quality test is margin stability: whether merchandise-margin gains survive refund reinvestment, freight pressure and modest comparable-store sales without turning into more markdowns. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, MD&A — Gross Margin / Comparable Store Sales]
