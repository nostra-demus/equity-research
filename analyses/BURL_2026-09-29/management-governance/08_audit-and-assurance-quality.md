## 10. Read

BURL's assurance is **mixed — watch the named link**. Deloitte issued clean consolidated opinions for the last three reported years and an unqualified FY2025 ICFR opinion, while disclosed non-audit fees were only 5.7% of audit fees in FY2025. The weakest link is not a modified BURL opinion; it is the combination of a 43-year audit-firm tenure, historical PCAOB discipline of Deloitte, no current signing-partner identification, and no disclosed component-auditor coverage. Trust would increase with the current BURL Form AP/partner record, a partner-specific discipline sweep, and an Audit Committee disclosure of Deloitte's principal/component coverage plus all network-firm fees.
