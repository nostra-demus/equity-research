## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Management’s strategy is to grow the store base while improving the off-price model: buy more merchandise in season, shift purchases between suppliers and categories as conditions change, and pursue a long-term 2,000-store opportunity. The 2,000-store objective has no stated completion date, so it is an aspiration rather than a target that can yet be scored. [S05: FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Our Off-Price Sourcing and Merchandising Model; Item 1 — Store Expansion and Real Estate Strategy]

For FY2026, after Q2 management guided to 10%–11% total-sales growth, 3%–4% comparable-store-sales growth, about 115 net new stores, 20–40 basis points of Adjusted EBIT-margin expansion, and Adjusted EPS of $11.77–$11.97. These are company-defined non-GAAP targets where stated; they remain untested as of September 29, 2026. [S04: Q2 FY2026 earnings release, Outlook, 2026-08-27]

## 5. Management Read

The CEO/CFO core has been stable for roughly seven and four years respectively, and the completed scorecard has three dated targets that cleared.
