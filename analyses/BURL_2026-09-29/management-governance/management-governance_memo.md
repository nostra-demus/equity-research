## What This Module Found

Management execution is the main positive: all three completed targets reviewed were met or exceeded, and FY2025 cash from operations divided by profit after tax was 2.02x while cash from operations divided by EBITDA was 99.2%. [01_management-and-track-record, Sections 2–3; earnings/06_earnings-quality, Sections 1–3]

Capital allocation—how management directs cash—has mixed results. FY2022–FY2025 capex of $2,880.2m supported growth, and $1,094.8m of buybacks reduced diluted shares 2.69%, but strict net debt—financial debt minus cash—rose from $849.8m at FY2025 year-end to $1,209.9m at Q2 FY2026; repurchases were not tested against intrinsic value. [FY2024 Form 10-K, pp.52–53; FY2025 Form 10-K, p.46; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.5; CIQ Financials Annual—Income Statement, FY2022–FY2025]

Pay partly rewards per-share results, but the annual bonus depends entirely on absolute adjusted EBIT and long-term awards remain 35% time-based. Insiders own 1.3%; the CEO owns about 0.74%, 72.1% of his disclosed position is options or near-term restricted stock units, and the reviewed year had five open-market sales, no open-market buys, and net sales of 9,231 shares. [FY2026 DEF 14A, Compensation Discussion & Analysis and Security Ownership; Form 4 review through 2026-09-29]

Minority holders have one vote per share and an independent chair, but five legally independent directors have served more than ten years, and holders cannot call a special meeting or act by written consent. [FY2026 DEF 14A, pp.3–4, 11–17, 21–24]

Disclosure is mixed. Management quantified the tariff refund, but repeated lease, impairment, and litigation exclusions continued, including $21.8m in FY2025, so one transparent event does not erase the recurring adjusted-profit pattern. [FY2025 Form 10-K, MD&A / non-GAAP reconciliation; 06_candor-and-disclosure-quality, 2026-09-29]

The single killer risk is that debt-funded expansion and buybacks are rewarded through adjusted EBIT and adjusted EPS even if per-share value weakens. It becomes thesis-killing stewardship evidence if leverage rises while repurchases stop reducing diluted shares or cash returns deteriorate. [02_capital-allocation-scorecard, Sections 1–5; FY2026 DEF 14A, Compensation Discussion & Analysis]

The gate still passes: the board is 91.7% independent, the chair is independent, current audit and internal-control opinions are clean, FY2025 reportable Item 404 transactions were $0, and no hard disqualifier was found. Incomplete legal and person screens mean “nothing material found” is not a clearance. [FY2026 DEF 14A, Board and Certain Relationships; FY2025 Form 10-K, Auditor Report; business-model/01_disqualifier-scan]

## Bottom Line

- **What is missing:** Complete identifier-linked legal/person checks, formal 2026 voting results, entity-level cash/debt/guarantee mapping, FY2026 exclusion-to-cash reconciliation, and the current audit partner's inspection record.
- **One thing to watch next:** Whether strict net debt and diluted shares fall together while management removes recurring exclusions; otherwise the current execution record will not prove owner-like stewardship.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Stewardship:** How management and the board protect and grow shareholder value over time.
- **Capital allocation:** How management directs cash among operations, expansion, debt, buybacks, and other uses.
- **Adjusted EBIT:** Operating profit before interest and tax after management's stated exclusions.
- **Adjusted EPS:** Earnings per share after management's stated exclusions.
- **Performance stock unit:** A share award earned only if stated performance conditions are met.
- **Restricted stock unit:** A share award that generally vests after time or other stated conditions.
- **RPT:** Related-party transaction, meaning a deal involving the company and a connected person or entity.
- **Contingent liability:** A possible obligation whose amount or payment depends on a future event.
- **Accounting forensics:** Tests for signs that reported accounting may not reflect the underlying economics.
- **Strict net debt:** Financial debt minus cash, excluding operating-lease liabilities.
- **Intrinsic value:** An estimate of what a business is worth based on its future cash generation.
- **ICFR:** Internal control over financial reporting, the processes used to prevent or detect material reporting errors.
- **PCAOB:** The U.S. audit regulator for public-company auditors.
