## 5. Minority-Shareholder Protection Read

**Adequate.** The board has a 91.7% legal-independence ratio, an independent chair, fully independent standing committees, majority voting in uncontested elections and proxy access. [FY2026 DEF 14A, pp. 3–4, 21, 24, 32–34]

The main counterweight is true independence: five proxy-date independent directors had served more than 10 years, meeting the Registry's Red threshold even though none was given a Material or Disqualifying `07` grade. Minority holders also cannot call a special meeting or act by written consent, and the phased declassification and 75% removal hurdle remain until the 2027 annual meeting. [FY2026 DEF 14A, pp. 11, 21, 24; FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, p. 17; 07, Person Register]
