## 5. Blunt Read

Management has 1.3% beneficial ownership, but the disclosed total includes options and RSUs, and no open-market purchases appear in the last 12 months. The cleaner behavior signal is five open-market sales totaling 9,231 shares; it is modest relative to 62.8m shares outstanding and is not evidenced as sales ahead of weak results. The main positive is control fairness: no controller, one vote per share, and no disclosed pledging; the main limitation is that the filing does not establish how much insider stock was bought with cash rather than received through compensation.
