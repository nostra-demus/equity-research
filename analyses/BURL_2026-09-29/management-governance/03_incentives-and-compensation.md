## 4. Incentive Alignment Read

**Mixed.** Long-term pay has a real per-share element: 65% of the fiscal-2025 LTIP is in three-year Adjusted-EPS PSUs, while 35% is time-vesting equity. But annual cash pay is 100% tied to absolute Adjusted EBIT, and the fiscal-2025 calculation excluded $35m of lease-related expense; there is no ROIC or FCF-per-share measure. The biggest signal is therefore a real but incomplete shift toward per-share rewards, rather than a pay design focused only on per-share value creation. [S1: FY2026 Proxy Statement, pp. 53–55]
