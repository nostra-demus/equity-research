This scorecard covers FY2022–FY2025 (fiscal years ended January 28, 2023 through January 31, 2026), with H1 FY2026 shown separately where it updates the assessment. The cross-module quick read and historical-financials report were available and used; upstream `01_management-and-track-record.md` was also available. No multi-year-data cap applies.

## 2. M&A Scorecard

No material M&A in the period. The annual cash-flow series shows no cash-acquisition line in FY2022–FY2025, and the FY2025 filing describes the large investment program as stores, supply chain, IT and a California distribution-centre purchase rather than a business acquisition. This is evidence against a serial-acquirer pattern, not proof that every immaterial asset purchase was absent. [CIQ Financials Annual—Cash Flow, FY2022–FY2025; FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—Capital Expenditures, p.33]

**Serial-acquirer + opportunity-cost test (CLAUDE.md §24, Filter 4).** No serial-acquirer pattern is proven from the available data, so RF-CAP-004 and its score cap do not apply. The absence of acquisition spending does not itself prove value creation; it removes one common source of value destruction. The principal allocation question is whether the organic investment produces cash and per-share earnings after the expansion cycle.

## 5. Capital-Allocation Read

**Mixed.** The best completed use is the four-year buyback program: cash repurchases exceeded stock issuance and the fully diluted weighted-average share count fell 2.69%, although H1 FY2026’s program was offset at the outstanding-share level by the note exchange and other issuance. The worst and most consequential use is the $2.88bn capex program: it consumed 80.9% of four-year CFO while reported FCF was only $679.7m, and Burlington does not disclose maintenance-versus-growth capex or project-level returns. No material M&A or debt-funded serial-acquirer pattern is visible; the single biggest capital-allocation signal is therefore whether new stores and supply-chain capacity convert this cash spend into durable per-share returns.
