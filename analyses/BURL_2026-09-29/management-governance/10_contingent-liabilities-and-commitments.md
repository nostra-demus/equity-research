## 11. Read

Burlington’s quantified current contingent obligation is $58.0m of letters of credit, only 2.90% of $2,002.248m of stockholders’ equity and 9.51% of FY2025 PAT of $610.153m. The largest disclosed contractual commitment is instead $2,558.5m of goods not yet received—127.78% of equity—which should be monitored as inventory-buying exposure, not folded into the CL total as though it were a loss. Tax provisions are explicitly recognized and the gross unrecognized-tax-benefit balance fell from $3.132m to $1.566m over two years; the real disclosure limit is that legal, regulatory, group-guarantee, recourse-financing and FX exposures are not quantified or separated. A rational minority holder should watch whether the current Q2 increase in LC arrangements and inventory commitments turns into higher working-capital strain, and request case-level amounts, provisions and outcomes if any legal matter becomes material.
