## 5. Contingency Read

The largest debt-like exposure is $5.735bn of gross operating-lease commitments, of which $610.5m is for signed sites not yet occupied; $3.993bn is already recognized, but future minimum payments exceed that discounted liability. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 3 — Lease Commitments, pp.10–11] The $2.559bn goods commitment is live and large relative to $2.002bn of equity, but it purchases inventory rather than representing a disclosed loss; cash impact depends on delivery, sales conversion and working-capital timing. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 11 — Purchase Commitments, p.20; p.5] Legal proceedings are active and unquantified: management's view that final resolution will not be materially adverse is not the same as a remote classification. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 11 — Legal, p.20]
