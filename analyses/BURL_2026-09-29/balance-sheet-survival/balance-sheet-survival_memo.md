## What This Module Found

BURL has $1,919.5m of gross financial debt, built from a $1,711.7m Term Loan, $186.1m of converts, and $21.8m of finance leases. After $703.7m of cash, strict net debt is $1,215.8m and strict net leverage—net debt divided by EBITDA, or profit before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortisation—is 0.84x on filing-derived latest-12-month EBITDA of $1,440.2m. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 10, 12–13; FY2025 Form 10-K, p.29]

The direction is mixed: strict net debt rose from the FY2025 year-end but was $75.9m lower than at the same seasonal point a year earlier. [FY2025 Form 10-K, p.43; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5–6, 30] At least $223.7m matures within 24 months, and $703.7m of cash covers that amount 3.15x; the larger refinancing concentration is the calculated $1,624.0m Term Loan residual due in 2031. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Notes 3–4; FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 4]

Liquidity is $1,645.7m, built from $703.7m of cash plus $942.0m of asset-based lending availability. Normalised free cash flow, or FCF—cash from operations minus total capital spending—is $357.0m after subtracting a $55.5m tariff refund from $412.5m of reported latest-12-month FCF, so the stated run-rate has no finite cash runway end. This is a run-rate result, not a forecast; peak seasonal working-capital needs are undisclosed and the lending facility depends on its borrowing base. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.5, p.20, p.30, Notes 3–4]

The biggest risk is an inventory and working-capital shock that turns FCF negative while also reducing the $942.0m borrowing base. That could remove liquidity while $2,558.5m of purchase commitments and gross lease commitments of $5,124.6m plus $610.5m for uncommenced leases still require cash over their respective periods; these are different bases, not a simultaneous-loss total. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Notes 3, 4 and 11]

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** Adequate. Near-term debt is covered, but the lender-test cushion cannot be measured.
- **Why it could be better than it looks:** Strict net debt was $75.9m lower year over year on the matched seasonal date, cash covers at least the next 24 months of maturities 3.15x, and the zero-mitigation 60% EBITDA-down case remains cash-positive. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.5, Notes 3–4; CIQ Financials, Income Statement, LTM 2026-08-01 — vendor export]

- **What is missing:** The current covenant-compliance calculation and exact rules governing cash transfers from subsidiaries to the Parent. This keeps covenant headroom Not assessable and caps usefulness at 75.
- **One thing to watch next:** Whether BURL funds the $186.1m 2027 Parent convert without materially reducing liquidity. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 4 and p.30]

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Liquidity runway:** How long available cash and committed funding can cover expected cash needs.
- **Refinancing risk:** The risk that debt cannot be repaid or replaced on acceptable terms when it comes due.
- **Covenant headroom:** The distance between current performance and the point where a lender test would fail.
- **Downside resilience:** How well the balance sheet holds up when earnings fall.
- **Strict net leverage:** Financial debt minus cash, divided by EBITDA; operating-lease liabilities are excluded here and shown separately.
- **EBITDA:** Profit before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortisation.
- **Free cash flow (FCF):** Cash from operations minus total capital spending.
- **Asset-based lending:** A credit facility whose availability depends on eligible assets, such as inventory and receivables.
- **Borrowing base:** The value of eligible assets that supports how much can be drawn under an asset-based facility.
- **Operating-lease liabilities:** Recorded obligations for future rent payments under operating leases.
