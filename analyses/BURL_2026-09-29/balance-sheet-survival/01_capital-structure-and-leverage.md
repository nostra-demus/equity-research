# Capital Structure & Leverage — BURL

Burlington Stores, Inc. is a U.S. GAAP operating company reporting in USD. Amounts are USD millions and are measured at August 1, 2026 unless stated otherwise. “Strict” net debt means interest-bearing financial debt, including finance leases, less cash and cash equivalents; it excludes operating-lease liabilities, consistent with §15. This report does not make a solvency verdict.

## 5. Leverage Ratios

Latest LTM “reported EBITDA” below is a filing-derived GAAP calculation of $1,440.2m, not a GAAP subtotal reported by BURL. It is the consistent calculation used in the earnings cross-module read. Company-defined adjusted LTM EBITDA is $1,461.1m: FY2025 adjusted EBITDA of $1,293.3m, less prior-year first-half adjusted EBITDA of $483.8m, plus current first-half adjusted EBITDA of $651.6m. ## 6. Leverage Trend

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | Latest | Direction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Net debt (strict §15 basis) | 483.3 | 716.1 | 849.8 | 1,215.8 | Up versus fiscal year-end |
| Net debt / EBITDA (same strict basis) | 0.57x | 0.68x | 0.68x | 0.84x | Up versus fiscal year-end |

The fiscal-year trend is upward: strict net debt rose $732.5m from FY2023 to the latest point, and the ratio rose from 0.57x to 0.84x. The near-term driver is cash rather than higher gross financial debt: cash fell $528.8m in H1 FY2026 while BURL spent $426.7m on reported capital expenditures, $167.4m on repurchases, and $128.6m in cash in the convertible-note exchange. [FY2024 Form 10-K, pp.50, 52–53; FY2025 Form 10-K, pp.43, 45–46; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5–6, 30; Note 4, p.13]

There is a relevant counter-series: at the same seasonal point one year earlier, strict net debt was $1,291.7m, so the latest $1,215.8m is $75.9m lower year on year. This does not overturn the fiscal-year-end direction; it shows that the current increase from $849.8m at January 31 is seasonal and capital-deployment-related, while the same-date comparison has improved. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.5; FY2025 Form 10-K, p.43]

## 7. Leverage Anchor Summary (canonical numbers for downstream agents)

- **Gross debt:** **$1,919.5m**, financial debt including finance leases and excluding operating leases; this is `$1,711.7m` Term Loan Facility + `$186.1m` convertible notes + `$21.8m` finance leases, before $5.9m deferred financing costs. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.10, 12, 30]
- **Canonical net debt:** **$1,215.8m strict §15 basis** (`$1,919.5m − $703.7m` cash). **Broad net debt is also $1,215.8m**, because no incremental liquid short-term investment balance is reported. Do not substitute the CIQ $5,202.4m lease-inclusive measure for either figure.
- **Cash and liquid investments:** **$703.7m cash and equivalents; $0.0m separately reported liquid short-term investments.** Restricted/trapped cash is not separately disclosed.
- **EBITDA base:** **$1,440.2m latest LTM filing-derived GAAP EBITDA** and **$1,461.1m latest LTM company-defined adjusted EBITDA**. Both are latest, not normalised; Q2 includes a $55.5m tariff refund. The normalised sensitivity proxy is **$947.0m** four-year CIQ-standard EBITDA, labelled inference rather than a reported EBITDA base.
- **Net debt / EBITDA:** **0.84x strict / filing-derived GAAP** and **0.83x strict / company-defined adjusted**. On the normalised CIQ-standard proxy, strict net leverage is **1.28x**.
- **Reporting currency:** **USD millions.**

The material qualification downstream agents must carry is the split between financial debt and leases: operating-lease liabilities are $3,992.6m and CIQ’s $5,202.4m vendor net-debt figure is lease-inclusive. The strict $1.216bn figure is the canonical leverage anchor; the lease liability remains a separate fixed-commitment risk.
