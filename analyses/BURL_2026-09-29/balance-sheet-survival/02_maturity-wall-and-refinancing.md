## 5. Refinancing Read

Only $20.1m is due in the next 12 months and at least $223.7m within 24 months, versus $703.7m cash; the refinancing issue is therefore timing and future cost, not a near-term wall. The $1.624bn calculated 2031 Term Loan residual is 84.6% of financial debt, and the five-year 6.74% proxy is 166 bps above the 5.08% contractual weighted coupon, or 297 bps above the 3.77% swap-adjusted rate. The single biggest risk is that the 2031 bullet must be refinanced at a wider credit spread or after the swap protection ends; its actual future market spread is not proven from available data.

BURL survives the next 12 months under a market-closure assumption of no new unsecured issuance: $20.1m of stated current debt is covered 34.9 times by consolidated cash before using the $942.0m disclosed ABL availability. This assumes that consolidated cash and the ABL remain legally available to the relevant borrowers and that optional buybacks are suspended; the filing does not quantify all Parent/subsidiary distribution constraints. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 30–32; Note 4, pp.12–14; FY2025 Form 10-K, Schedule I, Note 1]
