# Balance-Sheet-Survival Module — BURL (Synthesis)

## Abstract

BURL’s 0.84x strict net leverage—net debt divided by filing-derived EBITDA, or pre-interest/tax/depreciation/amortisation profit—rose from 0.68x at FY2025, but matched-season net debt improved year over year. [Q2 FY26 10-Q, pp.5,10,12–13; FY25 10-K, p.43] At least 11.7% matures within 24 months, covered 3.15x by cash; the calculated $1.624bn bullet is due in 2031. [Q2 FY26 10-Q, Notes 3–4] $703.7m cash plus $942.0m asset-based credit availability leave no finite run-out date on normalised cash after capital spending, but the lender-test cushion is unknown. The zero-mitigation cash stress survives a 60% EBITDA fall; its first limit is a non-contractual 4.0x marker at 77.7%. [Q2 FY26 10-Q, pp.5,20,30, Note 4; CIQ Financials, LTM 2026-08-01] Verdict: Adequate; Parent cash access and covenant terms remain unclear.

## 1. Solvency Verdict

- **Verdict:** Adequate
- **Reporting basis:** U.S. GAAP operating retailer; USD millions; balance-sheet amounts are at 1 August 2026 and flow measures are latest-12-month (LTM) to that date unless stated otherwise. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, cover and financial statements]
- **Net leverage (net debt / EBITDA):** **0.84x strict basis**, using $1,215.8m of financial net debt (`$1,711.7m` Term Loan + `$186.1m` converts + `$21.8m` finance leases − `$703.7m` cash) and $1,440.2m of latest LTM filing-derived GAAP EBITDA, a calculation from GAAP lines rather than a company-reported GAAP subtotal. **Gross leverage is 1.33x on the same EBITDA basis.** The same strict net debt is 0.89x the more conservative $1,360.7m CIQ-standard EBITDA used in the stress test. Operating-lease liabilities are excluded from strict net debt and carried separately. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 10, 12–13; FY2025 Form 10-K, p.29; CIQ Financials, Income Statement, LTM 2026-08-01 — vendor export]
- **Liquidity runway (how long cash sources cover uses):** **No finite runway on the stated normalised latest-12-month free cash flow (FCF, cash from operations minus total capital spending) run-rate**; $1,645.7m of committed liquidity equals $703.7m cash plus $942.0m disclosed asset-based lending (ABL) availability, while $357.0m normalised FCF (`$412.5m` reported LTM FCF − `$55.5m` tariff refund) exceeds the $20.1m next-12-month maturity by $336.9m. This is a run-rate result, not a forecast; peak seasonal working-capital need is undisclosed and the ABL is borrowing-base-dependent. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.5, p.20, p.30, Notes 3–4]
- **Maturity wall (concentrated debt due dates; % within 24 months):** **At least 11.7%**, or $223.7m comprising $20.1m in year one and $203.6m in year two; the percentage excludes unallocated long-term finance-lease timing. The largest maturity is the calculated $1,624.0m Term Loan residual due in 2031, 84.6% of financial debt. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Notes 3–4; FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 4 — Scheduled Maturities]
- **Tightest covenant + headroom (distance before a lender test fails):** **Not assessable.** No maintenance threshold, current covenant calculation, covenant-EBITDA definition, addback cap, springing trigger, or cure right is disclosed. The 3.50x ratio in Schedule I is a restricted-payment condition, not proven to be a live maintenance test. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Schedule I, Note 1; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 4]
- **Stress break point (EBITDA decline that breaks it):** **No contractual break point is assessable.** In the zero-mitigation bound, no modelled cash break occurs through a 60% EBITDA decline; the first displayed limit is the explicitly non-contractual 4.0x maximum-strict-net-leverage marker at a **77.7%** decline. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 4; CIQ Financials, Income Statement, LTM 2026-08-01 — vendor export]
- **Solvency strength /100:** **70**, capped because $186.1m of Parent debt is material and exact subsidiary-upstreaming access is unclear.
- **Liquidity runway /100:** **82**, reflecting the $1,645.7m liquidity build above and positive normalised FCF, reduced for undisclosed peak seasonal need and borrowing-base sensitivity.
- **Refinancing risk /100 (higher = worse; inverted):** **24**, reflecting cash coverage of the next 24 months but the concentrated 2031 residual.
- **Covenant headroom /100:** **Not assessable**
- **Downside resilience /100:** **80**, reflecting cash survival through the zero-mitigation −60% case, reduced because contractual covenant survival cannot be tested.
- **Data quality /100:** **78**, with recent filings, cash flow, maturities and ABL availability but no usable covenant test or primary rating report.
- **Overall usefulness /100:** **75**, hard-capped by the absence of a usable covenant-compliance test.
- **Biggest solvency risk:** A severe inventory and working-capital shock could reduce both the $357.0m normalised FCF run-rate (`$412.5m` reported LTM FCF − `$55.5m` tariff refund) and the $942.0m borrowing-base availability while $2,558.5m of purchase commitments and $5,735.1m of gross operating-lease commitments (`$5,124.6m` future minimum payments + `$610.5m` uncommenced leases) still require cash over their respective periods. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.20, Note 3, Note 4]

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** Strict leverage omits $3,992.6m of recognised operating-lease liabilities; CIQ’s $5,202.4m lease-inclusive net-obligation measure (`$1,913.6m` debt carrying value + `$3,992.6m` operating leases − `$703.7m` cash) is 3.82x its $1,360.7m LTM EBITDA, so the 0.84x headline does not capture the full fixed-cost burden. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 3; CIQ Financials, Balance Sheet and Income Statement, Q2/LTM 2026-08-01 — vendor export]
- **Strongest bull point:** $703.7m of cash alone covers the at-least $223.7m next-24-month maturity load (`$20.1m` in year one + `$203.6m` in year two) 3.15x, while EBITDA covers gross interest 18.4x. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.5, Notes 3–4; CIQ Financials, Income Statement, LTM 2026-08-01 — vendor export]
- **Single killer risk:** An inventory shock that both turns FCF negative and cuts the ABL borrowing base could remove the facility precisely when purchase and lease commitments draw cash; no borrowing-base stress sensitivity is disclosed. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Notes 3, 4 and 11]
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** Evidence against the conservative Adequate call is that strict net debt is $75.9m lower than at the same seasonal point a year earlier, and the zero-mitigation −60% EBITDA cash case remains positive. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.5; CIQ Financials, Income Statement, LTM 2026-08-01 — vendor export]

## 5. Survival Summary

BURL has $1,919.5m of gross financial debt (`$1,711.7m` Term Loan + `$186.1m` converts + `$21.8m` finance leases) and $1,215.8m of strict net debt after $703.7m cash. Gross leverage is 1.33x and strict net leverage is 0.84x latest LTM filing-derived GAAP EBITDA; net leverage worsened from the fiscal year-end, but strict net debt improved by $75.9m year over year on the matched seasonal date. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, pp.5, 10, 12–13; FY2025 Form 10-K, p.43] The at-least $223.7m due within 24 months (`$20.1m` year one + `$203.6m` year two) is covered 3.15x by cash, so it is self-funded on the consolidated balance-sheet read; the refinancing dependence sits mainly in the calculated $1,624.0m 2031 Term Loan residual. Cash of $703.7m plus $942.0m of ABL availability and the normalised FCF run-rate support no finite runway end, but the ABL depends on its borrowing base and Parent cash access is not quantified. Contractual covenant proximity cannot be stated, despite 18.4x EBITDA/interest and 4.8x post-capex EBITDA/interest coverage. The zero-mitigation bound shows no cash-driven need for an equity raise or distressed asset sale at 30%, 40% or 60% EBITDA declines; the −60% cash result remains positive, but survival without a covenant waiver cannot be tested. [CIQ Financials, Income Statement, LTM 2026-08-01 — vendor export; Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 4]

## 6A. Survival Playbook (non-speculative levers)

- **Refinancing actions already taken:** BURL repaid a $50.6m promissory note in February 2026, exchanged $111.0m of converts in March 2026, extended the ABL to July 2030 in July 2025, and added $500m of Term B-7 loans in June 2025. These actions show prior execution, not a committed plan for the remaining $186.1m converts or the 2031 bullet. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 4]
- **Asset sales:** No asset-sale programme is announced in the available specialist evidence.
- **Capex cuts:** The filing gives an approximately $875m FY2026 net-capex plan, but the specialists found no evidence establishing how much can be cut without harming the store and supply-chain programme. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, p.30]
- **Shareholder returns:** The remaining $217.6m repurchase authorisation is discretionary and no dividend is committed. *Inference, not from filings:* suspending future buybacks would preserve cash. BURL spent $167.4m on repurchases in the first half. [Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 8 and p.30]
- **Covenant amendments:** No prior amendment pattern or lender commitment supporting a future waiver is evidenced in the specialist outputs.
