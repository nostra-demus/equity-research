# Revenue Drivers — KAR

Karoon Energy Ltd (ASX: KAR) is an upstream oil & gas producer reporting in US dollars under IFRS (AASB). All figures below are USD unless stated. The company's H1/FY2026 half-year result was scheduled for release today, 27-Aug-2026 [`2Q26 Activities Report (Jul-22-2026)`, p.7], and is not present in this data pool — every figure in this report is built from the FY2025 Annual Report (year ended 31-Dec-2025, filed 25-Feb-2026), the FY2025 results call (26-Feb-2026, verbatim), and quarterly Activities Reports through 2Q26 (period ended 30-Jun-2026). Treat this as the pre-print baseline, not a post-print read.

## 1. Segment Decomposition Status

Segment decomposition applied — business-model `03_segment-map.md` is available and confirms Karoon reports three geography-based operating segments: Brazil (Baúna, 77.9% of FY2025 revenue), USA (Who Dat/Dome Patrol/Abilene, 22.1%), and Corporate/Other (no revenue, cost/financing only) [FY2025 Annual Report, Note 2(a), p.85]. Brazil clears the module's own effective-single-segment threshold on gross profit (91.2% of consolidated gross profit) but not on revenue (77.9%, just under the 85% bar) — this report treats Karoon as a Brazil-led, two-asset business, decomposing both segments in Section 5.

## 2. Revenue Driver Tree

| Business Type | Revenue Formula |
|---|---|
| Commodity producer | Production × realized commodity price |

Company-specific formula: **Revenue = Σ over {Baúna oil, Who Dat NRI oil/condensate/NGLs, Who Dat NRI sales gas} of (volume sold in the period × the realized price for that stream)** — Baúna's realized price is a cargo-sale price benchmarked to global crude (Brent-linked, before Brazilian royalties/export tax, which are deducted further down the P&L as cost of sales, not netted from the revenue line); Who Dat's realized prices are already stated on a Net Revenue Interest (NRI) basis, i.e. after government and overriding royalties are deducted [`Q4 2025 Activities Report`, p.3, notes 4–5]. Karoon is a **price-taker**: it outsources 100% of Baúna marketing to Shell Western Supply and Trading and holds no oil-price hedges as of the most recent filing [business-model `06_value-chain.md`; FY2025 Annual Report, Note 20(b)]. There is no volume-independent "ARPU" or "take rate" concept — revenue moves almost mechanically with barrels sold and the price realized on those barrels.

**Separating market growth from company execution:** none of Karoon's FY2025 revenue decline is due to a shrinking customer base, lost market share, FX, or a divestiture — it is entirely a price-taking exposure to a global commodity benchmark plus a modest sales-timing effect (§6). ## 6a. Decomposition Attribution and Residual (MODULE_RULES "Driver Attribution" / §15)

**Price and Volume rows: asserted from primary disclosure, not modelled ratios.** The CFO stated the $100.4m (price) and $47.5m (volume) figures directly on the FY2025 results call — these are reported numbers, not this report's own multiplication of a ratio, so no derivation is shown for them; they are cited at face value from a verbatim transcript [FY2025 Earnings Call, 26-Feb-2026, p.5].

**Independent cross-check — this report's own segment-level reconstruction, showing the arithmetic (§15).** Built from the `Q4 2025 Activities Report`'s year-to-date (Jan–Dec) production, sales, revenue and average-realized-price table (p.3) — the FY2024 vs FY2025 full-year columns, same basis both years (annual sales volume × annual average realized price per product stream). This ratio (each stream's FY2024 average realized price) is applied only to the FY2024→FY2025 volume change for that same stream, never carried to a different period or a different stream:

```
Baúna oil:
  Volume effect = (Sales_FY25 − Sales_FY24) × Price_FY24 [Q4 2025 Activities Report, p.3]
                = (7.35 − 7.83) MMbbl × $77.60/bbl = −0.48 × 77.60 ≈ −$37.2m
  Price effect  = (Price_FY25 − Price_FY24) × Sales_FY25
                = ($66.57 − $77.60)/bbl × 7.35 MMbbl = −11.03 × 7.35 ≈ −$81.1m
  Modelled Baúna revenue change ≈ −$118.3m vs actual −$116.8m (606.2→489.4)
  → basis matches (same product, same two adjacent years, no cross-basis use); residual +$1.5m (rounding of 2-decimal-place disclosed volumes/prices)

Who Dat liquids (oil/condensate/NGLs, NRI):
  Volume effect = (1.95 − 2.07) MMbbl × $75.84/bbl ≈ −$9.1m
  Price effect  = ($63.35 − $75.84)/bbl × 1.95 MMbbl ≈ −$24.4m
  Modelled change ≈ −$33.5m vs actual −$33.3m (157.1→123.8); residual −$0.2m

Who Dat sales gas (NRI):
  Volume effect = (3.66 − 4.49) Bcf × $2.95/mcf × 1,000 ≈ −$2.4m
  Price effect  = ($4.20 − $2.95)/mcf × 3.66 Bcf × 1,000 ≈ +$4.6m
  Modelled change ≈ +$2.2m vs actual +$2.0m (13.3→15.3); residual −$0.2m

Sum of this report's own model: Volume ≈ −$48.7m, Price ≈ −$100.9m, Total ≈ −$149.6m
  → basis matches throughout (all six effects use each stream's own FY2024→FY2025 volume/price, no substitution across products or periods)
```

**Reconciliation:** this report's independent bottom-up model (Volume −$48.7m, Price −$100.9m, Total −$149.6m) sits within **$1.7m (≈1.1%) of the CFO's own stated total decline of $147.9m**, and within $1.2m of the CFO's own Volume figure and $0.5m of the CFO's own Price figure. This is a small, rounding-driven residual (the Activities Report discloses volumes and prices to two decimal places, not to the precision the audited P&L uses) — **not a material unexplained gap.** Both the primary-disclosed bridge (§6) and this report's own independent segment-level reconstruction agree that **price explains roughly 68% of the FY2025 revenue decline ($100.4m of $147.9m)** and volume/sales-timing explains the remaining ≈32%. Section 7's "single biggest driver" claim rests on this well-reconciled arithmetic, not a rounded-away gap.

## 7. The Single Biggest Revenue Driver

**Realized oil/liquids price (Brent-linked at Baúna, Mars-differential-linked at Who Dat) is the single biggest revenue driver.** It explains roughly 68% of FY2025's $147.9m revenue decline by both management's own disclosed bridge and this report's independent segment-level reconstruction (§6a) — more than double the volume/sales-timing effect. A 10–20% move in realized price would swing group revenue by roughly $60–125m (10–20% of FY2025's $628.6m), against a production base that is ~93% oil/liquids-weighted [business-model `10_external-dependency.md`] and currently carries **zero active price hedges** [FY2025 Annual Report, Note 20(b); `2Q26 Activities Report`, p.5] — so essentially none of that move would be cushioned. The current direction is genuinely two-sided and should not be read as a clean "improving" signal: FY2025's full-year trend was a 14–16% YoY price decline, while 2Q26 realized prices spiked 33–55% quarter-on-quarter and 47–58% year-on-year, explicitly attributed by management to a geopolitical risk premium (Middle East conflict, Strait of Hormuz shipping disruption risk) rather than a demand recovery [Shareholder/Analyst Call, 21-May-2026, p.5]. Critically, **management's own 2H26 planning assumption is $60–70/bbl** [`2Q26 Activities Report`, p.2] — well below the $94.56/bbl Baúna price actually realized in 2Q26 — which is the company's own evidence that the recent spike is not being treated internally as a durable run-rate. Any reader extrapolating the 2Q26 price level forward into the imminent H1 2026 result or beyond is working from a base the company itself does not use.
