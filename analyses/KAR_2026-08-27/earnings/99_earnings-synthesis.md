# Earnings Module — KAR (Synthesis)

## Abstract

Karoon Energy's earnings setup is mixed: full-year revenue fell 19.0% in FY2025 (US$776.5m to US$628.6m) and adjusted EBITDA margin compressed 641bps once two one-off gains are stripped, while a 2Q26 oil-price spike (Baúna crude +33% quarter-on-quarter to US$94.56/bbl) is being offset by a twice-cut CY2026 production guide (down ~11% at the midpoint since January) and management's own 2H26 planning assumption of US$60–70/bbl, below the 2Q26 print itself. Consensus is present and revenue is well pre-known for the imminent half, but revision breadth is negative across every metric and the H1 2026 EBITDA bar rests on one analyst. The biggest risk is that Karoon carries zero active price hedges against a single dominant, ~US$110.7m-swing oil-price variable, while the H1/FY2026 half-year result — due the same day as this report — is not yet in the data pool, so every figure here is pre-print positioning. Cash conversion is genuinely well-disclosed (CFO/EBITDA 66.0% FY2025, above 90% in two of the last four years), but the red-flag scan rates the overall setup "Material concerns." Verdict: mixed earnings setup, not yet a clean acceleration or deceleration.

## 5. Earnings Setup Summary

### Revenue Setup

The current revenue trajectory is not a single sustainable trend — it is two offsetting forces moving in opposite directions at the same time. The FY2025 decline was driven almost entirely by realized price (68% of the US$147.9m drop), a variable Karoon does not control and does not hedge, while the 2Q26 recovery is the same variable moving the other way on a geopolitical risk premium that management's own 2H26 planning assumption (US$60–70/bbl) explicitly does not treat as durable [`02_revenue-drivers.md` §7]. The single factor that would flip the direction cleanly is whether the Middle East/Strait of Hormuz risk premium persists or reverses before 2H26 volumes are sold — with zero hedges, there is no cushion either way. A second, independent factor pulling the other way regardless of price is volume: CY2026 production guidance has been cut ~11% at the midpoint since January 2026 on the Who Dat E-riser leak (no confirmed resumption inside the 12-month window) and the Baúna SPS-92 shut-in, so even a favourable price outcome sits on top of a shrinking volume base. Reported and underlying revenue tell the same story here (no accounting-adjustment gap on the revenue line itself), but the reported-vs-run-rate gap is real: the 2Q26 price level is roughly 42–52% above management's own 2H26 planning assumption, a gap this synthesis treats as the key limit on reading the 2Q26 print as "revenue accelerating."

### Margin Setup

Current statutory margins are not sustainable as reported: the FY2025 statutory EBITDA-margin "expansion" (+257bps) and statutory NPAT-margin "expansion" (+357bps) are both artefacts of two large one-off gains (US$35.3m FPSO-disposal gain, US$21.2m contingent-consideration fair-value gain) that will not repeat, and once stripped, adjusted EBITDA margin actually compressed 641bps and Underlying NPAT margin compressed 1,046bps [`01_historical-financials.md` §6; `03_margin-drivers.md` §3]. The cleaner, decision-useful measure — Underlying EBITDAX margin — compressed a much smaller 155bps, because unit production costs are largely fixed and moved favourably (US$13.20/boe FY2025 vs US$13.60/boe FY2024) [`03` §4]. The segment or driver that would take the largest bite out of consolidated margins on a 10–20% adverse move is the same one dominating revenue — realized oil price, worth ~US$110.7m of average EBITDAX swing against ~US$21.9m for volume, ~US$11.55m for unit production cost [`07_earnings-sensitivity.md` §3] — because cost of sales moved only 2.5% against a 19% revenue decline in FY2025, so nearly all of a price shock flows straight through to margin. Karoon has no contractual pass-through or hedging mechanism protecting margin: it sells every barrel at a same-day benchmark-linked price with no negotiated escalator to lag and holds zero active price hedges, so it is a pure price-taker on its dominant margin driver, with only the mechanical, non-discretionary offset of ad valorem royalties (which fall automatically as price falls, providing a small buffer estimated at ~9.5% of a given revenue shock) [`03` §3, citing `business-model/06_value-chain.md` §2].

### Quality Check

The single largest gap between reported and economic earnings is the effective tax rate, which swung from 46.7% (FY2024) to 12.4% (FY2025) on a non-cash, Brazilian-real-denominated deferred-tax FX translation line worth more than US$87m across the two years — a gap `06_earnings-quality.md` names as the single biggest quality concern, ahead of the FPSO-disposal and contingent-consideration gains, and one that is widening in unpredictability rather than narrowing, since it flipped sign year over year [`06` §8, §10]. The adjustments management uses to bridge statutory to "Underlying" results are not genuinely stable one-offs: a different basket of items has been labelled "non-recurring" in every disclosed year (flotel/transition/relocation costs in FY2025; advisory/transaction costs in FY2024), while unsuccessful-exploration-well write-offs (US$10.9–12.0m/year) recur in both disclosed years yet are still excluded as if one-off [`06` §4]. To model normalized earnings for next year, this synthesis would start from Underlying EBITDAX (not statutory EBITDA, not GAAP/IFRS NPAT, and not the company's "Underlying NPAT" line as published), because Underlying EBITDAX is the only measure `03_margin-drivers.md` §4 finds is not currently distorted by the FY2025 one-off gains — and even then, the analyst should add back the unsuccessful-well write-offs as a recurring cost the company's own "Underlying" reconciliation improperly excludes.

### Consensus Bar

For Karoon to beat the current H1 2026 bar by a material margin, the swing would have to come from below the EBITDA line, not revenue — revenue is unusually well pre-known (0.99 ratio to already-disclosed Q1+Q2 cash sales revenue, `04_guidance-consensus.md` §1A) — specifically via unit production cost tracking the low end of guidance or D&A/interest/tax items landing more favourably than the single H1 analyst modelled, echoing the FY2024/FY2025 pattern of Net Income/EPS beating even when Revenue/EBITDA missed. The bar is most likely set incorrectly on the cost side, not the revenue side: the entire H1 2026 EBITDA estimate comes from one "Initiation of Coverage" analyst dated 2026-08-04, and US$46.5m of already-incurred flotel/FPSO integrity spend — disclosed before that estimate was set — is roughly 37% of the US$125.9m EBITDA bar, leaving real risk that this single-analyst model either fully captured or materially missed that cost line [`05_beat-miss-setup.md` §3, §10]. A meaningful share of the currently improving consensus revision picture is not a durable tailwind but a lagging response to a real, filed operational deterioration (the twice-cut CY2026 production guide and twice-raised capex guide), so revision breadth being negative should not be read as sandbagging — `04` §7 states plainly the cuts reflect genuine operational deterioration, not analyst over-caution, meaning any reversal of that negative breadth would itself require a genuine operational improvement, not just conservative repositioning unwinding.

## 9. Simple Summary

- Revenue fell 19% in FY2025 on falling oil prices (68% of the drop) and a smaller sales-timing effect (32%); it spiked back up in 2Q26 on a geopolitical price premium, but production guidance was cut twice and management itself does not treat the price spike as durable.
- Reported margins look like they improved in FY2025 (EBITDA margin +257bps, NPAT margin +357bps) — both are one-off accounting gains (an FPSO-sale gain and a contingent-payment fair-value gain), not real improvement; strip them and the underlying margin compressed 641bps.
- Earnings are largely clean and cash-backed: the company turns most of its EBITDA into real cash (66-96% over the last four years), and the one negative free-cash-flow year is fully explained by a one-time asset purchase, not weak operations.
- The consensus bar for the next result is fair, not easy — the top line is already well known from disclosed quarterly sales, but the profit estimate rests on just one analyst's guess about a large already-spent maintenance cost.
- The next-quarter setup is balanced: could go either way, hinging on costs below the profit line, not on revenue surprise.
- The single biggest swing factor is the oil price Karoon sells at — it moves earnings roughly five times more than the next-biggest factor, and the company has zero price insurance (hedges) in place.
- Earnings volatility is high (74/100 on a scale where higher is worse) because of that unhedged price exposure, a single production vessel carrying most of the profit, and a non-operated US asset with an unresolved leak.
- This module is useful for the master synthesizer: it gives a clear, well-cited picture of a genuinely two-sided setup, but its biggest limitation is timing — the actual H1 2026 results were due the same day this report was written and are not yet available, so every number here should be checked against the real print as soon as it lands.
