## 7. Bar Assessment

**Bar is high.** There is no formal revenue or GAAP-EPS guide from management to compare directly with the FQ3 consensus, but the consensus hurdle has risen after Q2: FQ3 revenue is up 7.0% versus three months ago, FY2026 revenue is up 4.4%, and FY2026 GAAP EPS is up US$0.02. [Capital IQ Estimates — Trends, FQ3 2026 and FY2026] Last-month breadth is also positive at 8 EPS increases and no cuts, plus 5 revenue increases against 2 cuts. [Capital IQ Estimates — Revisions, Last Month, FY2026]

The latest Q2 result was a GAAP-EPS beat, but the preceding Q1 result missed both revenue and EPS; this history does not neutralize the higher revised bar. [Capital IQ Estimates — Surprise, Fiscal Quarters, Q1–Q2 2026] Management's approximate 20% full-year efficiency-ratio objective and non-numeric risk-adjusted-NIM commentary do not establish that the revenue or EPS consensus is achievable. [Q2 2026 earnings call, prepared remarks and Q&A, 2026-08-13, pp.8–11]
