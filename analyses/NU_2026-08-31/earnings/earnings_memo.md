## What This Module Found

The latest quarter improved sharply, but the longer trend did not: Q2 2026 IFRS revenue—revenue under NU's accounting rules—rose 50.29% to US$5.513bn and diluted EPS—earnings per share after potential dilution—rose 66.3% to US$0.2162, while FY2025 revenue growth slowed to 37.0% from 43.4% in FY2024. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, p.5; Nu Holdings Ltd., Form 20-F, FY2025, F-6–F-7]

The main driver was card and loan interest, which rose US$1.320bn and supplied 35.99 percentage points, or 71.6%, of Q2's 50.29% revenue growth; the credit portfolio reached US$39.4bn and grew 37% FX-neutral, meaning without currency movements. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, Note 6, pp.14–15; Q2 2026 Earnings Presentation, slide 13]

Sequential risk-adjusted NIM—net interest margin after credit costs, the cost of expected borrower losses—rose 294 basis points, a unit for changes in percentage rates; credit income supplied 178 basis points, or 60.5%, lower credit cost supplied 115 basis points, and the bridge residual was 1 basis point, while the resulting 12.4% level is an expansion-period result that management said was not a floor. [Q2 2026 Earnings Presentation, slide 16; Q2 2026 Earnings Call, Q&A, pp.10–11]

That gain is not proven durable because deliberate risk expansion added 24 basis points, or 0.24 percentage points, to early delinquency and US$170m to the allowance bridge, while the disclosed downside macro state raises expected credit loss (ECL) by US$504.700m before tax. [Q2 2026 Earnings Call, prepared remarks, pp.7–8; H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, Note 3(a), p.15]

The FQ3 consensus requires US$5.937bn of revenue, or 42.3% year-on-year growth, and 39.0% reported EPS growth after the revenue estimate rose 7.0% in 90 days; management says originations are normalising, gives no numeric revenue or EPS guide, and identifies purchase-volume seasonality in Q4 rather than Q3. [Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus and Trends, FQ3 2026; Q2 2026 Earnings Call, Q&A, p.9–10]

Earnings quality is not cleanly cash-backed: H1 cash from operating activities was US$(1.242)bn, filing-built trailing CFO was US$(1.382)bn, ECL coverage rose 149 basis points to 16.86%, and the US$991.0m deferred-tax benefit equalled 51.3% of H1 net income. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, pp.5, 12 and 37; Notes 13–14 and 30]

Management's adjusted net income adds back recurring share-based compensation—pay awarded in shares—of US$359.0m in FY2025, after US$408.2m in FY2024 and US$289.3m in FY2023, so the synthesis starts from reported IFRS income rather than the adjusted figure. [Nu Holdings Ltd., Form 20-F, FY2025, Item 5, “Adjusted Net Income (Loss)” reconciliation]

The strongest bull evidence is Q2's 50.3% revenue growth, 66.3% diluted-EPS growth, and the US$39.4bn credit portfolio; the strongest bear evidence is the raised FQ3 hurdle and the possibility that later credit losses erase the spread gain. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, p.5; Q2 2026 Earnings Presentation, slides 13, 16 and 18; Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus and Trends, FQ3 2026]

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** Mixed earnings setup; the next-quarter setup favors miss.

- **Why it could be better:** Q2 revenue grew 50.3%, diluted EPS grew 66.3%, and a US$39.4bn portfolio growing 37% FX-neutral can still feed interest income with a lag. [H1 FY2026 Interim Financial Statements, p.5; Q2 2026 Earnings Presentation, slide 13]

- **Why it could be worse:** The FQ3 bar requires 42.3% revenue growth after a 7.0% estimate rise, while later losses from higher-risk lending could reverse the margin gain. [Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus and Trends, FQ3 2026; Q2 2026 Earnings Call, pp.7–8]

- **What is missing:** Country/product profit and credit-cost detail that separates Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, cards, and unsecured loans. [Earnings Data Triage — NU, §§3–6, 2026-08-30]

- **What to watch next:** Whether standalone FQ3 clears or misses its basis-matched revenue and EPS markers together with same-basis delinquency, ECL coverage, credit cost, and efficiency evidence. [Beat / Miss Setup — NU, §4, 2026-08-30]

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Consensus:** The combined analyst estimate.
- **ECL:** Expected credit loss, the amount a lender estimates it may not collect.
- **CFO / cash from operating activities:** Cash generated or used by operating activity.
- **Deferred-tax benefit:** A tax benefit whose cash effect may occur in another period.
- **Allowance:** The balance set aside for expected credit losses.
- **Accruals:** Reported income or expense not yet matched by cash.
- **EBITDA:** Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization.
- **IFRS revenue or income:** Revenue or income reported under NU's accounting rules.
- **Diluted EPS:** Earnings per share after potential dilution.
- **FX-neutral:** Measured without the effect of currency movements.
- **Risk-adjusted NIM:** Net interest margin after credit costs.
- **Credit cost:** The cost of expected borrower losses.
- **Basis points:** A unit used for changes in percentage rates.
- **Adjusted net income:** Management's income measure after its stated adjustments.
- **Share-based compensation:** Pay awarded in shares.
- **Same basis:** Measured using the same period, scope, and definition.
