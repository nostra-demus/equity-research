## Plain-English Glossary

- **Peer read-through:** A peer-company signal used to form a view on NU.
- **Peer coverage:** The share of NU's disclosed business exposure addressed by eligible peer calls.
- **Reportable segment:** A business unit disclosed separately in formal financial reporting.
- **Benchmark data-sufficiency:** The breadth and quality of the peer evidence available for comparison.
- **Read-through weight:** How much influence an eligible peer signal should have on the NU view.
- **Direction confidence:** Confidence that the peer signal points positively or negatively for NU.
- **FX-neutral:** Measured without the effect of currency movements.
- **Profit pool:** The profit earned by a defined market or customer group.
- **Dispersion:** How widely peer results or views differ.
- **Outlier:** A peer whose result or view differs materially from the group.
- **Candor:** How directly and completely management communicates.
- **Consensus:** A combined analyst estimate.
- **Consolidated IFRS revenue:** Group revenue reported under NU's accounting rules.
- **Line item:** A specific reported financial measure.
- **Comparable:** The matching period and measurement basis used for a valid comparison.
- **Falsification boundary:** A specific result that would show a peer-derived inference was wrong.
- **Inference:** A conclusion drawn from evidence rather than a directly reported NU fact.
