## 6. Sufficiency Verdict

- **Verdict:** Insufficient data
- **Reason:** Financial institution — requires a separate solvency framework (CET1 / LCR / NSFR / asset quality). NU is a regulated banking and payments group, so debt/EBITDA, FCF runway, and corporate covenant tests would give a misleading survival read despite the presence of primary financial statements. [FY25 Form 20-F, Item 4.B — Prudential Framework and Capital Requirements, pp. 58–59; Q2 FY26 Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Notes 22–25]
- **Sections that can run:** None under the balance-sheet-survival corporate framework. A dedicated financial-institution review could use the audited and interim financials, regulatory-capital disclosures, deposits, asset quality, liquidity, and funding notes.
- **Critical missing items:**
  - Current regulatory-capital ratios and buffers by regulated entity, including CET1 / Tier 1 / total capital, risk-weighted assets, and the binding minimums.
  - Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR), deposit concentration and runoff assumptions, and the liquid-asset eligibility mix.
  - Asset-quality and provisioning detail by vintage, delinquency bucket, geography, and secured/unsecured product.
  - Current facility availability, covenant thresholds / calculation terms, and change-of-control or cross-default provisions.
- **Single highest-value missing document:** A Q2 FY26 regulatory-capital, liquidity, and asset-quality disclosure pack for the consolidated prudential group and each material regulated subsidiary.
