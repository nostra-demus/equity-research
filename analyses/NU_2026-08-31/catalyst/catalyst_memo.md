## What This Module Found

NU has real scheduled catalysts—events that can change the investment view—over the next 12 months, but timing confidence is mixed because only three of five calendar entries have hard dates. [analyses/NU_2026-08-31/catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md, §§1–5]

The nearest event is FQ3 results on a vendor-scheduled 12 November 2026, but NU has not confirmed that date. [data/NU/Capital IQ Events Calendar, 2026]

FQ3 is also the most important event: it tests consensus—the vendor's combined analyst estimate—of US$5,936.74m revenue and US$0.22164 GAAP EPS, the vendor's reported earnings-per-share field, on a standalone-quarter basis with no reported stub to add. [data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, header and Fiscal Quarters, FQ3 2026; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md, §§1A, 7]

That bar requires 42.3% revenue growth and 39.0% GAAP-EPS growth from Q3 2025, so a miss or weaker credit-cost, delinquency, or NIM—net interest margin, the lending spread after funding costs—outlook is the biggest bearish catalyst. [data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §§1, 3]

The single killer risk is treating the vendor date as firm or a numerical beat as durable even though NU has no numeric revenue or EPS guide and temporary credit-cost relief could flatter the print. ## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** Dated, evidenced near-term catalysts — the 1 January 2027 Brazilian tax start is filing-set and inside six months.

- **Why it could be better:** The tax start could remove a known uncertainty if disclosure shows a limited net effect or proven pricing, mix, or cost offsets. [data/NU/H1 FY2026 Unaudited Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1.1, p. 12]

- **Why it could be worse:** The vendor-scheduled FQ3 print requires 42.3% revenue growth and 39.0% GAAP-EPS growth from Q3 2025, and a nominal beat may not last if credit quality weakens. [data/NU/Capital IQ Estimates — Consensus, FQ3 2026; analyses/NU_2026-08-31/earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md, §§1, 3]

- **What is missing:** An issuer-confirmed FQ3 release date, the single biggest missing scheduled-event data point. [analyses/NU_2026-08-31/catalyst/00_catalyst-data-triage.md, §§1, 3]

- **What to watch next:** Whether NU confirms the 12 November 2026 FQ3 release date; until then it remains a vendor field that may change. [data/NU/Capital IQ Events Calendar, 2026]

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** An event that can change the investment view.
- **Consensus:** The vendor's combined analyst estimate.
- **Score cap:** A limit that prevents a score rising above a set level.
- **GAAP EPS:** The vendor's reported earnings-per-share field.
- **Standalone quarter:** One reporting quarter on its own, without earlier periods added.
- **Credit cost:** The cost of expected borrower losses.
- **NIM:** Net interest margin, the lending spread after funding costs.
- **AGM:** Annual general meeting.
- **Market-structure event:** A change in how shares or markets trade.
- **Risk-adjusted NIM:** Net interest margin after also considering credit losses.
- **Margin-loan credit facility:** The borrowing arrangement that reaches maturity on the stated date.
- **Liquidity:** Funds available to meet payments.
- **Repurchase authorization:** Permission, not an obligation, to buy back shares.
- **Fully diluted share count:** Shares outstanding after including potential share issuance.
- **Per-share benefit:** A gain measured for each share.
