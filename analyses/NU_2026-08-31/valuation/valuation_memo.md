## What This Module Found

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Fair value:** The module’s estimate of what one share is worth.
- **P/TBV:** Price-to-tangible-book-value; share price divided by tangible net assets per share.
- **NTM P/E:** Next-twelve-month price-to-earnings; share price divided by forecast earnings per share for the next year.
- **Residual income:** Profit left after charging shareholders’ required return against equity capital.
- **Cost of equity:** The return shareholders require for taking the investment risk.
- **ROE:** Return on equity; profit earned relative to shareholders’ equity.
- **Margin of safety:** The price cushion below estimated fair value; a negative figure means price is above fair value.
- **Diluted shares:** The share count after including potential shares that could reduce each holder’s ownership percentage.
- **Enterprise-value bridge:** A reconciliation between the value of the operations and the value attributable to equity holders.
- **DCF:** Discounted cash flow; a method that converts expected future value into today’s money using a required return.
- **SOTP:** Sum of the parts; valuing separate business units and adding them together.
- **Valuation football field:** The low-to-high range produced by several valuation methods.
