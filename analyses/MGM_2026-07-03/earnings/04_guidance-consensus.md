## 7. Bar Assessment

**Bar is fair** — consensus has been revised down materially from 18 months ago (FY 2026 EPS was $2.90 eighteen months ago vs. $1.95 now, a 33% cut; EBITDA was $5,047M vs. $4,624M now, down 8%), but the near-term FQ2 2026 bar looks only modestly beatable given that: (1) Q1 2026 was a miss on EPS; (2) the most recent 3-month revision breadth is still negative on EPS (8 down vs. 4 up for FY 2026); and (3) management provided no hard quantitative guidance to benchmark the market's bar against. Revenue estimates have actually drifted slightly higher over 3 months even as EPS came down, signaling ongoing margin compression concern rather than a low-set top-line bar. The FQ2 2026 consensus at $0.61 EPS Normalized and $4,441M revenue is achievable if the second quarter skips a repeat of the self-insurance surprise, and convention-season momentum holds — but the bar is not obviously depressed to the point where a beat is the base case. The clearest upside lever is table hold (which management cannot control and which swung FQ4 2025 EPS by an estimated +$0.10–$0.15 above normal).
