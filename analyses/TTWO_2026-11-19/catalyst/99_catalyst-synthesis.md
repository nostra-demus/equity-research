## Abstract

Take-Two has real, dated catalysts, not just a story, but only about half of its calendar carries a hard date. The nearest dated event is a small $29.4m convertible note maturing 2026-12-15, which tests whether the company repays from cash or leans on its revolver. The most important is the FQ3 results on 2027-02-09, the first reported period containing any Grand Theft Auto VI revenue: net bookings at or above $3,752.3m would confirm the launch, while anything below $3,573.6m misses the Street. The biggest bearish event is the same date, because simply reiterating full-year guidance implies a fourth quarter 13.5% below consensus. Timing is proven but the skew is down.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation *(CLAUDE.md §8)*

- **Strongest bear point (dated, skews DOWN):** **2027-02-09** carries a guide-down that needs **no operational disappointment at all** — the FY2027 reiteration arithmetic implies **Q4 ≈ $1,687.8 vs the Street's $1,950.7 (−13.5%)** while the Street's FQ3 bar of **$3,573.6 sits above the top of guidance ($3,500)**, so **landing at the top of the guided range is still a −2.1% consensus miss** [`01` §3; `earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md`]. Behind it sit two more dated downside legs: the **Maximum Cash Netting Amount falls from $2,000.0 to $1,500.0** from the quarter ending 2027-03-31, losing **$173.6 of netting and about 4.8 percentage points of covenant headroom with no change in trading** — disclosed **only in `FY25 10-K, Exhibit 10.41`**, not in the 10-K or 10-Q narrative; and the **2027-03-31 net-cash claim**, which needs roughly a **$1.37bn swing in six months** from strict net debt of **$1,370.4 at 2026-09-30** and **would have failed on each of the last two reported halves** (H1 FY27 CFO **−$173.5**; H1 FY26 **+$83.7**).
- **Strongest bull point (proven date + evidence it exists):** the **same 2027-02-09 print**, in the other direction — FQ3 Net Bookings **≥ $3,752.3** (**+113.6%** on the Q3 FY26 comparable of $1,757.1; **+9.56%** on the guided midpoint of $3,425, a margin reached once in the last four quarters) **with** management-basis EBITDA **≥ $1,013.5** (+205.4% on Q3 FY26's $331.9) **and** the FY2027 range raised to a top **≥ $8,554.7**. The EBITDA leg is the highest-quality bar in the set because **consensus $965.3 and the derived guidance midpoint $963.5 agree to +0.2%**, so a beat there is an earnings-power result rather than a tax or timing artefact. Both legs are **capable of failing**: tested on the last two reported periods, Q1 FY27 printed +1.9% vs the Street (neither line fires) and Q2 FY27 printed **−3.3% (miss line fires)**; Q3 FY26's +11.1% fires the beat line.
- **Single killer risk specific to the calendar:** **the headline catalyst has already half-happened, and what remains to be timed is the measurement, not the event.** GTA VI went on sale **2026-11-19 — the run date itself** — with **no reported period containing a dollar of it, no preorder count, no unit figure, no price, no attach rate, no disclosed tie ratio (management declined to quantify it), and no alternative-data panel in this pool** to read it early. The first measurement is **2027-02-09, a date taken from a tier-5 vendor header** because the pool's own event calendar expired. Everything that would extend the story past FY2027 — the PC version, seven "TBA" titles, a buyback restart, "accretive M&A" — is **undated**.
- **Catalyst sold as bullish that is actually vague or §24-flagged:** (i) the **buyback restart** — 10.0 shares (millions) of authorisation remain, but **zero shares were repurchased in FY2024, FY2025 and FY2026**, nil in every month of Jan/Feb/Mar 2026, and the programme "does not require us to repurchase shares and may be suspended or discontinued at any time", with **no expiry, record date or schedule**. **No dividend has ever been paid and none is expected.** A buyer should not price this. (ii) **"Accretive M&A"** — **§24 Filter 4**; bare "accretive" language is not evidence, the ~85% historical goodwill write-off rate is, and `balance-sheet-survival/04` names a **debt-funded deal as one of only two realistic paths to a covenant breach** (a **Qualified Acquisition** lifts the ceiling from 3.75x to **4.25x for four quarter-ends**).
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** the company **missed its own guidance in the quarter just reported** ($1,601.3 against $1,620–1,670, with recurrent spending at **−9% versus a guided −5%**); the Street has been **moving money between quarters rather than raising the year** (~$176 of Net Bookings and $0.29 of EPS pushed into the launch quarter in the ten days after the last print, FY2027 flat, FY2028 cut 1.8%, FY2028 revenue −2.6% / EPS −2.5% / EBITDA −2.0% over the month); **$2,149.7 (91.6%)** of the FY2026 capitalised development balance sat on **unreleased** titles = **61.2% of total stockholders' equity** (carried balance $2,577.3 at 2026-09-30, $43.6 already charged in H1 FY27), so a shortfall against the forecast inside that carrying value arrives as a non-cash write-down **after** the cash has gone out; and one management figure from the 2026-11-09 call **already fails to reconcile** — "geared at less than 1x LTM EBITDA" against **2.02x strict** (1.89x on vendor EBITDA, 1.25x broad-basis).
