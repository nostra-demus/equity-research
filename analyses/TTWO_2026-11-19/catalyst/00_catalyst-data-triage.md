## 3. Triage Verdict

Plainly: the calendar **will be able to carry proven dates, but only in two and a half of the eight categories**, and the dated spine is thin rather than rich.

What is genuinely dated and evidenced:
- **Refinancing is the strongest dated set**, and it comes from a filing note rather than a vendor: $29.4 of convertible notes due **2026-12-15** (under a month away) and **$600.0 of 3.70% 2027 Notes due 2027-04-14**, with $800.0 more at **2028-03-28**. Against an undrawn $1,000.0 revolver maturing 2030-05-19, these are small, well-telegraphed events — dated, yes; market-moving, the calendar agent must argue separately.
- **The next print has a date and an explicit bar**: **2027-02-09** for FQ3 FY2027, against company guidance of **$3.35bn–$3.50bn** net bookings versus **$1.76bn** a year earlier. Carry the source qualifier: the **date is vendor-derived** (Capital IQ Estimates header, §4 tier 5) because the pool's own Events Calendar export expired — its latest row is 2026-11-12. The **guidance figures** are the company's own, from the 2026-11-09 release and call.
- **One operational date, and the timing is awkward**: **GTA VI launched 2026-11-19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X\|S** — the run date itself. It is proven and material, but as of this report it is converting from an upcoming catalyst into an outcome. The forward read has to be built from what follows the launch (sell-through, recurrent spending, the undated PC version), not from the launch date.

What the calendar will **not** be able to date, and must therefore write as window or thematic:
- **No forward AGM or record date** (2026-09-17 meeting is past; the pool's DEF 14A export is an empty file; next meeting only a mid-September 2027 pattern, sourced from documents outside this pool).
- **No scheduled regulatory or legal decision** — the contingencies note discloses routine litigation only, with no named matter, court or date.
- **No dated policy decision** — the tariff dispute is live and undated; the tax changes have "multiple effective dates" that are not stated.
- **No dated capital-return event** — no dividend has ever been paid and none is expected; the buyback has 10.0 shares of authorisation left but recorded **zero** repurchases across three fiscal years and has no expiry or schedule.
- **No market-structure event** at all.
- **Seven of the eight announced future titles are "TBA"**, so the post-GTA VI slate is a pipeline, not a calendar.

Two caps the specialist and synthesis agents should carry forward, and one source caution:
- The MODULE_RULES cap for "no scheduled-event data at all" does **not** apply (there is scheduled-event data), and the "upstream modules did not run" cap does **not** apply (all five ran). The cap that **does** bite is category-level rather than global: with five of eight categories undated, **timing visibility cannot be scored as if the calendar were broadly dated**, and any catalyst outside earnings/refinancing/GTA VI must be labelled window or thematic, never dated.
- **M&A is explicitly on management's own agenda — "pursue accretive M&A" (CEO, 2026-11-09 release) — and it is undated.** Under §24 Filter 4 this is not a conviction-lifting catalyst and must not be presented as one; it belongs in the calendar as an undated, two-sided item.
- **Everything numeric here is extractor-UNCHECKED** (no `numeric_reading_check` on any of the 30 sources). Restating layers must carry that status rather than upgrade it.

No fail-fast abort is issued. Per `CLAUDE.md` §17 and this module's rules, a thin or partly undated calendar is a valid finding, and the specialist agent proceeds on the dated spine above while writing the rest honestly as undated.
