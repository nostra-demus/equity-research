## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

## 5. Calendar Read

**Only about half of this calendar is proven-dated, and the undated half is where the company's own growth story lives.** Seven rows carry a hard date — today's GTA VI launch, the $29.4 convertible on 2026-12-15, the quarterly covenant test from 2026-12-31, the FQ3 print on 2027-02-09 (vendor-dated, with the company's own bar), the 2027-03-31 fiscal year-end that carries both the net-cash deadline and the cash-netting step-down, ARPA on 2027-04-01, and the $600.0 of 2027 Notes on 2027-04-14. **The near-term skew is bearish, and the reason is arithmetic rather than judgment.** The launch is now behind the stock rather than in front of it; the next dated events are a covenant test, a mechanical loss of covenant headroom, a cash-flow claim that would have failed on both of the last two reported halves, and a print where the Street's FQ3 bar sits **2.1% above the top of management's own guided range** while a simple FY2027 reiteration implies a **Q4 13.5% below the Street's own number**.
