## 2. Triggered Disqualifiers — Detail

No disqualifier triggered.

Two items are recorded here because they are near the line or are genuine gaps, not because they trigger:

**Operating cash flow was negative in two of the last four years.** FY2024 (−$16.1m) and FY2025 (−$45.2m) were cash-negative at the operating line, and FY2023 cleared zero by $1.1m — three consecutive years in which the operations threw off essentially no cash, before FY2026 recovered to +$624.3m [FY25 10-K and FY26 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows]. The threshold for disqualifier #8 is three negative years of four; two is below it, so there is no lock. It is logged as a near-miss in Section 4 and handed to the balance-sheet-survival module, where the same pool's vendor credit panel scores overall relative credit health "Bottom" with operational and solvency categories also "Bottom" [CIQ Credit Health Panel → Summary — vendor export]. That panel is a tier-5 vendor score, not a filing, and it is not a disqualifier under any of the 8 tests.

**Integrity signal (CLAUDE.md §24, filter 1): none found, hard or soft.** The pool contains no allegation, enforcement action, investigation, restatement, material weakness, or media claim touching the honesty of the controller or senior management. There is therefore nothing to lock on and nothing soft to route. One governance **data gap** is routed to the management-governance module rather than resolved here: because the DEF 14A export is empty, this pool cannot show (a) whether any officer or director has pledged shares, or (b) the dollar size of the ZelnickMedia Corporation / ZMC Advisors related-party arrangements, which the exhibit index proves are live [FY26 10-K, Exhibit Index, Ex. 10.26–10.29]. Neither is evidence of a problem; both are facts this pool cannot see, and the correct answer is "not disclosed in available data," not an inferred figure.
