### The economic moat test (required)

> Return on capital **below** cost of capital: **−0.7% through-cycle ROIC (5-year FY2022–FY2026 average)** vs **~8.2% WACC** (**−890 bps**) — ROIC computed from the audited filings on a normalized 21.0% tax rate and cross-checked against the vendor series; WACC a labelled CAPM group estimate, **"Inference, not from filings"**, inputs shown below. On the latest LTM figure the gap is **−1,040 bps** (−2.2% vs ~8.2%); on the most generous 10-year through-cycle reading it is still **−400 bps** (+4.2% vs ~8.2%).

**Build 1 — the return, computed, not swallowed.** Management discloses no ROIC/ROCE headline, so there is no management figure to cross-check; the figure that circulates is the **vendor's**, and I have computed my own against it as the rules require.

- **Numerator (NOPAT).** EBIT comes from the filings, not the vendor: FY2026 loss from operations **$(104.2)**, stated as **(1.5)% of revenue** [`FY26 10-K, MD&A, p.43`]. LTM to Sep-30-2026 = FY26 $(104.2) − H1 FY26 $(76.4) + H1 FY27 $(153.0) = **$(180.8)** [`Q2 FY27 10-Q, p.2 — six months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025`]. **Flagged divergence:** the vendor records LTM EBIT at **$(112.5)** — about **100 bps of margin more favourable** than the filings-derived $(180.8). The filing wins (§4, §5) and the more conservative figure is used; the vendor read is printed in the table beside it.
- **Normalized structural tax rate: 21.0%.** FY26's **reported** effective tax rate was **(50.8)%** against a 21.0% US federal statutory rate — a distorted single-year rate, not the structural one [`FY26 10-K, MD&A — Provision for income taxes`; cross-checked in `ciq_facts.json`-adjacent `CIQ Financials → Income Statement`, which marks the FY2023–FY2026 effective rate "NM"]. **What I stripped to get to 21.0%:** the full valuation allowance maintained against US net deferred tax assets, the **$45.7** software-development tax-credit benefit (+1.2% in the rate reconciliation), the foreign-earnings effect (−0.5%), and goodwill-impairment-related non-deductible items; state and local tax net of federal benefit adds +0.4%, so 21.4% is the fuller structural rate and **21.0% is the figure used** [`FY26 10-K, Note — Income Taxes, rate reconciliation`]. **This 21.0% is the anchor the DCF must reconcile to** — `valuation/04_intrinsic-dcf` §1 must compute NOPAT on the same rate, not on the (50.8)% reported rate. Note the sign is invariant to the choice: with EBIT negative, a *higher* assumed tax rate makes NOPAT *less* negative, so 21.0% is the conservative end and no plausible rate turns the return positive.
- **Denominator (invested capital), on a gross basis.** Total debt + total equity, averaged: FY2026 = $2,958.3 + $3,510.9 = $6,469.2; FY2025 = $4,105.9 + $2,137.7 = $6,243.6; average **$6,356.4**. Sep-30-2026 = $2,911.4 + $3,673.6 = $6,585.0; average with FY2026 = **$6,527.1** [`FY26 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheets`; `Q2 FY27 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, p.1`; corroborated in `CIQ Financials_Annual → Balance Sheet` — vendor export].
- **Result.** FY2026 ROIC = $(104.2) × (1 − 0.21) ÷ $6,356.4 = **−1.3%**. LTM ROIC = $(180.8) × 0.79 ÷ $6,527.1 = **−2.2%**. Against the vendor's **−0.8% (FY26)** and **−1.0% (LTM)**: my computed figures are **40–120 bps more negative**. Same sign, same band, same verdict — but the divergence is flagged and the computed figure is the one used (§4).
- **Net-cash check — not applicable, and the cash-netted basis is worse anyway.** Take-Two is **net debt**, not net cash: net debt **$1,237.8** on the vendor basis (which may net short-term investments — a **broad** basis, per the sidecar's own caution), against total debt $2,911.4 and cash plus short-term investments $1,673.6 at Sep-30-2026 [`ciq_facts.json → net_debt_m, total_debt_m`; `CIQ Financials_Quarterly → Balance Sheet` — vendor export]. The invested capital above is therefore already **gross** (no cash netted). For completeness, netting cash out of the denominator makes the return *worse*, not better: FY2026 ROIC on an ex-cash capital base of $4,629.0 average = **−1.8%**.

**Build 2 — the through-cycle return, because the latest year is a cycle TROUGH, not a peak.** `07_business-quality.md §4` establishes the direction of the distortion and it runs the opposite way to the usual trap: the LTM window to Sep-30-2026 is the **pre-launch trough** between Borderlands 4 (September 2025) and **Grand Theft Auto VI, released today, November 19, 2026**, with Q2 FY27 net bookings −18% year on year [`Q2 FY27 earnings release, Nov 9 2026, p.1`; `Q2 FY27 10-Q`]. So the latest figure must not be used raw either — **it is labelled a trough and the test runs on a through-cycle average**:

| Through-cycle window | Basis | Average ROIC | vs ~8.2% WACC |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| FY2022–FY2026 (5 years, the post-Zynga company) | +9.0%, −4.3%, −3.1%, −4.2%, −0.8% | **−0.7%** | **−890 bps** |
| FY2017–FY2026 (10 years, the most generous reading) | 6.0, 6.9, 7.4, 11.2, 13.4, 9.0, −4.3, −3.1, −4.2, −0.8 (%) | **+4.2%** | **−400 bps** |

[`ciq_facts.json → multi_year_trajectory` (`CIQ Financials → Ratios`, "Return on Capital %") — tier-5 vendor export.] The **5-year window is the right structural one** — Zynga was acquired in May 2022, and the FY2017–FY2021 years describe a materially smaller, pre-acquisition company that had not yet written off 85% of its goodwill. The 10-year average is shown because it is the most favourable honest reading available, and it is **still 400 bps below the cost of capital.** `07_business-quality.md` also anchors the through-cycle **EBITDA margin at roughly 12%** (ten-year average EBITDA $471.8 on average revenue $3,919.8), almost exactly where FY26 (12%) and the LTM (11%) sit — so the trough is a trough in *absolute* profit, not a collapse away from the business's normal margin.

**Build 3 — the cost of capital, sourced in the required priority, never invented.**

1. **Company-disclosed figure — sought, and it does not exist as a number.** Take-Two states it uses *"risk-adjusted discount rates **based on our weighted average cost of capital**"* in its goodwill impairment testing, and lists "discount rate" among the significant assumptions audited as a critical audit matter — but **the numeric rate is nowhere in the filings** [`FY26 10-K, Critical Accounting Policies — Goodwill`; `FY26 10-K, Note 9 (Goodwill and Intangible Assets, Net), pp.83–84`; `FY25 10-K, Report of Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm — Critical Audit Matter`]. **Comparator only, not promoted to WACC:** the weighted-average **operating-lease discount rate of 4.74% (FY2026; 4.75% FY2025, 4.56% FY2024)** [`FY26 10-K, Note — Leases, p.91`]. That is an incremental borrowing rate on lease obligations, not the group's cost of capital, and it appears here only as a floor comparator.
2. **So a labelled group CAPM estimate is used — "Inference, not from filings" (CLAUDE.md §3).** Inputs shown:
   - **Risk-free rate 3.9%** — the company's **own** disclosed input: *"Risk-free interest rate 3.8% / 3.9%"* for FY2026 market-based awards, *"based on U.S. Treasury yields"* over a 2.8-year expected service period [`FY26 10-K, Note — Stock-Based Compensation, Market-Based Awards (Monte Carlo assumptions), p.99`]. Corroborated by the company's own market-rate quote: SOFR ~**3.91%** at September 30, 2026 [`Q2 FY27 10-Q, Note — Debt / Item 3`]. A longer-dated Treasury would be higher, which would only **widen** the gap — 3.9% is the conservative end.
   - **Beta 0.92** — Take-Two's own 5-year beta [`CIQ Comps → Operating Statistics, "5 Year Beta" (subject row)`, As-Of 2026-11-19 — vendor export; peer betas in the same column run −0.45 to 1.53, median 0.65].
   - **Equity risk premium 5.0%** — **judgment, not from filings.** No equity-risk-premium figure exists anywhere in this pool. This is the single weakest input and is sensitised below.
   - **Cost of equity = 3.9% + 0.92 × 5.0% = 8.5%.**
   - **Pre-tax cost of debt 5.4%** — the company's own most recent issue coupons (**5.400% Senior Notes due 2029**, **5.600% due 2034**) and its revolver pricing of **SOFR + 1.000%–1.625%** (= 4.9%–5.5% at SOFR 3.91%), with an alternate base rate of 7.00% [`FY26 10-K, Note 11 (Debt)`; `Q2 FY27 10-Q, Note — Debt`]. After tax at 21.0%: **4.3%**.
   - **Weights** — equity $40,549.3 (187.2m shares × $216.61 day close) = **93.3%**; total debt $2,911.4 = **6.7%** [`ciq_facts.json → shares_outstanding_m, current_price, total_debt_m` (`CIQ Comps → Financial Data`; `CIQ Financials → Balance Sheet`) — vendor export].
   - **WACC = 0.933 × 8.5% + 0.067 × 4.3% = 8.2%.** Sensitised on the judgment input: an ERP of 4.5%–6.0% gives a WACC of **7.8%–9.1%**.
3. **Reality-tested, not merely assembled (CLAUDE.md §16).** The 8.2% estimate sits **above** the company's own 4.74% lease borrowing rate (as a WACC should sit above a debt rate) and is not flattered by any observable yield: the trailing earnings yield is **negative** (LTM net loss $240.5), and FCF on the §15 basis (CFO − total capex) is **$367.1 − $304.5 = $62.6**, a **0.15%** yield on the $40,549.3 market capitalisation [`ciq_facts.json → ltm_ocf_m`; `CIQ Financials_Annual → Cash Flow, Capital Expenditure` — vendor export]. The market is plainly pricing a large forward recovery rather than current cash flow, which means 8.2% is not an implausibly low rate manufactured to create a spread.

**The test is robust to every input choice.** Even at the 4.74% lease-rate floor — a deliberately unreasonably low stand-in for a cost of capital — the 5-year through-cycle ROIC of −0.7% is **540 bps** below it. There is no positive cost of capital at which Take-Two's through-cycle return on capital clears the bar. **That, not the peer margin gap, is the central fact of this report.**

## 4. Where The Company Sits

1. **Relative to peers.** **Company sits at the bottom of named peers on margin; on capital efficiency the comparison cannot be made.** On margin the numbers are unambiguous: gross margin **57.7%** against the ten-peer median **66.9%** (−920 bps), and EBIT margin **−1.8% (vendor) / −2.8% (filings-derived)** against the median **+15.9%** — fourth-lowest of eleven, ahead only of Ubisoft (−86.7%), Roblox (−17.4%) and PLAYSTUDIOS (−10.9%), and below both direct structural rivals EA (+17.9%) and Playtika (+18.9%) [`competitive-map §2A`; `CIQ Comps → Operating Statistics`, As-Of 2026-11-19 — vendor export]. On capital efficiency: **no peer ROIC, ROCE or ROE figure exists anywhere in this pool**, so **"Insufficient data to compare against named peers"** applies to the return-on-capital comparison specifically, in either direction [`competitive-map §5, gap 2`]. Two qualifiers travel with the margin read: peer windows end on different dates (March 31, 2026 for Ubisoft through November 2026), and every peer figure is the vendor's normalised read rather than that peer's audited accounts.

2. **Absolute (the economic moat test).** **The company earns a return on capital below its cost of capital** — **−0.7% through-cycle ROIC (5-year) vs ~8.2% WACC**, a gap of **−890 bps**; **−2.2% LTM vs ~8.2%**, −1,040 bps; **+4.2% on the most generous 10-year reading vs ~8.2%**, still −400 bps. This is the decisive test, and it is **not** "Not assessable": the return is computed from the audited filings and cross-checked against the vendor series, and the cost of capital is a labelled CAPM estimate whose every input is shown and whose conclusion survives the full sensitivity range (7.8%–9.1%) and even a 4.74% floor. **Whatever advantage exists in Section 2 is therefore structural, not economic.**

## 5. Moat Verdict

# No moat proven

Specifically the **"moat in structure, not economics"** case the rules name: Take-Two has a real, cited brand asset and real scale, and **neither lifts the return on capital above the cost of capital**.

The strongest moat is **brand, at 60/100** — Grand Theft Auto has sold-in over 465 million units, is described in the filing as the industry's most iconic brand, still produces record engagement thirteen years after GTA Online launched, and just carried an **$80** price against the industry's **$70** [`FY26 10-K, Item 1, pp.1–6`; `Q1 FY27 transcript, Aug 7 2026, Q&A`]. The durability test it must pass over the next five years is precise and already measurable in the company's own reporting: **the new Grand Theft Auto base must build a second durable live-service annuity that holds the group return on capital above ~8.2% across a full release cycle, not for one peak year** — tested by recurrent consumer spending as a share of net bookings against the guided fall to ~65% in FY27 from 78% in FY26, and by RCS growth against the −9% it printed in Q2 FY27 versus guidance of −5% [`Q2 FY27 investor deck, Nov 9 2026, slides 4, 5 and 8`]. Two near-dated items sit inside that window and belong to other agents but bear on brand durability: all three console publisher licences (Sony and both Microsoft agreements, covering 39.0% of FY26 revenue) **expire March 31, 2027**, and the loot-box rulemaking in six jurisdictions points at the 78.1% of revenue that is in-game monetisation.

**The hard rule is applied, not argued around.** Because the return on capital is **below** the cost of capital on every basis tested (§3), the verdict **cannot be "Strong moat"** regardless of any peer-relative reading — and in this case the peer-relative reading runs the same way anyway (bottom of the set on margin). **Durability is further discounted** because `07_business-quality.md` scored industry rate-of-change **35 (Weak, ≤40)**: half the revenue base (mobile, 50.1%) sits in a market whose barriers to entry the company itself calls *"relatively low"*, and the proof that disruption already bit is in the accounts — **$5,887.3 of accumulated goodwill impairment against $6,949.2 of gross goodwill** taken because of *"industry conditions and changes in our strategies"* (CLAUDE.md §24, Filter 5) [`FY26 10-K, Note 9, pp.83–84`].

### Moat trajectory — **eroding (not confirmed)**

**Naming and adjudicating every contradicting row (CLAUDE.md §3).** The panel is **split — three rows support erosion, one is neutral, two contradict it and one is mixed — so the word "confirmed" is not available here and is not used.**

- **Cash conversion contradicts, and it genuinely does.** CFO ÷ EBITDA went from 38.5% to 79.2% in FY26. It does not overturn the erosion read for a stated reason: the same series collapsed to **0.2% (FY23), −3.7% (FY24) and −11.9% (FY25)** in between, with operating cash flow **negative in two of the last four years** [`FY26 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows`], so this is a volatile release-timing series rather than an improving structural one — and conversion of EBITDA says nothing about whether the capital behind it earns a return, which is the row that defines the moat.
- **Realised price contradicts, and it is the strongest point against the erosion read.** An $80 price on the flagship against a $70 industry standard is a real, exercised pricing advantage and is exactly why the brand row still scores 60. It does not overturn the read because **GTA products were 12.4% of FY26 revenue**, the CEO states the policy as *"Our goal is not to maximize price"*, NBA 2K27 pricing was held flat, and the company cuts wholesale price *"typically… three to nine months after a product's initial launch"* — so the premium is narrow in coverage and deliberately not generalised [`FY26 10-K, Item 1 — Marketing, p.5`; `Q1 FY27 transcript, Aug 7 2026, Q&A`].
- **The share proxy is mixed, and `competitive-map` already adjudicated it — its qualifier is carried intact.** LTM revenue growth of +3.1% against a peer median of +14.6% points to losing ground, but FY26 revenue grew **+18.2%** and FY27 guidance implies **+19% to +22%**. Competitive-map's own verdict is quoted rather than hardened: *"Losing share on the latest twelve-month window; the read is a one-metric proxy inside a stated launch trough, is not confirmed, and is not durable evidence of competitive decline"* [`competitive-map §3`]. No market-share figure of any kind exists in this pool.
- **Gross margin is neutral, not supporting.** It is roughly flat over the window. The erosion lives entirely **below** the gross line — in the $1,770.8 marketing line (26.6% of revenue), $1,074.6 of R&D (16.1%), and development amortisation — which is why the EBITDA, EBIT and ROIC rows all move while gross margin does not [`FY26 10-K, MD&A, pp.43–44`].

**What still makes the direction "eroding" despite a split panel.** One structural fact in the window is not a cycle artefact at all: Take-Two wrote off **$5,887.3 of goodwill against $6,949.2 of gross goodwill — ~85% of all goodwill ever recorded** — explicitly because of *"industry conditions and changes in our strategies for games within the reporting unit"*, plus $577.4 and $465.3 of Developed Game Technology impairments [`FY26 10-K, Note 9, pp.83–84`]. A permanent destruction of invested capital on that scale, five consecutive years of negative ROIC, and **two disclosed unit lifetimes that both shortened in the latest filing** (player service period "six and fifteen" → **"five and fifteen"** months; title amortisation "12 to 36" → **"9 to 36"** months) are evidence about the durability of the economics, not about when a title shipped. **So: eroding on the return-on-capital, operating-margin and unit-life lines; offset so far on pricing and cash conversion; and NOT confirmed, because the window ends in a stated pre-launch trough and two panel rows plus the share proxy point the other way.** Downstream language must match that calibration — this is an eroding-but-unconfirmed read, not confirmed erosion, and it must not be carried into a scenario driver or a kill criterion as though it were settled.

**Which way I would be wrong, and when it lands.** **The single next data point is Take-Two's Q3 FY27 result — the first full quarter with Grand Theft Auto VI on sale (it released November 19, 2026) — covering the quarter ending December 31, 2026 and expected on or about February 9, 2027** [`CIQ Estimates → Consensus, "next release flagged Feb-09-2027"`; `CIQ Events Calendar` — vendor export; `Q2 FY27 10-Q`]. The bar is explicit and already in the pool: FY27 guidance of **$8,000–8,200** of net bookings against FY26's $6,721, and the vendor's consensus column carries an FY2027 estimate of **$1,570.9 of EBIT on $8,554.7 of revenue** (an 18.4% margin) [`Q2 FY27 investor deck, slides 5 and 8`; `CIQ Financials_Annual → Income Statement, estimate column` — vendor export, estimate not fact]. If that is delivered, NOPAT at the 21.0% normalized rate would be roughly **$1,241 on ~$6,500 of invested capital ≈ +19% ROIC**, i.e. roughly **1,080 bps above** the ~8.2% cost of capital — and the trajectory read flips from eroding to widening, with the moat level reopening to Narrow or better once a full cycle (not one peak year) is on that footing. If instead the launch quarter lands materially short of the guided range while the FY27 implied second-half RCS share keeps falling toward ~65%, the erosion read moves from unconfirmed to confirmed and feeds the permanent-impairment / declining-perpetuity trigger in `valuation/04` §5 and `valuation/07`.
