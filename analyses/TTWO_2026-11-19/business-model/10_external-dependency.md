# External Dependency Check — TTWO

**Filing regime:** US / SEC domestic filer, US GAAP, reporting currency USD, fiscal year ends March 31 (so "FY2026" = year ended March 31, 2026). Per triage `00_data-triage.md`. All dollar figures below are USD millions unless stated, and are taken from the filings in their own units.

**Scope note:** this report covers only what management cannot control. Business quality, management decisions, and valuation are other agents' work. Two pool items are excluded by triage instruction: the DEF 14A export (empty shell, no proxy content) and `Alphabet Inc NasdaqGS GOOGL Key Developments` (a misfiled export for a different issuer).

**Two important qualifiers on the FX sensitivity, both from the company's own words.**

1. **It is a revenue-line sensitivity, not an earnings one, and it is stated gross of the natural cost offset.** TTWO's own sentence immediately after the figure: "In our opinion, a substantial portion of this fluctuation would be offset by cost of revenue and operating expenses incurred in local currency." The company does not size "a substantial portion," so the profit effect of a given FX move cannot be computed from the pool. Treat the ±4.0% revenue figure as a **bound on the revenue line**, not a forecast of the earnings hit (CLAUDE.md §9).
2. **The hedge book does not cover this exposure.** The forward and option-collar contracts — $748.7 to sell foreign currency and $327.8 to buy at September 30, 2026, up from $349.2 and $140.3 at March 31, 2026, all under one year — are explicitly for "non-functional currency denominated cash balances and intercompany funding loans… accounts receivable and… accounts payable," are "not designated as hedging instruments," and carried an immaterial fair value at both dates. They manage balance-sheet translation, not the translation of foreign revenue. The company says so plainly: "Our hedging programs are designed to reduce, but do not entirely eliminate, the effect of currency exchange rate movements." `Q2 FY27 10-Q, Item 3`; `FY26 10-K, Item 7A (Balance Sheet Hedging Activities)`.

## 3. Classification

**Partly externally driven.**

The reasoning, stated against the alternatives:

- It is **not** company-controlled. 40.8% of revenue is non-USD with a published ±4.0%-per-10% sensitivity; 80.6% of revenue passes through five customers, four of whom each take more than 10% and each of whom can rewrite its own fee structure at will; 39.0%–41.4% of revenue rides on a console installed base two other companies build and price; and 78.1% of revenue sits in exactly the in-game monetisation that regulators in Australia, Brazil, the E.U., the U.K., Spain and Portugal are actively working on. Those are not background noise.
- It is **not** mostly externally driven either. The variables that normally make a stock a wrapper — commodity prices, freight, floating-rate debt, policy subsidies that fund the revenue line — are absent or immaterial here: no commodity input, 97.0% digital delivery, zero floating-rate debt, and the only quantified policy benefit is a $45.7 tax-credit effect on the tax line, not on revenue. Management also holds real levers: a hedge book it more than doubled in six months on the balance-sheet exposure, a direct-to-consumer channel the CEO says has "had a material effect positive on our margins in the Mobile business," a multi-platform release strategy, and pricing and content decisions. Most of all, the observed driver of results in both reported FY27 quarters was its own product — the Q2 net-bookings miss was attributed to NBA 2K content, not the economy, and the entire Q2 FY27 call was about the Grand Theft Auto VI launch (November 19, 2026) rather than any macro variable. No analyst on that call asked a macro question.
- So: **material, partly un-hedgeable exposure, sitting under outcomes whose first-order driver is the company's own release slate.** That is the middle category.

## 4. External Dependency Risk Score

# 52 / 100

**Direction warning: this score is INVERTED — HIGHER = WORSE (more dangerous external dependence).** Lower is better.

Bands: 0–20 company-controlled · 21–40 partly externally driven, hedgeable / actively managed · **41–60 material external exposure, mixed mitigation** · 61–80 mostly externally driven · 81–100 pure pass-through.
