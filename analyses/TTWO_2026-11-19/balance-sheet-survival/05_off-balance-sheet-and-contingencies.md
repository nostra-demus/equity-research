## 5. Contingency Read

The largest item is not a contingency at all in the usual sense — it is **$740.1m of binding, dated contractual commitments** to third-party developers, intellectual-property licensors, sports leagues and players' associations, and service suppliers, of which **$323.7m falls in the twelve months to 2026-03-31** and which the Q2 FY27 10-Q confirms has not materially changed; that is live, non-cancellable ordinary-course spending that `06`'s stress test must treat as a floor on cash outflow even if earnings fall, because it does not shrink with net bookings. The genuinely contingent exposure that could crystallize as a surprise is the **tax file**: $274.5m of gross unrecognized tax benefits including $55.6m of accrued interest and penalties, with **US federal, state and foreign authorities currently examining eight fiscal years (FY2018 through FY2025)** and the company stating that amounts ultimately paid "may differ materially from the amounts accrued" — and the cash is already going out in non-US jurisdictions, $83.6m to Turkey alone in FY2026 against nil US federal. On the other side, three of the four classic hidden exposures are genuinely clean here: no defined-benefit pension deficit exists to fund, no receivables have been sold under the $215.0m facility in either FY2026 or the six months to 2026-09-30, and the company asserts no structured-finance or variable-interest entities at all. If the whole $1,567.8m maximum crystallized at once it would consume 44.7% of the $3,510.9m of equity — but it cannot crystallize at once, because roughly 82% of it is dated lease and commitment payments spread over six-plus years rather than a callable claim, and the single callable item (the $2,525.0m change-of-control put at 101% of principal) is only $25.0m incremental to debt `01` already counts.

**Why the tag below is emitted, and what it does and does not mean.** The mechanical thresholds trip: maximum ÷ equity is **44.7%** against a 15% rule of thumb, and maximum ÷ recognized is **3.70x** against a 3x rule of thumb once the already-recognized operating leases are taken out of both sides — and the underlying matters are live, not dormant, on the company's own language ("currently examining" eight tax years; "reasonably possible" excess litigation loss; $323.7m of commitments due within twelve months). The tag records that the gap between what is on the balance sheet and what the company is contractually on the hook for is wide enough to matter to `06` and `99`. It is **not** a finding of a hidden or undisclosed liability: everything above is disclosed, dated and quantified except the four items named in §4 as having no quantified ceiling, and the biggest component is ordinary-course content and marketing spend rather than a claim against the company.
