## Abstract

Take-Two carries 2,519.8 of gross borrowings against 1,149.4 of cash — net debt of 1,370.4 on the strict basis, or 2.02x a company-defined, non-GAAP trailing EBITDA of 677.6 and 2.42x through the cycle, after leverage fell from 5.81x and then rose again. The wall is near: 56.5% of principal falls due within 24 months, covered 1.17x by cash and bank deposits with no market access. Liquidity runs 31.0 months on the conservative basis, and the one covenant — a 3.75x leverage ceiling — sits at 1.07x, or +53.8% headroom on conservative netting. Stress cash and earnings together and that covenant breaks near a 46% EBITDA fall. Verdict: adequate, with the 24-month refinancing resting on one unreported product cycle.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation *(CLAUDE.md §8; fix F37)*

- **Strongest bear point:** the cash engine behind the structure is roughly half what the EBITDA headline implies, and the next two years' funding depends on it reversing in one product cycle. CFO/EBITDA was **50.6%** over the LTM and below 70% in five of six periods [`earnings/06` via `04` §1]; free cash flow was negative in **three of the last five fiscal years** ((185.2) FY2024, (234.9) FY2025, (356.2) H1 FY27); and on the cash-backed EBITDA base of **373.2** net leverage is already **3.67x today** and crosses **6.12x at a −40% haircut** [`06` §2]. For guided FY2027 operating cash flow of **over 1,000.0** to land after a first half that printed **(173.5)**, the six months to 2027-03-31 must generate **over 1,173.5** — against a best-ever full year of **434.1** [`03` §3a].
- **Strongest bull point (the steelman):** the debt terms are about as benign as a levered structure gets, and the near wall is already paid for. **100% of drawn borrowings are fixed-rate** (weighted-average coupon 4.59%, no swaps needed), **100% unsecured with no collateral and no borrowing base**, **exactly one maintenance covenant** with **no springing trigger, no minimum-liquidity test and no interest-coverage test**, a **committed 1,000.0 revolver to 2030-05-19 with 997.9 available** and two one-year extension options, **no dividend and no committed buyback**, and the 12-month maturities of 629.4 covered **1.83x by cash & equivalents alone** [`01` §1, §7; `02` §3–§4; `04` §2; `03` §1]. A **+200 bp** rate shock changes drawn interest cost by **$0.0**, and the company's net interest exposure runs the *other* way — it earned **90.0** of interest income over the LTM, so falling rates are the adverse direction [`02` §3].
- **Single killer risk (solvency-specific):** the **cash-only-netting covenant anchor at −40% plus a working-capital shock**. The 3.75x ceiling is the only financial test that can be failed, its cushion is mostly the contractual netting of cash, there is **no equity cure right** — a breach needs a lender waiver or amendment — and the conservative anchor already sits at **+4.7%** of headroom in the own-history −43.4% scenario and at **−1.1%** at −40% plus a repeat of the LTM 225.0 deferred-revenue outflow [`04` §2, §3; `06` §2].
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** plenty, in both directions. (a) Management's own framing does **not** reconcile with the engine's numbers and is **not relied on** — on the Q2 FY27 call the Executive Chairman said the balance sheet is "aimed at being very conservative and very strong… we're geared at less than 1x LTM EBITDA", which is below **every** basis computed (strict 2.02x / 1.89x, broad 1.25x / 1.17x, vendor broad-and-lease-inclusive 1.71x), with no stated basis [`02` §5]. (b) The vendor credit panel puts TTWO in the **bottom quartile on all four of overall, operational, solvency and liquidity** against 25 interactive-entertainment peers, alongside an **S&P BBB** indication — both tier-5 vendor reads, with **no rating-agency rationale or outlook in the pool**, so no rating trajectory may be inferred [`01` §7; `02` §5]. (c) Against the bear case, the engine's own covenant build is deliberately conservative — covenant EBITDA of 797.2 omits seven uncapped add-back clauses and the broad clause-(c) reading that would lift it to 1,209.1, so the error in 1.07x runs toward **too little** headroom, not too much [`04` §6 qualifier 3]. (d) The measured realised-offset case (+178.5) shows a −40% shock becoming −13.7% effective with the 24-month liquidity gap disappearing — the mitigated base rate beside the zero-mitigation bound [`06` §2].

## 5. Survival Summary

**(a) How levered, and which way it is moving.** Moderately levered, and the direction has reversed twice for different reasons. Net leverage fell from **5.81x (FY2025) to 1.23x (FY2026)** and has since risen to **1.89x–2.02x** — but the fall was bought with **shareholders' money, not operating cash** (1,150.0 of notes repaid using proceeds from a May 2025 equity issuance that lifted the share count 61.7% since 2022, with zero buybacks in FY2024–FY2026), and the rise since is **pure cash burn with no new borrowing**, reconciled to the dollar. Gross leverage is **3.72x** on the company-defined base (4.30x including operating leases). The number that matters most is neither of those: on the **cash-backed** base of 373.2 the company is at **3.67x today**, because it spends roughly 1.7x as much cash on games (716.3 over the LTM) as it charges in amortisation (411.9), and EBITDA converted to operating cash at only **50.6%**.

**(b) Is the near-term wall self-funded or refinancing-dependent?** Self-funded for twelve months, refinancing-or-launch-dependent for twenty-four. The 629.4 due inside twelve months is covered **1.83x by cash & equivalents alone** and nothing breaks under a modelled 12-month market closure at any haircut tested. The 1,429.4 due inside twenty-four months is covered **1.17x by cash plus liquid bank deposits with no market access** and **1.87x** including 997.9 of committed, non-borrowing-base revolver availability — so it is payable without an open bond market, but **paying it leaves about 244.2 on the balance sheet**, and replenishing that rests on guided FY2027 operating cash flow of **over 1,000.0** after a first half that printed **(173.5)**, i.e. **over 1,173.5 in six months against a best-ever full year of 434.1**. There is **no announced issuance and no announced asset sale in this pool, and none is assumed**.

**(c) Runway, and how close the tightest covenant is.** The conservative gross-obligations runway is **31.0 months** (19.4 months on in-hand liquidity before the revolver and before any cash generation), and it does not fall below the 12-month window in any case this evidence supports — including the company's own **worst audited year** of cash generation (37.1 months) and a **zero-mitigation bound** annualising the latest half-year (23.9 months). The single maintenance covenant in the whole structure — a **3.75:1.00 maximum Leverage Ratio**, with **no springing trigger, no minimum-liquidity test, no interest-coverage test and no equity cure right** — sits at **1.07x (+71.4%)** on full contractual cash netting and **1.73x (+53.8%)** on the conservative cash-only anchor. Two dated, mechanical tightenings arrive before any trading deteriorates: the 29.4 Zynga convertible repays **2026-12-15** and the netting allowance steps down **2,000.0 → 1,500.0 on 2026-12-31**, together costing about **4.8 percentage points** of headroom from the quarter ending 2027-03-31 — visible only in the credit-agreement exhibit, in neither filing's narrative.

**(d) Where it first fails, and is a normal recession survivable?** **The covenant fails first, not the cash box — and on the conservative anchor it fails at about a 46% EBITDA decline** (45.6% joint cash-and-EBITDA stress with cash-only netting; 59.9% on full netting; 71.4% only if cash is frozen, which this covenant's own arithmetic forbids, because its numerator rises as cash drains while its denominator falls). **A normal recession is survivable without an equity raise, an asset sale or a waiver:** at **−30%** the structure needs a 37.2 revolver draw by the 24-month mark, and at **−40%** a 172.7 draw — both well inside the 693.3 the covenant permits — with committed liquidity still showing a 1,833.7 twelve-month surplus, covenant headroom of +40.6% on full netting, and interest covered 3.00x. **A −60% decline is not survivable unaided:** the covenant breaches at the 12-month quarter-end on **both** netting cases, and the 443.7 draw the 24-month gap needs **exceeds the 95.4 the covenant permits**, so a **lender waiver or amendment** becomes necessary with no equity cure available. Two cases in between deserve naming rather than averaging: the **own-history −43.4%** scenario (FY2022 peak 671.0 to FY2025 trough 379.5) leaves only **+4.7%** of headroom on the conservative anchor, and **−40% plus a repeat of the LTM 225.0 deferred-revenue outflow breaches it outright (−1.1%)**. First *visible* strain is far earlier: a **27.3%** decline empties cash plus deposits over 24 months and converts the funding plan from a balance-sheet fact into a covenant-conditional one. Every one of those figures is a **zero-mitigation bound**; at the **measured** realised cost response of +178.5 a year, a −40% shock becomes a **−13.7%** effective shock, the 24-month gap disappears, and headroom stays above +66%.

## 7. Note To The Final Synthesizer

- **Leverage, direction, both bases.** **Gross debt 2,519.8** (borrowings at carrying value; face 2,529.8; **2,911.4 including operating leases** — US GAAP keeps them off the debt line) at **3.72x** EBITDA. **Net debt 1,370.4 on the strict §15 basis — the module's canonical figure — at 2.02x**; broad basis 846.2 at 1.25x; vendor 1,237.8 (broad **and** lease-inclusive) at 1.71x. **Direction: halved then partly rebounded** — 5.81x → 1.23x → 1.89x — with the fall bought by an **equity issuance, not operating cash**, and the rebound caused by **cash burn, not new borrowing**. The multiple you quote depends entirely on the base: **2.42x mid-cycle is the central estimate, 1.30x on company guidance is a floor not a forecast, and 3.67x on cash-backed EBITDA is the cash-truthful figure**. **There is no GAAP EBITDA** — the GAAP operating result is a **loss** of (180.8) LTM — so every multiple is **non-GAAP**, and the canonical 677.6 is **partly engine-computed**. Do not headline a single multiple without its base and cycle label.
- **The wall, and whether refinancing is secured.** **56.5% of principal (1,429.4) within 24 months; 24.9% (629.4) within 12; weighted-average maturity 2.93 years** — not laddered. **Refinancing is NOT secured**: there is no announced or committed issuance and no announced asset sale in this pool. It is **pre-funded instead**: 24-month cover of **1.17x** on cash plus liquid deposits with no market access, **1.87x** including the revolver. The fiscal-2028 "cliff" of 1,400.0 is **two payments 11.5 months apart** (600.0 on 2027-04-14, 800.0 on 2028-03-28) — carry both readings. Refi step-up roughly **+90 to +145 bps** over a 4.59% coupon, the upper leg being an **indicative, unverified, mismatched-tenor bound**.
- **The runway and what it depends on.** **31.0 months** on the conservative gross-obligations basis; **19.4 months in-hand** before the revolver and before any cash generation; **24 to 57 months** across the net-of-FCF cases; **no finite runway** if FY2027 guidance lands. **Zero of the first 19.4 months needs free cash flow to appear.** It depends on three things in sequence: time (the in-hand cash box), then the revolver staying available (which needs the 3.75x covenant to hold), then Grand Theft Auto VI converting into cash. **82.2% of the cash (945.0) is offshore with an unquantified repatriation tax**, and the **peak intra-quarter working-capital need is not disclosed**, so treat every runway figure as the **upper** end of its case.
