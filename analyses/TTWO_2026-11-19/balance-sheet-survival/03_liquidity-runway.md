The coverage **multiple** on the same inputs is `2,671.5 ÷ 1,034.2` = **2.58×**, i.e. 2.58 years of obligations covered — **2.58 × 12 = 31.0 months**. The multiple is not the month count; stating 2.58 as "months" would understate the runway roughly twelve-fold.

## 5. Liquidity Read

**The runway is 31.0 months on the conservative gross-obligations basis — 19.4 months on cash and deposits alone, before the revolver and before any free cash flow — and it does not drop below the 12-month window in any case this evidence supports, including the company's own worst audited year of cash generation (37.1 months) and a zero-mitigation bound that annualises the latest half-year's −356.2 free cash flow (23.9 months).** The reason is simple and it is a balance-sheet fact, not a forecast: **1,673.6 of cash and liquid bank deposits against 629.4 of debt maturing in the next twelve months**, with **no dividend, no committed buyback, no minimum-liquidity covenant to reserve against, no borrowing base on the revolver, and 100% of drawn debt at fixed rates** [`Q2 FY27 10-Q, Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets`, `Note 10 (Debt)`; `04` §2].

**What the runway depends on is time, not this year's cash flow.** Zero of the first 19.4 months needs free cash flow to appear; the next 11.6 months need the revolver to stay available, which needs the 3.75x leverage covenant to hold (engine-computed at 1.07x today, with a dated netting step-down on 2026-12-31 that costs ~4.8 points of headroom by itself); and everything past roughly 31 months needs the Grand Theft Auto VI cycle to convert into cash. The size of that dependency is quantifiable and uncomfortable: for the company's guided FY2027 operating cash flow of **over 1,000.0** to land after a first half that printed **(173.5)**, the six months to 2027-03-31 must generate **over 1,173.5 of operating cash flow and over ~1,046.8 of free cash flow** — against a best-ever full year of **434.1**.

**The single biggest liquidity risk is the composition of the cushion, not its size: 82.2% of the cash (945.0) sits offshore with an unquantified repatriation tax, 37.4% of the committed liquidity is a revolver whose availability is conditional on a covenant the engine — not the company — has computed, and the whole 24-month funding plan reduces to one unreported product launch.** If GTA VI under-delivers, paying the 1,429.4 of 24-month maturities from liquidity leaves about **244.2** on the balance sheet, and the alternative is going to market from the **bottom credit-health quartile of a 25-peer group** on the only rating read in this pool — a tier-5 vendor indication of S&P **BBB**, with no rating-agency rationale or outlook available [`CIQ Credit Health Panel → Summary`, LTM ending 2026-09-30 — vendor export].
