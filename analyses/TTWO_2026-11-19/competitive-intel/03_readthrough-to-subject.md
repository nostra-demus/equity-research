## 5. What Would Change This

**There is no material read-through to restate, so there is no falsifier to score against the coming print.** Writing a testable confirm/falsify boundary here would be worse than writing nothing: it would imply a peer signal stands behind it, and the §19 forecast ledger would inherit a trigger with no evidence underneath it. **Zero ledger entries are offered from this agent.**

What follows instead is the **structure any future read-through on this name must carry**, recorded so the next intake builds it correctly rather than re-deriving it:

- **Line-item, boundary, comparable and basis, all four.** The subject metric must be one the 10-Q or release actually prints — Net Bookings, GAAP total net revenue, recurrent consumer spending growth, or the mobile/console/PC platform split — with an explicit directional or numeric boundary, measured against **the same period a year earlier on the same reporting basis** (§17). A boundary that does not name which of the two it is measured on is untestable.
