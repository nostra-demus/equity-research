## 5. Management Read

**Tenure and stability are exceptional and not in question: one CEO for 15.9 years, one CFO for 19.4 years, one President for 13.5 years, and no Section-16 officer or director departure in nine years** — the opposite of the churn this test screens for, though the *completeness* of that departure record is capped by the pool holding no 8-K and no TTWO material-event history at all. **The record splits sharply by which promise you measure.** On the metric management chooses to headline — Net Bookings — it met or beat its own final annual guidance in **six consecutive years, FY2021 through FY2026**, the strongest single fact in this report. **The single biggest management signal is negative, and it is a product promise, not a financial one:** *Grand Theft Auto VI* slipped from "**Fall of Calendar 2025**" to "**May 26, 2026**" to "**November 19, 2026**" — roughly **13–14 months across two revisions**, both disclosed in the filings against the company's own prior dates rather than quietly re-based [`FY25 10-K, Item 1`; `FY26 10-K, Item 1`]. So the position is blunt: this is a stable, long-tenured, candid team with a near-perfect record on the number it guides, a poor record on cash and GAAP earnings, one year of delivered operating improvement (returns still negative at **−0.8%** in FY2026 and **−1.0%** on the latest twelve months), and an unverifiable launch carrying the next twelve months. Competence at hitting a self-chosen top-line target is proven; competence at converting it into returns on capital is not.
