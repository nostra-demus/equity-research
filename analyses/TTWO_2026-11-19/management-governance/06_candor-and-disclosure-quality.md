## 3. Disclosure Transparency

**On the financial side, disclosure is more complete than the pool first suggests; on the legal side it is pure boilerplate, and that is where the candor question actually sits.** The company discloses its KPI set identically in both annual filings in the pool, each with a **three-year rolling column** — Net Bookings, recurrent consumer spending ($5,196.6m / 78.1%, $4,474.6m / 79.4%, $4,213.5m / 78.8%), full game and other, net revenue by platform (mobile / console / PC and other), by distribution channel (digital online 97.0% / 96.4% / 95.6%), by content, and by timing of recognition — and it quantifies the one related-party channel in the **third note of the financial statements**: ZMC Advisors' consulting expense of **$16.6m / $10.2m / $6.5m** and stock compensation on units granted to ZMC of **$62.9m / $56.2m / $52.8m** (FY2026 / FY2025 / FY2024), with a risk factor naming the dependency outright ("Our continued success will depend to a significant extent on our senior management team and **our relationship with ZMC Advisors, L.P.** … Our Executive Chairman/Chief Executive Officer and President are **partners of ZMC**") and every amendment listed in the exhibit index. That is disclosed, not buried.

**Three genuine transparency limits, and one disclosure that is compliant and uninformative at the same time.** (1) **No segment economics exist to disclose.** The group reports **one** operating and reportable segment, the CODM is the CEO, and the filing states he "does not review segment assets at a level other than that presented on the Consolidated Balance Sheet" — so there is **no profitability split for Rockstar, 2K or Zynga**, in a business where mobile is **50.1% of net revenue at a structurally lower gross margin** than console or PC. That is a permitted ASC 280 judgment, not a breach; it is still the single biggest thing a reader cannot see. (2) **The presentation of the CEO's pay is technically complete and practically misleading**: the Summary Compensation Table reports him at **$403,019** (FY2026) while **$79.5m** went to his own firm for supplying him and the President — a **197×** gap between the two correct numbers, with ZMC's equity leg alone taking **20.6% of the company's entire $305.3m stock-compensation expense**; the $403,019 figure itself is tier-10/unverified because the pool's DEF 14A export is empty, and I carry agent `03`'s qualifier rather than hardening it. (3) **Group scope is unresolvable from the filings**: `09` records **145 entities with no published ownership chain**, ~20 subsidiaries with no identifiable purpose, **82.2% of cash offshore**, and an **unnamed counterparty** behind a **$62.1m non-cash purchase of non-voting shares** that quintupled the unconsolidated investment balance (18.5 → 93.0) in six months — the only material movement in that module whose counterparty nobody outside the company can identify, and the one structured in the share class that keeps a stake that size below significant influence.

**Did any disclosure get LESS transparent over time? One, and it is the handoff I was asked to adjudicate.** The legal note is **identical boilerplate across four consecutive filings** (FY25 10-K Note 14, FY26 10-K Note 14, Q1 FY27 10-Q Note 12, Q2 FY27 10-Q Note 12) and **names not one case**; a grep returns **zero** occurrences of "class action", "putative", "derivative action", "tribunal" or "subpoena". Over the same window the public record holds a **$44.9m adverse IBM jury verdict against Zynga** (Sept-2024), a **five-week UK blacklisting and union-victimisation trial against Rockstar with judgment pending as of today**, and **at least one apparent federal securities class action over the GTA VI delay disclosures**. My adjudication, and I keep agent `12`'s qualifiers exactly as it set them:

- **The securities class actions and the tribunal quantum stay unconfirmed.** `12` deliberately did not fire **RF-CMP-001** because the plaintiff-firm notices are not court records and no primary docket was reachable (Stanford's clearinghouse offline, PACER and Justia returning no TTWO securities docket). I do not harden either matter into a confirmed one, and I do not score candor against them. §3's rule cuts in the company's favour here exactly as it would cut against it.
- **The IBM verdict is confirmed, and it is a candor finding — but a narrow one, so size it rather than inflate it.** $44.9m = **1.28% of FY2026 net worth** ($3,510.9m) — **Medium** materiality, below the 2% High line — so treating it as not individually material is defensible on the threshold, and the material-event duty *was* discharged, on time, in the right form. What is not defensible is the shape of what followed. The FY25 10-K tells a reader that G&A rose partly on "legal fees and contingencies related to **the IBM case against Zynga**"; the FY26 10-K tells the same reader G&A fell "primarily driven by **lower** legal fees and contingencies related to the IBM case against Zynga". So the company names the case twice, in two years, as a driver big enough to explain its own expense line — while the legal note two hundred pages later says nothing in its litigation is worth naming. **A reader of the legal note learns there is no case; a reader of MD&A learns there is one case, with no amount, no forum and no outcome.** That is a self-inconsistency inside one document, and the verdict, the $44.9m and the settlement are nowhere in any periodic filing. **Amber, and the biggest single drag on the specificity component.** No registry tag fits it: A9-10 and RF-CMP-001 belong to `12`, which tested and declined them; A7-01 (timeliness) belongs to `12`, which graded it Green; RF-DISC-001 is about commentary contradicting the numbers, and this contradicts another note, not the numbers. I therefore record it as an owned Amber candor finding without inventing a flag for it.

## 4. Tone in Bad Times

**Q2 FY27 (quarter ended 2026-09-30, reported 2026-11-09) is the worst recent period for which a verbatim transcript exists, and the tone on the call is candid — measurably so.** Net Bookings came in below the guided range, recurrent consumer spending fell **9%** against a guided **5%** decline, Net Bookings were down **18%** year on year, the half was down **11.7%** ($2,987.2m vs $3,383.6m), and the GAAP result was a **$54.0m loss** — and both the CEO and the CFO opened with the miss, named NBA 2K as the cause, and quantified the RCS variance against their own forecast, in a quarter where they could instead have led with a GAAP revenue beat, an EPS beat and an EBITDA beat. The same CEO declined to monetise the one thing he could have: asked why extraordinary pre-orders were not being put into guidance, he answered "we're days away from the launch of GTA VI, and **we tend to avoid claiming success before it occurs** … There is really no need to update numbers right now", and earlier, "we haven't sold one unit yet. **You can cancel a preorder** … we're sort of **allergic to victory laps**". That is a management declining to raise guidance on good news it cannot yet bank — the opposite of promotional.

**Q&A was answered, but thinly, and one answer is a real ding.** Thirteen questions were taken in Q1 and fourteen in Q2, none cut off, with few deflections (three instances of "in the fullness of time", two of "I'm not going to", one "not going to comment"). But: asked directly whether he still stood by a prior public statement that the company could grow revenue and earnings in the year *after* the GTA VI launch — "is that a comment you still stand by? Or have things evolved such that you don't want to stand on that comment anymore?" — the CEO **neither reaffirmed nor retracted it**: "Tempting as it is, Doug, I'm not going to give you guidance for fiscal '28 today. We have said that we expect to set a new baseline for performance going forward." An unwillingness to guide FY2028 is legitimate; an unwillingness to say whether a statement already made in public still stands is not the same thing, and this is the clearest Q&A-transparency failure in either call. Asked about the reported GTA VI budget he answered with a riddle rather than a number ("someone saying something is not more than something does not mean it's close to something, half of something, 1/4 of something or 1/10 of something"), and the CFO's two substantive variance questions — full-year gross margin guided lower, Q4 operating expenses implied up ~10% — drew directionally honest but **wholly unquantified** answers ("it's due to the mix of the business, and it's being offset by some changes in the operating expenses"; "mostly being driven by increases in R&D as well as some modest growth in selling and marketing and also investment in IT expenses") while the company's **own outlook table on page 3 of the same release carried the figures** — cost of revenue guidance raised ~$141m and operating expenses cut ~$72m versus the Q1 outlook. The specificity in this company lives in the written tables, not in the spoken answers.

## 5. Candor Read

**Management can be trusted to tell the truth about a number it has already printed, and not to be trusted on the adjective it puts in front of it.** The strongest evidence for them is Q2 FY2027: in its worst reported quarter it led with the one line it missed rather than the three it beat, quantified the variance against its own forecast, refused to raise guidance on pre-orders it could not yet bank, disclosed both *Grand Theft Auto VI* slips against its own superseded dates with the old date left in the sentence, owned $5.9bn of goodwill write-offs as its **own acquisition's** consequence in its own risk factor, publishes the complete line-by-line build of both adjusted layers **and of the forward outlook**, and has filed 12 of 12 10-Qs and 4 of 4 10-Ks on time with zero late-filing notifications in the issuer's entire history (`12`, A7-01 Green). The strongest evidence against them is the written channel and the legal note: a Q1 FY2027 release that called a quarter with Net Bookings **−3%**, recurrent spending **−1%** and a GAAP loss **186.6% wider** "excellent … positive trends"; a Q2 release whose headline and CEO quote omit the guidance miss the call owned two hours later; a headline earnings measure that is roughly **30% non-cash by construction** and excludes **$462m of guided FY2027 stock compensation** while the share count rose 177.1m → 187.2m; and a legal note that has named **zero cases in four consecutive filings** while the same filings' MD&A twice names "the IBM case against Zynga" as a driver of its own expense line and never once mentions the **$44.9m** verdict behind it. **The single biggest candor signal is that split: candid where a number forces it, promotional where only an adjective is required — and, in Q1 FY2027, promotional past the point its own printed numbers supported.** Verdict: **mixed.**

**Weighing the unresolved integrity signal rather than setting it aside (CLAUDE.md §24, Filter 1).** `business-model/01_disqualifier-scan` routed nothing — "Integrity signal: none found, hard or soft" — and `01` correctly did not emit RF-MGT-005 on that basis. But agent `07` **did** fire **RF-MGT-005 at High** on a different item: *Marks Capital, Inc. & Steven Marks v. Harry Strauss Zelnick*, civil fraud and California securities claims against the Executive Chairman/CEO **personally** (LA Superior Court 24STCV20137, stayed pending arbitration 2025-02-04; sanctions motion denied 2026-01-27), plus a pro se derivative action in Delaware Chancery (2025-0816). Nothing is adjudicated, the primary dockets were not reached, and the dispute concerns his private investment activity — I re-fire nothing and add nothing to `07`'s grade.
