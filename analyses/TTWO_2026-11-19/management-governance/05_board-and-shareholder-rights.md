## 5. Minority-Shareholder Protection Read

The board is a **real, structurally capable check in form** — nine of ten directors independent, all three key committees and the new technology-risk committee fully independent, a designated Lead Independent Director who convened eleven independent-only executive sessions in one year, eleven board meetings, a skills matrix with no uncovered competency, annual elections with majority voting and a resignation policy, no poison pill, no classified board, one share one vote, no supermajority provisions, and shareholders retaining both the right to call special meetings and to act by written consent. **Rights, on paper and in the one place it can be tested — the ballot — are strong**: every resolution across three annual meetings passed, no director fell below 90.1% support, and the one pay item that drew 13.9% dissent in 2024 was engaged on and fixed to 95% support by 2025.

What stops this being "strong protection" is the single structure everything else has to police. **The CEO and President are not employees — they are supplied to the company by ZMC Advisors, L.P., a firm the CEO founded and runs, under a contract paying $79.5m in FY2026 (1.19% of revenue; cash consulting expense of $16.6m sits marginally above the agreement's own $16.5m stated cash ceiling, unexplained in the note) and running only to 2029-03-31** — and the board doing the policing has an **average tenure of 12.6 years**, with three directors seated since March 2007 and the **audit-committee chair at 12.7 years**. Pricing of that contract is asserted arm's-length with **no benchmarking, no peer set and no disclosed performance thresholds**; there is **no majority-of-minority vote** on it; its equity leg runs through the one plan shareholders pushed back hardest on (**22.40% against, 2025**, with no disclosed board response to that vote); and the CEO personally carries an **unresolved, un-adjudicated civil fraud and California-securities claim** (24STCV20137, stayed to arbitration 2025-02-04) that `07` graded Minor concerns with RF-MGT-005 at High. **RF-OWN-004 does not apply** — there is no government control, no value-maximising parent and no sprawling conglomerate; `04`'s structurally-unaligned-owner filter is not engaged by a register that is ~85% index and institutional money, so the §24 Filter 6 floor is not imposed here.

**Verdict: adequate.** Not strong — a twelve-and-a-half-year-average board cannot be called an arm's-length counterparty to the man who has chaired it for eighteen of those years, and the price of the arrangement it approves is unverifiable from outside. Not weak or entrenched either — nothing is hidden, nothing is unvoted, the defences are absent rather than stacked, and the dissent that exists is small, declining and demonstrably listened to. **The single biggest risk to minority holders is not leakage but capture: a $79.5m-a-year related-party contract that supplies the company's two most senior executives, priced by a process no outside holder can inspect, renewed or replaced by 2029-03-31 by a board whose median independent director has sat alongside that counterparty for nearly a decade.**
