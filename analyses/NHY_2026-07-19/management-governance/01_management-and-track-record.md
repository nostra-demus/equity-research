## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Management says it is executing a "2030 strategy" built on four pillars: growing Recycling and Extrusions profitably, scaling renewable power generation, executing a decarbonization/technology roadmap, and shaping the low-carbon aluminium market through commercial partnerships [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, "Letter to stakeholders," p.4]. The explicit, checkable targets are: an adjusted return on average capital employed (ARoaCE — profit as a percentage of the money tied up in the business, adjusted for one-offs) of 10% over the industry cycle [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, "Profit," p.~44]; a cumulative NOK 6.5 billion in cost/procurement/commercial "improvement program" savings by 2030 against a 2024 baseline [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, "Profit," p.~44]; and net debt-to-adjusted-EBITDA held below 2.0x through the cycle [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, "Financial policy" section]. On top of these, the company disclosed capacity/volume targets for specific growth bets (e.g., 850kt of post-consumer scrap (PCS) recycling capacity, meeting "the lower-end target for 2030") and a CO2-reduction target (10% by 2025, later revised) [nhy-investor-day-2025.pdf, "2025 | Delivering on our key strategic priorities," p.6].

## 5. Management Read

The top three leadership roles (CEO, CFO, Chair) all changed hands within the same 12-month window in 2024, but every one of those changes was a continuity appointment — a 26-year Hydro veteran and former CFO became CEO, a 29-year Hydro veteran became CFO after his predecessor left for a rival producer, and an existing director since 2020 became Chair — so this reads as a coordinated but orderly succession rather than instability, with roughly two years of track record in the new configuration so far. On the record itself: management met or beat its two headline, quantified targets (ARoaCE 10.2% vs a 10% goal; NOK 1.4bn of improvement-program savings vs an internal NOK 0.6bn estimate — more than double) and beat its CO2 target, while missing and explicitly admitting a miss on its core 2030 growth pillar for Recycling and Extrusions ("2025 EBITDA targets not reached; 2030 targets postponed," in management's own words at the November 2025 investor day). The single biggest management signal is that mix itself: this team hits its cost- and efficiency-side targets reliably but has twice fallen short on its stated downstream-growth ambitions and said so plainly rather than reframing the metric — a track record of disciplined execution on what it controls (cost, capital return) paired with a real, acknowledged shortfall on what it does not fully control (Recycling/Extrusions demand).
