## What This Module Found

Hydro's calendar carries genuine dated catalysts, not just a thematic story: second-quarter results land 22-Jul-2026 — three days from this report's date — followed by third-quarter results on 23-Oct-2026 [First Quarter Report 2026, "Financial calendar" box].

The single most important catalyst is not one event. It is the two prints together, testing whether Hydro's raised profit margin (Adjusted EBITDA margin of 13.9% in FY2025 — the cash profit from operations as a share of sales) holds, or fades toward its own mid-cycle 13.0% level. That is the exact bet a reverse-DCF (working backward from today's NOK 84.96 share price to see what growth the price assumes) says the price already builds in [`valuation/05_reverse-dcf.md` §5; `catalyst/01_catalyst-calendar.md` §3]. Behind that margin sits one question: whether the Middle East supply shock lifting current aluminium prices proves lasting or unwinds.

The near six-month calendar does not skew cleanly bullish or bearish — it skews evidence-revealing. Hydro issues no formal group-level guidance (only capex), so a buyer has to track this quarter by quarter through segment bridges, not time it to one decision.

The biggest bearish catalyst is the next Alumetal (Poland/Hungary recycling) impairment test — a check of whether an asset's book value is still worth its carrying amount — expected ~Feb-2027 (inferred from prior filing timing, not a disclosed date). It sits on only 18% recoverable-value headroom, and management's own words flag a future impairment as likely [`management-governance/02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md`, Finding 02-004].

## What Would Change This Read

The synthesis's own bullish/bearish triggers on the load-bearing catalysts:

- **22-Jul-2026 Q2 print — better:** a beat where the ~33% unbooked Q2 volume prices near the elevated spot (USD 3,467/mt) and capex tracks at or below the guided NOK 13,500m — both support the "margin holds" bet.
- **22-Jul-2026 Q2 print — worse:** a miss where guided cost ranges (energy, carbon, seasonal fixed cost) land above their top ends, or the Metal Markets NOK 900m trading gain reverses harder than management's "normalizing" language implies.
- **~Feb-2027 Alumetal test — better:** headroom widens as European demand stabilizes, closing off the flagged impairment risk.
- **~Feb-2027 Alumetal test — worse:** an impairment charge is booked, confirming the thin-headroom flag and extending the FY2025 pattern of four separate impairment events across three segments.
- **Extrusions closures (§24-flagged):** only completed closures with confirmed European demand stabilization, showing up in filed segment numbers, would be the first hard evidence the margin decline has actually reversed. Until then, treat any "margin fix" narrative as unproven.

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** Dated, evidenced near-term catalysts — real dates exist, but they reveal information rather than force a re-rating, and the State's blocking stake caps the upside.
- **Could be better than it looks:** the 22-Jul print has a genuinely lowered bar — estimates cut on profit while revenue estimates rose, and ~33% of Q2 volume still unbooked at the higher spot price.
- **Could be worse than it looks:** the ~Feb-2027 Alumetal impairment test on only 18% headroom, against an FY2025 pattern of four impairments across three segments that could repeat before it is priced.
- **What's missing:** a disclosed 2027 AGM date and any vote-tally history from the 2025/2026 AGMs — so minority-shareholder opposition levels cannot be checked. Timing is proven for only 6 of 13 calendar rows.
- **Watch next:** the 22-Jul-2026 Q2 results (three days out) — does the elevated 13.9% margin hold or start fading toward the 13.0% mid-cycle level. Do NOT treat the Extrusions plant closures as a proven turnaround catalyst.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Catalyst:** a specific, dated event that could move the share price or reveal new information.
- **Adjusted EBITDA margin:** the cash profit from core operations as a share of sales, before interest, tax, and one-off items.
- **Reverse-DCF:** working backward from today's share price to find out what future growth and margin the price already assumes.
- **Impairment test:** a check of whether an asset is still worth its book value; a shortfall forces a write-down that hits reported profit.
- **Recoverable-value headroom:** the cushion between an asset's estimated worth and its carrying value; 18% means only a small buffer before a write-down.
- **Maturity bucket / maturity wall:** debt coming due in a given period that must be repaid or refinanced.
- **Blocking-minority stake:** an ownership share large enough to veto certain decisions even without majority control.
- **Re-rating:** the market deciding to pay a higher (or lower) price for the same earnings.
