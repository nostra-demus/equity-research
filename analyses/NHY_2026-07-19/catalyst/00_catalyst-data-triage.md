## 3. Triage Verdict

**Partial.**

The calendar can carry at least one genuinely proven, near-dated, hard-evidenced catalyst — the Q2 2026 results release on **22-Jul-2026**, three days from this report's as-of date, backed by both the company's own published financial calendar and the Capital IQ consensus workbook's release-date field, with a documented beat/miss setup (cost-driven consensus cuts, capex running below Street's model) already built by the earnings module. The full instrument-level debt maturity wall (2026/2027/2028/2029/2030 dates) is also hard-dated and well evidenced, though the balance-sheet-survival module's own read is "low refi risk," which limits how much tension that maturity wall can carry as a bullish or bearish trigger. A Q3 2026 print (23-Oct-2026) extends the calendar a further quarter.

Beyond those two categories, the calendar is thin. The FY2025/FY2026 AGM cycle (7-May-2026) has already passed and no 2027 AGM date is yet scheduled. There is no active buyback authorization to point to as a forward capital-return event. The policy/regulatory items (EU ETS phase-out, CBAM revisions, Norway ICC scheme, Tomago's 2028 power-contract expiry) are real and evidenced but are multi-year windows, not single dated decisions — useful for a "long-dated / low-visibility" catalyst bucket, not a near-term dated one. No scheduled regulatory/legal decision, market-structure event, or dated M&A/divestiture process was found anywhere in the pool.

Net: the calendar will carry one proven near-term date (the Q2 print) and a proven multi-year debt-maturity ladder, surrounded mostly by thematic/windowed exposures (commodity, FX, EU carbon policy, Tomago power-contract renewal) rather than additional hard dates. This is a mixed calendar — not purely thematic, but not rich in dated events either.
