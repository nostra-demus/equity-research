# Historical Financials — NHY

Norsk Hydro ASA (Oslo Børs: NHY) reports under IFRS Accounting Standards as adopted by the EU, in Norwegian Krone (NOK millions), fiscal year ending 31 December [Integrated Annual Report 2025, p.140]. All figures below are NOK million unless stated as per-share (NOK). This is not a US SEC or India SEBI-LODR filer; the local-equivalent documents are the Board-approved Quarterly Report (10-Q/6-K equivalent) and the Integrated Annual Report (10-K equivalent) [00_earnings-data-triage.md].

**Data-quality note on this report (read before the tables):** Norsk Hydro's audited income statement uses an IFRS "nature of expense" format with no Cost-of-Goods-Sold or Gross Profit subtotal, and no EBITDA/EBIT subtotal that matches the CIQ workbook's own "Operating Income" reclassification. A direct check found the CIQ Financials.xls Income Statement tab's "EBITDA" and "EBIT" fields (e.g., FY2025 EBITDA 51,454; EBIT 41,218) do **not** reconcile to the company's own audited/disclosed "Earnings before financial items and tax (EBIT)" and reported EBITDA (FY2025: EBIT 14,401; EBITDA 25,696) [Integrated Annual Report 2025, p.140 (EBIT); p.36, "Other performance measures" table (EBITDA)]. The gap (~NOK 27bn) tracks almost exactly to a large recurring "Other Non-Operating Inc.(Exp.)" line embedded in CIQ's reclassification that has no counterpart of that size in the audited statement — this looks like a CIQ template/extraction mismatch for Hydro's income-statement structure, not a real economic item. Per CLAUDE.md §4/§5 (audited filing beats vendor; cite the source a number actually came from), this report uses the **company's own reported EBITDA/EBIT** wherever available (FY2023–FY2025, and the two quarterly filings) and does **not** use CIQ's EBITDA/EBIT fields at all. Revenue, Net Income, diluted EPS, CFO, and Capex from the CIQ Financials.xls workbook were spot-checked against the audited FY2025/FY2024 statements and matched exactly, so those lines are used as sourced. Gross Profit/Gross Margin is a CIQ reclassification (Revenue less "Raw material and energy expense") with no company-disclosed counterpart; it is retained but flagged as vendor-derived, cross-checked to the filing for FY2024–FY2025 only (COGS component matched exactly for those two years) [Integrated Annual Report 2025, p.36, Note 5.3].

## 5. Quarterly Seasonality Table (last 3 fiscal years)

*Insufficient quarterly history for seasonality analysis.* This pool contains full-year figures for FY2023–FY2025 and only 5 individual quarters (Q1 2025–Q1 2026, several of them approximate — see §3). Building a 3-fiscal-year, 4-quarter-each seasonality table would require FY2023 and FY2024 quarterly filings, which are not in this pool. No quarter can be flagged as structurally >30% or <20% of annual revenue from the data available; do not infer seasonality from the 5-quarter window in §3, which spans a period of falling alumina and aluminium prices rather than a repeatable seasonal pattern [see nhy-presentation-q1-2026.pdf, p.10, PAX/alumina price commentary].

## 6. Key Trend Summary

Revenue growth is **decelerating and has turned negative on a trailing basis**: FY2022's +38.9% commodity-price spike gave way to a −6.9% FY2023 correction, only modest +5.2%/+2.1% growth in FY2024/FY2025, and the latest TTM (ended Mar-2026) is down −3.2% versus FY2025, with Q1 2026 revenue down −11.7% year-on-year on a 40% year-on-year drop in the alumina price index (PAX) [first-quarter-report-2026.pdf, p.9]. Margins are **volatile rather than trending in one direction**: reported EBITDA margin bounced between 12.0% (FY2023) and 13.0% (FY2024) before easing to 12.4% (FY2025) on a company-reported basis, while adjusted EBITDA margin on a quarterly view fell from 16.7% (Q1'25) to a trough of 11.8% (Q4'25) before rebounding to 17.2% (Q1'26) — a swing driven substantially by non-cash unrealized-derivative adjustments rather than a steady operating trend (§4). There is **no seasonality read available** from this pool (§5) — the quarter-to-quarter swings visible in §3 track commodity-price and FX moves, not a repeatable calendar pattern. The clearest **inflection point** is Q4 2025: reported EBITDA fell to NOK 1,974 million (a 4.2% margin, the weakest point in the visible window) on a large unrealized LME-derivative timing loss, before both reported and adjusted metrics recovered sharply in Q1 2026 (adjusted EBITDA +55% QoQ, adjusted EPS +196% QoQ) — company commentary attributes the Q1 2026 rebound to higher all-in aluminium prices, higher alumina and metal sales volumes, and stronger Metal Markets/Extrusions results, partly offset by lower alumina prices, a stronger NOK, and reduced power production [first-quarter-report-2026.pdf, p.5]. Net debt on the company's own APM basis has also inflected twice in the visible window — rising from NOK 8.2bn (FY2023) to NOK 16.0bn (FY2024), falling back to NOK 9.7bn (FY2025), then rising again to NOK 12.9bn at Q1 2026 on operating-capital build — leverage (Adjusted net debt/Adjusted EBITDA) stayed within the company's own sub-2x target throughout at 0.2x–0.9x (§1, footnote 5).
