## 6. Stage-One sheet comment
Nothing new landed at Norsk Hydro in the three weeks since our last update. The next quarterly print is not due until 23 October, so the two open questions from before are still open: whether the adjusted EBITDA margin fades back toward the mid-teens as aluminium prices soften, and whether the receivables spike from the first quarter ever gets explained. The shares are up about 3.9 percent since our 17 July reference price, but that trails the broader Oslo market, which rose about 5.3 percent over the same stretch, while beating the aluminium price itself, which was up less than 1 percent. Qatalum, the Qatari smelter running at reduced output since a gas-supply disruption in March, is still stuck at about 60 percent of capacity with no restart date given. None of this changes the call. We remain on the watchlist with no new position, waiting on the October print and on price falling toward our fair-value band before revisiting.
