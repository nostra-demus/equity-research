## 3. Did the original thesis play out?

Too early to say — this is a two-week-old Watchlist on a 12-month horizon. Nothing in the quarter breaks it, and nothing resolves it.

What the quarter *did* settle is the record's own scorecard, and it split:

- **Right on revenue.** NOK 56.5bn cleared the consensus bar.
- **Wrong on profitability.** The record forecast that adjusted EBITDA would **not** clearly beat consensus. It beat by about 4%. Aluminium price, premiums and recycling margin did more work than the note allowed — and the miss was in the company's favour.
- **Right on leverage.** Net debt rose NOK 3.4bn in a quarter that reported NOK 4bn of free cash flow. That is the direction the balance-sheet read flagged, and it is the reason this stayed on the watchlist.

**Luck vs skill (§10):** not assessable. Two weeks is not a price outcome, and `decision_quality` is recorded accordingly.

*No benchmark comparison is recorded. The record itself labels both benchmarks "convention default, not pool-cited", and neither the OSEBX nor the aluminium sector return over 17–31 July could be sourced. The call is therefore **not yet benchmark-scoreable** and will not enter the calibration hit rate until one is established. Substituting the raw +2.12% would misstate it.*

## 6. Stage-One sheet comment

Norsk Hydro reported second quarter results on 22 July, three days after our note. Revenue of 56.5 billion kroner and adjusted EBITDA of 8.9 billion kroner both came in ahead of consensus. The EBITDA beat is worth flagging because we had specifically said it would not clearly beat, so that call was wrong and wrong in the company's favour - higher aluminium prices, better product premiums and improved recycling earnings did more work than we allowed. Net debt went the way we expected, rising from 12.9 to 16.3 billion kroner over the quarter even with four billion of free cash flow, so the leverage concern that kept this on the watchlist is intact. Qatalum is running at about 60 percent of capacity and the quarter absorbed that hit anyway. The shares are 86.76 kroner against our 84.96 reference, up 2.1 percent in two weeks. Nothing here changes the recommendation. We are not moving off watchlist on one print.
