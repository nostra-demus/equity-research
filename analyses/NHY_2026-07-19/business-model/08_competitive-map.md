# Competitive Map — NHY

## 1. Dominant Segment

Hydro Aluminium Metal (primary aluminium smelting and casting) is the dominant segment by profit — 39.5% of FY2025 group Adjusted EBITDA (NOK 11,409m of NOK 28,889m) and 48.9% of group reported EBIT — even though it is only the fourth-largest segment by revenue (7.1%) [`analyses/NHY_2026-07-19/business-model/03_segment-map.md`, §2]. That dominance rotates with the alumina-vs-aluminium price spread (Bauxite & Alumina led in FY2024), so this map also notes where the same peers show up in the upstream alumina business.

## 2. Named Competitors

Hydro's own FY2025 Integrated Annual Report names its remuneration/performance benchmarking peer group as: "Nalco, Rusal, Alcoa, Century Aluminium, Hindalco, Chalco, Grupa Kety, Constellium, Kaiser, ProfilGruppen, Tredegar Corporation" [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, Remuneration / TSR peer group note, p.2992 (extract)]. Separately, Hydro's own Capital IQ-generated comparable-company set (relevancy-scored, not hand-picked by Hydro) lists Anglo American, Boliden, Shandong Hongqiao, ProfilGruppen, Grupa Kety, Antofagasta, RUSAL, Constellium, Hindalco Industries and Aluminum Corporation of China (Chalco) [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Business Description tab]. The three names below sit at the intersection of both lists (or are the closest scale-and-segment match in the CIQ set) and compete directly in primary aluminium — Hydro Aluminium Metal's segment — making them the most credible peer set, not the diversified miners (Anglo American, Antofagasta, Boliden) that only touch aluminium tangentially or not at all.

### Competitor A — United Company RUSAL (SEHK:486)

- **Ticker / listing:** SEHK:486 (Hong Kong-listed; also Moscow Exchange RUAL)
- **Where they compete:** Primary aluminium production, alumina refining and bauxite mining — the same integrated bauxite-to-metal chain as Hydro's Bauxite & Alumina and Aluminium Metal segments, but Russia-based with global sales into China, India, Turkey and elsewhere [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Business Description tab]. Named directly as a peer in Hydro's own annual report [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, TSR peer group note, p.2992].
- **Scale:** LTM total revenue USD 14,812m (vs Hydro's USD 20,883m) — within Hydro's ~1.4x range [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Financial Data tab, as of 2026-07-18].
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM EBITDA margin 7.0% and LTM EBIT margin 2.9% — both well below Hydro's 23.7% EBITDA / 18.5% EBIT margins in the same export; LTM net income margin -3.1% (loss-making on a trailing basis) and LTM total debt/EBITDA of 9.3x (vs Hydro's 0.7x) [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Operating Statistics tab]. Return on equity was reported at roughly 6.9% in a recent period per a third-party financial data site, but this is described there as volatile quarter to quarter — this ROE figure is a dated, unverified web read, not from RUSAL's own filing [Web: wisesheets.io ROE page, accessed 2026-07-19 (unverified)].
- **Source named in:** Hydro's FY2025 Integrated Annual Report TSR peer group [p.2992]; also present in Hydro's Capital IQ comparable-company set.
- **One-line read:** A larger-volume but structurally weaker-margin, much more leveraged peer — sanctions exposure and a heavier debt load separate it from Hydro's balance sheet, even though the underlying business (bauxite-alumina-aluminium) is the closest direct match to Hydro's own chain.

### Competitor B — Aluminum Corporation of China Limited ("Chalco") (SEHK:2600)

- **Ticker / listing:** SEHK:2600 (also Shanghai: 601600.SS)
- **Where they compete:** Bauxite mining, alumina refining and primary aluminium smelting in China and internationally, plus its own captive energy segment (coal, thermal, wind, solar power) — the same five-part upstream structure as Hydro's Bauxite & Alumina, Aluminium Metal and Energy segments [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Business Description tab]. Named directly as a peer in Hydro's annual report (as "Chalco") [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, TSR peer group note, p.2992].
- **Scale:** LTM total revenue USD 35,973m — about 1.7x Hydro's USD 20,883m [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Financial Data tab, as of 2026-07-18].
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM EBITDA margin 18.6% and LTM EBIT margin 14.7% — both below Hydro's 23.7%/18.5% in the same export, though closer than RUSAL's; LTM net income margin 6.0% (positive, unlike RUSAL) [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Operating Statistics tab]. Total debt/EBITDA 1.3x (vs Hydro's 0.7x). A third-party site reports Chalco's return on equity at roughly 18.6% and return on invested capital at roughly 10.1% for a recent period — this is a dated, unverified web read, not confirmed against Chalco's own primary filing in this pool [Web: search aggregation citing Chalco metrics, accessed 2026-07-19 (unverified)].
- **Source named in:** Hydro's FY2025 Integrated Annual Report TSR peer group [p.2992]; also present in Hydro's Capital IQ comparable-company set as "Aluminum Corporation of China Limited."
- **One-line read:** The largest-revenue peer in the set, state-linked and China-domestic-demand-driven, with mid-pack margins that sit between Hydro's and RUSAL's.

### Competitor C — Hindalco Industries Limited (BSE:500440)

- **Ticker / listing:** BSE:500440 / NSE:HINDALCO (India)
- **Where they compete:** Integrated aluminium (upstream mining/smelting and downstream flat-rolled/extrusion products) plus copper, with a global downstream arm (Novelis) — directly overlapping Hydro's upstream Aluminium Metal and downstream Extrusions segments, though Hindalco is copper-diversified where Hydro is not [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Business Description tab]. Named directly as a peer in Hydro's annual report [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, TSR peer group note, p.2992].
- **Scale:** LTM total revenue USD 28,550m — about 1.4x Hydro's USD 20,883m [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Financial Data tab, as of 2026-07-18].
- **Profitability / return on capital:** LTM EBITDA margin 12.9% and LTM EBIT margin 9.9% — below Hydro's 23.7%/18.5%; LTM net income margin 4.9% [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Operating Statistics tab]. Total debt/EBITDA 2.8x (vs Hydro's 0.7x). A third-party financial-data aggregator cites Hindalco's FY2025 return on capital employed (ROCE) at roughly 14.6% and return on equity at roughly 13.9% — both above Hydro's own group-level Adjusted return on average capital employed (RoaCE) of 10.1% "over the cycle" as reported by Hydro itself [Q1 2026 earnings call transcript, p.109] — but the Hindalco figures are a dated, unverified web read, not from Hindalco's own primary filing in this pool [Web: alphaspread.com / gurufocus.com Hindalco ratio pages, accessed 2026-07-19 (unverified)].
- **Source named in:** Hydro's FY2025 Integrated Annual Report TSR peer group [p.2992]; also present in Hydro's Capital IQ comparable-company set.
- **One-line read:** A larger, copper-diversified integrated producer whose reported return on capital may currently exceed Hydro's own cycle-adjusted RoaCE — worth flagging to `moat` as a case where a peer's return figure is not clearly below the company's, not just above.

## 3. Competitive Position

Position vs peers: not disclosed as a clean market-share trend, but two proxies exist. First, Hydro states it "produces 30 percent of all primary aluminium in Europe" [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, p.645-646] and holds "14 percent [share] in Europe and 18 percent" for Hydro Extrusions in an unspecified second geography [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, p.732] — both are point-in-time shares with no prior-year comparison given, so a gaining/losing read cannot be made from them alone. Second, Hydro Extrusions itself is contracting, not growing: five European plants were proposed for closure in FY2025 with impairment/restructuring charges booked, and the segment's EBIT margin fell from roughly 4% (2020-2023) to -2.2% (2025) [`03_segment-map.md`, Hydro Extrusions row] — a proxy for share loss or capacity rationalization in the downstream business, even without a stated share number. In the dominant Aluminium Metal segment, Hydro's LTM EBITDA margin (23.7%) sits above all three named peers (RUSAL 7.0%, Chalco 18.6%, Hindalco 12.9%) [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Operating Statistics tab], which supports "holding or gaining ground on cost position" but is not itself a volume/share metric. Net read: Position vs peers in primary aluminium — not disclosed as a share trend; margin data supports a cost-position edge, and Extrusions shows proxy evidence of contraction, not gain.

## 4. Competitive Shape

The global primary aluminium and alumina industry is oligopoly-leaning at the top but has a long fragmented tail: Hydro's own comparable-company set spans only 10 credible aluminium-focused or aluminium-adjacent public peers worldwide [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Business Description tab], and the largest of them (Chalco, Shandong Hongqiao, RUSAL, Hindalco) are each state-linked, closely-held, or geographically concentrated rather than globally diversified challengers to Hydro's specific European/Brazilian footprint. Within Europe specifically, Hydro's disclosed 30% share of regional primary-aluminium production [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, p.645-646] indicates a concentrated regional structure (Hydro plus a small number of others make up the bulk of European supply), even though globally the industry (led by Chinese producers such as Chalco and Hongqiao, which together already show combined LTM revenue of roughly USD 59.3bn against Hydro's USD 20.9bn [Company Comparable Analysis Norsk Hydro ASA.xls, Financial Data tab]) is better described as several large national-scale producers competing without a single dominant global leader — no HHI or top-N global share figure is available in this pool to size the global structure precisely.

## 5. Caveat

Global primary-aluminium production shares (Hydro's absolute global tonnage as a percentage of world primary aluminium output) are not disclosed anywhere in this pool — only the regional (Europe, 30%) figure is given. A global HHI or top-5-producer share figure would sharpen the "competitive shape" read in §4. Peer return-on-capital figures (ROE/ROIC/ROCE) for RUSAL, Chalco and Hindalco are sourced from third-party web aggregators, not each company's own primary annual filing (which is outside this data pool) — these are dated 2026-07-19 and labelled unverified; the `moat` agent should treat them as directional, not audited, and prefer the CIQ-sourced margin and leverage figures above them where precision matters.
