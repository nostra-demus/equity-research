## 3. Most Severe New Flag

The most material new flag is the thin impairment headroom on the Alumetal recycler group: Hydro's own FY2025 test shows the recoverable amount clears the NOK 2.7bn carrying value by only about 18%, and management's own words say continued weak recycling-market conditions are "likely" to force an impairment in a future period. This is not a hypothetical stress scenario invented by this sweep — it is the company's stated forward view on an asset from its only material acquisition of the last five years (per `11_capital-allocation-governance.md`). The synthesizer should treat this as a near-term earnings and capital-allocation risk (a bolt-on deal already showing cracks), separate from — and additive to — the already-flagged Extrusions plant-closure impairments, because it sits in a different segment (Metal Markets/recycling) and reflects deal-specific execution risk rather than the broader European demand downturn.

## 4. Cross-Cutting Patterns

Impairment activity in FY2025 is broader than any single upstream report captures in one place: five Extrusions plants written down (NOK 397m) plus two further Extrusions CGUs in the UK and France (NOK 192m + NOK 96m) on weak European demand, a hydrogen-technology impairment (NOK 265m) tied to the already-noted de-prioritization of renewables, and now the Alumetal indicator sitting at only 18% headroom — four distinct impairment events across three different segments (Extrusions, Energy, Metal Markets) in the same fiscal year, on top of the already fully written-down Tomago smelter. Individually each is disclosed and modest in size, but together they describe a business currently absorbing write-downs across most of its downstream and diversification bets simultaneously, while the upstream segments (Bauxite & Alumina, Aluminium Metal) carry the group's profit. That pattern — upstream commodity segments funding a downstream/diversification book that is currently shedding value — is consistent with, and reinforces, the segment-rotation dynamic already identified in `03_segment-map.md` and the weak-pricing-power read in `07_business-quality.md`.
