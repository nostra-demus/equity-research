## What This Module Found

Norsk Hydro mines bauxite (aluminium ore), refines it into alumina, smelts that into aluminium, and shapes some into extruded products, all backed by its own Nordic and Brazilian hydropower. The model is clear and consistently described, which earns a business clarity score of 75/100 [`02_business-identity.md`].

The single most important driver is cost position. Captive bauxite and captive/contracted hydropower place Hydro's smelting at the 16th percentile of the global cost curve (cheaper to produce than roughly 84% of the world's capacity), giving it the best profit margin (23.7% EBITDA margin — earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization, as a share of revenue) of three named peers: RUSAL 7.0%, Chalco 18.6%, Hindalco 12.9% [`09_moat.md`; `08_competitive-map.md`]. Profit is concentrated in Hydro Aluminium Metal, which produced 39.5% of FY2025 adjusted EBITDA (NOK 28,889 million) on just 7.1% of revenue [`03_segment-map.md`].

The single most important risk is that Hydro is almost entirely a price-taker. Business quality scored only 34/100, dragged down by commodity dependence (12/100) and cyclicality (15/100) [`07_business-quality.md`]. Group return on equity (profit earned on shareholders' money) swung from 24.9% (2022) to 2.6% (2023) [`07_business-quality.md`]. A sustained ~20% fall in the London Metal Exchange (LME) aluminium price (~USD 515/mt) would push the profit-dominant Aluminium Metal segment's cash margin (~USD 532/mt, FY2025) close to breakeven, and a NOK 1.00 move in the USD/NOK exchange rate swings adjusted EBITDA by NOK 4,900m — about 17% of FY2025 adjusted EBITDA [`04_unit-economics.md`; `10_external-dependency.md`]. No automatic disqualifier triggered on any of the eight checks.

- **segment-map:** No segment exceeds 85% of revenue or profit; the profit center rotates by cycle — Aluminium Metal led FY2025, Bauxite & Alumina led FY2024.
- **unit-economics:** Creates value at current pricing, but the cash margin cushion (~USD 532/mt) is smaller than a single 20% price swing (~USD 515/mt).
- **customer-geography:** Concentrated by geography, not provably by customer — Europe is 53.5% of FY2025 revenue with no long-term contract securing it.
- **value-chain:** Strong upstream (captive bauxite and power), weak downstream — every stage touching an external customer is a price-taker.
- **business-quality:** Aggregate 34/100 (Weak); commodity dependence and cyclicality are the lowest, most heavily-weighted factors.
- **competitive-map:** Tops the named peer set on margin (23.7% EBITDA margin); Extrusions shows share loss (five proposed plant closures), not gain.

- **capital-allocation-governance:** Standard professional management, 62/100; off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities are the most material signal.
- **red-flags-sweep:** Three flags at severity ≥40 (below) plus four impairment events across three segments in FY2025 alone.

## What Would Change This Read

The verdict would move on specific, observable events:

- **Upgrade condition:** a strong, evidenced view that the current mid-cycle aluminium/alumina price and spread setup persists or improves — backed by evidence beyond the company's own hedging book — would be needed to raise conviction [`07_business-quality.md`; `09_moat.md`].
- **Downgrade triggers (each independently):** a further Alumetal impairment; an adverse ruling on the Brazilian tax / Alunorte litigation; or a 20%+ fall in the LME aluminium price [`12_red-flags-sweep.md`].

**Red flags carried forward (severity ≥40):**
- Alumetal (2023 acquisition) has only ~18% impairment headroom over its NOK 2.7bn carrying value, and management says continued weak recycling conditions are "likely" to force a future write-down — severity 55 [`12_red-flags-sweep.md`].
- Off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities — Brazilian tax disputes (~NOK 3.6bn possible loss) plus unquantifiable Alunorte/Cainquiama litigation — severity 45 [`11_capital-allocation-governance.md`].
- Q1 2026 receivable-days spike from 33.0 days (FY2025) to 56.7 days (LTM Mar-2026), with receivables roughly doubling quarter-on-quarter (NOK 17,212m to NOK 31,384m) and unexplained in the pool — severity 40 [`11_capital-allocation-governance.md`].

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict:** Cyclical business — worth deeper work only with a strong timing edge on the aluminium/alumina price cycle. The model is clear, but the business itself is a price-taking commodity producer, not a durable compounder.
- **Why it could be better than it looks:** captive bauxite and hydropower put smelting at the 16th percentile of the global cost curve, giving the best margin (23.7% EBITDA margin) of three named peers and a 5-year through-cycle return on capital of 13.3% versus a 10% target.
- **Why it could be worse than it looks:** business quality is 34/100 by design; a ~20% LME price fall pushes the profit-dominant segment close to breakeven, and the moat is eroding downstream — Hydro Extrusions' operating margin fell from ~4% (2020–2023) to -2.2% (2025).
- **What evidence is missing:** bauxite/alumina reserve life and ore grade/strip ratio for the Paragominas mine and Alunorte refinery are undisclosed, capping how confidently the durability of the whole cost advantage can be judged. Also missing: 6 pages of the auditor's report and any customer-level revenue split.
- **One thing to watch next:** the Alumetal impairment cushion (~18%) and the unquantified Brazilian tax / Alunorte contingency — the item most able to push governance from "standard" toward "red flags" if it worsens.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **EBITDA:** earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization — a rough measure of operating cash profit before financing and accounting charges.
- **EBITDA margin:** that profit as a share of revenue; higher means more profit kept per NOK of sales.
- **Bauxite / alumina:** bauxite is aluminium ore; refining it produces alumina, which is smelted into aluminium metal.
- **Cost curve (16th percentile):** a ranking of all producers from cheapest to most expensive; 16th percentile means cheaper than roughly 84% of world capacity.
- **LME aluminium price:** the London Metal Exchange benchmark price that sets most of Hydro's selling price; it is outside management's control.
- **Cash margin per tonne (USD/mt):** cash profit earned on each tonne of aluminium sold.
- **Return on equity / return on capital:** profit earned on shareholders' money / on all invested capital; measures how efficiently money put in is turned into profit.
- **Contingent liability:** a possible future cost from an unresolved dispute that is not yet booked as a firm liability.
- **Net debt/EBITDA (0.24x):** total debt minus cash, divided by yearly operating cash profit; a low figure means little borrowing relative to earnings.
