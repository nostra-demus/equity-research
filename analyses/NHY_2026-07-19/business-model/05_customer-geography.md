## 4. Read

Norsk Hydro's revenue is concentrated by geography, not provably by customer: Europe alone is 53.5% of FY2025 revenue (NOK 111,235m of NOK 207,971m) [integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf, Note 1.4, p.152], while the customer side shows no disclosed top-customer or top-3-customer percentage anywhere in this pool — consistent with a business built on thousands of construction, HVAC, and industrial buyers plus commodity-priced metal sales, though that read is an inference from segment structure, not a filed fact. Where concentration exists, it is largely naked, not contractually secured: the Metal Markets segment (~36% of FY2025 external revenue) prices to the month-prior average market price rather than to fixed customer contracts, and only a subset of the Extrusions automotive book carries disclosed 4–7 year OEM contracts. The single biggest dependency the synthesizer should carry forward is the Europe concentration (53.5% of revenue) sitting against a Board-disclosed weak European extrusion market that already triggered five plant-closure proposals in late 2025 [Note 1.3 Significant events, p.150] — a large regional revenue base exposed to a demand environment the company itself calls weak.
