## 3. Classification

**Mostly externally driven.** Hydro's earnings move first and most on the LME aluminium price, the alumina price/index it is contractually tied to, the USD/NOK exchange rate, power prices, EU carbon policy, and — as Q1 2026 showed in real time — geopolitical supply shocks and US tariff policy. Management's levers are real but narrow: forward hedging on the LME (up to about three years), NOK-denominated debt to partly match the cost base, a lower-cost position on the industry cost curve, a higher mix of value-add/recycled product versus standard ingot, and operational flexibility (curtailment) in a downturn. None of these levers changes the direction of the LME price, the carbon price, or a currency move — they only cushion the size of the swing.

## 4. External Dependency Risk Score

**74/100** (this score is INVERTED — higher = worse / more dangerous external dependence).

Rationale: the FX sensitivity alone (NOK 4,900m per NOK 1.00 move, ~17% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA) and the commodity/geopolitics evidence (a single quarter's LME move of over USD 400/mt driven by a Middle East supply shock) show that quarter-to-quarter results are dominated by variables management does not set. The score sits in the 61-80 "mostly externally driven, limited management levers" band rather than the 81-100 "pure pass-through" band because Hydro does have a genuine, evidenced cost-curve advantage (competitive, partly self-generated hydropower) and an active hedging program that measurably dampens — though does not eliminate — the swings.

## 5. The Single Biggest Lever

The USD/NOK exchange rate: Hydro's revenue is set in US dollars (LME-linked) while its dominant cost base is in Norwegian kroner, and the company's own disclosed sensitivity — NOK 4,900 million of Adjusted EBITDA per NOK 1.00 move in USD/NOK — implies that a 20% adverse move (a materially stronger NOK, roughly NOK 2.00 off the FY2025 realized rate of 10.39) would swing Adjusted EBITDA by close to NOK 9.8 billion, more than a third of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA of NOK 28,889m, on a factor Hydro cannot control and only partially hedges through the currency of its debt [FY2025 Annual Report, p.38, "Key financial exposures"].
