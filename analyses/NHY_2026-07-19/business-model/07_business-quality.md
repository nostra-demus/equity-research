## 2. Aggregate Quality Score

**Aggregate: 34/100 (Weak).**

Band anchor: the second-lowest row score is Commodity dependence at 12; the aggregate of 34 sits 22 points above it, just outside the strict ±20 band — justified because two rows (Industry rate-of-change 68, Disclosure quality 68) are genuinely strong, evidence-backed positives that the aggregate must still reflect, not because the core commodity read is discounted. Weighting: Commodity dependence (12) and Cyclicality (15) are given the heaviest weight (roughly 40% combined) because they are the two factors that most define what kind of business this is and they reinforce each other on the same evidence (the FY2022→FY2023 ROE collapse, the Q1 2026 segment swings). Pricing power (18), Margin stability (30), and Capital intensity (28) carry the next-heaviest weight (~35% combined) as direct consequences of being a price-taker with fixed heavy assets. Industry rate-of-change (68) and Disclosure quality (68) are weighted lightly (~15% combined) — they describe the *pace of change* and *transparency* of the business, not its underlying economics, and cannot offset a structurally cyclical, price-taking cost structure. Regulatory dependence (48), Repeat revenue (42), and Customer stickiness (38) fill the remainder (~10%) as secondary, mixed-evidence factors.

## 4. Read

Norsk Hydro is a vertically-integrated commodity aluminium producer, not a durable compounder: nearly every dollar of revenue and most of its cost base are set by the LME aluminium price, the alumina index price, and the USD/NOK exchange rate — none of which Hydro controls [02_business-identity.md §2, §4] — and that price-taking shows up directly in the numbers, with group ROE collapsing from 24.9% (2022) to 2.6% (2023) and back to 7.7% (2025) [Norsk Hydro ASA OB NHY Financials.xls, Ratios tab]. FY2025's reported returns (ROE 7.7%, EBITDA margin 24.7%) sit roughly mid-cycle relative to this five-year band — well below the 2022 price-spike peak (ROE 24.9%, EBITDA margin 28.5%) and well above the 2023 trough (ROE 2.6%) — so the headline FY2025 figures should be read as a mid-cycle point, not a run-rate, and a buyer should not extrapolate either the 2022 peak or the 2023 trough forward. The industry rate-of-change row scored 68 (above the ≤40 threshold), so this is not a technology-cycle bet; it is a cyclical, price-taking commodity business whose main quality risk is the interaction of thin pricing power (score 18) with heavy fixed assets (capital intensity score 28) — a combination that turns commodity-price swings directly into large ROE and segment-margin swings, as already seen in the FY2023 trough and the Q1 2026 alumina-vs-aluminium rotation. The single factor a buyer should watch over the next 24 months is Cyclicality (score 15): whether the LME aluminium price and the alumina-vs-aluminium spread stay near FY2025's mid-cycle level, revert toward the 2023 trough, or extend the current Aluminium Metal-led upswing, given Q1 2026 already showed an 85% quarter-on-quarter Adjusted EBITDA collapse in Bauxite & Alumina against a near-doubling in Aluminium Metal [03_segment-map.md, segment table].
