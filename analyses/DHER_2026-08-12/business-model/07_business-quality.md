## 4. Read

This is a hyper-competitive, capital-light, low-and-unstable-margin marketplace that is mid-transition from a pure commission agent into a part-owned-inventory quick-commerce retailer — closer to a sector/technology-cycle bet than a durable compounder. FY2025's first-ever Group operating profit (€93.7m, a 0.7% margin, up from years of losses) is not a cyclical peak to anchor against — it is a fragile, recent inflection funded by continued heavy marketing spend (3.0% of GMV) and still sitting on a gross margin that fell from 29.9% to 24.4% over two years [FY24 Annual Report, p.108; Delivery Hero SE XTRA DHER Financials.xls, Income Statement tab] — the trend line matters more than the single positive quarter. The industry rate-of-change / disruption row scored 26 (≤40), so per CLAUDE.md §24 Filter 5 this thesis is flagged as a sector / technology-cycle bet rather than a durable compounder — reinforced by the fact that DHER itself is currently the subject of a live Uber acquisition offer (M&A call, Jul 16, 2026; expected close H2 2027) rather than being valued as a standalone, steady-state business [`02_business-identity.md`, §4, citing Uber Technologies, Inc., Delivery Hero SE - M&A Call, Jul 16, 2026]. The single factor a buyer should watch over the next 24 months is **margin stability**: whether the FY2025 inflection to positive operating income holds as own-delivery/Integrated Verticals mix keeps rising, competitive intensity in Asia persists, and rider-classification and EU antitrust costs continue to surface.
