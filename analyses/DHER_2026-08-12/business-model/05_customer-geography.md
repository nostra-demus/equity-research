# Customer And Geography Map — DHER

## 1. Customer Map

Delivery Hero does not disclose any single named customer or counterparty (expected for a consumer marketplace — its revenue is earned from millions of individual consumer orders and roughly 500,000+ restaurant/vendor partners, none of which could plausibly reach a disclosure threshold). The company instead discloses revenue by **nature/fee type** in its audited revenue note, which is the closest available proxy for "who pays." Figures below are FY2024 (year ended Dec-31-2024), the latest audited period.

This is not usable for revenue-concentration purposes and is excluded from the table above.

## 4. Read

This is a geography-concentrated business, not a customer-concentrated one: the customer side is inherently diffuse (millions of consumer orders, hundreds of thousands of restaurant/vendor partners, no named counterparty anywhere near a disclosure threshold), but South Korea alone supplies 23.8% of FY2024 revenue and is explicitly named by the company as its "largest market" [FY24 Annual Report, Economic Report, p.~103], with the broader Asia region (48% of Group GMV) dominated by that one country. None of this concentration is contractually secured — Korean consumers and restaurant partners transact on a pay-per-order, at-will basis with no long-term contract disclosed (Inference, not from filings), so the revenue is naked to Korean-won FX moves, local competitive intensity (e.g., discount events, subscription rebuilds discussed on the FY2025 earnings call), and Korea-specific regulatory action. The single biggest dependency the synthesizer should know about: roughly a quarter of Group revenue rests on one country (South Korea) that the company itself calls its largest market, with no long-term contract or hedge disclosed against that exposure.
