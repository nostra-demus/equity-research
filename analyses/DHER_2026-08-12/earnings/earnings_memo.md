## What This Module Found

DHER's earnings picture is split. Statutory operating income (profit from running the business, before financing and tax) turned positive for the first time in five years, and management's Adjusted EBITDA guidance — its own preferred profit measure, before interest, tax, depreciation and one-offs — has landed in-line or better for three straight years [04_guidance-consensus §6]. That is the real bull point.

The single biggest earnings driver has been an own-delivery take-rate shift — DHER delivering more orders itself and keeping a bigger cut, now 78% of group order value (GMV) in FY2025, up from 67% [02_revenue-drivers §4, §7]. It drove 61% of the one year this data can decompose exactly (FY2024), but management says it is running out of room in FY2026, shifting the load to raw order growth [02_revenue-drivers §7].

The biggest risk sits in cash. FY2025's headline "positive free cash flow" of +€250m (the cash left after running the business and capital spending) only works by excluding an implied ~€600–650m legal/regulatory cash outflow tied to how delivery riders are classified [06_earnings-quality §5, §10]. That is the same exposure KPMG's auditor flags as a going-concern-adjacent "Material Uncertainty" at the Glovo Spain subsidiary, with a €440–770m contingent liability (a possible future bill, not yet fixed) [08_earnings-red-flags §2.7, §5]. Cash conversion — cash generated versus reported profit — collapsed to 8.8% in FY2025 from 92.2% the year before [06_earnings-quality §2].

One measurement problem cuts across all of it: management's stated revenue growth runs 12-plus points above the growth computed from reported numbers (Q1 2026: +18% stated vs +5.8% computed; FY2025: +23% vs +14.4%), with no bridge disclosed anywhere [01_historical-financials §3, §6]. The synthesis treats this gap as genuinely unresolved, not settled toward either figure.

## The Specialists, Briefly

- **earnings-data-triage:** Data sufficient. DHER is inside a live Uber takeover approach (Jul 16, 2026), and no audited FY2025 annual report exists in the pool, so FY2025 figures rest on lower-tier data sources.
- **historical-financials:** Revenue growth is decelerating (+46.5%→+15.9%→+23.7%→+14.4%, FY2022–FY2025); the stated-vs-computed 12-point gap is unresolved.
- **revenue-drivers:** The own-delivery take-rate shift is the biggest revenue driver but is running out of room faster than the order-growth driver replacing it.
- **margin-drivers:** Adjusted EBITDA margin still expanding but at a sharply slowing pace; gross margin has fallen two years running. Rider-cost regulation is the biggest margin driver, with no way to pass the cost on.
- **guidance-consensus:** Bar is fair on Adjusted EBITDA. FY2026 normalized EPS consensus was cut ~€1.78 over the past year; the price-target/recommendation consensus is distorted by the Uber offer.
- **beat-miss-setup:** Balanced. EPS has missed Street five years straight; the revenue-reconciliation gap is the most likely failure mode.
- **earnings-quality:** 36/100. The FCF/cash-conversion story excludes an implied ~€600–650m outflow, carried forward as FY2026 policy.
- **earnings-sensitivity:** Volatility 66/100 (inverted). The rider-cost bear case (-€344.5m) is ~38% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA — the biggest, most one-sided variable.

**Most important disagreement:** earnings-quality framed the ~€600–650m cash-outflow exclusion as a disclosure/definition issue, while the business-model scan showed KPMG's auditor attaches going-concern-adjacent language to the same Glovo Spain exposure. The synthesis adopts the more source-grounded auditor read (audited filings beat vendor exports, CLAUDE.md §4) and escalates it to Critical — the main reason the verdict is "Mixed" rather than more constructive [Reconciliation §3.2].

## What Would Change This Read

**Would upgrade it:** the Aug-27 FQ2 2026 print resolves the revenue-reconciliation gap toward management's higher framing on a reported-currency basis; FY2026 free cash flow lands on guide without a comparably large "extraordinary" exclusion; an audited FY2025 annual report narrows or removes the going-concern language on Glovo Spain.

**Would downgrade it:** the Aug-27 print confirms reported growth slowing toward the ~4.7–5.8% computed rate; Italy's rider-reclassification follows Spain's precedent, crystallizing a comparable or larger cash cost; FY2026 FCF again misses the >€200m floor via the same exclusion method.

**Data needed:** an audited FY2025 annual report (to confirm or update the going-concern note and the €440–770m contingent liability); a quarterly Adjusted EBITDA / cash-flow / FCF series for a clean trailing read [Section 7].

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict: Mixed earnings setup** — improving profit level, fading growth engine, poor cash quality; they do not average into one direction.
- **Could be better than it looks:** management's Adjusted EBITDA guidance has been in-line or better three years running, and it raised its own confidence to the upper half of the FY2026 range 35 days after setting it, citing accelerating order growth.
- **Could be worse than it looks:** cash conversion fell to 8.8%, and the "positive FCF" headline depends on excluding a ~€600–650m outflow tied to an auditor-flagged going-concern-adjacent risk — if it recurs, FY2026 can look "on guide" while real cash disappoints.
- **Evidence missing:** an audited FY2025 annual report to confirm or update the going-concern note and the €440–770m contingent liability, currently FY2024-vintage and unconfirmed for FY2025.
- **Watch next:** the Aug-27 FQ2 2026 print — specifically whether the 12-point revenue-reconciliation gap resolves and whether a comparable "extraordinary" cash outflow recurs.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Adjusted EBITDA:** management's preferred profit measure — earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization, and certain one-off items.
- **Statutory operating income / EBIT:** profit from running the business, before financing costs and tax, as reported under accounting rules.
- **GMV (gross merchandise value):** the total order value flowing through the platform.
- **Take-rate:** the share of order value DHER keeps as its own revenue.
- **Free cash flow (FCF):** cash left after running the business and capital spending.
- **Cash conversion (CFO / Adjusted EBITDA):** how much of reported profit actually shows up as operating cash.
- **Gross margin:** revenue left after the direct cost of delivering orders, as a percent of revenue.
- **Contingent liability:** a possible future cost that is not yet fixed or paid.
- **Going-concern-adjacent uncertainty:** an auditor's flag that there is material doubt about a unit's ability to keep operating.
- **Consensus:** the average of Wall Street analysts' estimates.
- **Basis points (bps):** hundredths of a percent; 100 bps = 1 percentage point.
