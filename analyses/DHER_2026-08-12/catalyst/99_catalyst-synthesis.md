# Catalyst Module — DHER (Synthesis)

## Abstract

DHER's calendar is not a thematic story — it carries one hard-proven date and a set of real, evidenced windows, but the single event actually driving the price lacks a contractual timeline. The nearest dated catalyst is the FQ2 2026 results release on 2026-08-27, which tests whether Adjusted EBITDA holds in the upper half of the €910m–€960m guided range. The single most important catalyst is Uber Technologies' acquisition of Delivery Hero, guided to close in H2 2027 at a disclosed €41.50/share cash offer sourced only from an unverified web pull — bullish if regulators clear on schedule, bearish (repricing toward a €18.59 standalone bear case) if the deal stalls. The biggest bearish catalyst is the unaddressed 2029 debt-maturity cluster (€2,145.4m, proven dates). Verdict: catalysts exist, but timing on the dominant one is vague.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** the 2029 maturity cluster (€2,145.4m, 46.4% of total debt: €480.9m due 2029-03-10, €1,110.3m due 2029-12-01, plus the KRW facility later in 2029) is proven-dated and unaddressed — management's own "visibility all the way into 2028" language stops short of it [`balance-sheet-survival/02_maturity-wall-and-refinancing.md §2, §4`]. If the Uber deal breaks, this cluster resurfaces as the market's next dated concern.
- **Strongest bull point:** the FQ2 2026 results release, 2026-08-27, is a proven date carrying a three-year in-line-or-better Adjusted EBITDA track record against guidance, with management explicitly steering to the upper half of €910m–€960m as of 2026-04-30 [`earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md §1`, `earnings/05_beat-miss-setup.md`].
- **Single killer risk specific to the calendar:** the number that anchors nearly the entire current price — the €41.50/share Uber offer — is not in the data pool; it is a web-sourced, unverified figure. Treating the deal-arbitrage spread as a proven, timeable catalyst risks false precision on both the price and the H2 2027 close window, which sits entirely outside company control (multi-jurisdiction merger-control approval).
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** this report's own standalone base-case fair value (€39.65/share) sits close to but below the disclosed offer price, and the standalone bear case (€18.59/share) is far below the current price (€37.20) — meaning a broken deal does not just remove a premium, it exposes real standalone downside [`valuation/07_scenario-and-fair-value.md §3`]. A five-year EPS miss streak and a -€366m FY2025 free-cash-flow swing against guide are also already on the record, undercutting any assumption that the Aug-27 print is a clean read on operating momentum.

## 4. Note To The Final Synthesizer

- The nearest dated, evidenced catalyst is FQ2 2026 results on **2026-08-27** (15 days from this report's date). It tests Adjusted EBITDA against the €910m–€960m guide and the unreconciled +18% (management) vs. +5.8% (vendor-computed) revenue-growth gap — but the calendar module itself flags that Uber-deal news could swamp the stock's reaction to this print.
- The single most important catalyst is the Uber acquisition close, guided **H2 2027**. A buyer cannot precisely time it: no contractual close date, no disclosed break fee in the data pool, and a guided window spanning roughly 12–17 months. The price-anchoring offer price (€41.50/share) is web-sourced, unverified, and absent from the data pool — treat it as directional, not proven.
- The near-term calendar (next 3–4 months) skews toward routine operating-execution risk rather than a clean bullish or bearish lean: DHER's five-year EPS miss streak and FY2025 free-cash-flow guidance swing (-€366m) argue for caution on Aug-27, even though Adjusted EBITDA (the metric management actually guides to) has a clean three-year track record.
- The biggest negative catalyst is the unaddressed **2029 debt-maturity cluster** (€2,145.4m, 46.4% of total debt; proven dates 2029-03-10 and 2029-12-01), which only becomes relevant again if the Uber deal breaks and standalone credit risk resurfaces.
- Any resumption of DHER's own debt-funded M&A if the deal fails must NOT be read as conviction-lifting — it carries a proven §24 Filter 4 serial-acquirer flag (severity 75/100, ~€2.05bn cumulative goodwill impairment, per `business-model/11_capital-allocation-governance.md`).
- Timing is **mixed-to-vague**: one proven near-term date exists, but the dominant, price-determining catalyst is a guided window outside company control. This should drive a real confidence cap in the master synthesizer's §7 / §17 catalyst-timing discipline — do not treat the deal-close window as a proven date.
