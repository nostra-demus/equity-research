## What This Module Found

- The calendar is real but lopsided. One catalyst is hard-dated: FQ2 2026 results on **2026-08-27** (15 days from this report). Several debt maturities also carry exact dates out to 2030. But every catalyst that could move the stock more than a routine amount — the deal close, tender mechanics, the Taiwan sale, EU rider-law policy — is a window, not a date.
- The single most important catalyst is **Uber Technologies buying Delivery Hero**, guided to close in **H2 2027**. At €37.20 (2026-08-07) against the disclosed €41.50/share cash offer, the arbitrage spread — the gap between today's price and the offer — is about +11.6%. Bullish if regulators clear it on schedule; bearish if it stalls.
- The single biggest weakness in the calendar: the €41.50/share offer price that anchors nearly the whole current price is **web-sourced and unverified — it is not in the data pool.** Treat it as directional, not proven, on both price and the H2 2027 close window (which sits outside company control, in multi-country merger approval).
- The biggest bearish catalyst is the unaddressed **2029 debt-maturity cluster** — €480.9m of convertible bonds due 2029-03-10 and a $/€1,110.3m term facility due 2029-12-01, €2,145.4m in total (46.4% of all debt), both proven dates, with no disclosed refinancing. It only matters again if the Uber deal breaks and DHER trades on its own credit risk.
- A broken deal is not just a lost premium: the synthesis's own standalone base-case fair value is €39.65/share and the standalone bear case is €18.59/share — well below today's €37.20 — so a failed deal exposes real downside, not just the giving-back of a bid premium.
- Even the one dated event carries caution: DHER has a five-year run of missing earnings-per-share estimates and swung €366m worse than its own free-cash-flow guide (the cash the business throws off after spending) in FY2025 — so the Aug-27 print may not be a clean read on operating momentum, and deal-related trading could swamp the stock's reaction to it anyway.

## What Would Change This Read

Observable events the synthesis names that would move the module's read:

- **Uber deal close (H2 2027 window).** Better: multi-country merger approvals clear on or ahead of schedule and the deal completes at €41.50/share or higher (a topping bid is not excluded — DoorDash previously showed interest). Worse: a regulatory block or extended delay, repricing toward the €18.59 standalone bear case.
- **Offer Document / tender start (undated in 2026).** Better: swift BaFin approval and publication narrows the spread. Worse: a delayed or contested document, or a competing bid reopening price discovery.
- **FQ2 2026 results (2026-08-27, proven).** Better: Adjusted EBITDA in the upper half of €910m–€960m and revenue growth resolving toward management's own framing. Worse: growth resolves to the lower vendor-computed trend, a third straight year of margin compression, or another free-cash-flow miss.
- **Taiwan disposal close (H2 2026 window).** Better: closes on time at the disclosed ~$600m plus €520m, cash used mainly for debt reduction ahead of 2029. Worse: delay or lower proceeds, with Asia revenue/EBITDA stepping down and no offset.
- **EU Platform Work Directive transposition (~Nov 2026, calculated).** Better: case-by-case rider-classification flexibility survives. Worse: Spain's employment-based model spreads EU-wide, crystallizing costs near the disclosed ≈−€344.5m stress test (~38% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA).

## Bottom Line

- Verdict: catalysts exist but timing is vague — one dated event (Aug-27 earnings), one dominant undated one (Uber close, H2 2027).
- Could be better than it looks: if the Uber deal clears near €41.50/share, there is ~+11.6% upside from €37.20, and Prosus/Naspers is already irrevocably committed to tender.
- Could be worse than it looks: a broken deal does not just remove a premium — the standalone bear case is €18.59/share, roughly half today's price.
- What's missing: a contractual close date and disclosed break fee for the Uber deal, and a data-pool (not web) confirmation of the €41.50/share offer price — the single biggest number in the calendar is unverified.
- One thing to watch next: the Aug-27 FQ2 results — the only catalyst the thesis can lean on for timing, though deal-related trading could drown out the print.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Adjusted EBITDA** — a rough profit measure before interest, tax, and asset write-downs; the number management guides to.
- **Free cash flow** — the cash the business generates after its spending; DHER swung €366m worse than its own guide in FY2025.
- **Arbitrage spread** — the gap between today's share price and a takeover offer price; here ~+11.6%.
- **Change of control / squeeze-out / domination agreement** — German-law steps that let a controlling buyer, past set ownership levels, cash out or compress remaining minority holders.
- **Debt-maturity cluster** — a bunch of borrowings falling due close together (€2,145.4m across 2029) that must be repaid or refinanced.
