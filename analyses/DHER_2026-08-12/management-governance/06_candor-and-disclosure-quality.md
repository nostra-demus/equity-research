# Candor & Disclosure Quality — DHER

**Data basis.** Two verbatim S&P Global Market Intelligence earnings-call transcripts are in the pool — the FY2025 full-year results call (Mar 26, 2026, CEO Niklas Oestberg and CFO Marie-Anne Popp, full prepared remarks plus a 9-question Q&A) and the Q1 2026 Sales/Trading Statement Call (Apr 30, 2026, same speakers). Both are genuine speaker-attributed transcripts with analyst names and firms, not paraphrases or press releases — so tone, Q&A directness, and "owning the miss" ARE assessable here, unlike a partial-data case. No prior shareholder letter exists under German AktG practice; the Management Board's letter inside the FY2024 Annual Report substitutes, per `01_management-and-track-record.md`. No FY2025 audited Annual Report is in the pool, so the newest written (non-transcript) disclosure available is ~15.6 months old (FY2024 Annual Report, published Apr 25, 2025).

**RF-DISC-002 triggered.** The goodwill-impairment-every-year, restructuring-costs-every-year, and SBC-excluded-every-year pattern is a recurring "one-off" / aggressive non-GAAP add-back pattern by definition — five straight years for two of the three items, six straight years for the third.

## 3. Disclosure Transparency

Management discloses granular operating KPIs (GMV like-for-like growth, own-delivery mix shift 67%→78%, subscriber penetration 43–61% of GMV by market, AdTech revenue run-rate) with real specificity, and the company's SASB disclosure of 8 data-breach incidents in FY2024 (all involving personal data, 35,343 users affected) is a genuine, non-obvious transparency point most companies would omit (`business-model/12_red-flags-sweep.md`). Set against that: segment-level profit is disclosed only on the heavily adjusted Adjusted EBITDA basis with a €683.0m unallocated bridge back to the audited Group operating result (`business-model/12_red-flags-sweep.md`, segment-map finding), and the Free Cash Flow definition itself was changed effective Jan 1, 2025 to newly exclude "extraordinary outflows" — a genuine reduction in comparability adopted in the same year those exclusions first did large (~€600–650m) work, which is the single disclosure change in this record that got LESS transparent over time rather than more.

## 4. Tone in Bad Times

The worst recent period by the numbers is FY2025: a -€782.9m statutory net loss, cash conversion (CFO/Adjusted EBITDA) collapsing to 8.8%, and a headline FCF outcome that is negative once normalized. The CEO's opening framing of that year was unreservedly upbeat — "2025 was a defining year," "I'm proud to say that we did both" — built entirely around the Adjusted EBITDA growth story, with the statutory net loss's absolute size never spoken aloud (only the €183m year-over-year improvement was verbalized) and GAAP operating profit not mentioned in prepared remarks at all. Q&A was mostly direct: Popp gave a specific, non-evasive answer on the FCF exclusion methodology and on the €520–860m Glovo Spain contingent-liability range when pressed by Barclays and Citi analysts respectively, and Oestberg answered a pointed question about the Uber "process overhang" plainly ("it's obviously a clear problem for us... it's not our decision"). One partial exception: pressed on a 40% Korean order-growth figure, Oestberg first deflected ("we don't want to comment on potential external data... it's pretty random") until the analyst pointed out the number was from the company's OWN press release, at which point he downplayed his own disclosed figure ("I wouldn't draw too much around that") — a minor but real instance of distancing from a number the company itself had published.

## 5. Candor Read

Delivery Hero's management answers direct, pointed analyst questions candidly and with real specificity — the FCF-exclusion mechanics, the Spain contingent-liability math, and the Uber-overhang admission were all handled without evasion when an analyst pushed. Set against that: the unprompted, prepared-remarks framing consistently leads with the flattering non-GAAP number (Adjusted EBITDA "profitable growth," "second consecutive year of positive free cash flow") while the absolute size of the statutory net loss is never spoken and the GAAP operating result goes unmentioned — a pattern of selective emphasis rather than fabrication, but selective emphasis all the same, compounded by adjustments that recur every year rather than being genuinely one-off (Section 2) and by a FCF-definition change adopted in the same year the exclusion first mattered most. The single biggest candor signal is the gap between the two registers: management is honest under cross-examination but consistently chooses the more flatteringframing when it controls the microphone — a pattern this report classifies as **"mixed"** rather than "candid / high-trust" or "promotional / low-trust." Per CLAUDE.md §24 Filter 1 / §13, no unresolved adverse personal-integrity signal was routed from `business-model/01_disqualifier-scan` to weigh against this (`01_management-and-track-record.md` §4A confirms none found) — but the Glovo Spain going-concern disqualifier itself (a subsidiary-level auditor emphasis-of-matter, not a candor finding on its own) sits alongside this mixed communication record rather than being netted out against it, consistent with the instruction that a promotional tone alongside unresolved integrity/going-concern questions compounds rather than offsets.
