## What This Module Found

Delivery Hero's founder-CEO has run the company for 15 years and has hit his own guided Adjusted EBITDA target — a measure of operating profit that strips out many costs — within a point or two in each of FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025. That is a real, checkable three-year record on the metric he controls [`01_management-and-track-record.md`, Findings 01-008–01-010].

The single most important driver against the stock is what the company did with its money. Two debt-and-stock-funded mega-deals (Woowa, roughly $4.0bn; Glovo, €2.3bn) led to €2,054m of cumulative goodwill impairment — write-downs admitting the businesses were worth far less than was paid — over FY2021–FY2025. Book value per share (the accounting net worth backing each share) fell 75.4%, from €22.32 to €5.50, and the share count rose 49.6% with no buybacks to offset it [`02_capital-allocation-scorecard.md`, Findings 02-009, 02-020, 02-015].

The single most important risk is structural, not behavioral: the auditor (KPMG) flagged a going-concern warning — doubt about the ability to keep operating — on the Glovo Spain subsidiary, tied to a Spanish rider-classification case with a contingent liability (a possible future bill) of €440–770m [FY24 Annual Report, Independent Auditor's Report, p.222–223]. This is a subsidiary-level warning inside an otherwise unmodified group audit opinion, not doubt over the whole group — but it hard-locks the verdict regardless of the operating record.

Layered on top: a live Uber tender offer in which the Management Board's unvested pay-related shares fully vest if they resign within three months of a change of control — a personal payout detached from the price other shareholders receive [`03_incentives-and-compensation.md`, Finding 03-018]. Insiders own just 0.38% of the company, all of it from pay packages rather than open-market purchases [`04_ownership-and-insider-behavior.md`, Finding 04-002].

## The Specialists, Briefly

- **Data triage:** Enough data to run all six specialists, with a partial cap on ownership detail; the Glovo Spain going-concern note locks the downstream verdict.
- **Management & track record:** Stable founder-CEO who reliably hits guided Adjusted EBITDA, but statutory profit and cash flow stay fragile — management quality capped at 60.
- **Capital allocation:** Value-destructive — €2,054m of goodwill write-offs and 49.6% share dilution with zero buybacks — scored 40.
- **Incentives & compensation:** A genuinely improved FY2024 framework (70% of long-term pay on relative shareholder return) sitting on legacy options that rewarded pure growth, still vesting through 2029.
- **Ownership & insider behavior:** Thin, pay-derived insider ownership (0.38%); the dominant fact is the live control event — Uber holds 37% economic exposure and reference holder Prosus has committed to tender, pushing Uber "over 50%".
- **Board & shareholder rights:** Clean on paper (one-share-one-vote, no poison pill), but Uber's stated intent to "evaluate the most appropriate ownership structure" above 50% raises squeeze-out risk for minority holders.
- **Candor & disclosure:** Candid under direct questioning, but consistently leads with the flattering non-GAAP number — the absolute FY2025 statutory net loss (−€782.9m) was never spoken aloud on the earnings call.

**Most important disagreement the synthesis reconciled:** capital allocation scores the five-year record 40 (value-destructive) while incentives score the new framework 57 (mixed, improving). The synthesis resolved this conservatively by separating timelines — the new pay design is real but one year old and untested, and does not repair the value already destroyed under the old framework whose legacy options vest through 2029.

## What Would Change This Read

**Would strengthen it:** the Glovo Spain liability settles at the low end with no escalation; the FY2025 audited Annual Report shows the going-concern language lifted; the Uber deal closes with a clean full squeeze-out (95%+) or a fair appraisal mechanism for minority shares; FY2026 shows a second straight year of clean statutory operating profit with no one-off flattery.

**Would weaken it:** the €440–770m Glovo Spain liability crystallizes at or near the high end; a second major goodwill impairment in FY2026 repeats the pattern; the Uber deal closes below the squeeze-out threshold, leaving a minority stub under a value-maximizing parent with no clear protection.

**Data needed:** the FY2025 audited Annual Report (not yet in the pool); the final Uber tender-acceptance rate and deal structure; an insider-transaction log for 2025–2026; the FY2024 say-on-pay vote outcome.

## Bottom Line

- **Verdict: Serious governance concerns**, rating Weak (Governance Score 50/100, confidence-adjusted 36/100) — capped and floored by the hard disqualifier, not by opinion.
- **Could be better than it looks:** the FY2024 pay redesign is a real improvement (70% of long-term pay on relative shareholder return, clawback extended to all variable pay), and the three-year Adjusted EBITDA delivery record is genuine.
- **Could be worse than it looks:** if the €440–770m Glovo Spain liability lands near the high end, or a new impairment repeats the Woowa/Glovo pattern, the capital story deteriorates further.
- **Missing evidence:** an insider-transaction log for 2025–2026 — the single highest-value next request — plus the not-yet-published FY2025 audited Annual Report to confirm whether the going-concern language persists.
- **One thing to watch next:** how the Uber tender closes — a full squeeze-out versus a minority stub left under a majority owner pursuing its own value, with management's equity fully vesting either way.

## Plain-English Glossary

- **Adjusted EBITDA:** a company-defined profit figure that strips out many costs; management's guided target, hit three years running.
- **Goodwill impairment:** a write-down admitting an acquired business is worth less than was paid; here €2,054m over five years.
- **Book value per share:** the accounting net worth backing each share; down 75.4%.
- **Tangible book value per share:** book value excluding intangibles like goodwill; negative in every year of the window.
- **Contingent liability:** a possible future bill that depends on an unresolved event; the Glovo Spain figure is €440–770m.
- **Going-concern note:** an auditor warning about the ability to keep operating; here on a subsidiary, inside an otherwise unmodified group opinion.
- **Buyback:** a company buying back its own shares to offset dilution; DHER did none while share count rose 49.6%.
- **Change-of-control vesting / squeeze-out:** unvested pay-shares that fully convert on a takeover; a squeeze-out is a majority owner forcing out remaining minority holders.
- **Non-GAAP:** a company-adjusted number that departs from standard accounting rules — the "flattering" figure management led with.
