## 6. Stage-One sheet comment

Real news on HealthCare Global this quarter, and it cuts both ways. First quarter revenue for the year came in at 6.95 billion rupees, up 13 percent, below the roughly 7.34 billion rupee bar we were watching, which confirms our earnings-deceleration view. Separately, the company had another CFO change, with Srinivasa Raghavan leaving on 20 August and Ruby Ritolia named to replace him, which is a real deepening of the governance concern we already flagged as a top risk. Against that, adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 19.4 percent from 18.2, adjusted profit nearly tripled off a small base, the fertility business sale closed, a rights issue raised 4.25 billion rupees, and the stock is up about 10.5 percent since our note versus roughly 2.9 percent for the Nifty 50. That improvement is exactly the risk our own red-team flagged as the way this Avoid could be wrong: the newer hospitals ramping up faster than we modelled. We are not changing the rating. Two of three concrete facts this quarter support staying away; the third is a real, early warning sign worth taking seriously, not dismissing. The audited profit figure for the full year still needs checking against the actual annual report, not press coverage.
