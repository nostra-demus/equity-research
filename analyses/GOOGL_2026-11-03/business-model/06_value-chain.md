[data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-Q(Oct-29-2026).doc, Q3 2026, Item 2 — Cost of Revenues] |

The filings support cost absorption and mix management, not a measured customer price pass-through. The Q3 2026 TAC rate fell from 20.1% to 20.0% of advertising revenue because revenue mix shifted toward Search, while the Search TAC rate itself was substantially unchanged; that is a mix effect, not evidence that advertisers paid more for the same service. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-Q(Oct-29-2026).doc, Q3 2026, Item 2 — Cost of Revenues] Alphabet also says it seeks data-centre efficiency through model design and TPU/GPU infrastructure, and it has long-term supply, energy-service, and leasing agreements. Those are mitigation tools, but the filings do not quantify their cost offset or separate any customer price increase from internal cost reduction. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 7 — Trends in Our Business; data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-Q(Oct-29-2026).doc, Q3 2026, Item 1A — Risk Factors] Realised recovery cannot be calculated as `1 − observed margin impact / gross pre-mitigation impact` because the disclosed data do not supply that gross input-impact measure.

**Supplier / input concentration (quantify).** Alphabet does not disclose the share of COGS or purchases represented by its largest supplier or top three suppliers. It does disclose a material *type* of dependency: some technical-infrastructure and device components are available from one or limited sources, and specialised AI-chip, server, and networking supply is limited to a small number of qualified suppliers. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1A — Manufacturing and Supply Chain Risks] This is a material single-/limited-source risk, but no percentage can be assigned from the pool.

## 3. Customer Pricing Power

Alphabet has direct evidence of pricing control for Google Network advertising: it says it controls the inventory before it reaches customers and has discretion in establishing price. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-Q(Oct-29-2026).doc, Q3 2026, Note 2 — Revenue Recognition] That does not prove it can impose a broad price increase without volume loss. The FY2025 10-K says advertisers, distributors, publishers, and content providers may terminate at any time if Google does not create more value than alternatives; ad budgets also move with macro conditions. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1A — Risks Specific to Our Company] In the reviewed 10-K, 10-Q, and Q2/Q3 2026 earnings-call material, no explicit broad price increase and associated volume reaction were disclosed. For Cloud, the filing describes competitive and changing pricing and delivery models. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1A — Google Cloud Risks] Accordingly, customer price power is established for certain Network inventory, but the resilience of demand after a general price rise is not proven from available data.

## 4. Economic Control Verdict

**Mixed.** Alphabet controls Google Network advertising inventory and has discretion in its pricing; it also had no individual customer or affiliated customer group above 10% of revenue in 2023–2025. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Note 2 — Revenue Recognition and Customer Concentration] But customers and partners can terminate, Cloud pricing is competitive, and the company’s AI and Cloud expansion relies on power, land, water, and limited-source hardware supply. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1A — Risks Specific to Our Company; Manufacturing and Supply Chain Risks] It controls important parts of the revenue interface, but not the full economics of the AI-capacity build-out.

## 5. The Single Biggest Bargaining Risk

The binding relationship is with limited-source AI infrastructure suppliers and power providers: failure to secure specialised chips, servers, network equipment, or power on acceptable terms could restrict capacity to train models and serve Cloud customers. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1A — Manufacturing and Supply Chain Risks]
