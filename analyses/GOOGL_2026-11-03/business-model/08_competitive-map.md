# Competitive Map — GOOGL

## 1. Dominant Segment

Google Services is the dominant segment: it produced FY2025 revenue of $342.721bn (85.08% of $402.836bn total) and $139.404bn of segment operating income; the frozen CIQ sidecar reports the same revenue build, including the $0.127bn unallocated hedging loss. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Note 15, pp. 86–87; CIQ Financials→Segments (Revenues, latest annual column) — vendor export]

## 2. Named Competitors

The profit figures below are reported GAAP. Return on equity (ROE) means annual net income divided by average beginning- and end-period stockholders’ equity; the calculations are shown because none of these companies reports a directly comparable pure-search or pure-advertising ROE.

### Competitor A — Meta Platforms

- **Ticker / listing:** META / NasdaqGS.
- **Where they compete:** Meta’s Family of Apps sells advertising against consumer attention, discovery, and advertiser budgets; Alphabet identifies online advertising platforms and social networks as competitors. This is product-overlap inference, not a filed statement that every Meta product substitutes for Google Search. [Meta FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Competition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026003942/meta-20251231.htm); [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1 — Competition]
- **Scale:** FY2025 revenue was $200.966bn; the more relevant Family of Apps segment had $198.759bn of revenue. [Meta FY2025 Form 10-K, Financial Statements and Note 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026003942/meta-20251231.htm)
- **Profitability / return on capital:** FY2025 operating margin was 41.4% ($83.276bn operating income / $200.966bn revenue). FY2025 ROE was 30.2% ($60.458bn net income / average equity of $199.940bn, calculated as ($217.243bn + $182.637bn) / 2). [Meta FY2025 Form 10-K, Financial Statements, pp. 88–90](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026003942/meta-20251231.htm)
- **Source named in:** Capital IQ’s frozen Alphabet comparable-company set names Meta; Alphabet’s filing names online-advertising platforms and social networks as competitive categories. [data/GOOGL/Company Comparable Analysis Alphabet Inc.xls, Business Description, as of 2026-11-03 — vendor export; data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1 — Competition]
- **One-line read:** Meta is the clearest large rival for advertising attention and video/social ad spend, rather than a pure general-search rival.

### Competitor B — Amazon.com

- **Ticker / listing:** AMZN / NasdaqGS.
- **Where they compete:** Amazon sells sponsored, display, and video advertising to sellers, vendors, publishers, authors, and others; it also identifies web search engines, comparison-shopping sites, social networks, and advertising providers among its competitors. Its commerce-search and retail-media overlap with Google Services is therefore real but narrower than general web search. [Amazon FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Business and Competition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm); [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1 — Competition]
- **Scale:** FY2025 consolidated net sales were $716.924bn; advertising-services revenue was $68.635bn. [Amazon FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 10 — Segment Information](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm)
- **Profitability / return on capital:** FY2025 operating margin was 11.2% ($79.975bn operating income / $716.924bn sales). FY2025 ROE was 22.3% ($77.670bn net income / average equity of $348.518bn, calculated as ($411.065bn + $285.970bn) / 2). These are consolidated figures, not retail-media margins. [Amazon FY2025 Form 10-K, Financial Statements, pp. 39, 67–68](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm)
- **Source named in:** Capital IQ’s frozen Alphabet comparable-company set names Amazon; its own filing describes advertising services and treats web search engines and advertising providers as competitive categories. [data/GOOGL/Company Comparable Analysis Alphabet Inc.xls, Business Description, as of 2026-11-03 — vendor export; Amazon FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Competition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm)
- **One-line read:** Amazon is a large vertical-search and retail-media competitor for commercial queries and advertiser budgets, not a like-for-like Google Search business.

### Competitor C — Microsoft

- **Ticker / listing:** MSFT / NasdaqGS.
- **Where they compete:** Microsoft’s Search Advertising business comprises Bing, Copilot, Microsoft News, Edge, and third-party affiliates; Microsoft says that business competes with search engines, websites, social platforms, and portals. Alphabet lists general-purpose search engines as a competitive category. [Microsoft FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Search Advertising and Competition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm); [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-K(Feb-05-2026).doc, FY2025, Item 1 — Competition]
- **Scale:** FY2026 consolidated revenue was $331.839bn; separately disclosed Search Advertising revenue was $15.176bn. [Microsoft FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7 and Note 18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm)
- **Profitability / return on capital:** FY2026 operating margin was 46.8% ($155.237bn operating income / $331.839bn revenue). FY2026 ROE was 34.0% ($133.749bn net income / average equity of $392.933bn, calculated as ($442.387bn + $343.479bn) / 2). These are consolidated GAAP results, not the return from Search Advertising. [Microsoft FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 8 — Income Statements and Balance Sheets](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm)
- **Source named in:** Capital IQ’s frozen Alphabet comparable-company set names Microsoft; Microsoft’s own filing explicitly identifies Bing and Copilot search advertising. [data/GOOGL/Company Comparable Analysis Alphabet Inc.xls, Business Description, as of 2026-11-03 — vendor export; Microsoft FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Search Advertising](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm)
- **One-line read:** Microsoft is the clearest named general-search rival, with Bing/Copilot and Edge tied directly to its advertising offering.

## 3. Competitive Position

Position vs peers: not disclosed. Alphabet does not disclose Google Services market share or a global digital-advertising share. As a partial, non-filed proxy, StatCounter estimates Google at 91.05% and Bing at 4.80% of worldwide general web-search referrals in October 2026; that supports current search scale but does not measure the broader Google Services segment or a like-for-like year-on-year trend. [Web: StatCounter Global Stats, worldwide search-engine market share, October 2026, retrieved 2026-11-03 — indicative, unverified](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/worldwide/)

Alphabet’s Q3 2026 filing says Google Search & other revenue rose by $6.8bn year on year, driven by higher search queries, advertiser spending, and improved ad formats and delivery. That is a useful operating signal, but without an industry or competitor growth denominator it does not establish share gain, holding, or loss. [data/GOOGL/Alphabet_Inc_-_Form_10-Q(Oct-29-2026).doc, Q3 2026, Item 2 — Google Advertising]

## 4. Competitive Shape

General web search is **monopoly-leaning**: the October 2026 StatCounter read places Google at 91.05% and Bing at 4.80%, so the two sum to 95.85% of that measured referral market. The source is an indicative, unverified web estimate, not a filed market-share disclosure. This classification applies only to general web search; Google Services also contains YouTube, the network, subscriptions, Play, and devices, for which the available evidence does not provide a comparable top-N share or HHI. [Web: StatCounter Global Stats, worldwide search-engine market share, October 2026, retrieved 2026-11-03 — indicative, unverified](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/worldwide/)

## 5. Caveat

Alphabet’s FY2025 filing names competitive categories rather than individual companies. The named three-company set is therefore anchored by the frozen Capital IQ comparable-company export and then validated against each rival’s own filing and product disclosures. Peer financials use different fiscal year-ends and are consolidated businesses: they are not margins or returns for the precise advertising/search overlap. A segmented, source-backed global share series covering general search, social/video ads, and retail media would be needed to make a defensible share-gain or share-loss conclusion for Google Services.
