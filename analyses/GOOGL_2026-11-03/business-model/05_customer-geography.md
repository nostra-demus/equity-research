## 4. Read

Alphabet is not concentrated in a disclosed individual customer: the FY2025 audited filing caps every individual customer or affiliated group below 10% of revenue, although that disclosure is not refreshed in the Q3 2026 filing. Geography is concentrated: US customer addresses supplied 51% of nine-month 2026 revenue, while EMEA added 27%. The core advertising customer base is not shown as contractually secured — advertising supplied $240.9bn, or 67.4%, of nine-month revenue and many advertiser contracts are terminable at any time — whereas Cloud has separately disclosed contractual backlog. The single biggest dependency is therefore broad advertising demand, especially in the United States, rather than reliance on one named outside customer. [Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 2]; [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A and Note 2]
