# Unit Economics — GOOGL

## 1. Natural Unit

The natural economic unit for Google Services is a monetized advertising engagement—chiefly a paid Search click; Google Search & other generated $224.532bn, or 65.5% of the segment's $342.721bn FY2025 revenue. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Revenues; Note 15, pp. 86–87; CIQ Financials→Segments (Revenues, latest annual column) — $342.721bn, vendor export, agrees]

Secondary units are YouTube ad views, Google Network impressions, subscriptions, app transactions, and device sales. [Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2, p. 44]

## 3. Value Creation Read

**Verdict: Unclear from available disclosure.** The observable revenue side improved: paid clicks rose 6% and cost-per-click rose 7% in FY2025; in Q3 2026, paid clicks rose 8% and cost-per-click rose 4%. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Monetization Metrics; Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2, p. 49] But Alphabet does not disclose absolute paid-click volume, dollar revenue per click, per-click TAC or infrastructure cost, contribution margin, or advertiser lifetime; Google Services also mixes Search with YouTube, Network, subscriptions, apps, and devices. Contribution margin multiplied by unit lifetime therefore cannot be compared with unit-acquisition cost, so the single most valuable missing disclosure is contribution profit per incremental paid Search click, including the click volume and TAC/infrastructure allocation that builds it.

## 4. Sensitivity

With paid-click volume held constant—a sensitivity, not a forecast—a 20% change in cost-per-click would change click-driven revenue by 20%, because cost-per-click is defined as click-driven revenue divided by paid clicks. [Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2, p. 44] It is the input that most directly changes this read, but the filing does not disclose the absolute cost-per-click, click count, or per-click cost, so the dollar and profit effects cannot be calculated. Recent disclosed changes do not establish which input is more volatile: FY2025 paid clicks and cost-per-click changed +6% and +7%, respectively, while Q3 2026 changed +8% and +4%. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Monetization Metrics; Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2, p. 49]
