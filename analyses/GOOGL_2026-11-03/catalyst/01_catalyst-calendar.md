## 2. The Nearest Dated Catalyst

The closest stated corporate action is the 15 November preferred dividend, but GOOGL common holders cannot newly receive it because its record date was 1 November and it belongs to the preferred series. The nearest dated event relevant to GOOGL holders is the 7 December common-dividend record date followed by 14 December payment; it is a $0.22 declared distribution, not a material operating or valuation test. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 11 (Preferred and Common Dividends), p.37]

## 3. The Single Most Important Catalyst

The vendor-scheduled 2 February 2027 FY2026 results release is the decisive event. A $142.319bn Q4 revenue bar requires 25.0% growth from Q4 FY2025's $113.828bn, while the release also tests the $195bn–$205bn full-year CapEx range and management's 2027 Cloud/TPU margin commentary. A buyer can plan around the vendor date but cannot treat it as issuer-confirmed; timely TPU revenue is also not proof of the post-build free-cash-flow conversion the valuation work says the current price needs. [Capital IQ Estimates Report — Consensus, FQ4 FY2026; Q3 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks; analyses/GOOGL_2026-11-03/valuation/05_reverse-dcf.md, §§3–5]

## 4. Negative / Bearish Catalysts

- **2 February 2027, vendor-scheduled results:** revenue below the $142.319bn Q4 bar, an FY2026 CapEx outcome outside $195bn–$205bn, or further Cloud-margin pressure would challenge the 2027 cash-conversion case. [Capital IQ Estimates Report — Consensus, FQ4 FY2026; Q3 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks]
- **Calendar 2027 TPU ramp:** the filing makes recognition conditional on delivery and customer use. Delayed conversion or incremental depreciation and third-party-compute costs could make revenue growth less valuable than it appears. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2 (Google Cloud); Q3 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks]
- **April 2027 revolver expiry:** it is low immediate risk because the $4.0bn facility was undrawn, but a non-renewal alongside an unexpected funding need would remove optional liquidity. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 6 (Credit Facility), p.27]
- **Active legal and regulatory matters have no dated decision in the frozen pool.** Search appeals, ad-tech remedies/appeals, EC ad-tech litigation, DMA appeals, and the state-attorneys-general ad-tech trial remain downside risks, but none may be used as a timed catalyst. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 10 (Legal Matters)]

## 5. Calendar Read

The calendar has three filing-supported dated corporate events, one exact but issuer-unconfirmed vendor earnings date, and one broad 2027 operating window. The only material scheduled test is the February results event, which is two-sided but currently carries earnings-module downside risk from the high revenue hurdle and stated cost pressure. The central timing caveat is that legal/regulatory outcomes and the TPU ramp lack issuer-set decision or launch dates, so neither can support precise entry timing.
