# Catalyst Module — GOOGL (Synthesis)

## Abstract

GOOGL has dated corporate actions, but its material tests—the vendor-scheduled FY2026 release and calendar-2027 TPU ramp—lack issuer-confirmed timing. The nearest event a GOOGL buyer can receive is the 7 December 2026 common-dividend record date, followed by 14 December payment; it tests routine execution, not operating progress. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 11, p.37] The main event is the vendor-scheduled 2 February 2027 results release: the $142.319bn Q4 revenue bar and $195bn–$205bn FY2026 CapEx range give it two-sided tests. [Capital IQ Estimates Report — Consensus, FQ4 FY2026; Q3 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks] That print is also the biggest bearish event because management flagged harder comparisons and higher costs. Verdict: Catalysts exist but timing vague.

## 1A. Module Disconfirmation

- **Strongest bear point:** The vendor-scheduled 2 February 2027 print can skew down if Q4 revenue is below $142.319bn, FY2026 CapEx is outside $195bn–$205bn, or management gives a weaker Cloud-margin and capacity read. [Capital IQ Estimates Report — Consensus, FQ4 FY2026; Q3 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks]
- **Strongest bull point:** The same print is the most credible conviction-lifting event if revenue meets or exceeds $142.319bn, CapEx stays within $195bn–$205bn, and Cloud/TPU conversion offsets cost pressure; however, its exact date is vendor-scheduled, not issuer-confirmed. [Capital IQ Estimates Report — Consensus, FQ4 FY2026; Q3 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks]
- **Single killer risk:** The calendar-2027 TPU ramp could be treated as a bullish event even though it has no disclosed launch date and sits in a fast-changing industry; revenue recognition alone would not prove durable post-build free-cash-flow conversion. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2 (Google Cloud); Capital IQ Key Developments — 2026-10-27, Google/Oracle TPU announcement, data/GOOGL/Alphabet Inc NasdaqGS GOOGL Key Developments.xls — vendor export; analyses/GOOGL_2026-11-03/valuation/05_reverse-dcf.md, §§3–5]
- **Disconfirming evidence already visible:** Q3 revenue missed consensus by 0.26%, and management warned of a larger Q4 foreign-exchange drag, a harder Search comparison, and higher depreciation and data-centre costs; Q2 beat consensus by 2.41%, so a repeated-miss pattern is not proven. [Capital IQ Estimates Report — Consensus and Surprise, Q2–Q3 FY2026; Q3 FY2026 earnings call, prepared remarks]
