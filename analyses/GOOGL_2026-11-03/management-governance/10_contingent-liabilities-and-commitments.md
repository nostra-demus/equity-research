## 6. Contractual Commitments

Alphabet disclosed $924.4bn of material purchase commitments at 30 September 2026, including $802.1bn of contractual obligations longer than one year. These cover long-term technical infrastructure and inventory supply, energy agreements with take-or-pay and termination provisions, and content licences; some energy obligations run through 2054. $213.4bn is due within 12 months. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2; Note 10]

**Assessment: Amber.** The disclosure is specific enough to establish a very large cash-commitment burden, but it does not provide a disclosed annual capex-plan denominator or a like-for-like FY2025 bridge. It is therefore evidence of commitment concentration and cash-flow sequencing risk, not a quantified future loss.

## 8. Group / Related-Entity Contingencies

Crux AI is described as an Alphabet minority-owned equity-method joint venture and unconsolidated VIE. Alphabet disclosed up to $10.0bn of potential future data-centre and energy backstops for Crux, within $31.9bn of potential future VIE funding and financial support. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 5; Related-Party Transactions 09, A5-03 and A11-07]

**Assessment: Amber.** The support is separately visible and is about 1.5% of Q3 net worth. The filing does not disclose the commercial terms, collateral, fees or draw conditions, and the supplier/customer graph has no Crux relationship record within its limited recent-disclosure scope. That absence is not evidence of arm’s-length terms. [Relationships vendor graph, frozen 2026-11-03, scope notes]

Other subsidiary, associate and JV contingent liabilities are **not separately disclosed** in the reviewed notes, so a complete group-contingency test is **Insufficient Data**.

## 9. Provisioning Candor

Alphabet’s policy is to accrue legal liabilities when a loss is probable and reasonably estimable, and it records tax uncertainty. The reviewed Q3 balance includes $13.8bn of legal/regulatory accruals and $12.2bn of long-term income-tax payable, while the Android fine has been paid and new DMA and PriceRunner charges have been recorded. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Notes 10 and 14; Balance-sheet-survival 05, §3]

That is evidence against an obvious pattern of keeping every adverse matter as merely “possible.” It is not proof that all provisions are sufficient: Alphabet also says it cannot estimate a loss range for some material proceedings, and it does not provide a case-level bridge from provisions to settlements, judgments, reversals and payments. **Assessment: Amber; RF-CL-002 and RF-CL-003 are not triggered on available primary evidence.**

## 10. Forex & Derivative Exposure

Alphabet uses foreign-exchange, interest-rate, commodity, credit and equity derivatives for risk management. At 30 September 2026, foreign-exchange derivative notional amounts were $25.141bn cash-flow hedges, $6.987bn net-investment hedges and $54.084bn non-designated instruments. Credit derivatives were $48.012bn and equity derivatives $21.091bn; data-centre credit derivatives can run up to 15 years and are measured using Level 3 inputs. [Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, Note 3]

**Assessment: Amber.** The filing supports the presence of hedging activity, including FX forwards, options and collars; it does not disclose the company’s gross unhedged foreign-currency exposure. Credit and equity derivatives add complex counterparty and valuation exposure, even where they economically support data-centre arrangements. No exotic derivative-loss claim is proven from available data.

## 11. Conclusion

On the narrow quantified contingent-support measure, $98.5bn equals 14.5% of $677.549bn net worth and 74.5% of FY2025 PAT; it is a gross maximum, not an expected loss. The larger $924.4bn purchase-commitment figure is a separate multi-year cash obligation, and the balance-sheet module’s $1.1753tn gross envelope is a mixed-basis monitoring figure rather than a liability total. The clearest worsening is financial guarantees, from $5.7bn in FY2025 to $22.8bn in Q3 FY2026, alongside $31.9bn of potential VIE support including the $10bn Crux arrangement. Alphabet has recorded material legal and tax balances, but unquantified legal ranges, category gaps and incomplete related-JV terms leave provisioning candor **mixed rather than proven**.
