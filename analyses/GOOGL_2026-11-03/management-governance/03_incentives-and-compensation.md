## 4. Incentive Alignment Read

**Mixed — not assessable from the available compensation disclosure.** Alphabet has a real company-wide equity mechanism—RSUs generally vest over four years, and PSUs are part of the award population—but the pool does not reveal the CEO's or CFO's cash/equity mix, performance measures, weights, targets, or realized payouts. The single biggest signal is therefore the disclosure gap: without the definitive proxy, it is not proven whether executive pay rewards per-share value, returns, or absolute size.
