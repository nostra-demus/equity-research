## 9. Read

**Hygiene lapses, watch.** The public record confirms, rather than contradicts, Alphabet’s filing disclosure of a concentrated continuing competition-regulation burden: $13.8bn accrued, equal to 2.04% of September 2026 equity. The worst fact is the combination of final Android payment, new DMA and ad-tech penalties, and U.S. conduct remedies that can alter distribution and advertising practices. Reporting channels exist, but public complaint statistics and independent testing of effectiveness are not disclosed. This is a High red-flag input for synthesis (RF-REG-001), but the available evidence does not trigger a hard-disqualifier lock.
