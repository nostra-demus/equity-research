## 5. Minority-Shareholder Protection Read

**Weak / entrenched.** The indicated 70% independent board and split chair/CEO roles are positive formal features, but Page and Brin’s 52.7% voting power, Class C’s lack of votes, the ban on written consent and cumulative voting, board-controlled vacancies, and blank-check preferred stock leave other holders unable to impose change in practice. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp.21–22]

No current evidence establishes self-dealing, a director-integrity breach, or a transaction priced unfairly. The biggest minority-holder risk is therefore entrenchment, compounded by the absence of the 2026 DEF 14A and voting results needed to test committee independence, board renewal, RPT approvals and dissent.
