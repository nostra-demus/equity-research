# Related-Party & Group Forensics — GOOGL

**Scope and reporting basis.** Alphabet Inc. is a U.S. issuer reporting in USD under U.S. GAAP. This report uses the frozen evidence generation cited logically as `data/GOOGL/`. The full 2026 definitive proxy statement (and therefore its Item 404 table and vote records) is not in the frozen pool; the available June filing is a DEFA14A water-stewardship communication, not the definitive proxy. That is a material coverage limitation, not evidence that related-party transactions (RPTs) are nil. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 13; data/GOOGL/ frozen inventory, 2026-11-03]

## 1. RPT Intensity & the Applicable Regime (A5-01)

Applicable materiality rule per period: Alphabet is subject to U.S. SEC Item 404. It requires disclosure of transactions since the start of the prior fiscal year above **$120,000** in which a director, executive officer, 5% holder, or their immediate family has a direct or indirect material interest. It is a related-person disclosure threshold, not a consolidated-revenue materiality line. The FY2023–FY2025 annual reports incorporate Item 13 from the proxy, but the relevant 2024–2026 definitive proxy tables are absent from this frozen pool. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 13; FY2024 Annual Report, Item 13; data/GOOGL/ frozen inventory, 2026-11-03]
