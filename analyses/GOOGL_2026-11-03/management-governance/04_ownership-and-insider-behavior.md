## 5. Ownership / Insider Read

**Verdict: Mixed — founder ownership is stable-to-rising, but the dual-class structure leaves minority holders without voting parity.** The open-market record is modest net selling, not enough to infer a major change in insider conviction, and grants/exercises must not be confused with sales. Pledging is not disclosed in the supplied pool, so that risk cannot be cleared; the main proven issue is the 52.7% founder voting block rather than a detected pledge or market-conduct breach.
