## 3. Stated Strategy & Targets

Management's stated strategy is to use its AI stack—models, technical infrastructure, Cloud, and consumer products—to increase Search and YouTube utility and monetization while serving enterprise demand through Google Cloud. Alphabet's FY2025 filing says it has been AI-first since 2016 and expects technical-infrastructure investment to rise materially in 2026; the Q3 2026 call retained a $195bn–$205bn FY2026 CapEx range (midpoint $200bn). [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 — Business and Item 7 — Increased Investment in Technical Infrastructure; Q3 2026 earnings call, prepared remarks, 2026-10-28]

Management did not give formal revenue, EBITDA, or EPS guidance in the current guidance record. It instead gave directional Q4 comments on foreign-exchange drag, harder Search comparisons, and Cloud-margin pressure from third-party compute. [Q3 2026 earnings call, prepared remarks, 2026-10-28; earnings/04_guidance-consensus.md, §2]

## 5. Management Read

Pichai's 11-year Google CEO tenure, Schindler's 11-year commercial-lead tenure, and a 6.0-year median across the four named core roles support a stable senior operating group; Ashkenazi's 2.3-year CFO tenure is the main recent transition. [Capital IQ Professionals export, current professionals — Pichai, Ashkenazi, Schindler and Walker, data as of 2026-11-03]

The record shows delivery on the one mature, directional infrastructure commitment that can be tested—FY2025 CapEx rose 74.1%—but it does not prove a broad promise-keeping record because historical revenue, earnings, margin, and return targets were not available. The biggest management signal is a willingness to fund the AI build at scale: nine-month 2026 CapEx of $132.0bn was more than double the comparable prior period, while its return economics remain outside this agent's remit. [FY2024 Annual Report, MD&A — Capital Expenditures; FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 — Capital Expenditures; Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2 — Capital Expenditures]

For synthesis, apply the partial-data rule to the candor/promise read: no multi-period numerical management-guidance series was available, so disclosure candor cannot exceed 65 absent evidence from the candor specialist.
