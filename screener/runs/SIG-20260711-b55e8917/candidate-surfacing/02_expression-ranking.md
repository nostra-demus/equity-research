## 3. Ranking Rationale

CVNA ranks first because it is the only name in the universe where the thesis mechanism is the business — used-car retail is essentially all of its revenue, it directly owns and operates the seven franchise sites, and the WC-002 volume uplift (700+ units/month at Casa Grande) is a direct Carvana operational result, not a secondary effect. No unrelated revenue dilutes the signal. AN ranks second on the short side: new-vehicle revenue is the largest single segment (~46% of Q1 2026 revenue), the displacement is already visible in the reported numbers (new-vehicle same-store units down 9% in Q1 2026 YoY), and it is the simplest, highest-purity expression of the harmed franchised dealer party. What separates AN from LAD at rank three is speed-to-earnings: the displacement is already showing in AN's quarterly numbers, whereas LAD's 680-store breadth dilutes the effect per store. ALLY at rank four captures the named financing-partner relationship and has the highest secondary-tier exposure purity (auto finance is 70.4% of revenue), but the bank-holding-company rate/credit structure introduces co-factors that the thesis alone does not explain, keeping it below the pure operational pair. PAG and KMX rank fifth and sixth because the international revenue dilution (PAG) and the indirect channel-tailwind mechanism (KMX) each reduce the connection between the thesis mechanism and these companies' earnings in a way that prevents high-conviction primary expression.
