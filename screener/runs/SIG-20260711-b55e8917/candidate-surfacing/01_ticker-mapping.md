## 3. Unmappable Parties

| Party | Why no investable listed expression |
|---|---|
| HARM-002 — Commission-based automotive sales workforce / dealership staffing | The harm here falls on individual dealership salespeople whose commission-based roles are eliminated at acquired sites — not on a publicly listed staffing company with material exposure to automotive dealership floor-sales headcount. Broad-based staffing companies (e.g. Robert Half, Manpower, Kelly Services) derive a negligible and unquantifiable fraction of revenue from automotive commission salespeople specifically; their stocks would not move from dealership-workforce displacement at seven stores. The harm is real but it is embedded inside the dealership-group stocks (DIR-002 party, already mapped) as a labor-cost item, not a separate investable. No pure-play listed expression exists. |
